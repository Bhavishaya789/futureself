# Ratio Analysis and Leverage
minutes: 40
yt: CA Intermediate Ratio Analysis and Leverage
@study
Liquidity, solvency, profitability and efficiency ratios; operating and financial leverage.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Current ratio = CA/CL**: rule
- **Quick ratio = (CA - Inventory)/CL**: rule
- **Debt equity = Debt/Equity**: rule
- **DOL = Contribution/EBIT**: rule
- **DFL = EBIT/EBT**: rule
- **DCL = DOL × DFL**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Ideal current ratio is 2:1.
- Ideal quick ratio is 1:1.
- Debtors turnover equals credit sales divided by average debtors.
- Operating leverage arises from fixed operating costs.
- Financial leverage arises from fixed financial charges.
- Combined leverage is the product of operating and financial leverage.
- Du Pont analysis breaks ROE into margin, turnover and leverage.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In CA Intermediate, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
