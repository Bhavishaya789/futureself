## Before each visit
Confirm the time, travel and safety plan; carry ID, notebook, charged phone, water and materials; inform your mentor; dress appropriately for the community.

## During field work
- Introduce yourself and the purpose; greet respectfully.
- Listen more than you speak; use simple words and local language.
- Be punctual and reliable; maintain attendance sheets of sessions.
- Be neutral on religion, caste and politics; do not make promises you cannot keep.
- Take notes (who, what, when, outcomes, problems).
- Take photos only with consent; protect children's identity.
- Handle conflicts calmly; ask the host or mentor for help.

## The internship diary / log
Record every day or visit:
| Date | Hours | Activity | Learning | Problems/Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 03-11 | 3 | Survey in 12 households | Many cannot use UPI safely | Prepare a UPI safety handout |
Total hours = sum of the hours column; check that it meets the university requirement. Keep it honest and signed by the mentor if required.

## Evidence file
Attendance sheets, photos (with consent), survey forms and results, handouts, feedback forms, host letter, certificate. Store a digital copy (cloud folder) and a printed set.

## Using your technical skills
- Make a Google Form or paper survey; build simple charts in a spreadsheet.
- Create a poster or short video for awareness.
- A small prototype (for example a low-cost soil moisture sensor with ESP32 from the IoT module) with a clear user guide in the local language.

## Self-care
Teamwork, breaks, safe travel, drinking water, and sharing difficulties with your mentor.

## Professional behaviour
Respect the host's rules, keep confidences, give credit, and send thank-you notes.

## Moderate
P: List the items that a daily field diary entry should contain.
A: Date, place and working hours; activities done; people met (with permission); observations and data collected; what was learned; problems faced; plan for the next day; supervisor remarks or signature.

## Advanced
P: The internship needs 90 hours and you work 5 hours a day. How many days are needed? Describe how you will keep evidence while respecting privacy and consent.
A: 90 / 5 = 18 days. Evidence: a daily diary, attendance sheet signed by the supervisor, photographs, survey forms and certificates. Privacy and consent: ask permission before photographing people, avoid showing faces of children without parental consent, anonymise personal data (use codes instead of names in the report) and store forms safely.
