# Nazism and the Rise of Hitler
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 history nazism and the rise of hitler
@study
After Germany's defeat in World War I the Weimar Republic was weakened by the Treaty of Versailles, hyperinflation and the Great Depression. Adolf Hitler and the Nazi Party rose by promising to restore national pride and jobs. Hitler became Chancellor on 30 January 1933, set up a dictatorship, persecuted Jews and others and led Germany into World War II. The Holocaust killed millions.
## Deep dive: the Weimar Republic
After Germany's defeat, the Weimar Republic had a democratic constitution but was unpopular, associated with the harsh Treaty of Versailles. Germany lost territory and colonies, had to pay reparations and reduce its army. In 1923 hyperinflation ruined savings. The Great Depression from 1929 caused huge unemployment. People lost faith in democracy.
## Deep dive: Hitler's rise
Hitler used propaganda, mass rallies and promises of jobs and national pride. The Nazis won votes among the middle class, farmers and the unemployed. Hitler became Chancellor on 30 January 1933. The Reichstag Fire Decree and the Enabling Act removed civil rights and gave him dictatorial power. He banned other parties, controlled the press and set up the Gestapo and SS.
## Deep dive: persecution and war
Nazi racist ideology treated Jews as enemies. The Nuremberg Laws, Kristallnacht and concentration camps followed. The Final Solution led to the Holocaust, killing about six million Jews and many others. Germany invaded Poland in September 1939, starting World War II. Hitler died in 1945 and the Nuremberg Trials punished leaders.
