# The French Revolution
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 history the french revolution
@study
The French Revolution began in 1789 when the people of France rose against the absolute monarchy of Louis XVI. Heavy taxes on the Third Estate, a financial crisis, food shortages and new ideas of liberty and equality led to the storming of the Bastille on 14 July 1789. France became a constitutional monarchy, then a republic, and passed through the Reign of Terror before Napoleon came to power.
## Deep dive: causes
Louis XVI inherited an empty treasury. The wars and the support to the American War of Independence added debt. The privileged First and Second Estates paid no direct taxes, while the Third Estate paid taille, tithe and indirect taxes. Population growth raised the demand for grain and bread prices rose, causing a subsistence crisis. Thinkers such as Locke, Rousseau and Montesquieu spread ideas of equality, liberty and separation of powers.
## Deep dive: the events
The Estates General met on 5 May 1789 but voting by estate left the Third Estate powerless; they declared themselves the National Assembly and took the Tennis Court Oath. On 14 July 1789 the people stormed the Bastille. Peasants attacked manors in the Great Fear and feudal dues were abolished. The Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen followed, and the Constitution of 1791 made France a constitutional monarchy with active and passive citizens.
## Deep dive: republic, terror and Napoleon
War with Austria in 1792 led to the fall of the monarchy; France became a republic, and Louis XVI was executed in 1793. Under Robespierre the Reign of Terror killed thousands. After his fall the Directory ruled, and Napoleon seized power in 1799, becoming emperor in 1804. The Revolution spread ideas of liberty and equality worldwide. Women like Olympe de Gouges demanded equal rights, and slavery was abolished in 1794 but restored by Napoleon.
