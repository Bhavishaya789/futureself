**Speed** = distance/time; **velocity** has direction; **acceleration** = (v - u)/t. Equations of motion v = u + at, s = ut + at^2/2, v^2 = u^2 + 2as.
**Newton's laws:** inertia; F = ma; action-reaction. Force 1 N gives 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s^2. Example: 5 kg with a = 2 m/s^2 needs F = **10 N**. **Momentum** p = mv.
**Gravitation:** F = G m1 m2 / r^2; g = 9.8 m/s^2; weight W = m g; mass is constant, weight changes with place (about one-sixth on the Moon).
**Work, energy:** W = F s; KE = mv^2/2; PE = mgh; power = work/time. **Sound:** v = f x wavelength: f = 170 Hz and speed 340 m/s give wavelength **2 m**. **Density** = mass/volume: 54 g in 20 cm^3 is **2.7 g/cm^3** (aluminium).
## Deep dive: motion
**Distance and displacement.** Distance is the total path length (scalar, never negative). Displacement is the shortest straight-line change of position with direction (vector, can be zero or negative). For a full circle the distance is 2 pi r but the displacement is 0.
**Speed, velocity, acceleration.** Speed = distance / time. Velocity = displacement / time. Average speed = total distance / total time. Acceleration a = (v - u) / t, unit m/s^2; negative acceleration is retardation. Uniform motion means equal distances in equal times; non-uniform motion does not.
**Graphs.** On a distance-time graph the slope is the speed; a straight line means uniform speed, a horizontal line means rest. On a velocity-time graph the slope is the acceleration and the area under the graph is the displacement.
**Equations of uniformly accelerated motion.** v = u + a t; s = u t + (1/2) a t^2; v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s. Free fall uses a = g = 9.8 m/s^2 (take 10 for quick work). For an object thrown up, speed is zero at the top.
**Circular motion.** At constant speed v in a circle of radius r, the velocity changes direction, so the motion is accelerated; v = 2 pi r / T.
## Deep dive: force and laws of motion
**Force** changes the state of rest or motion, speed, direction or shape. Balanced forces do not change motion; unbalanced forces do. Unit: newton (1 N = 1 kg m/s^2).
**Newton's first law (inertia).** A body stays at rest or in uniform motion unless an unbalanced force acts. Inertia depends on mass. Examples: passengers jerk forward when a bus stops; dust falls off a beaten carpet.
**Momentum** p = m v (kg m/s). **Second law:** the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the force: F = m a. **Third law:** to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, acting on different bodies (rocket, recoil of a gun, walking). **Conservation of momentum:** in an isolated system total momentum before a collision equals total momentum after: m1 u1 + m2 u2 = m1 v1 + m2 v2.
**Friction** opposes relative motion; it is needed for walking and braking and is reduced by lubrication and wheels.
## Deep dive: gravitation, work and energy
**Universal law of gravitation.** F = G m1 m2 / r^2 with G = 6.67 x 10^-11 N m^2/kg^2. Doubling the distance cuts the force to a quarter. **Free fall acceleration** g = G M / R^2 = 9.8 m/s^2 on Earth, independent of the mass of the falling body; on the Moon g is about one sixth. **Mass** is the amount of matter (kg, same everywhere); **weight** W = m g (newton, varies with place).
**Thrust and pressure.** Thrust is force perpendicular to a surface; pressure = thrust / area (pascal). **Buoyancy.** An object in a fluid feels an upward force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced (Archimedes). It floats when its density is less than the fluid's. **Density** = mass / volume; relative density = density of substance / density of water.
**Work and energy.** Work W = F s cos(angle) (joule); no work when there is no displacement or the force is perpendicular to the motion. Kinetic energy = (1/2) m v^2; potential energy = m g h. Energy changes form but the total is conserved. Power = work / time (watt); 1 hp = 746 W.
**Sound.** A mechanical longitudinal wave needing a medium. v = f x lambda. Speed is greatest in solids, least in gases (about 340 m/s in air at room temperature). Echo needs a reflector at least 17 m away. Loudness depends on amplitude, pitch on frequency. Audible range 20 Hz to 20000 Hz.
