# Motion, force and gravitation (Class 9)
minutes: 20
yt: class 9 science force and motion
@study
**Speed** = distance/time; **velocity** has direction; **acceleration** = (v - u)/t. Equations of motion v = u + at, s = ut + at^2/2, v^2 = u^2 + 2as.
**Newton's laws:** inertia; F = ma; action-reaction. Force 1 N gives 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s^2. Example: 5 kg with a = 2 m/s^2 needs F = **10 N**. **Momentum** p = mv.
**Gravitation:** F = G m1 m2 / r^2; g = 9.8 m/s^2; weight W = m g; mass is constant, weight changes with place (about one-sixth on the Moon).
**Work, energy:** W = F s; KE = mv^2/2; PE = mgh; power = work/time. **Sound:** v = f x wavelength: f = 170 Hz and speed 340 m/s give wavelength **2 m**. **Density** = mass/volume: 54 g in 20 cm^3 is **2.7 g/cm^3** (aluminium).
