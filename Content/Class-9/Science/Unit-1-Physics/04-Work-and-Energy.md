Work done W = F s cos theta (joule). Kinetic energy = (1/2) m v^2; potential energy near the Earth = m g h; the sum is conserved. Power P = W/t (watt); 1 kWh = 3.6 x 10^6 J. Work done is zero when displacement is zero or perpendicular to the force.
## Deep dive: work
Work is done when a force produces displacement in the direction of the force: W = F x s (for a force along the displacement) or W = F s cos theta. The SI unit is the joule (1 J = 1 N m). Work is positive when force and displacement are in the same direction (pushing a trolley), negative when opposite (friction on a sliding block, or gravity on an object being lifted), and zero when there is no displacement or the force is perpendicular to the displacement (a satellite in circular orbit, a coolie carrying a load along a level road). Work is a scalar quantity.
## Deep dive: kinetic and potential energy
Energy is the capacity to do work, measured in joules. Kinetic energy of a body of mass m moving at speed v is E_k = (1/2) m v^2; from the work-energy theorem the work done on a body equals the change in its kinetic energy. Potential energy is stored energy because of position or configuration: gravitational potential energy E_p = m g h (g = 9.8 m/s^2, or 10 for simple work); elastic potential energy is stored in a stretched spring or bow. Forms of energy: mechanical, heat, light, sound, chemical, electrical, nuclear.
## Deep dive: conservation of energy and power
Law of conservation of energy: energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed from one form to another; the total energy before and after a transformation is the same. For a freely falling body the sum of kinetic and potential energies is constant: m g h = (1/2) m v^2 at the bottom, so v = sqrt (2 g h). Power is the rate of doing work: P = W/t (unit watt, 1 W = 1 J/s; 1 horsepower = 746 W). Commercial unit of energy: 1 kilowatt-hour = 1 kW x 1 h = 3.6 x 10^6 J = 1 unit of electricity. Average power = total energy consumed/total time. The efficiency of a machine = useful output/total input.
