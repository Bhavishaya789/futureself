# Force and Laws of Motion
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 science force and laws of motion
@study
A force changes or tends to change the state of rest or motion of a body. Newton's first law (law of inertia) states that a body stays at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by an unbalanced force. The second law states that the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force; F = ma. The third law states that to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. Momentum p = mv. In an isolated system the total momentum is conserved.
## Deep dive: inertia
Galileo observed that a ball rolling on a smooth surface keeps moving. Newton's first law formalised this: a body continues in its state of rest or uniform motion unless acted on by an external unbalanced force. Mass measures inertia. Examples: passengers lurch backward when a bus starts and forward when it stops.
## Deep dive: second law and momentum
Momentum p = mv. Rate of change of momentum is proportional to the force. F = (mv - mu)/t = ma. One newton gives 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s². Impulse = Ft = change in momentum, so a longer time of contact reduces the force.
## Deep dive: third law and conservation
Action and reaction are equal, opposite and act on different bodies, so they do not cancel. For an isolated system m1u1 + m2u2 = m1v1 + m2v2. A gun recoils with velocity v = -(m_bullet v_bullet)/m_gun.

Q: What is a force?
A: A push or pull that can change the state of rest or motion, speed or direction of a body.
Q: What is the SI unit of force?
A: Newton (N).
Q: What is 1 newton?
A: The force that gives a mass of 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s².
Q: What are balanced forces?
A: Forces that are equal and opposite and produce no change in motion.
Q: What are unbalanced forces?
A: Forces whose resultant is not zero and change the motion.
Q: State Newton's first law of motion.
A: A body remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless an external unbalanced force acts on it.
Q: What is inertia?
A: The natural tendency of an object to resist a change in its state of rest or motion.
Q: What is a measure of inertia?
A: Mass.
Q: Which has more inertia, a heavy or light body?
A: The heavy body.
Q: Name the types of inertia.
A: Inertia of rest, inertia of motion and inertia of direction.
Q: Give an example of inertia of rest.
A: A passenger falls backward when a bus suddenly starts.
Q: Give an example of inertia of motion.
A: A passenger moves forward when a moving bus stops suddenly.
Q: What is momentum?
A: The product of mass and velocity, p = mv.
Q: What is the SI unit of momentum?
A: kg m/s.
Q: Is momentum a scalar or vector?
A: Vector.
Q: State Newton's second law.
A: The rate of change of momentum is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force.
Q: What is the formula for force?
A: F = ma.
Q: What is impulse?
A: The product of force and time, equal to the change in momentum.
Q: Why does a cricketer pull back the hands while catching a ball?
A: To increase the time of contact and so reduce the force.
Q: State Newton's third law.
A: To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
Q: Do action and reaction act on the same body?
A: No, on different bodies.
Q: Give an example of Newton's third law.
A: A gun recoils backward when a bullet is fired forward.
Q: What is the law of conservation of momentum?
A: Total momentum of an isolated system remains constant when no external force acts.
Q: Write the momentum conservation equation for a collision.
A: m1u1 + m2u2 = m1v1 + m2v2.
Q: Why does a gun recoil?
A: By conservation of momentum, the backward momentum equals the bullet's forward momentum.
Q: What is friction?
A: A force that opposes the relative motion between surfaces in contact.
Q: What is the SI unit of impulse?
A: N s.
Q: Who gave the laws of motion?
A: Sir Isaac Newton.
Q: Which law explains the working of rockets?

Q: Why do we wear seat belts?
A: Because of inertia of motion, to stop us moving forward in a sudden stop.
Q: Why does dust fall off when a carpet is beaten?
A: Inertia of rest keeps the dust at rest while the carpet moves.
