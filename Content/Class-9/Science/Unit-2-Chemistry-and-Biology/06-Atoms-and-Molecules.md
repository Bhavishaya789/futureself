# Atoms and Molecules
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 science atoms and molecules
@study
Matter is made of atoms and molecules. Antoine Lavoisier gave the law of conservation of mass and Joseph Proust gave the law of constant proportions. Dalton's atomic theory explained these laws. Atoms combine in fixed ratios decided by valency to form molecules and compounds. The atomic mass unit u is 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. A mole is 6.022 × 10^23 particles (the Avogadro constant); the mass of one mole of a substance in grams equals its atomic or molecular mass in u. Formulae are written by crossing over valencies.
## Deep dive: laws of chemical combination
In a closed system the total mass of reactants equals the total mass of products (Lavoisier). A given compound always contains the same elements in the same ratio by mass (Proust): water has H:O = 1:8 and CO2 has C:O = 3:8 by mass.
## Deep dive: atoms, molecules and ions
Dalton's theory: matter consists of indivisible atoms; atoms of an element are identical; atoms combine in simple whole-number ratios. Molecules of elements are O2, N2, O3, P4, S8; molecules of compounds are H2O, CO2. Ions are charged atoms or groups.
## Deep dive: writing formulae
Write symbols with valencies, cross them over and reduce to simplest ratio: Mg (2) and Cl (1) give MgCl2; Al (3) and O (2) give Al2O3; Ca (2) and (OH) (1) give Ca(OH)2.
## Deep dive: mole concept
Atomic masses: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39, Ca 40. Molecular mass of H2O is 18 u, of CO2 is 44 u. One mole contains 6.022 × 10²³ particles and has mass equal to its molar mass in grams. n = m/M and N = n × NA.
