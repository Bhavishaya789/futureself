# Improvement in Food Resources: Animal Husbandry
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 science improvement in food resources: animal husbandry
@study
Animal husbandry is the scientific management of livestock. It includes cattle farming for milk and draught labour, poultry farming for eggs and meat, fish production (marine and inland) and bee-keeping (apiculture) for honey and wax. Improved breeds, proper feed, shelter and disease control increase production. Cross-breeding of Indian and exotic breeds gives high milk yield and disease resistance.
## Deep dive: cattle farming
Cattle are of milch and draught types. Milk yield depends on the lactation period. Cross-breeding of Indian cows (Sahiwal, Red Sindhi) with exotic breeds (Jersey, Brown Swiss) gives high yield and disease resistance. Feed consists of roughage and concentrates. Clean shelter and vaccination prevent diseases.
## Deep dive: poultry
Layers give eggs and broilers give meat. Exotic Leghorn and Indian Aseel breeds are crossed for better chicks. Broiler feed is rich in protein with vitamins A and K. Cleanliness and vaccination prevent poultry diseases.
## Deep dive: fish production
Marine fisheries catch pomfret, mackerel, tuna, sardines and Bombay duck. Mariculture rears mullets, bhetki, prawns, mussels, oysters and seaweed. Inland composite fish culture uses catla, rohu, mrigal, silver carp, grass carp and common carp together. Induced breeding uses hormones.
## Deep dive: bee-keeping
Honeybee species used are Apis cerana indica, Apis dorsata, Apis florea and Apis mellifera. Italian bee collects more honey, stings little and stays in the hive longer. Honey quality depends on pasturage.

Q: What is animal husbandry?
A: The scientific management of animal livestock.
Q: What are milch animals?
A: Female cattle kept for milk production.
Q: What are draught animals?
A: Cattle used for agricultural work like ploughing and carting.
Q: What is the scientific name of Indian cattle?
A: Bos indicus.
Q: What is the scientific name of buffalo?
A: Bos bubalis.
Q: Name some Indian breeds of cows for milk.
A: Sahiwal, Red Sindhi.
Q: Name some exotic breeds of cows.
A: Jersey and Brown Swiss.
Q: What is cross-breeding?
A: Breeding of different varieties to combine desirable traits.
Q: Why are Indian and exotic breeds crossed?
A: To get high milk yield and resistance to heat and disease.
Q: What is lactation period?
A: The period of milk production after the birth of a calf.
Q: What is roughage?
A: Fibre-rich bulk feed for cattle.
Q: What are concentrates?
A: Feed rich in proteins and other nutrients.
Q: What should cattle shelter provide?
A: Clean, well-ventilated, dry housing protecting from heat, cold and rain.
Q: Name causes of cattle diseases.
A: Parasites, bacteria and viruses.
Q: How are cattle protected from diseases?
A: By vaccination and cleanliness.
Q: What is poultry farming?
A: Raising domestic fowl for eggs and meat.
Q: Which poultry birds are raised for eggs?
A: Layers.
Q: Which poultry birds are raised for meat?
A: Broilers.
Q: Name an exotic breed of layer.
A: Leghorn.
Q: Name an Indian breed of poultry.
A: Aseel.
Q: What do broilers need in their feed?
A: Protein-rich feed with adequate fats and vitamins A and K.
Q: Why are vitamins needed in poultry feed?
A: To keep birds healthy and improve growth.
Q: What diseases affect poultry?
A: Infections by viruses, bacteria, fungi and parasites.
Q: What are the two sources of fish?
A: Capture of wild fish and fish culture.
Q: What are marine fisheries?
A: Fishing in the sea and oceans.
Q: Name some marine fish.
A: Pomfret, mackerel, tuna, sardines and Bombay duck.
Q: What is mariculture?
A: Culture of marine fish, prawns, mussels, oysters and seaweed in sea water.
Q: What are inland fisheries?
A: Fisheries in freshwater and brackish water such as rivers, ponds and lakes.
Q: What is composite fish culture?
A: Rearing several fish species together in a single pond.
Q: Name fish species used in composite culture.
A: Catla, rohu, mrigal, silver carp, grass carp and common carp.
Q: What is induced breeding?
A: Using hormones to get fish to breed in ponds.
Q: What is apiculture?
A: Rearing of honeybees for honey and wax.
Q: Name bee varieties used for honey production.
A: Apis cerana indica, Apis dorsata, Apis florea and Apis mellifera.
Q: Which bee is also called the Italian bee?
A: Apis mellifera.
Q: Name two advantages of the Italian bee.
A: High honey collection and it stings little.
Q: What is pasturage?
A: Flowers available for bees to collect nectar and pollen.
Q: Which products do bees give?
A: Honey and wax.
Q: What is the white revolution?
A: The rise in milk production, linked to Operation Flood.
Q: What is the blue revolution?
A: The rise in fish production.
