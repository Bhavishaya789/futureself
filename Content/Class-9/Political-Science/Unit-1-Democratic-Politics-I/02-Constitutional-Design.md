# Constitutional Design
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 democratic politics constitutional design
@study
A constitution is the supreme law of a country and sets out how the government is organised. The chapter compares South Africa, which framed a constitution after ending apartheid, with India, whose Constituent Assembly framed the Constitution between 1946 and 1949. It came into force on 26 January 1950. The Preamble states the guiding values of the Constitution.
## Deep dive: South Africa
South Africa practised apartheid, a system that classified people by race and denied rights to the black majority. Nelson Mandela and the ANC fought against it. Mandela spent 27 years in jail, mostly on Robben Island. When apartheid ended, leaders of the ANC and the white minority framed a constitution in 1996 based on forgiveness and equality. It is considered one of the most progressive constitutions in the world.
## Deep dive: making of the Indian Constitution
The Constituent Assembly, elected in 1946, had 299 members after Partition and met for nearly three years. Rajendra Prasad was its President and B. R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950. It drew on the freedom movement, the Motilal Nehru report (1928), the Karachi resolution (1931) and world constitutions.
## Deep dive: the Preamble
The Preamble declares India a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic and promises Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity. It embodies the philosophy of the Constitution. Socialist and Secular were added in 1976. The Preamble guides the interpretation of the Constitution.
