# Entrepreneurial skills
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 entrepreneurial skills
@study
An entrepreneur starts and runs a business, taking risks to earn profit and create jobs. Entrepreneurship needs ideas, planning, resources and risk-taking. Qualities: creativity, leadership, risk-taking, decision-making, hard work and communication. Profit = selling price - cost price; loss when cost price is more. Sectors: primary, secondary and tertiary; businesses can be small, medium or large. Government schemes such as Startup India and Make in India support entrepreneurs.
## Deep dive: who is an entrepreneur
An entrepreneur sees a need, develops an idea, arranges resources (money, people, materials) and starts a business, accepting the risk of loss for the chance of profit. Entrepreneurs create jobs, bring new products and help the economy grow. Famous examples include Dhirubhai Ambani, Ratan Tata, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw and Falguni Nayar. An intrapreneur creates new ideas inside an existing company.
## Deep dive: qualities and the business idea
Qualities: creativity, risk-taking, leadership, self-confidence, hard work, decision-making, communication, problem solving and willingness to learn from failure. A business idea comes from a need or problem in the market. Market research asks who the customers are, what they need and what competitors offer. A business plan describes the product, target customers, costs, price, marketing and expected profit.
## Deep dive: types and sectors of business
Businesses may be sole proprietorship (one owner), partnership (two or more owners), company or cooperative. By size: micro, small, medium and large. By sector: primary (farming, mining), secondary (manufacturing) and tertiary (services such as banking, transport and teaching). Sources of money: own savings, family, loans from banks, investors and government schemes.
## Deep dive: profit, loss and marketing
Profit = selling price - cost price (when SP is more than CP). Loss = cost price - selling price. Profit percent = profit/CP x 100 and loss percent = loss/CP x 100. Marketing is promoting and selling products; the 4 Ps are product, price, place and promotion. Customer satisfaction, quality and honest dealing build a brand. Entrepreneurs must keep accounts of income and expenses.
