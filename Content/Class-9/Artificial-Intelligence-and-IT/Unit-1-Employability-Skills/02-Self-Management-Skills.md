# Self-management skills
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 self management skills
@study
Self-management is the ability to manage your own thoughts, feelings, time and actions to reach goals. It includes knowing your strengths and weaknesses, positive attitude, self-confidence, self-motivation, good grooming and hygiene, time management, stress management, goal setting (SMART goals) and working well with others. Self-awareness is the base; SWOT analysis helps list strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
## Deep dive: self-awareness and SWOT
Self-awareness means knowing your own strengths, weaknesses, values, interests and habits. A SWOT analysis lists Strengths and Weaknesses (inside you) and Opportunities and Threats (outside you). Knowing them helps you pick a career and improve. Strengths are things you do well; weaknesses are areas to improve; opportunities are chances you can use; threats are things that may harm your progress.
## Deep dive: attitude, confidence and motivation
Attitude is the way you think and react. A positive attitude looks for solutions; a negative one sees only problems. Self-confidence is belief in your own abilities and grows with practice and success. Self-motivation is the inner drive to do work without being pushed; it comes from clear goals, rewards, interest and positive self-talk. Habits of positive people: they smile, thank others, learn from failure and keep trying.
## Deep dive: time and stress management
Time management means planning and using time wisely: make a to-do list, set priorities (urgent and important first), avoid procrastination and keep a timetable. A priority matrix has four boxes: urgent and important, important but not urgent, urgent but not important, and neither. Stress is the body's response to pressure; healthy ways to manage it are exercise, enough sleep, hobbies, deep breathing, talking to someone and a balanced diet.
## Deep dive: grooming, hygiene and working with others
Grooming is taking care of your appearance: clean neat clothes, combed hair, clean nails and polished shoes. Personal hygiene includes bathing daily, brushing teeth, washing hands and wearing clean clothes. Teamwork means working together for a common goal: respect others, listen, share work, communicate openly and accept feedback. Good etiquette includes punctuality, politeness and honesty.
