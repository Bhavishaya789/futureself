# Green skills
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 green skills
@study
Green skills are the knowledge and abilities needed to live and work in a way that protects the environment. They support sustainable development: meeting present needs without harming future generations. Key ideas are the 3 Rs (reduce, reuse, recycle), saving energy and water, renewable energy, waste management and green jobs. Pollution, global warming and loss of biodiversity are major environmental problems.
## Deep dive: environment and sustainable development
The environment is everything around us: air, water, soil, plants and animals. Sustainable development means meeting the needs of the present without harming the ability of future generations to meet theirs. It balances economic growth, social well-being and environmental protection. India has set targets for clean energy, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (17 goals) guide the world.
## Deep dive: pollution and climate change
Air pollution comes from vehicles, factories and burning waste; water pollution from sewage, chemicals and plastics; soil pollution from pesticides and garbage; noise pollution from traffic and loudspeakers. Burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide, which traps heat and causes global warming and climate change. The ozone layer protects us from ultraviolet rays and is damaged by CFCs.
## Deep dive: saving resources and energy
Save water by fixing leaks, harvesting rainwater and using drip irrigation. Save electricity by using LED bulbs, switching off unused devices and using star-rated appliances. Renewable energy sources (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, geothermal) are replaced naturally; non-renewable sources (coal, petroleum, natural gas) are limited. Public transport, cycling and carpooling reduce fuel use.
## Deep dive: waste management and green jobs
Waste is biodegradable (food, paper, leaves) or non-biodegradable (plastic, glass, metal). Segregation into wet and dry waste helps recycling; composting turns wet waste into manure. E-waste from old electronics needs safe disposal. Green jobs include solar panel technician, wind turbine technician, waste manager, water conservation expert, organic farmer and environmental consultant.
