@study
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of a machine to do tasks that normally need human intelligence: learning, reasoning, understanding language and recognising images. AI is a broad idea, Machine Learning (ML) is a way of achieving it by learning from data, and Deep Learning (DL) is ML using neural networks. The three main AI domains are Data Science, Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing. AI must be used ethically, avoiding bias and protecting privacy.
## Deep dive: what is AI
Intelligence is the ability to learn, understand, reason, solve problems and adapt. Artificial intelligence is intelligence shown by machines. The term was coined by John McCarthy in 1956. Examples: voice assistants (Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant), recommendation systems (YouTube, Netflix), face unlock, self-driving cars, spam filters, chess programs and chatbots. Not every smart-looking machine is AI: a washing machine that follows fixed steps is automated, but an AI system learns from data and improves.
## Deep dive: AI, ML and DL
AI is the wider goal. Machine learning is a subset of AI in which machines learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed for each case. Deep learning is a subset of ML that uses multi-layer artificial neural networks to learn from large amounts of data such as images and speech. Types of machine learning: supervised (labelled data), unsupervised (unlabelled data, finds groups) and reinforcement learning (learning by reward and penalty).
## Deep dive: domains of AI
Data Science works with numbers and data to find patterns and make predictions (sales forecasting, price prediction). Computer Vision lets machines see and understand images and videos (face recognition, medical scans, self-driving). Natural Language Processing (NLP) lets machines understand human language (translation, chatbots, voice assistants, sentiment analysis). Other related fields are robotics and expert systems.
## Deep dive: AI ethics, bias and society
AI ethics asks that AI be fair, transparent, safe and respect privacy. Bias occurs when training data is unfair or incomplete, so the AI treats some groups unfairly (a hiring tool preferring one gender). Privacy means protecting personal data. Other issues are job changes, deepfakes, safety and accountability: humans must remain responsible. Good practice: use diverse data, test for bias, keep humans in the loop and be honest about what AI can do.
