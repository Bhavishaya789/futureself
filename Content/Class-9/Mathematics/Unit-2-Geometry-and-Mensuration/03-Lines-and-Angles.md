@study
Complementary angles add to 90 degrees, supplementary to 180 degrees. A linear pair adds to 180 degrees; vertically opposite angles are equal. A transversal across parallel lines makes equal corresponding angles and equal alternate interior angles, and interior angles on the same side add to 180 degrees. The angle sum of a triangle is 180 degrees.
## Deep dive: pairs of angles
Complementary angles add to 90 degrees; supplementary angles add to 180 degrees. A linear pair is two adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a line, so they are supplementary. Vertically opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines are equal. The sum of angles around a point is 360 degrees.
## Deep dive: parallel lines and a transversal
When a transversal cuts two parallel lines: corresponding angles are equal, alternate interior angles are equal, and interior angles on the same side are supplementary. The converse is also true: if any one of these holds, the lines are parallel. Lines parallel to the same line are parallel to each other.
## Deep dive: triangle angle properties
The angle sum of a triangle is 180 degrees. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles. In an equilateral triangle each angle is 60 degrees; in an isosceles triangle the angles opposite equal sides are equal. For a triangle with angles in the ratio a:b:c, the actual angles are 180 a/(a+b+c) and so on.
