Triangle congruence criteria: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS and RHS. Angles of a triangle sum to 180 degrees; exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles. **Pythagoras:** in a right triangle c^2 = a^2 + b^2; e.g. 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 triples.
**Heron's formula:** area = sqrt(s (s - a)(s - b)(s - c)), s = (a + b + c)/2. For sides 13, 14, 15: s = 21 and area = sqrt(21 x 8 x 7 x 6) = **84 square units**.
**Mensuration:** cylinder volume pi r^2 h, curved surface 2 pi r h, total 2 pi r (r + h); with r = 7 and h = 10 (pi = 22/7): V = **1540 cubic units**, total surface area **748 square units**. Sphere volume (4/3) pi r^3, surface 4 pi r^2.
## Deep dive: triangles
**Angle sum.** The three angles of a triangle add up to 180 degrees. The exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles, and it is greater than either of them. In an equilateral triangle each angle is 60 degrees; in an isosceles triangle the angles opposite the equal sides are equal.
**Congruence.** Two triangles are congruent when they have the same shape and size. Criteria: SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and the angle between them), ASA (two angles and the side between them), AAS (two angles and any side), and RHS (right angle, hypotenuse and one side). AAA and SSA are not criteria. Corresponding parts of congruent triangles are equal (CPCT).
**Inequalities.** The sum of two sides of a triangle is greater than the third. The side opposite the larger angle is the longer side. The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right triangle.
**Pythagoras.** In a right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c: c^2 = a^2 + b^2. The converse also holds. Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25 and their multiples.
**Area.** Half base x height. For an equilateral triangle of side a: area = (sqrt 3 / 4) a^2. For a right triangle: half the product of the legs.
**Heron's formula.** With s = (a + b + c) / 2, area = sqrt (s (s - a)(s - b)(s - c)). Use it when the three sides are given but the height is not. Quadrilaterals can be split into two triangles by a diagonal and their areas added.
## Deep dive: surface areas and volumes
**Cuboid** (l, b, h): volume l b h; total surface area 2 (lb + bh + hl); lateral surface 2h (l + b); diagonal sqrt (l^2 + b^2 + h^2). **Cube** (side a): volume a^3; total surface 6a^2; lateral 4a^2; diagonal a sqrt 3.
**Cylinder** (r, h): curved surface 2 pi r h; total 2 pi r (r + h); volume pi r^2 h. **Cone** (r, h, slant l): l = sqrt (r^2 + h^2); curved surface pi r l; total pi r (r + l); volume (1/3) pi r^2 h. **Sphere** (r): surface 4 pi r^2; volume (4/3) pi r^3. **Hemisphere**: curved surface 2 pi r^2; total 3 pi r^2; volume (2/3) pi r^3.
**Units.** Area is in square units, volume in cubic units. 1 m^3 = 1000 litres. Always convert to the same unit first, and use pi = 22/7 when the radius is a multiple of 7.
