The angle sum of a quadrilateral is 360 degrees. In a parallelogram opposite sides and angles are equal and diagonals bisect each other. A quadrilateral is a parallelogram if a pair of opposite sides is equal and parallel. A rectangle has equal diagonals; a rhombus has perpendicular diagonals; a square has both. The mid-point theorem: the line joining mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and half of it.
## Deep dive: parallelogram properties
A parallelogram has: opposite sides equal; opposite angles equal; diagonals bisect each other; each diagonal divides it into two congruent triangles; consecutive angles are supplementary. Conversely a quadrilateral is a parallelogram if (i) opposite sides are equal, or (ii) opposite angles are equal, or (iii) diagonals bisect each other, or (iv) one pair of opposite sides is equal and parallel.
## Deep dive: special parallelograms
Rectangle: a parallelogram with one right angle; all angles 90 degrees; diagonals equal. Rhombus: a parallelogram with equal sides; diagonals perpendicular bisectors; diagonals bisect the angles. Square: both rectangle and rhombus; diagonals equal and perpendicular and bisect each other at 90 degrees. Kite: two pairs of adjacent equal sides; one diagonal bisects the other perpendicularly. Trapezium: one pair of parallel sides.
## Deep dive: mid-point theorem
In triangle ABC, if D and E are the mid-points of AB and AC then DE is parallel to BC and DE = (1/2) BC. Converse: a line through the mid-point of one side parallel to another side bisects the third side. Joining mid-points of the sides of any quadrilateral in order gives a parallelogram. A diagonal of a rhombus of side a has d1^2 + d2^2 = 4a^2.
