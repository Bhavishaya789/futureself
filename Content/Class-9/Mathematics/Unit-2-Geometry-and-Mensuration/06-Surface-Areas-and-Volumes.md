# Surface areas and volumes
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 surface areas and volumes
@study
Cuboid: TSA 2(lb + bh + hl), volume lbh. Cube: TSA 6a^2, volume a^3. Cylinder: CSA 2 pi r h, TSA 2 pi r (r + h), volume pi r^2 h. Cone: CSA pi r l, TSA pi r (l + r), volume one-third pi r^2 h, with l^2 = r^2 + h^2. Sphere: 4 pi r^2 and (4/3) pi r^3. Hemisphere: CSA 2 pi r^2, TSA 3 pi r^2, volume (2/3) pi r^3.
## Deep dive: cuboid and cube
For a cuboid with length l, breadth b, height h: lateral surface area 2h (l + b), total surface area 2(lb + bh + hl), volume lbh, diagonal sqrt (l^2 + b^2 + h^2). For a cube of edge a: lateral surface area 4a^2, total 6a^2, volume a^3, diagonal a sqrt 3. Volume is measured in cubic units; 1 litre = 1000 cubic cm and 1 cubic metre = 1000 litres.
## Deep dive: cylinder and cone
Cylinder (radius r, height h): CSA = 2 pi r h, TSA = 2 pi r (r + h), volume = pi r^2 h. Cone: slant height l = sqrt (r^2 + h^2), CSA = pi r l, TSA = pi r (r + l), volume = (1/3) pi r^2 h. Hollow cylinder with radii R and r: volume = pi h (R^2 - r^2).
## Deep dive: sphere and hemisphere
Sphere of radius r: surface area 4 pi r^2, volume (4/3) pi r^3. Hemisphere: CSA = 2 pi r^2, TSA = 3 pi r^2, volume (2/3) pi r^3. A spherical shell with outer R and inner r has volume (4/3) pi (R^3 - r^3). Doubling the radius of a sphere multiplies its surface area by 4 and its volume by 8.
