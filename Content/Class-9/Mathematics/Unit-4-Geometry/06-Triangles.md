@study
Two triangles are congruent if they have exactly the same shape and size. The congruence rules are SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS and RHS. In congruent triangles corresponding parts are equal (CPCT). Angles opposite equal sides of a triangle are equal and the sides opposite equal angles are equal. The sum of the angles of a triangle is 180° and an exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles. In a triangle, the larger side has the larger angle opposite it, and the sum of any two sides is greater than the third side.
## Deep dive: congruence
Triangles ABC and PQR are congruent (written ABC ≅ PQR) if all corresponding sides and angles are equal. Rules: SAS (two sides and included angle), ASA (two angles and included side), AAS, SSS and RHS. SSA and AAA do not ensure congruence. Corresponding parts of congruent triangles are equal (CPCT).
## Deep dive: properties of triangles
Angles opposite equal sides are equal; sides opposite equal angles are equal. The three angles of a triangle add up to 180°. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles. An equilateral triangle has all angles equal to 60°.
## Deep dive: inequalities
The larger side has the larger angle opposite and vice versa. The sum of any two sides is greater than the third, so lengths 3, 4, 8 cannot form a triangle.
