# Coordinate geometry
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 coordinate geometry
@study
A point is located by its abscissa (x) and ordinate (y) from two perpendicular number lines, the x-axis and y-axis, which meet at the origin (0, 0) and split the plane into four quadrants.
## Deep dive: Cartesian plane
The x-axis (horizontal) and y-axis (vertical) intersect at the origin O (0, 0). A point (a, b) has abscissa a (distance from the y-axis) and ordinate b (distance from the x-axis). Signs: Quadrant I (+, +), II (-, +), III (-, -), IV (+, -). A point on the x-axis is (a, 0) and a point on the y-axis is (0, b). (a, b) and (b, a) are different points unless a = b.
## Deep dive: reflections and plotting
Reflection of (a, b) in the x-axis is (a, -b); in the y-axis (-a, b); in the origin (-a, -b). To plot, move a units along the x-axis (right if positive, left if negative) and b units parallel to the y-axis. Four quadrants and the axes together cover the whole plane. Joining plotted points in order forms shapes such as triangles and rectangles; the area of a rectangle with vertices (0,0), (a,0), (a,b), (0,b) is |a| x |b|.
