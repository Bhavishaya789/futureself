# Climate
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 geography climate
@study
India has a tropical monsoon climate. Weather is the day to day condition of the atmosphere, and climate is the average over a long period. Factors affecting climate are latitude, altitude, pressure and winds, distance from the sea, ocean currents and relief. There are four seasons: cold weather, hot weather, advancing monsoon and retreating monsoon.
## Deep dive: controlling factors
Latitude: the Tropic of Cancer divides India, so the south is hot and the north is cooler. Altitude: temperature falls about 6.5 degrees per 1000 m, so hills are cooler. Pressure and winds: the system of the sub-continent, with seasonal reversal of winds. Distance from the sea: coastal places have a moderate climate and interior places have extremes. Relief: the Himalayas block the cold winds and the Western Ghats cause rain on the windward side.
## Deep dive: monsoon and seasons
The summer heating of land causes low pressure over the north-west, which draws moist south-west winds from the Indian Ocean. They burst on the Kerala coast around 1 June and cover the country by mid-July, bringing most of the rain (about 75 percent). In winter the cold weather is clear with western disturbances. Hot weather has loo, mango showers and Kalbaisakhi. In the retreating monsoon the sky clears and the Tamil Nadu coast receives rain from the north-east winds.
## Deep dive: importance
The monsoon is the pulse of Indian farming and the economy. Delay or failure causes drought, and excess causes floods. The cycle of seasons unites the people of India culturally.
