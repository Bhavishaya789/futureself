# Population
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 geography population
@study
Population is studied through size, distribution and composition. The Census of 2011 gave India a population of about 1.21 billion, which is 17.5 percent of the world. Uttar Pradesh is the most populous state, and Sikkim the least. Population changes through births, deaths and migration. India's National Population Policy of 2000 aims at family welfare and a stable population.
## Deep dive: size and distribution
India's population in 2011 was 1,210,854,977, about 17.5 percent of the world, though India has only 2.4 percent of the area. Uttar Pradesh has about 200 million people, nearly 16 percent of the country. Density was 382 per sq km. Densely populated places are the plains, deltas and coasts with fertile soil and rainfall; sparsely populated places are mountains, deserts and forests such as Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram and Sikkim.
## Deep dive: growth, migration and structure
Growth depends on birth rate, death rate and migration. Since 1921 the death rate has fallen faster than the birth rate, causing rapid growth. Migration may be internal or international, rural to urban, for work or education. Age groups: children 0-14, working 15-59 and aged 60 and above. Adolescents (10-19 years) are about one-fifth of the population. India's occupational structure shows a big primary sector, and health and sanitation need improvement.
## Deep dive: policy
The National Population Policy 2000 aims to meet the needs of reproductive health care, reduce infant mortality, promote delayed marriage and achieve a stable population. Education of girls, health care and better quality of life are keys to controlling population.
