# India: size and location
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 geography india: size and location
@study
India lies in the northern hemisphere between latitudes 8 degree 4 minute N and 37 degree 6 minute N and longitudes 68 degree 7 minute E and 97 degree 25 minute E. The Tropic of Cancer divides the country almost in half. Area is 3.28 million sq km, about 2.4 percent of the world, the seventh largest country. The Indian Standard Time is based on the 82 degree 30 minute E meridian. The Suez Canal made India closer to Europe.
## Deep dive: location and extent
India lies entirely in the northern and eastern hemispheres. The mainland extends from Kashmir to Kanyakumari and from Arunachal Pradesh to Gujarat. The mainland lies between 8 degree 4 minute N and 37 degree 6 minute N, but the Indira Point in the Great Nicobar reaches 6 degree 45 minute N. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram, so the northern half is in the temperate zone and the southern half in the tropical zone. With a land frontier of 15200 km and a coastline of 7516.6 km, India is a vital link between East and West.
## Deep dive: time
The earth rotates 360 degrees in 24 hours, so places 15 degrees apart differ by one hour and each degree is 4 minutes. The sun rises two hours earlier in Arunachal Pradesh than in Gujarat. To avoid confusion, the 82 degree 30 minute E meridian near Naini (Prayagraj) is the Indian Standard Time, which is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time.
## Deep dive: India and the world
The Indian Ocean gives India a central position between West Asia, Africa and Europe on one side and South-East and East Asia on the other. Deccan Peninsula extends into the Indian Ocean. After the opening of the Suez Canal in 1869 the distance to Europe fell by about 7000 km. India has been in contact with the world through land routes and mountain passes since ancient times, which enriched Indian culture. Neighbours: Pakistan and Afghanistan in the north-west, China, Nepal and Bhutan in the north, Myanmar and Bangladesh in the east; Sri Lanka and the Maldives are island neighbours.
