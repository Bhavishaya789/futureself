# Physical features of India
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 geography physical features of india
@study
India has a variety of physical features: the Himalayan mountains, the Northern Plains, the Peninsular Plateau, the Indian Desert, the Coastal Plains and the Islands. The Himalayas are young fold mountains made when the Indian plate collided with the Eurasian plate. They have three parallel ranges: Himadri, Himachal and Shiwaliks. The Northern Plains are made of alluvium from the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra.
## Deep dive: the Himalayas
The Himalayas stretch 2400 km from the Indus to the Brahmaputra, about 400 km wide in Kashmir and 150 km in Arunachal Pradesh. The Himadri (Greater Himalayas) is the most continuous range with an average height of 6000 m and contains the highest peaks, including Kanchenjunga. The Himachal (Lesser Himalayas) is 3700 to 4500 m high and has ranges such as Pir Panjal and the hill stations Shimla, Mussoorie and Nainital. The Shiwaliks (900 to 1100 m) are made of unconsolidated sediments, with the duns such as Dehradun between them and the Himachal. The Purvanchal hills (Patkai, Naga, Manipur and Mizo hills) lie in the east.
## Deep dive: plains, plateau and desert
The Northern Plains (about 7 lakh sq km) are formed of alluvium from the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra, running 2400 km east to west. They have Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar and Khadar, and are the most fertile and densely populated region. The Peninsular Plateau is an old, stable block, with the Central Highlands north of the Narmada and the Deccan Plateau south of it, bounded by the Western Ghats (continuous) and Eastern Ghats (broken). The Thar Desert has barchans and receives less than 150 mm of rain.
## Deep dive: coasts and islands
The western coastal plain is narrow, divided into Konkan, Kannad and Malabar coasts. The eastern plain is wider with deltas of the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri, divided into the Northern Circar and the Coromandel coast. Lakshadweep, made of coral, has about 36 islands. The Andaman and Nicobar are bigger, more numerous and scattered, and include the active volcano Barren Island.
