# The little girl
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english the little girl
@study
A short story by Katherine Mansfield. Kezia is afraid of her stern father, who she thinks does not love her, and she finds comfort in her grandmother. After she tears the pages of his speech to make a pincushion as a birthday present, he scolds and punishes her. Later her mother is ill, and one night, when she has a bad dream, her father comforts her, and she learns that he loves her.
## Deep dive: plot
Kezia, a small girl, feels her father is a frightening figure: he is stern, busy and does not smile. Her grandmother is her comfort. On the day of her father's birthday she wants to make a present: a pincushion. She stuffs it with paper, which turns out to be the pages of her father's speech for the Port Authority. When he learns this he is angry and strikes her. She is terrified and sad. Soon her mother is ill and Kezia must sleep alone. At night a nightmare wakes her, and her father takes her into his bed and comforts her. She realises how much he loves her.
## Deep dive: characters
Kezia is sensitive and innocent. Her father is the strict head of the family who feels deeply but does not show it. The grandmother is gentle. The mother plays a small role.
## Deep dive: themes
Misunderstanding between parent and child, the need for love and reassurance, and how a moment of tenderness can change a relationship. The story is told in a simple style from the child's point of view.
