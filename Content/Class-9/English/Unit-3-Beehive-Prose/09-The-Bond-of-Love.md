# The bond of love
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english the bond of love
@study
A true story by Kenneth Anderson about a sloth bear cub named Bruno that the family adopted. The family fed him from a bottle, he became a playful and affectionate pet, and he grew very attached to them. The story shows the love between humans and animals, and the sadness of parting when the animal grew too big to be kept at home.
## Deep dive: story
The Anderson family adopts a sloth bear cub and names him Bruno. They feed him milk from a bottle and care for him like a child. Bruno becomes a funny and loving pet who follows them around. As he grows bigger and stronger he can no longer be kept safely at home. The family is sad to let him go.
## Deep dive: message
Animals feel love and attachment just like humans, and kindness is returned with love. A wild animal belongs to nature, and sometimes love means letting go.
## Hindi saransh
Anderson parivar ek bhaalu ke bachche Bruno ko paalta hai; unke beech pyaar ka rishta ban jata hai.
> Love between humans and animals is real and lasting.
