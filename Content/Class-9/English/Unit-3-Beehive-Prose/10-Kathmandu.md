# Kathmandu
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english kathmandu
@study
A travel account by the Indian writer Vikram Seth. He visits Kathmandu, the capital of Nepal, and describes two famous places: the Hindu temple of Pashupatinath and the Buddhist stupa of Boudhanath. He sees the crowds, hears the temple bells, and notes how Hindu and Buddhist practices live side by side.
## Deep dive: places
Vikram Seth describes Kathmandu's Pashupatinath temple, a Hindu shrine of Lord Shiva near the Bagmati river, and Boudhanath, a large Buddhist stupa. He notes crowds of worshippers, the sound of bells, the offerings and the smells of incense.
## Deep dive: theme
The chapter shows how Hindu and Buddhist traditions coexist in Kathmandu. It also shows that travel writing observes people, places and culture with curiosity and respect.
## Hindi saransh
Vikram Seth Kathmandu ke Pashupatinath mandir aur Boudhanath stupa ka varnan karte hain.
> Kathmandu shows how two faiths can share space in harmony.
