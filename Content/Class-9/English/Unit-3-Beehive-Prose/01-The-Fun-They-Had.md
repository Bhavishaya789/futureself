# The fun they had
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the fun they had
@study
A science fiction story by Isaac Asimov set in the year 2157. Eleven-year-old Margie and thirteen-year-old Tommy live in a world where children are taught alone at home by mechanical teachers. Tommy finds a real paper book about school in the old days and they wonder at a time when children studied together with a human teacher. Margie feels that those children had fun.
## Deep dive: plot
The story opens with Margie writing in her diary on 17 May 2157 that Tommy found a real book. It was very old, its pages yellow and crinkly, and the words stood still instead of moving on a screen. They talk about how, long ago, books were printed on paper and could be thrown away after reading. Margie has had trouble with her geography lessons, so her mother called the County Inspector, who adjusted the mechanical teacher and said that it was set too fast. Margie then reads about a school in which children studied together and had a human teacher, and she imagines how much fun they had. The story ends with her thinking about those happy children.
## Deep dive: setting and characters
The year is 2157. Margie is lonely and bored with lessons. Tommy is older and more interested in the real book. The mechanical teacher is a machine with a screen and slot for homework. Mrs Jones is a concerned mother who relies on technology. The Inspector is calm and practical. Margie, a child of her time, finds old schooling strange and wonderful.
## Deep dive: themes and literary devices
Themes: the loss of human contact in a technological world, the joy of shared learning, change and nostalgia. The author uses irony (the children find a printed book strange while we find it ordinary), contrast between old and new schools, and a simple child's viewpoint. The story warns that machines may help but cannot replace teachers and friends.
