# Packing
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english packing
@study
A humorous extract from Three Men in a Boat by Jerome K. Jerome. The narrator, J., with his friends George and Harris and the dog Montmorency, plans a boat trip on the Thames. The narrator boasts that he is an excellent packer and takes charge, while George and Harris lounge. The packing turns into a comic disaster: things are lost, broken and re-packed again.
## Deep dive: story
The narrator J. announces that he will pack for the boating trip and says he is an expert. George and Harris sit back and let him do all the work. As he packs, he forgets some things, packs others in the wrong place and has to open everything again. Montmorency helps by getting in the way. The comic effect comes from the narrator's exaggerated claim and his repeated failure.
## Deep dive: devices
Jerome uses humour, exaggeration, irony and self-mockery. The narrator is the butt of his own joke. The language is light, simple and conversational.
## Deep dive: lesson
Boasting about one's skill can lead to embarrassment, and a task is best done with the help of others.
## Hindi saransh
Teen dost naav yatra ke liye saaman pack karte hain aur sab kuch gadbad ho jata hai.
> Boasting before doing invites trouble; the humour comes from the narrator's overconfidence.
