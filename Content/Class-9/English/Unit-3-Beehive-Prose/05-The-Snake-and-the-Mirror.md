# The snake and the mirror
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english the snake and the mirror
@study
A humorous story by Vaikom Muhammad Basheer. A vain homoeopathic doctor, who dreams of marrying a rich and beautiful woman doctor, sits in his small rented room on a hot night. A snake drops onto his arm, he is frozen with fear and thinks it is a punishment for his pride. The snake becomes distracted by its own reflection in the large mirror, and the doctor slips away to safety.
## Deep dive: story
The narrator, a homoeopathic doctor, tells a story from his youth. After dinner at a cafeteria he returns to his rented room, takes off his suit and shirt, and opens two windows because of the heat. He sits with a medical book, then looks at himself in the large mirror and daydreams about marrying a rich and beautiful woman doctor. The rats stop their noise, and he hears a plunging sound. A snake falls on his arm. He is frozen and thinks it is God's punishment for his arrogance. Then the snake sees its own reflection in the mirror and moves toward it; the doctor slips out of the room and escapes.
## Deep dive: devices
Humour comes from the contrast between the doctor's pride and his helplessness. The author uses irony (a vain doctor saved by a mirror), suspense in the silence of the rats and a first-person narration. The snake's own vanity mirrors the doctor's.
## Deep dive: themes
Vanity and pride; the unpredictable nature of life and fate; helplessness before danger; and how humour can make a serious event enjoyable.

> Vanity is foolish; fear and humour mix in this story.
