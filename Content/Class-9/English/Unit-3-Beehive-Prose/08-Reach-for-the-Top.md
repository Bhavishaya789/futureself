# Reach for the top
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english reach for the top
@study
Two inspiring life stories. Part I is about Santosh Yadav, the mountaineer from Haryana who climbed Mount Everest twice, in 1992 and 1993, and was the first woman to do so. Part II is about Maria Sharapova, the Russian tennis player who left home as a child to train in the USA and won Wimbledon at the age of seventeen.
## Deep dive: Santosh Yadav
Santosh Yadav, from Haryana, loved the mountains and trained as a mountaineer. She climbed Mount Everest in 1992 and again in 1993 and became the first woman to do it twice. Her climbs show that courage and discipline can overcome fear and hardship. She was honoured for her achievements.
## Deep dive: Maria Sharapova
Maria Sharapova was born in Russia in 1987. As a young girl she showed great talent for tennis, and her father took her to the United States to train. Life was hard, but she worked with great determination. In 2004, at the age of seventeen, she won the Wimbledon title by defeating Serena Williams.
## Deep dive: message
Both women set high goals, worked hard, accepted sacrifices and reached the top. The chapter encourages young people to follow their dreams.
