# If I were you
minutes: 30
yt: class 9 english if i were you
@study
A one-act play by Douglas James. An intruder, a criminal, enters a house planning to kill its owner and take his place. The owner stays calm, outwits the intruder with clever talk and the police finally arrive and arrest him. The play is a thriller built on suspense and quick wit.
## Deep dive: plot
An intruder enters a house meaning to kill the owner and take his identity. The owner, instead of panicking, talks cleverly and keeps him engaged. As the play moves, the owner changes the situation in his favour. The police arrive and arrest the intruder.
## Deep dive: devices
The play builds suspense through dialogue, uses irony and has few characters and one setting. It teaches that intelligence and calm are stronger than force.

Ek apradhi ghar ke malik ki jagah lena chahta hai; malik apni chaaltaa se use pakda deta hai.
> Presence of mind can defeat a dangerous enemy.
