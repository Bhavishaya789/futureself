# The Happy Prince
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the happy prince
@study
A fairy tale by Oscar Wilde (1854 to 1900). A statue of the Happy Prince, covered with gold leaf, with sapphire eyes and a ruby on his sword, stands high above a city. A swallow delays his migration to Egypt and, at the Prince's request, carries the jewels and gold to poor people. The swallow dies of cold and the statue's leaden heart breaks. In the end an angel takes the heart and the dead bird to heaven.
## Deep dive: story
The Happy Prince is a statue above the city, covered with gold leaf, with sapphire eyes and a ruby on his sword. A swallow stops under his feet on the way to Egypt. The Prince, who can see the misery of the poor, begs the swallow to take his ruby, then his sapphire eyes and then his gold to the needy. The swallow grows weak in the cold and dies at the Prince's feet; the leaden heart breaks. The ungrateful city melts the shabby statue but the heart will not melt. An angel carries the heart and the dead swallow to heaven.
## Deep dive: message
The tale praises compassion, sacrifice and friendship, and criticises a society that ignores the poor.
