# Weathering the storm in Ersama
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english weathering the storm in ersama
@study
An account by the Indian writer and social worker Harsh Mander of the super cyclone that struck Odisha on 29 October 1999. Ersama in Jagatsinghpur district was devastated, thousands died and survivors were left without homes. The chapter describes the survivors' courage and how relief and rebuilding began with the help of volunteers.
## Deep dive: story
The super cyclone of 29 October 1999 struck the Odisha coast with ferocious winds and a surge of sea water. Ersama, in Jagatsinghpur district, was devastated. The chapter describes the shock of the survivors and then their courage as they came together, with volunteers, to rebuild their lives.
## Deep dive: message
The chapter shows human courage, cooperation and hope in the face of natural disaster.
