# A house is not a home
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english a house is not a home
@study
A story by Zan Gaudioso. It shows that a home is not made of walls and furniture but of the love and warmth of the people who live in it. When a house is lost, the family realises that what matters is being together.
## Deep dive: story
The story reflects on what makes a house a home. The family loses or leaves the house but finds that they are still a home so long as they are together. Walls, roofs and furniture can be replaced, but love and family ties cannot.
## Deep dive: message
A home is created by people, not by buildings. The story urges us to cherish loved ones.
## Hindi saransh
Ghar eent patthar se nahi, pyaar aur parivar se banta hai.
> Home is where the family is.
