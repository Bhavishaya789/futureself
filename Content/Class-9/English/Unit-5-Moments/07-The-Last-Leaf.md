# The Last Leaf
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the last leaf
@study
A short story by the American writer O. Henry (William Sydney Porter). In Greenwich Village two young artists, Sue and Johnsy, share a studio. Johnsy falls ill with pneumonia and believes she will die when the last leaf falls from the ivy outside her window. The old painter Behrman paints a leaf on the wall in the night. Johnsy recovers, but Behrman dies of pneumonia.
## Deep dive: story
Sue and Johnsy live in Greenwich Village. When Johnsy falls ill with pneumonia she imagines that she will die when the last leaf falls from the ivy outside her window. Sue tells old Behrman, who scoffs but is moved. On a stormy night the last leaf remains on the wall all night and through the next day. Johnsy takes it as a sign and regains her will to live. Sue then tells her that Behrman has died of pneumonia: he painted the leaf on the wall in the storm.
## Deep dive: message
The painted leaf is Behrman's masterpiece: an act of selfless love. The story shows the power of hope and sacrifice.
