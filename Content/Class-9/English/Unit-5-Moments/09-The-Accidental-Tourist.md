# The Accidental Tourist
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the accidental tourist
@study
A humorous essay by the American travel writer Bill Bryson. He recounts the funny mishaps and awkward moments that he faces as a traveller, from airports to hotels, and laughs at himself. He shows that travel is full of surprises, many of them embarrassing.
## Deep dive: essay
Bill Bryson tells of the small disasters of a traveller: lost luggage, awkward manners, confusing places and unexpected events. He laughs at himself with exaggeration. The essay is light, easy to read and meant to make the reader smile.
## Deep dive: message
Travel is full of unexpected incidents. A sense of humour makes them easier to bear.
## Hindi saransh
Bill Bryson apne yatra ke hasyaspad anubhav sunate hain.
> Laugh at your mishaps while travelling.
