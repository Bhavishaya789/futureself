# Iswaran the storyteller
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english iswaran the storyteller
@study
A short story by R. K. Narayan. Mahesh, the narrator, describes his cook Iswaran, who is a wonderful storyteller. His stories are full of ghosts and strange events and he says he has seen them. The narrator enjoys them even though he doubts that they are true.
## Deep dive: story
Mahesh describes Iswaran, his cook, as a wonderful storyteller. Iswaran tells stories of ghosts and strange happenings and says that he has himself witnessed them. Mahesh listens with delight although he does not fully believe them. The story shows the humour and charm of ordinary people.
## Deep dive: message
Imagination and storytelling make life more colourful. Narayan observes people with affection and gentle humour.
## Hindi saransh
Iswaran ek ras bhara kahani sunane wala rasoiya hai jiski kahaniyan rochak hoti hain.
> A good story-teller can hold anyone's attention.
