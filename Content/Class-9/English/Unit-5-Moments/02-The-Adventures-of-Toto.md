# The adventures of Toto
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the adventures of toto
@study
A humorous story by the Indian writer Ruskin Bond. His grandfather buys a mischievous little monkey named Toto for a few rupees and keeps him at home. Toto gets into all kinds of trouble, upsetting the family, especially Grandmother, but the grandfather keeps his affection for him.
## Deep dive: story
The narrator's grandfather brings home a young monkey named Toto. The family finds him full of mischief and he keeps causing trouble. Grandmother does not approve, while Grandfather loves him. The story is told with warm humour and shows how a pet can bring both delight and disorder.
## Deep dive: message
The story teaches kindness to animals and shows the love between a grandfather and his pet. It is a light, humorous account of life with a mischievous monkey.
