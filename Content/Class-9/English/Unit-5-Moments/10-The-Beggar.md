# The Beggar
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the beggar
@study
A short story by the Russian writer Anton Chekhov (1860 to 1904). A lawyer named Sergei meets a beggar, Lushkoff, who claims to be a former teacher. Sergei offers him wood-chopping work to earn his food. The cook Olga helps him and encourages him to change. Years later the beggar has reformed and gratefully thanks Olga.
## Deep dive: story
Sergei, a lawyer, meets a beggar called Lushkoff and gives him wood to chop. Lushkoff is unwilling. Olga, the cook, takes pity on him, helps him chop the wood and talks to him kindly. Moved by her kindness, he changes his ways and finds honest work. Years later he meets Sergei again as a reformed man and thanks Olga.
## Deep dive: message
Kindness and encouragement can change a person's life. Charity should give people dignity as well as money.

Bhikhari Lushkoff mehnat karna seekhta hai aur Olga ki dayalu madad se naya jeevan paata hai.
