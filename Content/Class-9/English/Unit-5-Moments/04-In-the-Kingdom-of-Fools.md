# In the kingdom of fools
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english in the kingdom of fools
@study
A folk tale from Karnataka. A guru and his disciple arrive in a kingdom where everything, whether vegetables or sweets, is sold at the same price. The guru wants to leave but the greedy disciple wants to stay. The foolish king and his ministers bring trouble, and the guru finally saves the disciple by a clever trick.
## Deep dive: story
A guru and his disciple travel to a strange kingdom where all things are priced the same. The guru senses danger and wants to go, but the greedy disciple loves the cheap food. The foolish king and ministers punish people without sense. The disciple lands in trouble and the guru rescues him with a clever plan.
## Deep dive: message
The tale satirises foolish rulers and warns against greed. It shows that a wise person can save a friend from danger.
## Hindi saransh
Murkhon ke rajya me guru apne lalchi shishya ko apni chaaturi se bachata hai.
> Wisdom is better than greed.
