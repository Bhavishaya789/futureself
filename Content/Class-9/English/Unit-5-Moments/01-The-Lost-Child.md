# The lost child
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the lost child
@study
A short story by the Indian writer Mulk Raj Anand. A small boy goes with his parents to a spring fair. He asks for sweets, toys, balloons and flowers, but they do not buy them, so he lags behind, looks at the shops, and is soon lost in the crowd. Frightened, he cries for his parents and in the end wants only them, not the toys.
## Deep dive: story
A small boy goes with his parents to a spring fair in a village. He sees sweets, toys, balloons and flowers and begs for them. His parents move on and he lags behind, absorbed in the shops. Suddenly he finds himself alone in a crowd, panics and cries for his parents. Now toys and sweets mean nothing. He wants only to be with his mother and father.
## Deep dive: message
The story shows that a child's deepest need is love and security. It also shows how easily a child can be lost in the world of attractions.
