# No men are foreign
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english no men are foreign
@study
A poem by the English poet James Kirkup (1918 to 2009). It says that all human beings are alike and belong to one earth, breathe the same air and share the same sun. War and hatred are therefore wrong, and we should live as one family.
## Deep dive: meaning
The poet says that no man is foreign. The same sun warms everyone, the same air is breathed by all, and the same earth holds all. People differ in language and country, but they are alike in blood and heart. War turns brothers into enemies and fills the earth with graves. The poet asks us to remember this and love one another.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses repetition, simple direct language and images of nature to express brotherhood. It carries a strong anti-war message.
## Hindi saransh
Sab manushya ek jaise hain; koi vidheshi nahi, isliye yuddh galat hai.
> All human beings are one family.
