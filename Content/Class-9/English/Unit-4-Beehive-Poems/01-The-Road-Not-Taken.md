# The road not taken
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the road not taken
@study
A poem by the American poet Robert Frost (1874 to 1963), first published in 1916 in the book Mountain Interval. The speaker stands where two roads divide in a yellow wood in autumn, chooses the one less travelled and says that this choice made all the difference.
## Deep dive: meaning
A traveller in a yellow autumn wood comes to a fork. He looks down one path as far as he can, then takes the other, which seems grassy and less worn. Yet the two are really almost the same. He keeps the first for another day, knowing that he will probably never return. Years later he will say he took the road less travelled and that it made all the difference.
## Deep dive: devices
The road is a metaphor for life choices. The poem uses imagery of the autumn wood, rhyme ABAAB, a reflective tone and a quiet ending. It suggests that people often explain their choices afterwards.
