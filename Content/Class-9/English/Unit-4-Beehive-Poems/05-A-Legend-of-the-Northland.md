# A legend of the Northland
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english a legend of the northland
@study
A ballad by the American poet Phoebe Cary (1824 to 1871). Saint Peter visits a village and asks an old woman for food. She bakes a cake for him, but each time she thinks it is too big, she bakes a smaller one. In the end she refuses and is turned into a woodpecker. The poem warns against greed and selfishness.
## Deep dive: story
Saint Peter, in disguise, visits an old woman and asks for something to eat. She puts a little dough in the oven, but the cake comes out too large, so she makes a smaller one. This happens again and again. At last she refuses to give him anything. As punishment she is turned into a woodpecker who must peck for food for ever.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem is a ballad with a narrative style, a magical transformation and a clear moral. It uses repetition to show the woman's greed grow.

Lalchi budhiya ko sant ki seva se mana karne par chidiya bana diya gaya.
> Greed brings punishment; generosity brings blessing.
