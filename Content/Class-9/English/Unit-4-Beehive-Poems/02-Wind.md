# Wind
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english wind
@study
A poem by the Tamil poet Subramania Bharati (1882 to 1921), translated into English. The poet asks the wind not to come too strongly. It breaks windows, shutters and doors, scatters papers and shakes houses and teaches that only the strong survive.
## Deep dive: meaning
The poet speaks to the wind and asks it not to come too wildly. It breaks window shutters, scatters papers and books, pulls down roofs and snuffs out fires. The wind is a god that cannot be blamed. The poet tells us to build strong homes and strong hearts so that wind, the force of nature, becomes our friend and not our enemy.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses personification, imagery of destruction, and a direct address. It suggests that life tests us, and only the strong will pass.
## Hindi saransh
Hawa kamjoron ko todti hai aur mazbooton ko sahara deti hai.
> Be strong and the wind will become your friend.
