# The Lake Isle of Innisfree
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english the lake isle of innisfree
@study
A poem by the Irish poet W. B. Yeats (1865 to 1939), first published in 1890. Living in busy London, the speaker longs to return to the peaceful island of Innisfree in Lough Gill, where he would build a small cabin, grow beans, keep bees and live in calm.
## Deep dive: meaning
The speaker says he will rise and go to Innisfree, where he will build a small cabin and grow beans and keep bees. There he will find peace, which comes slowly from the veils of the morning to where the cricket sings. He is standing on grey pavements in the city, but he hears the lake water in the deep of his heart.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses vivid imagery of colour and sound, a contrast between city and nature, repetition of "I will arise and go now" and a longing tone. It shows the human desire for peace in a busy world.
## Hindi saransh
London ke shor me kavi Innisfree ke shant dweep par jana chahta hai.
> Peace is found in nature, not in the noisy city.
