# On killing a tree
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english on killing a tree
@study
A poem by the Indian poet and painter Gieve Patel (1940 to 2023). It describes how hard it is to kill a tree. One has to hack it, uproot it, burn it and poison its roots before it finally dies. A tree is a living being that cannot be destroyed easily.
## Deep dive: meaning
The poet says that killing a tree is not a quick or easy job. A tree has grown slowly in sun, air and earth for years. One must hack it with an axe, but it will survive. To destroy it completely the roots must be pulled out, exposed to the sun and air until they dry and turn brown, and only then does the tree die.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses detailed imagery, personification, slow rhythm and a serious tone. It carries an environmental message about the value of trees.
## Hindi saransh
Ped ko maarna aasan nahi; use jad se nasht karna padta hai.
> Trees are strong; they deserve our protection.
