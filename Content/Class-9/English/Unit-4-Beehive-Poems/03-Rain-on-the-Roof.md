# Rain on the roof
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english rain on the roof
@study
A poem by the American poet Coates Kinney (1826 to 1904). The speaker lies in bed under the roof and listens to the rain drumming overhead. The sound brings back sweet memories of childhood and of his mother, and he feels warm and happy in the cosy room.
## Deep dive: meaning
The poet lies on his bed and hears the rain beat on the roof above him. The sound is like music, and he feels the past come alive: old friends, loving faces, and especially his mother. The memories are tender, joyful and a little sad. The simple sound turns into a bridge between present and past.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses imagery of sound, a soothing rhythm, repetition and a nostalgic mood. It shows how a small sensory experience can open the heart.
## Hindi saransh
Baarish ki aawaz kavi ko bachpan aur maa ki yaad dilati hai.
> A simple sound can bring back dear memories.
