# A slumber did my spirit seal
minutes: 25
yt: class 9 english a slumber did my spirit seal
@study
A short poem by the English Romantic poet William Wordsworth (1770 to 1850), one of the Lucy poems. In two stanzas the speaker says that love once made him feel unaware of time and death. Now that Lucy is dead, she is part of the earth, rolled round with rocks and trees.
## Deep dive: meaning
In the first stanza the speaker says a slumber sealed his spirit, so he did not fear the passage of time; Lucy seemed to him a thing that could not feel the touch of earthly years. In the second stanza she has died. She has no motion and no force, she neither hears nor sees, and she is rolled round in earth's daily course with rocks and stones and trees.
## Deep dive: devices
The poem uses contrast between before and after, simple language and a calm tone. It is an elegy that shows how nature absorbs all life.
## Hindi saransh
Kavi pyaar me samay aur mrityu ko bhool gaya tha; ab Lucy prakriti ka hissa ban chuki hai.
> Death makes all things part of nature.
