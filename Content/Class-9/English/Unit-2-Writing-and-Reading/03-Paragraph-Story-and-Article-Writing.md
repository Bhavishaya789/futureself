# Paragraph, story and article writing
minutes: 40
yt: class 9 english paragraph story article writing
@study
A paragraph is a group of sentences about one main idea, with a topic sentence, supporting sentences and a concluding sentence. A story has a title, a beginning, middle and end, characters, setting, conflict and a moral. An article has a catchy title, the writer's name (by-line), an introduction, two or three body paragraphs and a conclusion. Use linking words, correct tenses and about 100 to 150 words.
## Deep dive: writing a paragraph
A good paragraph has unity: one main idea. The topic sentence at the start states the idea; two to four supporting sentences give reasons, facts and examples; the concluding sentence repeats the idea or gives a result. Use linking words (first, besides, for example, therefore, in conclusion). A descriptive paragraph uses the senses (sight, sound, smell, taste, touch) and adjectives; a narrative paragraph tells events in order; an argumentative paragraph gives reasons.
## Deep dive: writing a story
Story writing is often based on given outlines or a first line. Plan the characters, setting, plot and ending. Structure: a title, an opening that sets the scene, rising action with a problem or conflict, a climax (the most exciting point), a resolution and, in many cases, a moral. Use the past tense, direct speech for liveliness, adjectives and adverbs and a variety of sentences. Keep the story within the word limit and end with a moral if asked (Honesty is the best policy).
## Deep dive: writing an article
An article presents ideas on a topic for readers of a magazine or newspaper. Parts: a catchy title; the writer's name (by-line); an introduction that states the topic and draws interest; the body in two or three paragraphs with causes, effects, facts and examples; and a conclusion with a suggestion or a thought for readers. Use a formal but lively style. Topics: pollution, road safety, importance of sports, use of mobile phones, plastic ban, digital India. Use facts, not rumours.
## Deep dive: skills and common errors
Plan before writing: brainstorm points and arrange them. Use connectors, a variety of sentence beginnings and the right tense. Avoid repetition, slang and run-on sentences. Check spelling and punctuation; read the final copy once. Marking looks at content, expression, format and accuracy. A word limit is about 100 to 120 words for a paragraph and 150 to 200 for a story or article, unless the question states otherwise.
