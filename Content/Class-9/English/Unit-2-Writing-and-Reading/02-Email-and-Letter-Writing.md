# Email and letter writing
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 english letter and email writing
@study
Letters are formal (to an editor, principal or officer) or informal (to friends and family). A formal letter has the sender address, date, receiver address, subject, salutation (Sir/Madam), body, and complimentary close (Yours faithfully) with name. An informal letter has an address, date, Dear friend, a friendly body and Yours lovingly. An e-mail has To, Cc, Bcc, Subject, a greeting, a short body and a closing with the name.
## Deep dive: formal letters
Formal letters include letters to the editor, complaints, requests, applications and enquiries. Layout: sender's address (top right), date, receiver's designation and address (left), subject line (short and clear, underlined), salutation (Sir/Madam), body in three paragraphs (introduction stating the purpose, details, request or action asked), a polite close (Thanking you / I shall be grateful), complimentary close (Yours faithfully) and the signature with name. Use a respectful, impersonal tone, no slang, and keep the length to about 120 words.
## Deep dive: informal letters
An informal letter is a personal letter to a friend or relative. Layout: sender address and date, salutation (Dear Aman), a friendly opening (Hope you are fine), the body in two or three paragraphs, a warm closing (Write soon) and Yours affectionately/lovingly/sincerely with the sender's first name. Contractions and a friendly tone are allowed, but avoid slang and errors. Topics: an invitation, congratulating a friend, describing a trip, giving advice or thanking someone.
## Deep dive: letter to the editor and complaints
A letter to the editor states a public issue (noise, garbage, traffic, pollution), its cause and effect, and a suggestion. A complaint letter states the problem, the date and the action expected. Give facts and avoid blaming people. A request or application clearly states what is asked and why. Add enclosures if documents are attached.
## Deep dive: writing an e-mail
An e-mail has a recipient address (To), copy (Cc) and blind copy (Bcc), a clear subject line, a greeting (Dear Sir/Hello), a short body, a closing (Regards, Thank you) and your name. Keep paragraphs short, check the address, attach files only when needed, and use a polite tone. Avoid capital letters (they look like shouting) and text abbreviations in formal e-mails. Re-read before sending.
