# Active and passive voice
minutes: 35
yt: class 9 english active and passive voice
@study
In the active voice the subject does the action (Ravi writes a letter); in the passive voice the object becomes the subject and the action is done to it (A letter is written by Ravi). Passive = object + be form + past participle (V3) + by + agent. The tense stays the same: only the be form changes (is/are, was/were, has been, will be). Intransitive verbs have no passive. Use the passive when the doer is unknown, unimportant or obvious.
## Deep dive: what voice means
Voice shows whether the subject performs or receives the action. Only transitive verbs (verbs that take an object) can be turned into the passive. The agent (doer) is introduced by by and is often omitted: The window was broken (by someone). Pronouns change case: I to me, he to him, she to her, we to us and they to them in the by-phrase: The cake was eaten by him.
## Deep dive: forming the passive
Steps: (1) find the subject, verb and object; (2) make the object the new subject; (3) choose the be form in the same tense as the active verb, matching the new subject; (4) add the past participle of the main verb; (5) add by + the old subject. Simple present: is/are + V3. Present continuous: is/are being + V3. Simple past: was/were + V3. Past continuous: was/were being + V3. Present perfect: has/have been + V3. Past perfect: had been + V3. Simple future: will be + V3. With modals: can/may/must/should + be + V3.
## Deep dive: special cases
Questions: Who wrote this letter? becomes By whom was this letter written? Imperatives use let: Open the door becomes Let the door be opened. Verbs with two objects: He gave me a book becomes I was given a book (or A book was given to me). Verbs such as sleep, come, go, arrive, die have no object and have no passive. Perfect continuous tenses and the future continuous are rarely used in the passive.
## Deep dive: when to use the passive
Use the passive when the doer is unknown (My bike was stolen), unimportant or obvious (The thief was arrested), when we want to stress the action or result, in news, science and formal writing (Water is boiled at 100 degrees), and in notices and rules (Smoking is prohibited). Avoid the passive where it makes writing dull or hides who is responsible.

Simple past | was/were + V3 | A letter was written
