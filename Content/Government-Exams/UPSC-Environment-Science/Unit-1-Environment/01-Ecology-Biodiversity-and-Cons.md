Ecosystems and conservation.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Biodiversity hotspot**: has high endemism and threat
- **India's hotspots**: include the Western Ghats and Himalayas
- **Ramsar convention**: protects wetlands
- **Wildlife Protection Act**: was enacted in 1972
- **Project Tiger**: was launched in 1973
- **IUCN Red List**: classifies endangered species

**Facts to remember**
- Biodiversity hotspot has high endemism and threat.
- India's hotspots include the Western Ghats and Himalayas.
- Ramsar convention protects wetlands.
- Wildlife Protection Act was enacted in 1972.
- Project Tiger was launched in 1973.
- IUCN Red List classifies endangered species.
- CITES regulates trade in endangered species.
- Biosphere reserve protects ecosystems and communities.
- Sundarbans is a mangrove ecosystem.
- Keystone species has a disproportionate effect on ecosystems.
- Eutrophication is nutrient enrichment of water.
- Biomagnification increases toxin concentration up the food chain.
- National parks have stricter protection than sanctuaries.
- Eco-sensitive zones surround protected areas.
