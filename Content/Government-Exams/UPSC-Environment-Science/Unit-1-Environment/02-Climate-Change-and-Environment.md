Global climate framework.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **UNFCCC**: was adopted at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit
- **Kyoto Protocol**: set binding emission targets
- **Paris Agreement**: aims to limit warming well below 2 degrees
- **Montreal Protocol**: phases out ozone depleting substances
- **Basel Convention**: controls hazardous waste movement
- **Convention on Biological Diversity**: was signed in 1992

**Facts to remember**
- UNFCCC was adopted at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit.
- Kyoto Protocol set binding emission targets.
- Paris Agreement aims to limit warming well below 2 degrees.
- Montreal Protocol phases out ozone depleting substances.
- Basel Convention controls hazardous waste movement.
- Convention on Biological Diversity was signed in 1992.
- IPCC assesses climate science.
- Carbon credit represents one tonne of carbon dioxide.
- International Solar Alliance was launched by India and France.
- Net zero target of India is 2070.
- Global warming potential is highest for fluorinated gases.
- National Green Tribunal was set up in 2010.
- Environment Protection Act was passed in 1986.
- Stockholm Conference took place in 1972.
