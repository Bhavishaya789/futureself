Five year plans and development.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **First Five Year Plan**: was based on the Harrod-Domar model
- **Second Plan**: was based on the Mahalanobis model
- **Planning Commission**: was set up in 1950
- **New Economic Policy**: began in 1991
- **LPG reforms**: stand for liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation
- **Poverty line**: is estimated by expert committees

**Facts to remember**
- First Five Year Plan was based on the Harrod-Domar model.
- Second Plan was based on the Mahalanobis model.
- Planning Commission was set up in 1950.
- New Economic Policy began in 1991.
- LPG reforms stand for liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation.
- Poverty line is estimated by expert committees.
- Human Development Index is published by UNDP.
- MGNREGA guarantees 100 days of work.
- Gini coefficient measures inequality.
- Mixed economy has both public and private sectors.
- Navratna is a category of public sector enterprises.
- Make in India was launched in 2014.
- Start-up India was launched in 2016.
- Atmanirbhar Bharat promotes self reliance.
