Financial system and public finance.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Reserve Bank of India**: was established in 1935
- **SEBI**: regulates the securities market
- **NABARD**: supports agriculture and rural development
- **Union Budget**: is presented under Article 112
- **Goods and Services Tax**: was launched on 1 July 2017
- **Direct taxes**: include income tax

**Facts to remember**
- Reserve Bank of India was established in 1935.
- SEBI regulates the securities market.
- NABARD supports agriculture and rural development.
- Union Budget is presented under Article 112.
- Goods and Services Tax was launched on 1 July 2017.
- Direct taxes include income tax.
- Indirect taxes include GST.
- Minimum Support Price is announced by the government for crops.
- Public Distribution System distributes foodgrains.
- Green Revolution began in the 1960s.
- White Revolution was led by Verghese Kurien.
- Jan Dhan Yojana promotes financial inclusion.
- UPI is run by NPCI.
- Disinvestment is selling government equity.
