Core macro concepts.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **GDP**: is the market value of all final goods and services in a year
- **GNP**: equals GDP plus net factor income from abroad
- **Inflation**: is a sustained rise in the price level
- **Consumer Price Index**: is used for retail inflation targeting
- **Wholesale Price Index**: measures wholesale price changes
- **Repo rate**: is the rate at which RBI lends to banks

**Facts to remember**
- GDP is the market value of all final goods and services in a year.
- GNP equals GDP plus net factor income from abroad.
- Inflation is a sustained rise in the price level.
- Consumer Price Index is used for retail inflation targeting.
- Wholesale Price Index measures wholesale price changes.
- Repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends to banks.
- Reverse repo rate is the rate at which RBI borrows.
- Cash Reserve Ratio is the share of deposits banks keep with RBI.
- Statutory Liquidity Ratio is kept in liquid assets.
- Fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowing.
- Monetary Policy Committee has six members.
- Inflation target is 4 percent with a band of 2 percent.
- Base year of GDP series was revised to 2011-12.
- Stagflation is inflation with low growth.
