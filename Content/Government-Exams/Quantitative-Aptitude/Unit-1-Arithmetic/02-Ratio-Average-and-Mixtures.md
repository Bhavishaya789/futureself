@study
- **Ratio:** divide 64 in the ratio 3:5 -> 64/8 = 8, so **24 and 40**.
- **Average** = sum/count. Five numbers average 20 (sum 100); adding a sixth number to make the average 22 needs total 132, so the new number is **32**.
- **Alligation (mixtures):** to get 30% from 20% and 50% solutions, ratio = (50 - 30):(30 - 20) = 20:10 = **2:1**.
- **Ages:** use equations. Father is 3 times his son; in 10 years he will be twice: 3x + 10 = 2(x + 10) -> x = **10** (son 10, father 30).
- **Partnership:** profit is shared in the ratio of (investment x time).
```python
print(64 / 8 * 3, 64 / 8 * 5, 22 * 6 - 100, (50 - 30) / (30 - 20))   # 24.0 40.0 32 2.0
```
