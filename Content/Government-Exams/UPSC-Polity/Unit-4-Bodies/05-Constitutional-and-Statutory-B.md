Commissions and bodies.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Election Commission**: is under Article 324
- **UPSC**: is under Article 315
- **CAG**: is under Article 148
- **Attorney General**: is under Article 76
- **Finance Commission**: recommends tax devolution
- **NITI Aayog**: replaced the Planning Commission in 2015

**Facts to remember**
- Election Commission is under Article 324.
- UPSC is under Article 315.
- CAG is under Article 148.
- Attorney General is under Article 76.
- Finance Commission recommends tax devolution.
- NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in 2015.
- NHRC is a statutory body.
- CVC is an anti-corruption statutory body.
- Lokpal was established by the 2013 Act.
- RTI Act was enacted in 2005.
- GST Council is under Article 279A.
- Official Languages Hindi is the official language of the Union.
- Eighth Schedule lists 22 languages.
- Tribunals were added by the 42nd Amendment as Part XIVA.
