Courts, governors and panchayats.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Supreme Court**: has 34 judges including the Chief Justice
- **Retirement age of Supreme Court judges**: is 65 years
- **High Court judges**: retire at 62 years
- **Original jurisdiction of Supreme Court**: covers Centre-state disputes
- **Judicial review**: is a basic feature
- **PIL**: was developed through judicial activism

**Facts to remember**
- Supreme Court has 34 judges including the Chief Justice.
- Retirement age of Supreme Court judges is 65 years.
- High Court judges retire at 62 years.
- Original jurisdiction of Supreme Court covers Centre-state disputes.
- Judicial review is a basic feature.
- PIL was developed through judicial activism.
- Governor is appointed by the President.
- Article 163 gives aid and advice to the Governor.
- 73rd Amendment deals with Panchayati Raj.
- 74th Amendment deals with urban local bodies.
- Gram Sabha consists of all voters in a village.
- Article 243 relates to panchayats.
- Finance Commission is set up under Article 280.
- Seventh Schedule has Union, State and Concurrent lists.
