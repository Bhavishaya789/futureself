Constituent Assembly and basic structure.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Constituent Assembly**: took 2 years 11 months 18 days to frame the Constitution
- **Dr Rajendra Prasad**: was President of the Constituent Assembly
- **Dr B R Ambedkar**: chaired the Drafting Committee
- **Objectives Resolution**: was moved by Nehru in 1946
- **Preamble**: begins with We the people of India
- **Words socialist and secular**: were added by the 42nd Amendment

**Facts to remember**
- Constituent Assembly took 2 years 11 months 18 days to frame the Constitution.
- Dr Rajendra Prasad was President of the Constituent Assembly.
- Dr B R Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.
- Objectives Resolution was moved by Nehru in 1946.
- Preamble begins with We the people of India.
- Words socialist and secular were added by the 42nd Amendment.
- Kesavananda Bharati case established the basic structure doctrine.
- Constitution of India is the longest written constitution.
- Federal system has a strong unitary bias.
- Parliamentary system was borrowed from Britain.
- Fundamental Rights were borrowed from the USA.
- Directive Principles were borrowed from Ireland.
- Article 368 deals with amendment of the Constitution.
- Constitution has 395 original articles and 8 schedules.
