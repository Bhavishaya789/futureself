Union government structure.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **President**: is elected by an electoral college
- **Minimum age for President**: is 35 years
- **Vice President**: is the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha
- **Lok Sabha**: has a maximum strength of 552
- **Rajya Sabha**: has a maximum strength of 250
- **Money Bill**: originates only in the Lok Sabha

**Facts to remember**
- President is elected by an electoral college.
- Minimum age for President is 35 years.
- Vice President is the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
- Lok Sabha has a maximum strength of 552.
- Rajya Sabha has a maximum strength of 250.
- Money Bill originates only in the Lok Sabha.
- Article 123 gives ordinance power to the President.
- Article 352 deals with national emergency.
- Article 356 deals with President's rule in a state.
- Article 360 deals with financial emergency.
- Speaker presides over the Lok Sabha.
- Anti-defection law is in the Tenth Schedule.
- Joint sitting is presided over by the Speaker.
- Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
