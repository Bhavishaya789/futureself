**Key formulas and rules**
- **RBI**: was nationalised in 1949
- **Headquarters of RBI**: are in Mumbai
- **Governor of RBI**: is appointed by the Central Government
- **Repo rate**: controls liquidity
- **NEFT**: is a deferred net settlement system
- **RTGS**: is real time gross settlement

**Facts to remember**
- RBI was nationalised in 1949.
- Headquarters of RBI are in Mumbai.
- Governor of RBI is appointed by the Central Government.
- Repo rate controls liquidity.
- NEFT is a deferred net settlement system.
- RTGS is real time gross settlement.
- UPI allows instant mobile payments.
- Bank nationalisation took place in 1969 and 1980.
- NABARD was set up in 1982.
- SIDBI finances small industries.
- Payment banks cannot give loans.
- KYC stands for know your customer.
- Non Performing Asset is a loan unpaid for 90 days.
- Basel norms set bank capital rules.
