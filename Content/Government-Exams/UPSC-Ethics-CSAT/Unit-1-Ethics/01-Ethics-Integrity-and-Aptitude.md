**Key formulas and rules**
- **Ethics**: studies right and wrong conduct
- **Integrity**: is consistency of values and action
- **Probity**: means uncompromising honesty
- **Empathy**: is understanding another person's feelings
- **Emotional intelligence**: includes self awareness and self regulation
- **Utilitarianism**: seeks the greatest good for the greatest number

**Facts to remember**
- Ethics studies right and wrong conduct.
- Integrity is consistency of values and action.
- Probity means uncompromising honesty.
- Empathy is understanding another person's feelings.
- Emotional intelligence includes self awareness and self regulation.
- Utilitarianism seeks the greatest good for the greatest number.
- Deontology judges actions by duty and rules.
- Virtue ethics focuses on character.
- Categorical imperative was given by Kant.
- Conflict of interest arises when private interest affects duty.
- Nolan Committee gave seven principles of public life.
- Whistleblowing is exposing wrongdoing in an organisation.
- Code of conduct lists rules of behaviour.
- Citizen's charter states service standards.
