@study
- **Start with the syllabus and previous papers** from the official website; list topics and weights.
- **Daily plan example (3 hours):** 1 hour quantitative aptitude/reasoning practice, 45 minutes English/Hindi, 45 minutes General Knowledge and current affairs, 30 minutes revision.
- **Current affairs:** read a reliable daily or monthly summary; note date, place, person and number for each item. The app's *Today's GK* card gives short daily facts.
- **Practice:** take timed mock tests; analyse mistakes in a notebook; revise every weak topic within a week.
- **Time per question:** a 100-question paper in 60 minutes gives about **36 seconds per question**. Solve easy questions first.
- **Negative marking:** with a 1/4 penalty, guess only if you can remove at least two options. Expected score for random guessing on 4 options = 0.25 - 0.75 x 0.25 = **0.0625** per question, very low.
- **Health:** sleep 7-8 hours, take breaks, and keep a stable routine.

print(60 * 60 / 100, 0.25 * 1 - 0.75 * 0.25)   # 36.0 0.0625
