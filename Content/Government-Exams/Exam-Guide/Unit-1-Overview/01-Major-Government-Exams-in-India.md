Patterns change often. **Always check the official notification** (ssc.gov.in, ibps.in, indianrailways.gov.in, upsc.gov.in) before you plan. The figures below are typical, not guaranteed.
- **SSC (Staff Selection Commission):** CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD. Usual subjects: General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English. Tier I of CGL has been 100 questions of 2 marks each, 60 minutes, with negative marking.
- **Banking:** IBPS PO/Clerk/RRB, SBI PO/Clerk, RBI Grade B. Prelims test English, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning (often 100 questions in 60 minutes); Mains add General/Banking Awareness and Computer Aptitude; PO has an interview.
- **Railways (RRB):** NTPC, Group D, ALP/Technician. CBT with Mathematics, Reasoning and General Awareness/Science.
- **UPSC Civil Services:** **Prelims** (GS Paper I plus CSAT Paper II, which is qualifying at 33%), **Mains** (9 papers: 2 qualifying language papers, an essay, 4 GS papers and 2 optional papers; 1750 marks counted) and **Interview** (275 marks), total 2025.
- **Defence:** NDA, CDS, AFCAT. **Teaching:** CTET/TET. **States:** State PSCs, police and patwari/clerk recruitments.
- **Eligibility** (age, education, attempts) varies by exam and category; check each notification. **Negative marking** is common: avoid blind guessing.
