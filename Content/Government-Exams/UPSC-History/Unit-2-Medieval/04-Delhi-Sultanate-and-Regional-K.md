Sultanate dynasties and Vijayanagara.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Qutbuddin Aibak**: began the Slave dynasty
- **Iltutmish**: introduced the silver tanka
- **Alauddin Khalji**: introduced market control
- **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**: shifted the capital to Daulatabad
- **Firoz Shah Tughlaq**: built canals
- **Timur**: invaded India in 1398

**Facts to remember**
- Qutbuddin Aibak began the Slave dynasty.
- Iltutmish introduced the silver tanka.
- Alauddin Khalji introduced market control.
- Muhammad bin Tughlaq shifted the capital to Daulatabad.
- Firoz Shah Tughlaq built canals.
- Timur invaded India in 1398.
- Lodi dynasty was the last of the Sultanate.
- Vijayanagara empire was founded in 1336.
- Krishnadevaraya was the greatest Vijayanagara ruler.
- Bahmani kingdom was founded by Alauddin Bahman Shah.
- Bhakti movement was led by saints such as Kabir.
- Sufi silsilas included Chishti and Suhrawardi.
- Amir Khusrau is called the parrot of India.
- Ibn Battuta visited in the Tughlaq period.
