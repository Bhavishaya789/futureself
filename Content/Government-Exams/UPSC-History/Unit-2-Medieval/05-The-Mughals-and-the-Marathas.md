Mughal administration and Maratha rise.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Babur**: won the first battle of Panipat in 1526
- **Humayun**: was defeated by Sher Shah at Chausa
- **Sher Shah**: introduced the rupee and Grand Trunk Road
- **Akbar**: followed Sulh-i-kul
- **Todar Mal**: reformed land revenue
- **Jahangir**: is known for the chain of justice

**Facts to remember**
- Babur won the first battle of Panipat in 1526.
- Humayun was defeated by Sher Shah at Chausa.
- Sher Shah introduced the rupee and Grand Trunk Road.
- Akbar followed Sulh-i-kul.
- Todar Mal reformed land revenue.
- Jahangir is known for the chain of justice.
- Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal.
- Aurangzeb reimposed jizya.
- Shivaji was crowned at Raigad in 1674.
- Ashtapradhan was Shivaji's council of ministers.
- Third Battle of Panipat took place in 1761.
- Peshwas became hereditary heads of the Marathas.
- Mughal painting flourished under Akbar and Jahangir.
- Fatehpur Sikri was built by Akbar.
