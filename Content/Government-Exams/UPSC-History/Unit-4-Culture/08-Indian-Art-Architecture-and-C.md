Temples, paintings, dance and music.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Nagara style**: is the temple style of north India
- **Dravida style**: has gopurams in south India
- **Vesara style**: combines both styles
- **Khajuraho temples**: were built by the Chandelas
- **Konark Sun Temple**: was built by Narasimhadeva I
- **Ajanta caves**: are known for Buddhist paintings

**Facts to remember**
- Nagara style is the temple style of north India.
- Dravida style has gopurams in south India.
- Vesara style combines both styles.
- Khajuraho temples were built by the Chandelas.
- Konark Sun Temple was built by Narasimhadeva I.
- Ajanta caves are known for Buddhist paintings.
- Ellora caves include the Kailasa temple.
- Kathak is a classical dance of north India.
- Bharatanatyam originated in Tamil Nadu.
- Odissi is a dance of Odisha.
- Kuchipudi is a dance of Andhra Pradesh.
- Hindustani music differs from Carnatic music.
- Madhubani painting is from Bihar.
- Warli painting is from Maharashtra.
