**Key formulas and rules**
- **Rigveda**: is the oldest Veda
- **Later Vedic period**: saw the rise of kingdoms
- **Sixteen Mahajanapadas**: existed in the sixth century BCE
- **Magadha**: became the most powerful Mahajanapada
- **Buddha**: was born at Lumbini
- **Mahavira**: was the 24th Tirthankara

**Facts to remember**
- Rigveda is the oldest Veda.
- Later Vedic period saw the rise of kingdoms.
- Sixteen Mahajanapadas existed in the sixth century BCE.
- Magadha became the most powerful Mahajanapada.
- Buddha was born at Lumbini.
- Mahavira was the 24th Tirthankara.
- Chandragupta Maurya defeated the Nandas.
- Arthashastra was written by Kautilya.
- Megasthenes wrote Indica.
- Ashoka's edicts were deciphered by James Prinsep.
- Kalinga war took place in 261 BCE.
- Sarnath lion capital is the national emblem source.
- Mauryan empire ended with Brihadratha's killing by Pushyamitra.
- Third Buddhist council was held at Pataliputra.
