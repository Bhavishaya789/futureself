**Key formulas and rules**
- **Kanishka**: patronised the Fourth Buddhist council
- **Gandhara school**: shows Greek influence
- **Mathura school**: is indigenous in style
- **Satavahanas**: ruled the Deccan
- **Samudragupta**: is called the Napoleon of India
- **Chandragupta II**: took the title Vikramaditya

**Facts to remember**
- Kanishka patronised the Fourth Buddhist council.
- Gandhara school shows Greek influence.
- Mathura school is indigenous in style.
- Satavahanas ruled the Deccan.
- Samudragupta is called the Napoleon of India.
- Chandragupta II took the title Vikramaditya.
- Fa-Hien visited during the Gupta period.
- Nalanda university was patronised by the Guptas.
- Aryabhata wrote the Aryabhatiya.
- Kalidasa wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam.
- Sangam literature belongs to early Tamil society.
- Cholas were known for their navy and temples.
- Pallavas built the Mahabalipuram shore temple.
- Chalukyas built the Aihole and Badami temples.
