Stone ages and Harappan culture.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Palaeolithic age**: used rough stone tools
- **Mesolithic age**: used microliths
- **Neolithic age**: saw the beginning of agriculture
- **Mehrgarh**: is an early Neolithic site in Baluchistan
- **Harappan town planning**: followed a grid pattern
- **Lothal**: had a dockyard

**Facts to remember**
- Palaeolithic age used rough stone tools.
- Mesolithic age used microliths.
- Neolithic age saw the beginning of agriculture.
- Mehrgarh is an early Neolithic site in Baluchistan.
- Harappan town planning followed a grid pattern.
- Lothal had a dockyard.
- Dholavira is known for its water conservation.
- Great Bath is at Mohenjo-daro.
- Harappan seals show the unicorn motif.
- Harappan script is yet to be deciphered.
- Kalibangan has evidence of ploughed fields.
- Rakhigarhi is in Haryana.
- Harappans did not use iron.
- Cotton was first cultivated in the Indus region.
