**Key formulas and rules**
- **Alluvial soil**: is fertile and rich in potash
- **Black soil**: retains moisture
- **Red soil**: is rich in iron
- **Laterite soil**: is found in heavy rainfall regions
- **Kharif season**: is June to September
- **Rabi season**: is October to March

**Facts to remember**
- Alluvial soil is fertile and rich in potash.
- Black soil retains moisture.
- Red soil is rich in iron.
- Laterite soil is found in heavy rainfall regions.
- Kharif season is June to September.
- Rabi season is October to March.
- Zaid season is the summer cropping season.
- Jharkhand has the most coal reserves in India.
- Odisha leads in iron ore.
- Rajasthan is a major producer of copper.
- Tata Iron and Steel is at Jamshedpur.
- Bhilai is a public sector steel plant.
- Cotton textile is concentrated in Maharashtra and Gujarat.
- Golden Quadrilateral links four metros.
