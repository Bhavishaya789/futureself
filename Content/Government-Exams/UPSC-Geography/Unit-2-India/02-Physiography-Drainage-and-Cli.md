**Key formulas and rules**
- **Himalayas**: are divided into Himadri, Himachal and Shiwaliks
- **Deccan Plateau**: is made of volcanic rock
- **Western Ghats**: include Anamudi, the highest peak in the south
- **Thar desert**: is in Rajasthan
- **Ganga**: rises from Gangotri
- **Indus**: flows through Ladakh

**Facts to remember**
- Himalayas are divided into Himadri, Himachal and Shiwaliks.
- Deccan Plateau is made of volcanic rock.
- Western Ghats include Anamudi, the highest peak in the south.
- Thar desert is in Rajasthan.
- Ganga rises from Gangotri.
- Indus flows through Ladakh.
- Godavari is called the Dakshin Ganga.
- Mahanadi flows into the Bay of Bengal.
- Chilika is the largest coastal lagoon.
- Southwest monsoon brings most rain to India.
- Western disturbances bring winter rain to the north.
- Mawsynram receives the highest rainfall.
- Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states.
- Kanchenjunga is the highest peak in India.
