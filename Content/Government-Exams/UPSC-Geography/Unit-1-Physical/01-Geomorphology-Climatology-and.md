# Geomorphology, Climatology and Oceanography
minutes: 40

Earth, landforms, climate and oceans.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Lithosphere**: includes the crust and upper mantle
- **Plate tectonics**: explains continental drift
- **Ring of Fire**: surrounds the Pacific Ocean
- **Weathering**: is in situ breakdown of rocks
- **Coriolis force**: deflects winds to the right in the northern hemisphere
- **Jet streams**: are fast upper-air winds

**Facts to remember**
- Lithosphere includes the crust and upper mantle.
- Plate tectonics explains continental drift.
- Ring of Fire surrounds the Pacific Ocean.
- Weathering is in situ breakdown of rocks.
- Coriolis force deflects winds to the right in the northern hemisphere.
- Jet streams are fast upper-air winds.
- El Nino is warming of the central Pacific.
- La Nina is the cooling phase.
- Ocean currents are driven by winds and temperature.
- Gulf Stream is a warm current.
- Tides are caused by the moon and sun.
- Albedo is the reflectivity of a surface.
- Inversion of temperature occurs when temperature rises with height.
- Koppen classified world climates.
