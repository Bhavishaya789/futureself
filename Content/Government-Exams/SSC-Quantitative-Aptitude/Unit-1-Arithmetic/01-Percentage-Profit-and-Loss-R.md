Core arithmetic.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Percentage change**: is change over original times 100
- **Successive discount of a and b**: equals a + b - ab/100
- **Marked price**: is the list price
- **Discount**: is calculated on the marked price
- **Ratio**: a:b has total parts a + b
- **Mixture and alligation**: uses the cross method

**Facts to remember**
- Percentage change is change over original times 100.
- Successive discount of a and b equals a + b - ab/100.
- Marked price is the list price.
- Discount is calculated on the marked price.
- Ratio a:b has total parts a + b.
- Mixture and alligation uses the cross method.
- Partnership shares profit in ratio of capital times time.
- Cost price profit is on the cost price.
- Population growth compounds like interest.
- Average is sum divided by number of observations.
