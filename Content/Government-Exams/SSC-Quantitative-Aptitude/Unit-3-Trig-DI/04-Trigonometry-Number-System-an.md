**Key formulas and rules**
- **Sin² + cos²**: equals 1
- **Tan 45 degrees**: equals 1
- **Sin 30 degrees**: equals one half
- **Divisibility by 3**: needs digit sum divisible by 3
- **Divisibility by 11**: uses alternating digit sum
- **LCM times HCF**: equals product of two numbers

**Facts to remember**
- Sin² + cos² equals 1.
- Tan 45 degrees equals 1.
- Sin 30 degrees equals one half.
- Divisibility by 3 needs digit sum divisible by 3.
- Divisibility by 11 uses alternating digit sum.
- LCM times HCF equals product of two numbers.
- Number of factors uses exponent plus one product.
- Unit digit cycles repeat every four powers.
- Pie chart shows parts of a whole.
- Bar graph compares categories.
- Line graph shows change over time.
- Table data needs careful row column reading.
- Average speed is total distance over total time.
- Remainder theorem gives f(a) as the remainder.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. Revise this chapter in three passes: the formula sheet, the facts above, then the generated numerical questions with a timer.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Attempt the generated questions in sets of ten, mark each answer, and note the reason for every miss. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
