Formulas for SSC.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **(a+b)²**: equals a² + 2ab + b²
- **A² - b²**: equals (a+b)(a-b)
- **Sum of angles of a triangle**: is 180 degrees
- **Pythagoras theorem**: states h² = p² + b²
- **Area of triangle**: is half base times height
- **Area of circle**: is πr²

**Facts to remember**
- (a+b)² equals a² + 2ab + b².
- A² - b² equals (a+b)(a-b).
- Sum of angles of a triangle is 180 degrees.
- Pythagoras theorem states h² = p² + b².
- Area of triangle is half base times height.
- Area of circle is πr².
- Circumference is 2πr.
- Volume of cylinder is πr²h.
- Volume of cone is one third πr²h.
- Volume of sphere is 4/3 πr³.
- Sum of interior angles of polygon is (n-2) × 180.
- Incentre is the intersection of angle bisectors.
- Centroid divides the median in 2:1.
- Circumcentre is equidistant from the vertices.
