# Syllogism, logic and puzzles | न्याय-निरूपण, तर्क और पहेलियाँ
minutes: 20
yt: syllogism venn diagram reasoning
@study
- **Syllogism:** use Venn diagrams. **All A are B; all B are C -> all A are C.** "**Some** A are B" -> "some B are A" (it reverses). "No A is B" -> "no B is A". "All A are B" does **not** mean "all B are A".
- **Possibility cases:** "Some A are B" does not say that some A are *not* B, though that is possible.
- **Statement-conclusion:** accept only what the statements certainly prove, never outside knowledge.
- **Seating and puzzles:** build a table or diagram, place definite facts first, then deduce. In circular arrangements, count left and right carefully.
- **Truth-teller puzzles:** assume one person lies and check consistency.
- **Venn-diagram numbers:** in a class of 40, 25 like tea, 20 like coffee, 10 like both: those who like at least one = 25 + 20 - 10 = **35**, so **5 like neither**.

## Moderate
P: In a class of 50, 30 play cricket, 25 play football and 10 play both. How many play at least one?
A: 30 + 25 - 10 = 45.
R: 1 | Formula
R: 1 | 45
## High
P: Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags. Is "some pens are bags" certain?
A: No. The books that are bags may not be pens.
R: 2 | Reason
R: 1 | No
## Advanced
P: Five friends sit in a row; P sits at the left end and Q sits next to P. R is not next to Q. List one valid arrangement.
A: P Q S R T (or P Q T R S).
R: 2 | Valid order
R: 1 | Conditions met
