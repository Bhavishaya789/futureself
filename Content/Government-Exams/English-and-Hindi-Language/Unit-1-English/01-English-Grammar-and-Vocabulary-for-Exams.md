@study
- **Eight parts of speech:** noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection.
- **Subject-verb agreement:** a singular subject takes a singular verb. *The list of items **is** long.* *Neither of the boys **has** come.* *Each of the students **was** present.*
- **Tenses:** present (simple, continuous, perfect), past, future. *He **has been** working since morning.* (present perfect continuous).
- **Articles:** *a* before a consonant sound, *an* before a vowel sound (**an** hour, **a** university).
- **Prepositions:** *at* (point), *on* (surface, day), *in* (enclosed, month/year). *She was born **in** 2005 **on** Monday **at** 6 a.m.*
- **Common errors:** *less* (uncountable) vs *fewer* (countable); *who* (subject) vs *whom* (object); *its* (possessive) vs *it's* (it is).
- **Idioms:** *a piece of cake* = very easy; *break the ice* = start a conversation; *once in a blue moon* = very rarely; *hit the nail on the head* = say exactly the right thing.
- **One-word substitutes:** *Omnipotent* = all-powerful; *Bibliophile* = lover of books; *Autobiography* = story of one's own life; *Posthumous* = after death.
- **Synonyms and antonyms:** *Benevolent* = kind (antonym *malevolent*); *Candid* = frank; *Frugal* = thrifty (antonym *extravagant*).
