# Grammar and Reading Comprehension
minutes: 45

@study
The Class 12 English Core language section tests reading comprehension and grammar through gap filling, editing, omission and sentence reordering. Reading comprehension: read the passage quickly for the main idea, then closely; look for the answer in the text, infer meanings of words from context, identify tone and the writer's purpose, and write brief answers in your own words; note-making and summarising use headings, abbreviations and a title. Tenses: simple present (habits), present continuous (now), present perfect (past action with present result), simple past, past continuous, past perfect (earlier of two past actions), simple future; the sequence of tenses requires agreement between main and subordinate clauses. Subject verb agreement: a singular subject takes a singular verb; collective nouns act as singular; either or and neither nor agree with the nearer subject; each, every, one and everyone are singular; none can be either. Determiners: articles a, an, the; quantifiers some, any, much, many, few, a few, little, a little; demonstratives this, that, these, those; each, every, either, neither. Prepositions show relation of time, place and direction: in, on, at, by, with, for, since, during. Modals: can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, ought to and need; they express ability, permission, possibility, obligation and advice. Reported speech: change pronouns, tenses (present becomes past), time and place words (now becomes then, today that day, tomorrow the next day, here there), and use say, tell, ask or order; questions use if or whether for yes or no questions and keep the wh word for others, with statement word order. Voice: active to passive uses the past participle of the verb with be in the right tense. Conditionals: type 1 (real: if present, will), type 2 (unreal present: if past, would), type 3 (unreal past: if had, would have). Editing: errors in tense, articles, prepositions, agreement and spelling. Omission: find the missing word. Gap filling: choose the correct word using context. Reordering: arrange words into a meaningful sentence. Linkers and connectors: and, but, so, because, although, however, therefore. Punctuation and spelling.
## Deep dive: Reading
Skim for gist, scan for details, use context for vocabulary and answer in your own words.
## Deep dive: Core grammar
Tenses, agreement, determiners, prepositions and modals form most of the grammar questions.
## Deep dive: Transformations
Reported speech, voice and conditionals transform sentences while keeping meaning.
