# Going Places
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 english flamingo going places a r barton sophie jansie danny casey fantasy
@study
Going Places by A. R. Barton is a short story about the dreams of a teenage girl and the gap between fantasy and reality. Sophie and her friend Jansie are two schoolgirls. Sophie is imaginative and ambitious: she dreams of becoming an actress, then of owning a boutique and becoming a famous fashion designer, and she is fascinated by Danny Casey, a famous footballer whom she imagines meeting. She builds a fantasy in which Danny Casey would come to her boutique. Jansie, who is practical and cautious, doubts Sophie's stories and tells her to be sensible. Sophie lives in a modest home with parents and an elder brother, Geoff; the family life is plain and Sophie dreams of escaping it. She talks about her dreams to Jansie, and in her imagination she goes to meet Danny Casey. In the end the reality is that Danny does not come; she is left with her hope. Themes: dream and reality, adolescent imagination, desire to escape an ordinary life, the pressure of a modest background, and hope. Style: simple narrative with dialogue; contrast between Sophie's romantic outlook and Jansie's practical one. Moral: dreams give hope and direction but must be balanced with a sense of reality.
## Deep dive: Sophie
Imaginative, ambitious and dissatisfied; she builds a fantasy around Danny Casey to escape a dull life.
## Deep dive: Jansie
Practical and realistic; she sees Sophie's fantasy as impossible.
## Deep dive: Theme
The story contrasts fantasy and reality and shows how dreams sustain young people from modest backgrounds.
