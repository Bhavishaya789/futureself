# The Interview
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 english flamingo the interview christopher silvester umberto eco mukund padmanabhan
@study
The Interview has two parts: an essay by Christopher Silvester from The Penguin Book of Interviews on the art and role of the interview, and an interview of the Italian scholar and novelist Umberto Eco by Mukund Padmanabhan of The Hindu. Part I: The interview is a major source of communication in modern society and has a central place in journalism. The first interview was published in 1859, by Horace Greeley of the New York Tribune with Brigham Young. Many famous people disliked it: Rudyard Kipling called it a crime and an offence against the rights of the individual, H. G. Wells described it as an ordeal, Saul Bellow compared it to having your fingerprints taken, Lewis Carroll refused to meet journalists, and V. S. Naipaul said that some people are wounded by interviews and that a little of the soul is taken away. Supporters say it is a supremely serviceable medium of communication and, in the hands of a skilled interviewer, a creative art that brings a person alive. Part II: Umberto Eco is a professor at the University of Bologna, a scholar of semiotics, and a bestselling novelist whose first novel was The Name of the Rose. In the interview Padmanabhan asks how he manages to write so much; Eco says he uses the empty spaces of life, those small gaps of time in waiting or travelling, to write, and that he does not have a fixed pattern. He is humorous and modest, and the interview shows the mind of a scholar who is also a novelist. Themes: the role and ethics of the interview, creativity, and the interplay of scholarship and fiction.
## Deep dive: The interview as a form
It dates from 1859; some great writers loathed it as an intrusion, others praised it as an art.
## Deep dive: Eco
Scholar and novelist; he uses the empty spaces of life to write; The Name of the Rose is his famous novel.
## Deep dive: Contrasting views
The essay balances attack and defence, the interview shows an example of a good interview.
