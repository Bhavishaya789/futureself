# Journey to the End of the Earth
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 english vistas journey to the end of the earth tishani doshi antarctica
@study
Journey to the End of the Earth is a travelogue by Tishani Doshi about her journey to Antarctica, the southernmost continent, on a student expedition organised by Students on Ice. The writer sailed from Argentina and crossed the rough Drake Passage to reach Antarctica. She explains that the continent holds the key to the earth's past, present and future. Its layers of ice hold records of climate, with ice cores that give information about hundreds of thousands of years. Millions of years ago, Antarctica was joined with India, Africa, South America and Australia in the supercontinent Gondwana, about 650 million years ago in the writer's account. Antarctica is the coldest, driest and windiest continent, and is protected by the Antarctic Treaty of 1959 for peace and science. India has research stations there. The writer saw icebergs, penguins and other wildlife and realised how fragile the ecosystem is. She cautions about global warming, melting ice and the ozone hole, first detected over Antarctica in 1985, and about the need for human beings to act responsibly. The visit made her think about time, the age of the earth and the smallness of humans. Themes: environmental awareness, global warming, unity of the earth, adventure and curiosity. Style: travel writing, vivid description, scientific facts and personal reflection.
## Deep dive: Antarctica
The continent holds a record of the earth's climate in its ice and shows how connected the earth's continents once were.
## Deep dive: Warning
Melting ice, global warming and the ozone hole warn of environmental danger.
## Deep dive: Personal view
The journey makes the writer reflect on human smallness and responsibility to the planet.
