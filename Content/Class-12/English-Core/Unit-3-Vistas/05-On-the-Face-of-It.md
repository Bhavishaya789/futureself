# On the Face of It
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 english vistas on the face of it susan hill mr lamb derry garden
@study
On the Face of It by Susan Hill is a one-act play about the meeting of two lonely people: Mr Lamb, an old man with a tin leg who lives alone in a garden, and Derry, a teenage boy whose face is badly scarred by acid burns on one side. Derry is bitter and ashamed because people shun him; he thinks no one will ever accept him. One day he climbs the wall of Mr Lamb's garden. Mr Lamb, a kind and cheerful man, welcomes him, offers him apples and tells him of the pleasures of the garden, the bees, the flowers and the smell of the earth. He tells Derry that the garden is open to all and that he should not hide from the world. Derry slowly begins to trust him. Mr Lamb says that the face is only the surface and that he should go out and meet people. At the end of the play, Mr Lamb dies in an accident while picking apples, but Derry goes on with the lesson Mr Lamb taught him. Themes: loneliness, physical difference, prejudice, acceptance, friendship and human contact. Symbols: the garden (life and acceptance), the wall (isolation), the gate (welcome), the tin leg (disability), the apple (friendship). Style: play, mostly dialogue, symbolic.
## Deep dive: Derry
A boy hurt by acid who is isolated by others' reactions to his face; he hides behind a wall.
## Deep dive: Mr Lamb
A kindly old man who accepts everyone and teaches Derry to face the world; the garden is his welcoming world.
## Deep dive: Symbols
The garden stands for life and acceptance; the wall stands for isolation; the open gate stands for welcome.
