# The Enemy
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 english vistas the enemy pearl s buck sadao hana prisoner of war
@study
The Enemy by Pearl S. Buck is set in Japan during the Second World War. Dr Sadao Hoki, a Japanese surgeon who studied in America under Dr Harley, lives by the sea with his wife Hana. One day they find a wounded American soldier washed ashore, a prisoner of war who has escaped. Sadao decides to treat him though it is risky to shelter an enemy; Hana is reluctant at first but helps him. Sadao operates on the man at home. The servants, shocked that an enemy is being cared for, leave. Sadao is torn between his duty as a doctor and as a patriot. He is the personal doctor of the General, who is old and ill, and he finally informs the General. The General promises to send assassins to kill the prisoner quietly, but they never come: the General has forgotten the matter. Fearing the man will be discovered, Sadao helps the prisoner escape in a boat to a nearby island with food, water and a torch, and tells him to signal to a Korean fishing boat. The soldier escapes. Themes: humanity beyond nationality, duty of a doctor, conflict between patriotism and compassion, prejudice in war. Characters: Sadao is humane and professional, Hana is brave and caring, the General is self centred, the servants are loyal but prejudiced. Style: third person narration with irony and suspense.
## Deep dive: Conflict
Sadao is caught between his duty to his country and his duty to save life.
## Deep dive: Humanity
The doctor's compassion overcomes national hatred; Hana joins him in helping the enemy.
## Deep dive: General
The General's selfishness lets Sadao off; he forgets the plan because he needs the doctor.
