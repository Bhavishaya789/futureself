# Physical Education and Sports for CWSN
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physical education children with special needs disability disorder autism ADHD paralympics special olympics deaflympics
@study
CWSN means Children With Special Needs. A disorder is a disturbance in the normal functioning of body or mind, while a disability is a physical or mental condition that limits movement, senses or activities. Disability is described as cognitive, physical and sensory. Cognitive disabilities include intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder (ASD), attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and specific learning disability (SLD) such as dyslexia (reading), dysgraphia (writing) and dyscalculia (mathematics). Physical disabilities include locomotor disability (difficulty in movement), visual impairment and hearing impairment. Physical activity gives CWSN better health, social inclusion, self-confidence, independence and motor skills. Strategies to make activities accessible include adapting rules, equipment and the environment, giving clear demonstration, keeping groups small, using teaching aids, ensuring safety and rewarding effort. Inclusive education and inclusive sports mean learning and playing together. Disability etiquette means people-first language and respect, for example saying a person with a disability, not a disabled person, asking before helping and speaking directly to the person. Organisations and events matter. The Paralympic Games grew from the work of Ludwig Guttmann at Stoke Mandeville, and the first Paralympic Games were held in Rome in 1960. Special Olympics was founded by Eunice Kennedy Shriver in 1968 for people with intellectual disability and its oath says Let me win, but if I cannot win, let me be brave in the attempt. Special Olympics Bharat is the Indian programme. The Deaflympics are for deaf athletes, first held in Paris in 1924. Counsellors, coaches, special educators and parents form the support team.
## Deep dive: Disability and disorder
A disorder disturbs functioning; a disability limits activity. Types include cognitive, physical and sensory.
## Deep dive: Inclusion
Adapted rules, equipment and attitudes let CWSN take part, gain confidence and become independent.
## Deep dive: Events
Paralympics, Special Olympics and Deaflympics give CWSN a competitive platform.
