# Physiology and Injuries in Sports
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physical education physiology injuries fracture sprain strain PRICER strength endurance speed flexibility fast twitch
@study
Physiological factors determine each motor quality. Strength depends on muscle cross-section, muscle fibre type, age, gender, nervous coordination and hormones. Endurance depends on aerobic capacity (VO2 max), anaerobic threshold, muscle fuel stores, heart and lung efficiency and tolerance of lactic acid. Speed depends on reaction ability, muscle fibre composition (fast twitch), strength and flexibility. Flexibility depends on joint structure, muscle elasticity, tendons and ligaments, age, gender, temperature and warm-up. Coordination depends on the central nervous system and sense organs. Exercise improves heart size and stroke volume, lowers resting heart rate, raises lung capacity and strengthens muscles and bones. With ageing, muscle mass, bone density and flexibility decline. Gender differences include greater muscle mass and haemoglobin in males and more body fat in females. Sports injuries are classified as soft tissue, bone and joint injuries. Soft tissue injuries include abrasion (scrape), contusion (bruise), laceration (irregular cut), incision (clean cut), sprain (ligament tear) and strain (muscle or tendon tear). Bone injuries are fractures: simple, compound (open), greenstick (partial, in children), comminuted (broken into pieces), transverse, oblique and impacted. A dislocation is displacement of a bone from its joint. Causes include poor warm-up, faulty technique, fatigue, bad equipment and ground, and overtraining. Prevention includes proper warm-up, conditioning, protective equipment and rest. First aid for soft tissue injury follows PRICER: Protection, Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation and Rehabilitation. Cramps are painful involuntary muscle contractions treated with stretching and fluids.
## Deep dive: Physiological factors
Strength, endurance, speed, flexibility and coordination depend on fibre type, aerobic capacity, nervous control and joint structure.
## Deep dive: Injuries
Soft tissue, bone and joint injuries arise from poor warm-up, fatigue, faulty technique and bad equipment.
## Deep dive: First aid
PRICER guides care: protection, rest, ice, compression, elevation and rehabilitation.
