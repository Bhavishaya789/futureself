# Yoga as Preventive Measure for Lifestyle Disease
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physical education yoga lifestyle disease obesity diabetes asthma hypertension back pain asanas pranayama
@study
Lifestyle diseases arise from daily habits such as a sedentary lifestyle, poor diet, stress and lack of sleep. Common examples are obesity, diabetes, hypertension, asthma and back pain. Yoga is a preventive and corrective measure because asanas, pranayama and meditation improve circulation, digestion, flexibility and mental calm. Obesity is excess body fat; beneficial asanas include Tadasana, Katichakrasana, Pavanmuktasana, Matsyasana, Halasana, Pashchimottanasana, Ardha Matsyendrasana, Dhanurasana and Ustrasana. Diabetes is a condition in which the body cannot control blood sugar; helpful asanas include Katichakrasana, Pavanmuktasana, Bhujangasana, Shalabhasana, Dhanurasana, Pashchimottanasana and Ardha Matsyendrasana. Asthma is a respiratory disorder; helpful asanas include Sukhasana, Chakrasana, Gomukhasana, Parvatasana, Bhujangasana and Matsyasana, with pranayama such as Anulom Vilom. Hypertension is persistently high blood pressure; helpful asanas include Tadasana, Vajrasana, Ardha Chakrasana, Pavanmuktasana and Bhujangasana with relaxation like Shavasana. Back pain is treated with Tadasana, Ardha Chakrasana, Vakrasana, Bhujangasana and Shalabhasana. Pranayama such as Anulom Vilom, Kapalbhati, Bhramari and Ujjayi aid breathing and calm. Shatkarmas are cleansing techniques: Neti, Dhauti, Nauli, Basti, Trataka and Kapalbhati. Practice precautions include empty stomach, a clean airy place, supervision, and avoiding force. Contra-indications must be known: a person with a heart problem should not do vigorous practices. Surya Namaskar is a series of twelve postures.
## Deep dive: Lifestyle diseases
Poor habits cause obesity, diabetes, hypertension, asthma and back pain, which yoga helps prevent.
## Deep dive: Asanas
Different asanas are recommended for each condition, along with pranayama and relaxation.
## Deep dive: Precautions
Practise on an empty stomach under guidance and follow contra-indications.
## Hindi saransh
Yoga aur jeevan-shaili rog: motapa, madhumeh, ucch raktchaap, dama aur kamar dard ke liye asan.
> Remember: Shavasana relaxes; Kapalbhati is also a shatkarma.
