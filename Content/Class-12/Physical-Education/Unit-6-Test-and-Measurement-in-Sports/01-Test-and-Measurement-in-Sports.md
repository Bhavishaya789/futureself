# Test and Measurement in Sports

@study
Measurement is the process of assigning numbers to a performance, while evaluation judges its quality against a standard. A test is a tool for measurement. Tests are used to assess fitness, select players, plan training and monitor progress. The Rikli and Jones Senior Citizen Fitness Test is for adults aged 60 and above and has seven items: chair stand test (lower body strength, 30 seconds), arm curl test (upper body strength, 30 seconds; 5 pound weight for women and 8 pound for men), chair sit and reach (lower body flexibility), back scratch (upper body flexibility), eight-foot up and go (agility and dynamic balance), and the six-minute walk or the two-minute step test (aerobic endurance). Motor fitness tests include the 50 m dash for speed, the 600 m run or walk for endurance, the standing broad jump for explosive leg strength, the medicine ball throw for arm strength and the sit and reach for flexibility. The Harvard Step Test measures cardiovascular fitness. The bench is 20 inches high for men and 18 inches for women, the stepping rate is 30 steps per minute for up to five minutes, and the pulse is counted from 1 to 1.5 minutes after exercise. The Physical Fitness Index is duration of exercise in seconds multiplied by 100, divided by 5.5 times the pulse count. A score above 90 is excellent, 80 to 89 good, 65 to 79 high average, 55 to 64 low average and below 55 poor. Measuring general motor fitness uses speed, strength, endurance and flexibility items together. Good tests are valid, reliable, objective and standardised.
## Deep dive: Measurement and evaluation
Measurement gives numbers; evaluation judges them against a standard for selection and planning.
## Deep dive: Rikli and Jones
A seven item test for older adults covering strength, flexibility, agility and endurance.
## Deep dive: Harvard Step Test
Measures cardiovascular fitness; the index is 100 times the duration divided by 5.5 times the pulse count.

## High
P: A subject steps for 300 seconds and the pulse count is 60. Find the PFI.
A: PFI = (300 x 100) / (5.5 x 60) = 30000 / 330 = about 90.9.
