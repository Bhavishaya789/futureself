# Management of Sporting Events
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physical education management of sporting events fixtures knockout league tournament committees marathon
@study
Sports event management is the planning, organising, directing and controlling of a sports event. Its functions follow the managerial pattern: planning (goals, resources, schedule), organising (committees and duties), staffing (officials and volunteers), directing (leadership and coordination) and controlling (monitoring and evaluation). Committees are formed before, during and after an event: pre-event committees handle publicity, finance, ground and equipment, transport, accommodation, food, medical and technical matters; during-event committees look after the conduct and supervision; post-event committees handle results, thanks and evaluation. Intramural competitions are held within one institution, while extramural competitions involve teams from different institutions. A tournament has formats. In a knockout tournament, a team that loses is out; the number of matches equals N - 1 for N teams; if N is not a power of 2, byes equal the next power of 2 minus N, and byes are distributed in upper and lower halves with seeding to keep strong teams apart. In a league or round robin tournament, every team plays every other team once; the number of matches is N(N - 1)/2 and rounds are N - 1 for an even N and N for an odd N. In a cyclic fixture, teams are arranged in rotation. A combination tournament mixes knockout and league stages. Special events include Marathon (42.195 km), Run for Fun and Run for Specific Cause such as health or awareness, and community sports events for participation. Planning a run involves route, safety, hydration, volunteers, medical teams and clearance. A sports meet needs a schedule, officials, scoring and records.
## Deep dive: Event management
Planning, organising, staffing, directing and controlling are applied to sports events through committees.
## Deep dive: Fixtures
Knockout, league and combination fixtures use formulas for the number of matches, rounds and byes.
## Deep dive: Run events
Marathons and awareness runs need route planning, safety, hydration and medical arrangements.
