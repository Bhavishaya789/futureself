# Production and Costs
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 economics production function law of variable proportions returns to scale cost curves revenue
@study
Production is the transformation of inputs into output. The production function expresses the maximum output from given inputs: Q = f(L, K). Short run: at least one factor is fixed; long run: all factors are variable. Total product (TP), average product (AP = TP/L) and marginal product (MP = change in TP / change in L, or TPn - TPn-1). Law of variable proportions (law of diminishing returns to a factor): when more units of a variable factor are combined with fixed factors, TP first rises at an increasing rate, then at a decreasing rate, reaches a maximum and falls. Stages: Stage I (increasing returns, MP rises up to its peak, AP rises till it equals MP); Stage II (diminishing returns, MP falls but is positive, TP rises at a decreasing rate until MP is zero; the rational stage); Stage III (negative returns, MP negative, TP falls). MP cuts AP at the maximum of AP; AP rises when MP is above AP. Returns to scale (long run): increasing, constant and decreasing returns when all inputs change in the same proportion. Costs: fixed cost (TFC; does not change with output), variable cost (TVC; changes with output), total cost TC = TFC + TVC. Average costs: AFC = TFC/Q (falls continuously; rectangular hyperbola), AVC = TVC/Q (U shaped), ATC or AC = TC/Q = AFC + AVC (U shaped), marginal cost MC = change in TC / change in Q = TCn - TCn-1 (equals the change in TVC; U shaped, cuts AVC and ATC at their minimum points from below). U shape arises from the law of variable proportions. Opportunity cost, explicit and implicit costs, sunk cost. Revenue: TR = P x Q; AR = TR/Q = price; MR = change in TR / change in Q. Under perfect competition AR = MR = price (horizontal). Under imperfect competition AR slopes downward and MR lies below AR. Profit = TR - TC. Break even: TR = TC. Shut down point: price equals minimum AVC. Supply: the quantity a seller is willing to offer at a price; law of supply: price and quantity supplied are directly related. Determinants: price of the good, input prices, technology, taxes and subsidies, goals and number of firms. Elasticity of supply: percentage change in quantity supplied / percentage change in price; a straight line supply curve through the origin has unit elasticity. Producer equilibrium: MC = MR and MC cuts MR from below (MC rising).
## Deep dive: Short run production
TP, AP and MP follow the law of variable proportions: MP rises, falls, then turns negative. Stage II is the rational stage.
## Deep dive: Costs
TC = TFC + TVC. MC is U shaped and cuts AVC and ATC at their minima. AFC falls throughout.
## Deep dive: Revenue and equilibrium
Under perfect competition AR = MR = price. A producer is in equilibrium where MC = MR with MC rising.
