# Theory of the Firm under Perfect Competition
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 economics perfect competition firm equilibrium supply curve price taker
@study
Market forms are classified by the number of firms, nature of the product, entry and exit and information. Perfect competition features: a large number of buyers and sellers, homogeneous products, free entry and exit, perfect knowledge, perfect mobility of factors and no government intervention. Each firm is a price taker and the industry is the price maker; the demand curve facing a firm is perfectly elastic (horizontal) at the market price; AR = MR = price. Profit maximisation: a firm maximises profit where MC = MR and MC is rising (cuts MR from below), i.e. MC slope greater than MR slope. The firm earns supernormal profit if P > AC, normal profit if P = AC and incurs a loss if P < AC; in the short run a firm continues if P >= AVC (covers variable cost); it shuts down if P < minimum AVC. Short run supply curve of a firm: the rising portion of the MC curve above the minimum AVC. Long run: free entry and exit eliminate supernormal profit and losses, so firms earn normal profit at minimum AC where P = MC = AC. Industry supply is the horizontal sum of the supply curves of all firms. Elasticity of supply Es = (percentage change in quantity supplied) / (percentage change in price) = (dQ/dP) x (P/Q). Determinants: nature of the good, cost of production, time period and technology. Types: elastic, inelastic, unitary, perfectly elastic, perfectly inelastic. Geometric method: a straight line supply curve meeting the quantity axis has Es<1, meeting the price axis has Es>1, and passing through the origin has Es = 1. Price ceilings (maximum price, e.g. rent control) cause shortages; price floors (minimum price, e.g. minimum support price, minimum wage) cause surpluses. Normal profit is the minimum reward to the entrepreneur, included in cost.
## Deep dive: Features
Many small firms, identical products, free entry and exit and perfect knowledge make the firm a price taker.
## Deep dive: Equilibrium
The firm produces where MC = MR with MC rising; the short run supply curve is the MC curve above minimum AVC.
## Deep dive: Long run
Free entry and exit lead to normal profit where P = MC = minimum AC.
