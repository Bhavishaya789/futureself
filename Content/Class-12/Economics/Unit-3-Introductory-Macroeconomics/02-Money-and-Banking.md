# Money and Banking
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 economics money functions supply RBI credit creation CRR SLR repo monetary policy
@study
Barter: exchange of goods for goods; its drawback is the lack of double coincidence of wants. Money is anything generally accepted as a medium of exchange. Functions: primary: medium of exchange and unit of account; secondary: store of value and standard of deferred payments; contingent: transfer of value. Money supply: M1 = currency with the public + demand deposits with banks + other deposits with RBI; broader aggregates M2, M3 and M4. Demand for money: transactions demand and speculative demand (inversely related to interest rate). Commercial banks accept deposits, give loans and create credit. Money creation: money multiplier = 1 / LRR, where LRR (legal reserve ratio) is the cash reserve ratio. Credit creation: initial deposit x money multiplier; banks create demand deposits when loans are given. Example: initial deposit 1000 and reserve ratio 20 percent gives multiplier 5 and total deposits 5000. Central bank (Reserve Bank of India, set up in 1935 and nationalised in 1949): issues currency (notes except one rupee), banker to government, banker's bank, controller of credit and custodian of foreign exchange. Quantitative tools of monetary policy: bank rate; repo rate (rate at which RBI lends to banks against securities; an increase reduces borrowing and tightens credit); reverse repo rate (rate at which RBI borrows from banks); CRR (cash reserve ratio: share of deposits held as cash with RBI); SLR (statutory liquidity ratio: share held in liquid assets such as gold and government securities); open market operations (buying/selling government securities; buying injects money, selling withdraws it); margin requirements. Qualitative tools: moral suasion, selective credit control. Monetary policy fights inflation by tightening and recession by easing. Fiat money is legal tender by decree; commodity money has intrinsic value. Legal tender: limited and unlimited. Demonetisation. Digital payments: UPI, cards and wallets. Banks: scheduled, commercial, cooperative, regional rural. Credit creation limited by the reserve ratio.
## Deep dive: Money
Money solves the double coincidence problem; it is a medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value and standard of deferred payment.
## Deep dive: Credit creation
Banks lend a part of deposits and so create money; the multiplier is the reciprocal of the reserve ratio.
## Deep dive: Monetary policy
RBI uses repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR and open market operations to control credit and inflation.
