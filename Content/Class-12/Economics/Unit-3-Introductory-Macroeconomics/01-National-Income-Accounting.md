# National Income Accounting
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 economics national income GDP GNP NNP circular flow methods real nominal GDP deflator
@study
Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole: output, income, employment and prices. Circular flow of income: households supply factors to firms and receive factor payments; firms supply goods and receive spending; leakages (savings, taxes, imports) and injections (investment, government spending, exports). Basic concepts: consumption goods and capital goods; final and intermediate goods (only final goods are counted to avoid double counting); stocks and flows (capital, wealth are stocks; investment, income are flows); gross and net investment; depreciation (consumption of fixed capital); GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced within the domestic territory in a year; GNP = GDP + net factor income from abroad (NFIA); NDP = GDP - depreciation; NNP = GNP - depreciation. At market price versus factor cost: GDP at factor cost = GDP at market price - indirect taxes + subsidies; NNP at factor cost = National Income (NI). Personal income and personal disposable income. Methods: (1) Production or value added method: GVA = value of output - intermediate consumption; sum of value added by all sectors; GDP at market price = GVA at basic prices + net product taxes. (2) Income method: compensation of employees + operating surplus (rent, interest, profit) + mixed income + net factor income from abroad = NNP at factor cost. (3) Expenditure method: GDP = C + I + G + (X - M). NNP at factor cost = GDP at MP - depreciation + NFIA - NIT (net indirect taxes). Nominal GDP is at current prices; real GDP at constant (base year) prices: Real GDP = Nominal GDP / Price index x 100. GDP deflator = (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) x 100. GDP and welfare: limitations include non-monetary transactions, externalities, distribution of income, unpaid household work and underground economy; GDP does not measure well being fully. Green GDP. Real income and price level. Exclusions from national income: second-hand sales, financial transfers, pensions, scholarships, windfall gains. Precautions to avoid double counting. Per capita income = National income / population.
## Deep dive: Circular flow
Households and firms exchange factors and goods; savings, taxes and imports are leakages while investment, government spending and exports are injections.
## Deep dive: Three methods
Value added, income and expenditure methods all give the same GDP in principle.
## Deep dive: Real and nominal
Real GDP removes price effects; the deflator is nominal over real times 100. GDP is an imperfect measure of welfare.
