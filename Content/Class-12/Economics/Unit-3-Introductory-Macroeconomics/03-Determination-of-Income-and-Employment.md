# Determination of Income and Employment
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 economics aggregate demand multiplier consumption function MPC investment inflationary deflationary gap
@study
Keynes: in the short run, income and employment are determined by effective demand, i.e. aggregate demand (AD) equal to aggregate supply (AS). Ex ante and ex post. Aggregate demand AD = C + I + G + (X - M); in a simple two sector model AD = C + I. Consumption function C = a + bY, where a is autonomous consumption and b is the marginal propensity to consume (MPC); APC = C/Y; MPC = change in C / change in Y; APS = S/Y; MPS = change in S / change in Y; APC + APS = 1; MPC + MPS = 1. Saving function S = -a + (1 - b)Y. Break-even point where C = Y, S = 0. Investment: autonomous investment is independent of income; induced investment depends on income. Equilibrium income: Y = C + I, or S = I (saving equals investment); at income above equilibrium, S > I and output falls (unplanned inventory accumulation); below equilibrium, S < I and output rises. Y* = (a + I)/(1 - b). Investment multiplier k = change in Y / change in I = 1/(1 - MPC) = 1/MPS. Larger MPC means a bigger multiplier. The multiplier works both ways (forward and backward). Full employment: employment of all who are willing and able to work at the prevailing wage rate; involuntary unemployment exists below full employment; Keynes rejected the classical idea that supply creates its own demand (Say's law). Excess demand (inflationary gap): AD exceeds the AD needed for full employment equilibrium, so the price level rises. Deficient demand (deflationary gap): AD falls short of the AD needed for full employment; output and employment fall. Measures to correct excess demand: fiscal (reduce government expenditure, raise taxes), monetary (raise CRR, repo rate, SLR, sell securities, tighter credit); to correct deficient demand: the opposite (more government spending, lower taxes, lower interest rates, buy securities). Unemployment types: frictional, structural, cyclical, disguised. Paradox of thrift: if everyone saves more, aggregate income falls so total savings may not increase.
## Deep dive: Equilibrium
Income is at equilibrium where AD = AS or planned saving equals planned investment.
## Deep dive: Multiplier
k = 1/(1 - MPC) = 1/MPS; a rise in investment raises income by a multiple.
## Deep dive: Gaps
Excess demand causes an inflationary gap; deficient demand a deflationary gap. Fiscal and monetary tools correct them.
