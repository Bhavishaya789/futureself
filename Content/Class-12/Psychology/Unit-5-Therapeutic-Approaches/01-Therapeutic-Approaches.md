# Therapeutic Approaches
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 psychology therapeutic approaches psychotherapy behaviour therapy cognitive therapy beck ellis rogers biomedical ECT rehabilitation
@study
Psychotherapy is a voluntary, systematic interaction between a client and a trained therapist to relieve distress and improve functioning. The therapeutic relationship, built on trust, empathy and confidentiality, is central. Psychodynamic therapy by Freud uses free association, dream analysis, interpretation, transference (feelings towards the therapist) and resistance, with the aim of catharsis and insight. Behaviour therapy applies learning principles. Joseph Wolpe’s systematic desensitisation uses reciprocal inhibition: the client learns relaxation, builds an anxiety hierarchy and faces feared stimuli step by step; flooding exposes the client at once; aversion therapy pairs an unwanted behaviour with an unpleasant stimulus; a token economy rewards desirable behaviour with tokens; modelling teaches by observation. Cognitive therapies change faulty thinking: Aaron Beck’s cognitive therapy corrects negative automatic thoughts in depression, and Albert Ellis’s rational emotive behaviour therapy uses the ABC model (Activating event, Belief, Consequence), disputing irrational beliefs; cognitive behaviour therapy combines both. Humanistic-existential therapies include Carl Rogers’s client-centred therapy, which uses unconditional positive regard, empathy and genuineness; Fritz Perls’s Gestalt therapy; and Viktor Frankl’s logotherapy, which seeks meaning. Biomedical therapies include drug therapy (antipsychotics, antidepressants, antianxiety drugs and mood stabilisers such as lithium), electroconvulsive therapy (introduced by Cerletti and Bini in 1938, used mainly for severe depression) and, rarely, psychosurgery. Group, family and couples therapy treat relationships. Alternative approaches include yoga, meditation and Vipassana. Rehabilitation helps people with mental illness live in the community through occupational therapy, social skills training and vocational training. The Rehabilitation Council of India regulates professionals, and the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 protects the rights of persons with mental illness. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can prescribe drugs; a clinical psychologist gives psychotherapy.
## Deep dive: Psychotherapies
Psychodynamic, behavioural, cognitive and humanistic therapies target different problems.
## Deep dive: Biomedical therapy
Drugs and ECT treat severe disorders alongside psychological help.
## Deep dive: Rehabilitation
Occupational, social and vocational training restore community life; the 2017 Act protects rights.
