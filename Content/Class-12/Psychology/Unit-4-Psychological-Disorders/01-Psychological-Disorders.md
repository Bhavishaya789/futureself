# Psychological Disorders

@study
Abnormal behaviour is judged by several criteria: deviation from social norms, statistical infrequency, personal distress, maladaptive behaviour and danger to self or others, often summarised as the four Ds (deviance, distress, dysfunction, danger). Explanatory models include biological, psychodynamic, behavioural, cognitive, humanistic-existential and the diathesis-stress model, in which a vulnerability combines with stress. Classification systems are the DSM of the American Psychiatric Association and the ICD of the World Health Organization. Anxiety disorders include generalised anxiety disorder (persistent worry), panic disorder (sudden intense attacks), phobias (irrational fear of an object or situation, such as specific phobia, social phobia and agoraphobia), obsessive-compulsive disorder (recurring obsessions and compulsions) and post-traumatic stress disorder (flashbacks and hyperarousal after trauma). Somatic symptom disorders involve physical complaints with psychological causes, such as conversion disorder and illness anxiety (hypochondriasis). Dissociative disorders involve a break in memory or identity: dissociative amnesia, dissociative fugue and dissociative identity disorder, along with depersonalisation. Mood disorders include major depressive disorder (low mood, loss of interest, sleep and appetite change, fatigue, guilt) and bipolar disorder (alternating mania and depression); mania features elevated, irritable mood and high energy. Schizophrenia is a severe disorder with positive symptoms (delusions, which are false beliefs, hallucinations, which are perceptions without stimuli, and disorganised speech), negative symptoms (flat affect, avolition, alogia) and psychomotor symptoms. Substance-related disorders involve dependence with tolerance and withdrawal. Behavioural and developmental disorders include ADHD, oppositional defiant disorder and conduct disorder, and autism spectrum disorder. Eating disorders include anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa and binge eating disorder. Mental illness should be met with empathy, as stigma delays help seeking; mental health professionals include psychiatrists and clinical psychologists.
## Deep dive: Concept of abnormality
Deviance, distress, dysfunction and danger; models include the diathesis-stress approach.
## Deep dive: Major disorders
Anxiety, somatic, dissociative, mood, schizophrenia, substance and developmental disorders.
## Deep dive: Stigma
Stigma delays help; understanding and professional treatment help recovery.
