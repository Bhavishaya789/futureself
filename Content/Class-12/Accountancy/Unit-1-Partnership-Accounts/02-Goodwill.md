# Goodwill
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 accountancy goodwill valuation average super profit capitalisation
@study
Goodwill is the value of the reputation of a firm in respect of profits expected in future over and above the normal profits. It is an intangible asset but not a fictitious one, and is a non-current asset that cannot be seen or touched. Features: it is an intangible asset, helps to earn super profits, its value fluctuates and it cannot be separated from the business. Factors affecting goodwill: nature of business, location, efficiency of management, market situation, special advantages such as patents, and the risk involved. Need for valuation: change in profit sharing ratio, admission of a partner, retirement or death of a partner, dissolution when the business is sold as a going concern, and sale of the business. Methods: (1) Average Profit Method: Goodwill = Average profit x Number of years purchase. Average profit = total profit of past years / number of years. (2) Super Profit Method: Super profit = Average profit - Normal profit; Goodwill = Super profit x Number of years purchase. Normal profit = Capital employed x Normal rate of return / 100. Capital employed = Total assets (excluding goodwill and fictitious assets) - Outside liabilities, or equals the sum of partners' capital plus reserves. (3) Capitalisation of Average Profit: Capitalised value = Average profit x 100 / Normal rate of return; Goodwill = Capitalised value - Capital employed (net assets). (4) Capitalisation of Super Profit: Goodwill = Super profit x 100 / Normal rate of return. Adjustments before calculating average profit: abnormal gains or losses are removed; non-recurring items are adjusted; changes in the value of closing stock are adjusted; items like interest on investments and tax are adjusted as required; remuneration of partners is deducted if not already charged. Weighted average profit method gives weights to the years (such as 1, 2, 3) with the most recent year having the highest weight: Weighted average = Sum of (profit x weight) / Sum of weights. Treatment of goodwill in an existing business: purchased goodwill is shown in the balance sheet as an asset. Goodwill when a partner is admitted: premium brought by the new partner. Hidden goodwill: when the capital of a new partner is given with his share, goodwill is calculated as the difference between the total capital of the firm and the net assets. Accounting Standard 26 (AS 26) prohibits recognition of self-generated goodwill; only purchased goodwill is recorded. Normal rate of return is the rate expected in similar business. Number of years of purchase is a matter of agreement. Adjusted average profit may need adjustments for abnormal items, such as a loss by fire (added back). Super profit arises when the actual profit exceeds normal profit.
## Deep dive: Concept and factors
Goodwill is the extra earning power due to reputation. Location, management, and market conditions affect it. It is recorded only when purchased.
## Deep dive: Methods
Average profit multiplies average profit by years of purchase. Super profit methods use the excess of actual over normal profit. Capitalisation methods divide by the normal rate.
## Deep dive: Adjustments
Profit must be adjusted for abnormal and non-recurring items and for partners' remuneration. Capital employed excludes goodwill.
