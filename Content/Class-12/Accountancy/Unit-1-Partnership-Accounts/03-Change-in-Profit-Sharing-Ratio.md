# Change in Profit Sharing Ratio
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 accountancy change in profit sharing ratio sacrificing gaining ratio reserves revaluation
@study
A change in profit sharing ratio among existing partners amounts to the reconstitution of the firm; the old firm is deemed to be dissolved but the business continues. Some partners gain and some sacrifice in profit share. Sacrificing ratio = Old ratio - New ratio (for the partner who sacrifices). Gaining ratio = New ratio - Old ratio (for the partner who gains). The gaining partners compensate the sacrificing partners for the loss of future profits through goodwill. Treatment of goodwill: (1) if goodwill already appears in the books, it is written off to partners' capital accounts in the old ratio (Partners' Capital A/cs Dr; To Goodwill) and then a new valuation is raised in the new ratio; (2) a premium for goodwill is adjusted by debiting the gaining partners' capital accounts and crediting the sacrificing partners' capital accounts in the sacrificing ratio. Goodwill is distributed according to the ratio of gain and sacrifice: Gaining Partners' Capital A/c Dr; To Sacrificing Partners' Capital A/c. Accumulated profits and losses (general reserve, profit and loss account balance, reserve fund) belong to old partners and are distributed among them in the old ratio: General Reserve A/c Dr; To Partners' Capital A/cs. Accumulated losses (debit balance of P&L and deferred revenue expenditure) are written off in the old ratio. Reserves can be retained in the books if the partners agree, in which case no adjusting entry is passed (or a memorandum is prepared). Revaluation of assets and reassessment of liabilities: since the values may have changed, a Revaluation Account is prepared; increase in assets and decrease in liabilities are credited (gains); decrease in assets and increase in liabilities are debited (losses). The profit or loss on revaluation is transferred to partners' capital accounts in the old ratio. Adjustment of capitals: partners may agree to adjust capitals in the new profit sharing ratio. Steps: calculate the new capitals of the partners on the basis of the total capital of the firm; compare with existing capital to find shortage or surplus; adjust through cash or current account. Past adjustments. Memorandum Revaluation Account is used where assets and liabilities are to be shown at old values after the revaluation. The reconstituted firm's balance sheet is then prepared. The treatment of goodwill may also be hidden within the agreed capitals. Where the question gives that the change in the ratio happens with the entire goodwill at a given value, then each partner's share in the goodwill is credited in the old ratio and debited in the new ratio. Net effect: Credit old ratio share, debit new ratio share.
## Deep dive: Sacrificing and gaining ratios
Compare old and new shares. The partner whose share falls sacrifices; the one whose share rises gains. The gainers compensate the sacrificers.
## Deep dive: Goodwill and reserves
Existing goodwill is written off in the old ratio. New goodwill is adjusted in gaining and sacrificing ratio. Reserves go to old partners in the old ratio.
## Deep dive: Revaluation
Revaluation updates assets and liabilities. Gain or loss goes to the old ratio. A memorandum account preserves book values.
