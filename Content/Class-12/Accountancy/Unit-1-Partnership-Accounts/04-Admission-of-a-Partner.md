# Admission of a Partner
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 accountancy admission of a partner new ratio sacrificing premium revaluation capital
@study
Admission of a new partner reconstitutes the firm: the old firm is dissolved and a new one formed, with the business continuing. Under Section 31 of the Partnership Act, a person can be admitted only with the consent of all existing partners. The new partner acquires a right to share in future profits and in the assets. Adjustments required: (1) new profit sharing ratio, (2) sacrificing ratio, (3) goodwill, (4) revaluation of assets and liabilities, (5) treatment of reserves and accumulated profits and losses, (6) adjustment of capitals. New profit ratio: the new partner's share is acquired from old partners either in an agreed proportion or equally. If the new partner takes 1/5 share from all partners in their old ratio, the old partners' shares reduce proportionately: Sacrificing ratio = Old ratio when the share is taken in the old ratio. If the new partner gets his share from specific partners, the sacrificing ratio = Old share - New share. Goodwill: the new partner brings premium for goodwill in cash because he acquires a right to future super profits; the premium is shared by the sacrificing partners in the sacrificing ratio. Cash A/c Dr; To Premium for Goodwill A/c. Premium for Goodwill A/c Dr; To Sacrificing partners' Capital A/cs (in sacrificing ratio). If premium is not brought in cash (the goodwill is paid privately), no entry is passed. If the new partner is unable to bring the premium, current account is debited: New Partner's Current A/c Dr. Hidden goodwill: when the capital brought by the new partner is more or less than his proportionate share, the difference implies goodwill. Total capital of new firm = New partner's capital x Reciprocal of his share; Goodwill = Total capital of firm - Net assets (actual combined). Revaluation: Revaluation A/c is prepared and profit or loss is shared among old partners in the old ratio. Reserves and accumulated profits and losses are credited or debited to old partners in the old ratio. Adjustment of capitals: if the old partners' capitals are to be in the new profit ratio, new capital of old partners = Total capital x New share; excess or shortage is adjusted by cash or current account. Treatment of goodwill already in books: written off in the old ratio. Preparation of the balance sheet of the new firm shows capitals, reserves and revalued assets. Ledger accounts: Partners' Capital Accounts, Revaluation Account, and Balance Sheet. Capital of the new partner may be given in cash or kind. Journal entry for the capital: Cash A/c Dr; To New Partner's Capital A/c.
## Deep dive: Reconstitution and ratios
Admission needs consent of all partners. The new partner acquires shares from old partners; sacrifice is computed as old share minus new share.
## Deep dive: Goodwill
The new partner pays a premium for the right to future super profits. Sacrificing partners receive it. Hidden goodwill is derived from capital.
## Deep dive: Revaluation and capital
Revaluation gain or loss goes to old partners. Reserves go to old partners. Capitals may be adjusted to the new ratio.

## Formulas
Sacrificing ratio = Old share - New share
Total capital = New partner capital / His share
Hidden goodwill = Total capital - Net assets
New capital of partner = Total capital x New share
Premium: Cash Dr; to sacrificers in sacrificing ratio
Reserves/Revaluation: old ratio
