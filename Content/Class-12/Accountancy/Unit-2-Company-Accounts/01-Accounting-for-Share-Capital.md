# Accounting for Share Capital
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 accountancy share capital forfeiture reissue oversubscription premium discount
@study
A company is an artificial person created by law with a separate legal entity, perpetual succession and limited liability. Share capital is the capital raised by issuing shares. Types of shares: equity shares (ordinary; carry voting rights and variable dividend) and preference shares (preferential right of dividend and repayment). Classes of share capital: Authorised capital (maximum capital as per the Memorandum of Association), Issued capital (offered to the public), Subscribed capital (taken up by the public), Called-up capital (the part of subscribed capital called by the company) and Paid-up capital (the part of called-up capital paid). Uncalled capital is the remaining and reserve capital is a part of uncalled capital that can be called only on winding up. Issue of shares: the share is issued by a prospectus; money is collected in stages as application, allotment and calls. Under Section 52 of the Companies Act 2013, securities premium is the amount received over the face value of shares; it can be used for issuing bonus shares, writing off preliminary expenses, issue expenses, premium on redemption of preference shares or debentures, and buying back shares. Minimum subscription is 90 percent of the issue (SEBI norms). Application money is the amount payable with the application; allotment is the acceptance of applications and the sum due on allotment; calls are the further demand. Journal entries: Bank A/c Dr; To Share Application A/c (on receipt). Share Application A/c Dr; To Share Capital A/c (on allotment); To Bank (refund). Share Allotment A/c Dr; To Share Capital; To Securities Premium. Bank A/c Dr; To Share Allotment A/c. Calls in arrears: the amount called but unpaid by a shareholder (a debit balance). Calls in advance: amount received before it is called (a credit balance; shown as a liability). Interest on calls in arrears per Table F is 10 percent (company may charge) and on calls in advance 12 percent. Over-subscription: when the applications received exceed the shares offered; the company may reject some applications and refund the money, allot shares in proportion (pro rata), or use a combination; the excess money is adjusted in allotment. Under-subscription: applications less than shares offered; allotment is made to all. Issue at par, at premium and at discount: shares can be issued at a discount only for sweat equity and not under normal conditions after the Companies Act 2013. Issue of shares for consideration other than cash: shares issued to vendors for purchase of assets; Sundry Assets A/c Dr; To Vendor; Vendor Dr; To Share Capital. Forfeiture of shares: cancellation of the shares for non-payment of calls. Share Capital A/c Dr (with called-up); To Calls in Arrears; To Share Forfeiture A/c (amount received). Securities premium unpaid is debited to the Securities Premium account when forfeited if the premium was not received. Reissue of forfeited shares: Bank Dr; Share Forfeiture Dr (loss); To Share Capital. The balance in the Share Forfeiture account after reissue (the gain) is transferred to Capital Reserve. The maximum discount on reissue is the amount credited to forfeited shares account for the shares reissued. Presentation in the balance sheet: Share capital under Shareholders' Funds, with details of authorised, issued, subscribed and paid-up capital.
## Deep dive: Share capital
Authorised, issued, subscribed, called-up and paid-up capital describe stages of raising funds. Shares may be equity or preference.
## Deep dive: Issue
Money is collected through application, allotment and calls. Securities premium arises above face value. Oversubscription needs pro rata allotment or refund.
## Deep dive: Forfeiture
Shares are forfeited for non-payment. Reissue may be at a discount. The profit goes to capital reserve.

## Formulas
Called-up = Subscribed amount called
Paid-up = Called-up - Calls in arrears
Premium = Issue price - Face value
Forfeiture: Share capital Dr (called-up); to Calls in arrears; to Forfeiture (received)
Reissue gain = Forfeited balance - Discount allowed; to Capital reserve
