# The Story of Indian Democracy
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 sociology story of indian democracy constitution preamble fundamental rights panchayati raj 73rd amendment judicial review
@study
India chose democracy through the Constitution, drafted by the Constituent Assembly (1946-49) in 2 years, 11 months and 18 days, with Dr B. R. Ambedkar as chairman of the drafting committee and Dr Rajendra Prasad as president; it was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950. The Preamble declares India a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic, with justice, liberty, equality and fraternity; the words socialist and secular were added by the 42nd Amendment (1976). The Constitution gives Fundamental Rights (enforceable) and Directive Principles (guidelines), and Fundamental Duties were added in 1976. Universal adult franchise was granted at the start, as a bold step in a poor, unequal society; the first elections were held in 1951-52. India has a parliamentary system and a federal structure with a strong centre, an independent judiciary and judicial review, and public interest litigation (PIL) widened access to justice. Ambedkar warned that political democracy needs social and economic democracy. The Constitution also focuses on social justice by reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and later OBCs. Panchayati Raj, rooted in Gandhi’s ideal of Gram Swaraj, was strengthened by the 73rd Constitutional Amendment (1992, in force 1993), giving a three-tier structure (village, block, district), regular elections, Gram Sabhas, and reservation of at least one-third of seats for women (many states use 50 per cent), and for SCs and STs. The 74th Amendment did the same for urban local bodies. Social movements, civil society and an active press deepen democracy. Challenges include corruption, criminalisation, communalism, caste mobilisation and inequality. The Emergency of 1975-77 tested democracy, but elections in 1977 showed its resilience. Democracy in India is a continuing process involving participation of diverse groups.
## Deep dive: Constitution
Drafted 1946-49, adopted 26 November 1949, in force 26 January 1950; Preamble with justice, liberty, equality, fraternity.
## Deep dive: Institutions
Parliamentary federal democracy with universal franchise, independent judiciary and PIL.
## Deep dive: Local democracy
73rd Amendment 1992 created three-tier Panchayati Raj with women’s reservation.
