# Alternating Current
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physics alternating current rms reactance impedance resonance power factor transformer LC oscillations
@study
An alternating voltage or current varies sinusoidally, v = v0 sin omega t and i = i0 sin omega t, with omega = 2 pi f. The average over a full cycle is zero; the root mean square value is i_rms = i0/sqrt 2 = 0.707 i0, and mains in India is about 230 V at 50 Hz. A resistor: voltage and current are in phase. An inductor: X_L = omega L and current lags voltage by 90 degrees. A capacitor: X_C = 1/(omega C) and current leads voltage by 90 degrees. In a series LCR circuit, the impedance is Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2) and the phase angle is tan phi = (X_L - X_C)/R. Resonance occurs when X_L = X_C, at omega0 = 1/sqrt(LC); impedance is minimum (Z = R) and current is maximum. The quality factor Q = omega0 L/R = (1/R) sqrt(L/C) measures sharpness, and the bandwidth is R/L in angular terms. Average power is P = V_rms I_rms cos phi, where cos phi = R/Z is the power factor. In a pure inductor or capacitor power factor is zero and the current is wattless. A choke coil reduces current with little power loss. LC oscillations have natural angular frequency 1/sqrt(LC), with energy exchanged between electric and magnetic forms. A transformer works on mutual induction: Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = Ip/Is for an ideal case. A step-up transformer has Ns greater than Np. Energy losses are copper loss, eddy current loss, hysteresis, flux leakage and humming, and a laminated soft iron core reduces them. An AC generator converts mechanical to electrical energy through electromagnetic induction.
## Deep dive: Phasors and reactance
Resistor in phase, inductor lags and capacitor leads by 90 degrees; X_L = omega L, X_C = 1/omega C.
## Deep dive: Resonance
At omega0 = 1/sqrt(LC), impedance equals R and current is maximum; Q = omega0 L / R.
## Deep dive: Power and transformer
P = VI cos phi; transformer ratio Vs/Vp = Ns/Np with core losses minimised by lamination.

## Key formulas
Angular frequency | omega = 2 pi f
RMS value | I_rms = I0 / sqrt 2
Inductive reactance | X_L = omega L
Capacitive reactance | X_C = 1 / (omega C)
Impedance | Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2)
Phase angle | tan phi = (X_L - X_C) / R
Resonant frequency | omega0 = 1 / sqrt(LC)
Quality factor | Q = omega0 L / R
Average power | P = V_rms I_rms cos phi
Power factor | cos phi = R / Z
Transformer | Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = Ip/Is
Efficiency | Output power / Input power
