# Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physics electrostatic potential capacitance capacitor dielectric equipotential energy stored combination
@study
Electrostatic potential at a point is the work done per unit positive charge in bringing it from infinity to that point, V = W/q, in volts (J/C). For a point charge V = kq/r; for a dipole on the axis V = kp/r^2 and on the equatorial line V = 0. Potential is a scalar and obeys superposition. The potential difference between two points is the work per unit charge, and the relation E = -dV/dr links field and potential. The potential energy of two point charges is U = k q1 q2 / r. An equipotential surface has the same potential everywhere; no work is done in moving a charge along it, field lines are perpendicular to it, and for a point charge these surfaces are concentric spheres. A conductor is an equipotential body. A capacitor stores charge and energy, with capacitance C = Q/V, in farad. For a parallel plate capacitor in air C = epsilon0 A/d. A dielectric of constant K raises capacitance to K epsilon0 A/d because polarisation reduces the field. In series, 1/C = 1/C1 + 1/C2 and charge is the same; in parallel, C = C1 + C2 and voltage is the same. Energy stored is U = (1/2) CV^2 = Q^2/(2C) = (1/2) QV, and energy density is (1/2) epsilon0 E^2. When a battery stays connected, V is constant; when it is disconnected, Q is constant. A dielectric slab of thickness t between plates gives C = epsilon0 A / (d - t + t/K). Sharing of charge between two connected capacitors conserves charge and loses energy as heat. Van de Graaff generators accumulate high voltages on a hollow sphere.
## Deep dive: Potential
V = kq/r is a scalar; E = -dV/dr; equipotential surfaces are perpendicular to field lines.
## Deep dive: Capacitance
C = Q/V; parallel plates give C = epsilon0 A/d and a dielectric multiplies it by K.
## Deep dive: Energy and combinations
U = (1/2)CV^2; series 1/C adds, parallel C adds.

P: Find the capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor of area 0.01 m^2 and gap 1 mm in air.

## High
P: Three capacitors of 2, 3 and 6 microfarad are in series across 12 V. Find the charge.
A: 1/C = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 = 1, so C = 1 microfarad and Q = 12 microcoulomb.

## Advanced
P: A 4 microfarad capacitor charged to 100 V is connected to an uncharged 6 microfarad capacitor. Find the common potential and energy lost.
A: Common V = 400e-6/10e-6 = 40 V; lost energy = 0.02 - 0.008 = 0.012 J.
