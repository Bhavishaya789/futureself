@study
**Current** I = Q/t. **Ohm's law** V = I R. Resistivity: R = rho L / A. Series: R = R1 + R2; parallel: 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2. Example: 4 ohm and 6 ohm in parallel = 24/10 = **2.4 ohm**.
**Cell:** terminal voltage V = E - I r. For E = 12 V, r = 1 ohm, R = 5 ohm: I = 12/6 = **2 A**, V = 12 - 2 = **10 V**.
**Kirchhoff's laws:** junction rule (sum of currents at a node is zero) and loop rule (sum of potential drops around a loop is zero). **Power** P = V I = I^2 R.
**Wheatstone bridge** is balanced when P/Q = R/S. **Potentiometer** compares EMFs accurately because it draws no current at balance.
