# Photoelectric effect and nuclei

@study
**Photoelectric effect:** light of frequency above the **threshold frequency** ejects electrons instantly. Einstein: h f = phi + K_max. Photon energy E (eV) = 1240 / lambda (nm). Example: lambda = 400 nm, work function 2.0 eV: E = 3.1 eV, K_max = **1.1 eV**. Stopping potential V_0 = K_max / e = 1.1 V. Intensity changes the number of electrons, not their maximum energy.
**de Broglie:** lambda = h / p.
**Nuclei:** mass defect gives binding energy E = (dm) c^2 (1 u = 931.5 MeV). **Radioactive decay:** N = N0 e^(-lambda t); half-life T = 0.693 / lambda. After 3 half-lives, 64 g becomes **8 g**. Alpha decay: Z falls by 2 and A by 4; beta decay: Z rises by 1.
**Semiconductor:** doping gives n-type (donor) and p-type (acceptor); a p-n junction conducts in forward bias only.
