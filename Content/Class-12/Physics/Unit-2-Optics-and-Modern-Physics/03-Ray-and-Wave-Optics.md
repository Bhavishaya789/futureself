# Ray optics and wave optics
minutes: 25

@study
**Mirror/lens formula:** 1/v - 1/u = 1/f (lens); 1/v + 1/u = 1/f (mirror); magnification m = v/u (lens) with sign convention (distances from the optical centre, direction of incident light positive).
Example: object at u = -30 cm, convex lens f = 20 cm: 1/v = 1/20 - 1/30 so v = **60 cm**, m = 60/(-30) = **-2** (real, inverted, twice the size).
**Refraction:** Snell's law n1 sin i = n2 sin r; critical angle sin c = 1/n; total internal reflection (optical fibres). **Lens maker's formula** 1/f = (n - 1)(1/R1 - 1/R2). Power P = 1/f (in metres) dioptres.
**Wave optics:** YDSE fringe width beta = lambda D / d; for lambda = 600 nm, D = 1 m, d = 1 mm: beta = **0.6 mm**. Diffraction single slit: first minimum at a sin t = lambda. Polarisation shows light is transverse; Brewster's law tan i_p = n.
