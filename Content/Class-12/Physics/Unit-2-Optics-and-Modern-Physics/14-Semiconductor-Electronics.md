# Semiconductor Electronics
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 physics semiconductor electronics p-n junction diode rectifier zener LED transistor logic gates
@study
In solids, energy bands form: valence band, conduction band and a forbidden gap Eg. Conductors have overlapping or no gap, insulators have Eg greater than about 3 eV, and semiconductors have Eg below 3 eV; silicon has 1.1 eV and germanium 0.67 eV. Pure semiconductors are intrinsic, with equal electrons and holes. Doping makes them extrinsic. An n-type semiconductor is doped with pentavalent atoms such as phosphorus, arsenic or antimony (donors); electrons are the majority carriers. A p-type semiconductor is doped with trivalent atoms such as boron, aluminium, gallium or indium (acceptors); holes are the majority carriers. In both, n_e n_h = n_i^2. A p-n junction forms a depletion region and a barrier potential of about 0.7 V for silicon and 0.3 V for germanium. In forward bias the barrier lowers and current flows; in reverse bias the barrier rises and only a tiny leakage current flows. A half-wave rectifier uses one diode and its ripple frequency equals the input; a full-wave rectifier gives ripple of twice the input frequency; a capacitor filter smooths the output. A Zener diode, operated in reverse breakdown, regulates voltage. An LED is forward biased and emits light of colour set by the band gap. A photodiode works in reverse bias and detects light; a solar cell generates voltage from light. A transistor has emitter (heavily doped), base (thin, lightly doped) and collector (larger); it was invented in 1947 at Bell Labs by Bardeen, Brattain and Shockley. In a common emitter, current gain beta = Ic/Ib, alpha = Ic/Ie and beta = alpha/(1 - alpha). A common emitter amplifier reverses phase by 180 degrees. As a switch, the transistor is off in cut-off and on in saturation. Logic gates are OR, AND, NOT, NAND, NOR and XOR; NAND and NOR are universal gates.
## Deep dive: Bands and doping
Eg decides conductor, semiconductor or insulator; n-type has donors, p-type has acceptors.
## Deep dive: Diodes
Forward bias conducts, reverse blocks; rectifiers, Zener regulation, LED and photodiode.
## Deep dive: Transistors and gates
beta = Ic/Ib; CE amplifier inverts; NAND and NOR are universal gates.

## Advanced
P: Draw the truth table of a NAND gate and show why it is universal.
A: Output is 0 only when both inputs are 1: 00 gives 1, 01 gives 1, 10 gives 1, 11 gives 0; tying inputs together gives NOT, and NOT after NAND gives AND, so all gates can be built.

R: 2 | AND from NAND
