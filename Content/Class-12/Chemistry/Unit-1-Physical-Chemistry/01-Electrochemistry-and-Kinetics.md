@study
**Galvanic cell:** E_cell = E_cathode - E_anode. Zn-Cu (Daniell) cell: E0 = +0.34 - (-0.76) = **1.10 V**. Gibbs energy dG = -n F E: for n = 2, F = 96500 C/mol: dG = **-212.3 kJ/mol**. **Nernst equation:** E = E0 - (0.059/n) log Q at 25 C. **Faraday's laws:** mass deposited m = Z I t.
**Kinetics:** rate = k [A]^n. **First order:** ln([A]0/[A]) = k t and half-life t1/2 = 0.693 / k (independent of concentration). For k = 0.0693 per min, t1/2 = **10 min**. **Arrhenius:** k = A e^(-Ea/RT); rate roughly doubles for a 10 K rise.
