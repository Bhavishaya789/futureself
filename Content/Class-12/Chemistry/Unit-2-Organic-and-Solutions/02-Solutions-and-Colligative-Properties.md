# Solutions and colligative properties
minutes: 20

@study
**Raoult's law:** partial pressure of a component equals its mole fraction times vapour pressure of the pure component. **Colligative properties** depend on the number of solute particles: relative lowering of vapour pressure, elevation of boiling point dTb = Kb m, depression of freezing point dTf = Kf m, osmotic pressure pi = C R T. **van't Hoff factor i** accounts for dissociation.
Example: 0.5 mol solute in 1 kg water (Kb = 0.52): dTb = 0.52 x 0.5 = **0.26 K**, so boiling point is 100.26 C.
