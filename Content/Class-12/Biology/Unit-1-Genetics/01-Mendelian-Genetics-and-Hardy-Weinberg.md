# Mendelian genetics and Hardy-Weinberg
minutes: 20
yt: mendel genetics class 12
@study
**Law of segregation:** alleles separate during gamete formation. **Law of independent assortment:** genes on different chromosomes assort independently. Monohybrid cross Aa x Aa gives 3:1 phenotypic and 1:2:1 genotypic ratio. Dihybrid cross AaBb x AaBb gives **9:3:3:1**. Test cross identifies an unknown genotype using a homozygous recessive.
**Beyond Mendel:** incomplete dominance (1:2:1), codominance (ABO blood groups: I^A and I^B codominant), polygenic traits. **Sex linkage:** colour blindness and haemophilia are X-linked recessive.
**Hardy-Weinberg:** p + q = 1 and p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1. If q^2 = 0.04 then q = 0.2, p = 0.8 and carriers 2pq = **0.32**.

## Advanced
P: A colour-blind man marries a normal woman whose father was colour-blind. What are the chances of colour-blind children?
A: The woman is a carrier X^C X^c. Cross X^c Y x X^C X^c: 50 percent of daughters and 50 percent of sons are colour blind; overall 50 percent.
