# DNA replication, transcription and translation
minutes: 25
yt: dna replication transcription translation class 12
@study
**DNA structure:** double helix of two antiparallel strands joined by hydrogen bonds: A pairs with T (2 bonds), G with C (3 bonds). **Replication** is semi-conservative (Meselson and Stahl): helicase unwinds, primase makes RNA primer, DNA polymerase III adds nucleotides 5' to 3'; leading strand continuous, lagging strand in Okazaki fragments joined by ligase.
**Central dogma:** DNA -> RNA -> protein. **Transcription:** RNA polymerase makes mRNA from the template strand. **Genetic code:** triplet codons, 64 codons, start AUG (methionine), stop UAA, UAG, UGA; code is degenerate and universal. **Translation:** ribosome reads mRNA, tRNA brings amino acids.
**Lac operon** shows gene regulation in bacteria: lactose acts as inducer, binding the repressor.

## Advanced
P: Describe the lac operon in the presence of lactose.
A: Lactose binds the repressor, which cannot bind the operator, so RNA polymerase transcribes the structural genes and lactose-metabolising enzymes are made.
