# Vector Algebra

@study
A vector has magnitude and direction, whereas a scalar has only magnitude. The position vector of a point P(x, y, z) is OP = xi + yj + zk, with magnitude sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2). The vector AB from A to B is the position vector of B minus that of A. A unit vector has magnitude 1 and the unit vector along a is a/|a|. Types include zero, unit, coinitial, collinear, equal and negative vectors. Direction ratios of a vector are its components a, b, c and the direction cosines are l = cos alpha = a/|a|, m = b/|a|, n = c/|a| with l^2 + m^2 + n^2 = 1. Addition follows the triangle law and parallelogram law; the section formula for a point dividing AB internally in ratio m:n is (m b + n a)/(m + n), and the midpoint is (a + b)/2. The scalar (dot) product a . b = |a||b| cos theta, and in components a1b1 + a2b2 + a3b3. It is commutative and distributive, is zero for perpendicular vectors, and a . a = |a|^2. The projection of a on b is (a . b)/|b|. The vector (cross) product a x b = |a||b| sin theta n, where n is a unit vector perpendicular to both given by the right-hand rule. It is anti-commutative, a x b = - b x a, zero for parallel vectors, and its magnitude equals the area of the parallelogram with sides a and b. In components it is the determinant with rows i j k, a1 a2 a3 and b1 b2 b3. The area of a triangle with sides a and b is half the magnitude of a x b. The scalar triple product [a b c] = a . (b x c) gives the volume of a parallelepiped, and three vectors are coplanar if it is zero. Also i x j = k, j x k = i, k x i = j, and i.i = 1, i.j = 0. The angle between two vectors satisfies cos theta = (a . b)/(|a||b|), and |a + b|^2 = |a|^2 + |b|^2 + 2 a . b.
## Deep dive: Basics
Position vectors, magnitude, unit vectors and direction cosines describe vectors in space.
## Deep dive: Products
The dot product gives a scalar and angle; the cross product gives a perpendicular vector and area.
## Deep dive: Triple product
The scalar triple product gives volume and tests coplanarity.
