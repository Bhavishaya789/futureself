# Integration and differential equations
minutes: 25
yt: integration class 12
@study
Integration is the reverse of differentiation. Standard results: integral of x^n = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C (n not -1); 1/x gives ln|x|; e^x gives e^x; sin x gives -cos x; cos x gives sin x.
**Definite integral:** integral from a to b of f(x) dx = F(b) - F(a) (area under the curve). Example: integral of sin x from 0 to pi = **2**; integral of x^2 from 0 to 3 = 27/3 = 9.
**Techniques:** substitution, integration by parts (integral of u dv = uv - integral of v du).
**Differential equations:** dy/dx = y gives ln y = x + c, so y = C e^x. Variable separable: separate and integrate both sides.
