# Applications of Integrals
minutes: 45

@study
A definite integral gives the area under a curve. The area bounded by the curve y = f(x), the x-axis and the lines x = a and x = b is the integral from a to b of y dx, provided f is positive; where the curve lies below the axis the integral is negative and the area is its absolute value. Similarly the area bounded by x = g(y), the y-axis and the lines y = c and y = d is the integral from c to d of x dy. The area between two curves y = f(x) and y = g(x) from x = a to x = b, where f is above g, is the integral from a to b of (f(x) - g(x)) dx. When the curves intersect, find intersection points by solving equations and split the region if the upper curve changes. Standard results: the area of a circle x^2 + y^2 = a^2 is pi a^2; the area of the ellipse x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1 is pi a b; the area of the region enclosed by the parabola y^2 = 4ax and its latus rectum is 8a^2/3; the area between the parabola y^2 = 4ax and the line y = mx is 8a^2/(3 m^3); the area of the triangle with vertices on a line can be found by integrating line equations. The area bounded by y = x and y = x^2 is 1/6. The area of one arch of sine curve between 0 and pi is 2. The steps are: draw a rough sketch, identify limits by solving intersection points, choose vertical or horizontal strips, set up the integral and evaluate. The use of symmetry halves the work: for symmetric regions, compute one quadrant and multiply. Applications include finding areas of land plots, regions under velocity-time graphs (distance), and probability distributions. Area under a curve between 0 and a for modulus function can be found by splitting.
## Deep dive: Area under a curve
Area is a definite integral; below the axis, take the absolute value.
## Deep dive: Area between curves
Integrate upper minus lower between intersection limits, splitting when needed.
## Deep dive: Standard areas
Circle pi a^2, ellipse pi a b and parabola with latus rectum 8a^2/3 are key results.

## Moderate
P: Find the area of a circle of radius a using integration.
A: Area = 4 times the integral from 0 to a of sqrt(a^2 - x^2) dx = pi a^2.
R: 3 | Four quadrants
R: 3 | pi a^2
## High
P: Find the area between y = x and y = x^2.
A: Intersections at x = 0 and 1; area = integral from 0 to 1 of (x - x^2) dx = 1/6.
R: 2 | Intersection
R: 2 | Upper minus lower
