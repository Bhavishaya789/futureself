# Human Development
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 geography human development HDI mahbub ul haq amartya sen equity sustainability productivity empowerment
@study
Human development is the process of enlarging people's choices and building human capabilities. The concept was introduced by Dr Mahbub ul Haq (Pakistani economist) and Amartya Sen (Indian economist, Nobel laureate), with the UNDP publishing its first Human Development Report in 1990. Growth versus development: growth is a quantitative change in output (such as GDP) while development is a qualitative change that improves well-being. Four pillars of human development: equity (equal access to opportunities), sustainability (continuity of opportunities for present and future generations), productivity (human labour productivity through investment in people) and empowerment (power to make choices). Approaches: income approach (human development is linked to income), welfare approach (humans as beneficiaries), basic needs approach (health, education, food, water, shelter) and capability approach (Amartya Sen: building capabilities in health, education and access to resources). Human Development Index (HDI): a composite index with three dimensions: a long and healthy life (life expectancy at birth), knowledge (mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling) and a decent standard of living (gross national income per capita in PPP dollars); each dimension ranges between 0 and 1. HDI is classified as very high, high, medium and low. Other indices: Human Poverty Index (HPI), Gender Development Index (GDI), Gender Inequality Index (GII), and Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI). Countries with high HDI are Norway, Switzerland and others; low HDI countries are often in Sub-Saharan Africa. International comparisons: size of the country does not decide HDI; small countries such as Sri Lanka and Costa Rica have done well in human development relative to income; Kerala has high human development in India. Indicators of human development: literacy, life expectancy, infant mortality, access to safe drinking water, and nutrition. Factors that influence: political commitment, governance, public spending on health and education, and equity. HDI is a relative measure. The 2010 Human Development Report introduced inequality-adjusted HDI.
## Deep dive: Concept
Human development enlarges choices and capabilities; Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen developed it.
## Deep dive: Pillars
Equity, sustainability, productivity and empowerment are the four pillars.
## Deep dive: HDI
HDI combines life expectancy, schooling and income per capita into a single index between 0 and 1.
