# Tertiary and Quaternary Activities
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 geography tertiary quaternary quinary activities services trade tourism outsourcing
@study
Tertiary activities involve the sale and exchange of goods and services: trade, transport, communication and services. People engaged in them are called white-collar workers. Services include professional and personal services such as teaching, medicine, law, banking and entertainment, and they are provided by people who have skills. Types: retail trading (sale of goods to the consumer), wholesale trading (sale in bulk to retailers), transport, communication, professional services (doctors, lawyers, teachers), personal services (barbers, domestic helpers), and tourism. Trade and commerce: trading centres include rural marketing centres, urban marketing centres, and periodic markets (weekly haats); retail trading is carried out in shops, supermarkets and malls, and door to door; wholesale trading by merchants. Tourism is a major service; tourist regions: the Mediterranean, the Alps, the Himalayas, coastal Southeast Asia; factors: climate, landscape, accessibility, heritage and facilities; medical tourism is growing in India. Quaternary activities are specialised tertiary activities involving knowledge: information gathering, research and development, and data processing; they are connected to outsourcing, where work is contracted out to another firm or country (BPO, KPO), in places with a lower cost of labour such as India. The digital divide arises because of unequal access to computers and the Internet. Quinary activities are the highest level of decision making: top executives, government officials, senior scientists, financial and legal consultants, and intellectual services. They are seen as a separate class of the service sector. Information and communication technology (ICT) and the growth of the service economy. Employment in the tertiary sector increases with economic development. Data entry and call centres in India are examples of outsourcing. Examples: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurgaon, Noida as IT hubs. Medical and legal services.
## Deep dive: Tertiary
Trade, transport, communication and services are the activities that exchange goods and provide skills.
## Deep dive: Quaternary
Knowledge-based work such as research, data processing and outsourcing forms the quaternary sector.
## Deep dive: Quinary
The highest-level decision makers and thinkers form the quinary sector.
