# Land Resources and Agriculture
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 geography india land use agriculture kharif rabi zaid crops green revolution irrigation
@study
Land is a natural resource of prime importance. India's land use categories: forests, land put to non-agricultural uses, barren and wasteland, permanent pastures and grazing land, miscellaneous tree crops, culturable wasteland, current fallow, other than current fallow and net sown area. Net sown area is about 46 percent of the reporting area; forest cover is below the 33 percent target of the National Forest Policy 1952. Common property resources (CPRs) such as community forests, pastures and village ponds are owned by the community. Land degradation is caused by deforestation, overgrazing, mining and salinisation; wastelands need reclamation. Agriculture is the main occupation of about half of the population. Cropping seasons: Kharif (June to September; rice, maize, jowar, bajra, tur, moong, urad, cotton, jute, groundnut, soyabean), Rabi (October to March; wheat, barley, peas, gram, mustard, linseed) and Zaid (summer; watermelon, cucumber, vegetables and fodder). Types of farming: primitive subsistence, intensive subsistence, commercial and plantation. Major crops: rice (staple of the east and south; needs high temperature, above 25 C, and rainfall above 100 cm; West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh lead); wheat (second most important cereal; rabi crop; cool growing season and warm ripening; 50-75 cm rainfall; Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh); millets (jowar, bajra, ragi) are coarse grains; maize; pulses (gram, tur); oilseeds (groundnut, mustard, soyabean); sugarcane (tropical crop; Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka); tea (Assam, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Kerala), coffee (Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu), cotton (black soil; Maharashtra, Gujarat), jute (Bengal, golden fibre) and rubber (Kerala). Green Revolution (1960s) increased yields of wheat and rice with HYV seeds, fertilisers and irrigation in Punjab, Haryana and western Uttar Pradesh. Problems: dependence on monsoon, small holdings, fragmentation, inadequate irrigation, soil degradation, and lack of credit. Government measures: land reforms, minimum support price, crop insurance, Kisan Credit Card and irrigation projects. Organic farming. Food security.
## Deep dive: Land use
Nine categories describe land use; net sown area is the main agricultural area.
## Deep dive: Seasons and crops
Kharif, rabi and zaid seasons sustain different crops; rice and wheat are the principal cereals.
## Deep dive: Issues
The Green Revolution raised output but regional gaps, small holdings and degradation remain.
