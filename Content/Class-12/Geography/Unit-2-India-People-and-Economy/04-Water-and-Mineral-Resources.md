# Water and Mineral Resources
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 geography india water resources groundwater irrigation rainwater harvesting minerals iron ore coal energy
@study
India has about 4 percent of the world's freshwater but 16 percent of its population. Water resources: surface water (rivers, lakes, ponds, tanks) and groundwater; the annual precipitation is about 4000 cubic km. Irrigation is needed because of uneven monsoons: sources are wells and tube wells (the main source in northern plains), canals (in plains of north India, Punjab, UP, Haryana), tanks (in the peninsular plateau, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh). Multipurpose river valley projects: Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud, Damodar Valley, Tungabhadra. Water scarcity and quality problems; groundwater depletion in Punjab and Haryana; pollution. Conservation: rainwater harvesting, watershed management and recycling. The Indira Gandhi Canal. Minerals: ferrous (iron ore, manganese, chromite), non-ferrous (bauxite, copper, lead, zinc, gold) and non-metallic (mica, limestone, salt, potash). India has abundant iron ore in Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Karnataka; manganese in Odisha and Madhya Pradesh; bauxite in Odisha; copper in Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan; mica in Jharkhand, Andhra Pradesh and Rajasthan; limestone in cement. Energy resources: conventional: coal (Gondwana coalfields hold the bulk of the reserves; Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro; lignite at Neyveli), petroleum (Mumbai High, Gujarat, Assam), natural gas (Krishna-Godavari basin, Mumbai High), hydroelectricity and thermal power, nuclear energy (uranium, thorium: Tarapur, Kota, Kalpakkam, Narora, Kakrapar); non-conventional: solar, wind (Tamil Nadu, Gujarat), biogas, tidal and geothermal energy (Manikaran, Puga), and biomass. Gondwana coal has about 98 percent of the reserves and 99 percent of production. Conservation of minerals: recycling, substitutes, efficient use. Energy efficiency. Mineral belts: north-eastern plateau, central, southern and western belts. Sustainable development.
## Deep dive: Water
India has limited per capita water; wells, canals and tanks irrigate; rainwater harvesting and watershed management conserve it.
## Deep dive: Minerals
Ferrous, non-ferrous and non-metallic minerals are concentrated in the peninsular plateau.
## Deep dive: Energy
Coal, oil and gas lead; nuclear and renewable sources are growing.
