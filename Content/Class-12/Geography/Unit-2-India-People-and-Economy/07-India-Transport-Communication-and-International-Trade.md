# India: Transport, Communication and International Trade
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 geography india roads railways waterways airways pipelines ports communication international trade
@study
India has one of the largest road networks in the world. Roads: National Highways (NHAI, established 1988), State Highways, district roads, rural roads, border roads (BRO set up 1960), and expressways. The Golden Quadrilateral Super Highway connects Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata; the North-South Corridor links Srinagar and Kanyakumari; the East-West Corridor links Silchar and Porbandar. Railways: the first train ran in 1853 between Mumbai and Thane; the Indian railways network is among the largest in the world, with 16 zones; broad gauge is 1.676 m; the Konkan Railway (1998) links Mumbai and Mangalore; Metro rail in cities. Pipelines: carry crude oil and natural gas from oil fields to refineries and ports (Naharkatiya to Barauni; Salaya to Mathura; Hazira-Vijaipur-Jagdishpur gas pipeline). Waterways: Inland National Waterways: NW-1 (Allahabad to Haldia on the Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hugli), NW-2 (Sadiya to Dhubri on the Brahmaputra) and NW-3 (West Coast Canal, Kottapuram to Kollam). Major ports: Kandla, Mumbai, Jawaharlal Nehru Port (Nhava Sheva), Marmagao, New Mangalore, Kochi, Tuticorin, Chennai, Visakhapatnam, Paradip, Kolkata (with Haldia). Airways: Airports Authority of India; Air India is the national carrier; international airports at Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and others. Communication: postal services (the largest network), telecom (landline, mobile, broadband), radio, television, newspapers and the Internet; satellites such as INSAT help communication. International trade: India's exports include gems and jewellery, engineering goods, petroleum products, textiles, chemicals and agricultural goods; imports include petroleum, electronic goods, gold and machinery. Major trading partners: USA, China, UAE and Saudi Arabia. Trade is influenced by the shift to a liberal economy after 1991. Ports act as gateways. India has a trade deficit usually. Information technology is a major service export. Tourism. Balanced trade.
## Deep dive: Roads and rails
Golden Quadrilateral and the corridors form the highway backbone; Indian Railways began in 1853.
## Deep dive: Ports and waterways
Major ports handle foreign trade; National Waterways use rivers and canals.
## Deep dive: Trade
Gems, engineering goods and petroleum products lead exports; crude oil leads imports.
