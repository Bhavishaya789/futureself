# US Hegemony in World Politics
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 political science US hegemony gulf war 9/11 iraq war soft power hard power structural power
@study
Hegemony means leadership or dominance. After the Soviet collapse in 1991 the United States became the sole superpower, and the world was called unipolar. Hegemony can be hard power (military), structural power (shaping the global economic order, as with Bretton Woods institutions and sea lanes) and soft power (ideas and culture, a term coined by Joseph Nye). The US military budget is larger than those of many nations together, and its economy and universities, Hollywood and the internet support its soft power. Key events were the Gulf War. Iraq under Saddam Hussein invaded Kuwait in August 1990; a US-led coalition under a UN mandate launched Operation Desert Storm in January 1991, and President George H. W. Bush spoke of a New World Order. The Clinton years emphasised economic and humanitarian action, including Kosovo and the missile strikes after the 1998 embassy bombings. On 11 September 2001 al-Qaeda attacked the World Trade Center and the Pentagon, and George W. Bush started the War on Terror: Operation Enduring Freedom in Afghanistan against the Taliban (2001) and Operation Iraqi Freedom in Iraq (2003) on the claim of weapons of mass destruction, none of which were found. The US prison at Guantanamo Bay drew criticism for human rights. The constraints on hegemony are the US’s own institutional architecture (separation of powers, a free press), the open nature of American society, and NATO, which may not always support it. India’s options towards the US are bandwagoning, hiding or resisting; India has chosen to engage on shared interests while keeping its strategic autonomy. Huntington’s clash of civilisations and Fukuyama’s end of history are debated interpretations. The US also stayed out of the Kyoto Protocol (2001) and the International Criminal Court.
## Deep dive: Types of hegemony
Hard power is military, structural power shapes the economy, soft power is cultural attraction.
## Deep dive: Events
The Gulf War in 1991, 9/11 in 2001, Afghanistan 2001 and Iraq 2003 define US unipolar action.
## Deep dive: Limits and strategy
Domestic institutions and allies constrain hegemony; states can bandwagon, hide or resist.
