# Regional Aspirations
minutes: 40

@study
Regional aspirations are demands for autonomy, identity or statehood within a democratic framework. India accepts diversity, and accommodation of regional demands has strengthened unity. In Jammu and Kashmir, Article 370 gave special status; the autonomy debate, the 1953 dismissal of Sheikh Abdullah, militancy from the 1980s, and the 1996 return of elections shaped politics; Article 370 was revoked in August 2019 and the state split into two union territories. In Punjab, the Akali Dal’s Anandpur Sahib Resolution of 1973 demanded autonomy; militancy led to Operation Blue Star in June 1984 at the Golden Temple, the assassination of Indira Gandhi on 31 October 1984, and the Rajiv Gandhi-Longowal Accord of July 1985; peace and elections returned by the 1990s. The Northeast saw demands for autonomy: in Assam the movement against illegal migrants, led by the All Assam Students Union (AASU) from 1979, ended with the Assam Accord of 1985; in Mizoram, the Mizo National Front under Laldenga rebelled, and the Mizoram Accord of 1986 led to statehood in 1987; in Nagaland, the Naga National Council under Phizo sought independence, and Nagaland became a state in 1963; Manipur and Tripura have their own issues. Sikkim merged with India in 1975 as the 22nd state, and Goa achieved statehood in 1987 after liberation in 1961. In Tamil Nadu, the Dravidian movement and DMK, formed in 1949 by C. N. Annadurai, opposed Hindi imposition. The Gorkhaland demand arose in Darjeeling. In 2000 Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand were created, and Telangana in 2014. The lessons are that regional aspirations are part of democratic politics, and that federalism, power sharing and respect for diversity solve conflicts.
## Deep dive: Kashmir and Punjab
Autonomy, militancy and accords shaped Jammu and Kashmir and Punjab.
## Deep dive: Northeast
Assam, Mizoram and Nagaland saw movements settled through accords and statehood.
## Deep dive: Accommodation
Federalism, accords and new states such as Sikkim, Goa and Telangana accommodate aspirations.
