# Era of One-Party Dominance
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 political science one party dominance first general election congress system opposition parties kerala 1957
@study
The Constituent Assembly chose democracy and universal adult franchise from the beginning. The Election Commission was set up in January 1950 with Sukumar Sen as the first Chief Election Commissioner. The first general elections were held in 1951-52, with about 17 crore voters, and the Congress won 364 of 489 Lok Sabha seats, with the Communist Party of India as the largest opposition party with 16 seats. Congress also won in most states. The elections were seen as a success of democracy, with voting by symbols for illiterate voters and polling in many phases. In 1957 and 1962 the Congress won again. India had a one-party dominant system but, unlike authoritarian one-party regimes, competition was free. The Congress was a rainbow coalition and an umbrella party, inherited the freedom movement legacy since 1885, and had social and ideological diversity. The Congress Socialist faction and others kept internal debate. Opposition parties included the Communist Party of India (1925), the Socialist Party (1948, later Praja Socialist Party), the Bharatiya Jana Sangh (1951, Syama Prasad Mookerjee), the Swatantra Party (1959, C. Rajagopalachari) and the Bharatiya Kranti Dal later. They were small but acted as a check. The Communists formed the first elected communist government in the world at the state level in Kerala in 1957 under E. M. S. Namboodiripad, which the Centre dismissed in 1959 under Article 356. Nehru dominated the Congress; opposition leaders such as Ram Manohar Lohia and Jayaprakash Narayan criticised it. Dominance of the Congress ended gradually in the 1967 elections.
## Deep dive: First elections
1951-52 elections under Sukumar Sen gave Congress 364 of 489 seats.
## Deep dive: Congress system
A broad umbrella with internal factions and a freedom movement legacy.
## Deep dive: Opposition
CPI, Socialists, Jana Sangh and Swatantra Party acted as a check; Kerala 1957 was the first communist government.
