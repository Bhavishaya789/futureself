# Challenges of Nation Building
minutes: 40
yt: class 12 political science challenges of nation building partition princely states integration states reorganisation language
@study
Independent India faced three challenges: to build a united nation respecting diversity, to establish a democratic constitutional order, and to ensure development and welfare of all. Independence on 15 August 1947 came with Partition on the basis of the two-nation theory, with the Radcliffe Line dividing Punjab and Bengal, and caused mass migration, violence and the deaths of many. The Constituent Assembly, with Dr Rajendra Prasad as president and B. R. Ambedkar as chairman of the drafting committee, framed the Constitution adopted on 26 November 1949 and in force on 26 January 1950. Nehru’s ‘tryst with destiny’ speech marked independence. Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated on 30 January 1948. Integration of about 565 princely states was led by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, as Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister, with V. P. Menon, using Instruments of Accession by the 1947 Indian Independence Act option. Most states acceded peacefully; Junagadh joined after a plebiscite, Hyderabad was integrated after the police action of September 1948, and Manipur merged in 1949. Kashmir’s Maharaja Hari Singh signed the Instrument of Accession on 26 October 1947 after tribal raiders attacked. Reorganisation of states on linguistic lines followed: Potti Sriramulu’s fast unto death in 1952 led to Andhra State in 1953; the States Reorganisation Commission (1953) under Fazl Ali with K. M. Panikkar and H. N. Kunzru led to the States Reorganisation Act of 1956, creating 14 states and 6 union territories. Later, Maharashtra and Gujarat were formed in 1960, Punjab and Haryana in 1966, and Goa became a state in 1987. Linguistic reorganisation strengthened democracy and unity. Hindi as the official language and English as an associate language, the three language formula, and the Official Languages Act addressed language issues.
## Deep dive: Partition and violence
1947 Partition on the two-nation theory caused mass migration and violence.
## Deep dive: Integration
Patel and V. P. Menon integrated about 565 princely states by accession, with special cases at Junagadh, Hyderabad and Kashmir.
## Deep dive: Linguistic states
Potti Sriramulu’s fast led to Andhra in 1953 and the 1956 States Reorganisation Act.
