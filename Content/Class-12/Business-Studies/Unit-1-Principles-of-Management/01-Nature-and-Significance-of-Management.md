# Nature and Significance of Management
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 business studies nature significance management levels coordination
@study
Management is the process of working with and through others to achieve organisational objectives efficiently and effectively. Effectiveness means completing the task on time (doing the right thing); efficiency means doing the task correctly with minimum cost (doing things right). Characteristics: management is a goal-oriented process, pervasive, multidimensional, a continuous process, a group activity and a dynamic function; it is intangible. Objectives: organisational (survival, profit, growth), social (benefit to society, use of eco-friendly methods, creating employment) and personal (needs of employees: financial benefits, social recognition, personal growth). Importance: achieving group goals, increasing efficiency, creating a dynamic organisation, achieving personal objectives and development of society. Management as science: systematised body of knowledge, principles based on repeated experimentation, but principles lack universal validity; management as art: theoretical knowledge, personalised application, creativity, based on practice and creativity; management as profession: well-defined body of knowledge, restricted entry, professional association (such as AIMA, All India Management Association) and ethical code, service motive; management is not yet a full profession. Levels of management: top level (board of directors, CEO; determine objectives and policies, integrate and coordinate), middle level (departmental heads, divisional managers; interpret policies and coordinate between top and lower levels) and supervisory or operational level (foremen, supervisors; direct workers' efforts). Functions of management: planning, organising, staffing, directing and controlling. Planning: deciding in advance what to do; organising: assigning duties, grouping tasks; staffing: filling positions with the right people; directing: motivating, leading, communicating and supervising; controlling: comparing actual performance with standards and correcting deviations. Coordination is the force that binds all functions; it is the essence of management. Features of coordination: integrates group efforts, ensures unity of action, a continuous process, an all-pervasive function, is the responsibility of all managers and a deliberate function. It is needed due to the size and complexity of the organisation. Importance of coordination: growth in size, functional differentiation, specialisation. Henri Fayol is regarded as the father of general management theory. Peter Drucker said that management is a multi-purpose organ that manages business, managers and workers. Key terms: management as an activity, as a group of people and as a discipline. Management and administration: administration is the determination of objectives and policies at top level, while management implements them; however, the two are used interchangeably. Managers in the modern context handle change and technology.
## Deep dive: Concept
Management gets work done through others. Efficiency and effectiveness together make it successful. It is goal oriented, pervasive and continuous.
## Deep dive: Nature
Management has aspects of science, art and profession. Levels are top, middle and operational. Functions are planning, organising, staffing, directing and controlling.
## Deep dive: Coordination
Coordination integrates the efforts of different departments. It is the essence of management and the responsibility of all managers.
