# Directing
minutes: 45
yt: class 12 business studies directing motivation maslow leadership communication supervision
@study
Directing is the function of instructing, guiding, counselling, motivating and leading people in an organisation to achieve its objectives. Features: it initiates action, takes place at every level, is a continuous activity and flows from top to bottom. Importance: initiates action, integrates efforts, means of motivation, provides stability and balance, and facilitates change. Elements of directing: supervision, motivation, leadership and communication. Supervision is the function of overseeing the work of subordinates; the supervisor is the link between workers and management and is a factor in discipline, morale and training. Motivation is the process of stimulating people to action to accomplish desired goals. A motive is an inner state that energises behaviour; a motivator is a stimulus. Features: an internal feeling, a goal-directed behaviour, can be positive or negative and a complex process. Maslow's need hierarchy theory: physiological needs (food, shelter), safety/security needs (job security), affiliation/belonging needs (friendship), esteem needs (status, recognition) and self-actualisation needs (realising one's potential); a satisfied need no longer motivates; assumptions: people's behaviour is based on their needs. Herzberg's two-factor theory: hygiene factors (salary, working conditions, job security, supervision) prevent dissatisfaction but do not motivate; motivators (achievement, recognition, responsibility, growth) motivate. Financial incentives: pay and allowances, productivity linked wage incentives, bonus, profit sharing, co-partnership/stock option, retirement benefits and perquisites; non-financial: status, organisational climate, career advancement opportunity, job enrichment, employee recognition programmes, job security and employee participation. Leadership is the process of influencing the behaviour of people to strive willingly for group goals. Features: influence, followers, common goal, a continuous process and situational. Importance: induces action, provides guidance, creates confidence, builds morale and coordinates. Styles: autocratic (authoritarian, all decisions by leader), democratic (participative, consults subordinates) and laissez-faire (free rein, leader gives minimum direction). Qualities: physical features, knowledge, integrity, initiative, communication skills, motivation, self-confidence, decisiveness, social skills. Communication is the process of exchange of ideas, views, facts, feelings between two or more persons to create common understanding. Elements: sender, message, encoding, media, decoding, receiver, feedback and noise. Formal communication follows the official channel: vertical (downward and upward) and horizontal; informal communication or grapevine includes single strand, gossip, probability and cluster chains. Barriers: semantic (words and symbols), psychological (premature evaluation, lack of attention, loss by transmission and poor retention, distrust), organisational (organisational policy, rules, status, complexity in organisational structure, organisational facilities) and personal barriers (fear of challenge to authority, lack of confidence of superiors in their subordinates, unwillingness to communicate, lack of proper incentive). Improving communication: clarify the ideas, communicate according to the needs of the receiver, consult others, be aware of the language, tone and content, convey things of help and value, ensure proper feedback and communicate for the present as well as the future and be a good listener.
## Deep dive: Motivation
Maslow and Herzberg explain what motivates people. Financial and non-financial incentives work together.
## Deep dive: Leadership
Leaders influence people toward goals. Autocratic, democratic and laissez-faire styles fit different situations.
## Deep dive: Communication
Communication needs a sender, message, medium, receiver and feedback. Barriers can be semantic, psychological, organisational or personal.
