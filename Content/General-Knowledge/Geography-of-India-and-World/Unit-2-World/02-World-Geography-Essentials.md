@study
- **7 continents:** Asia (largest), Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia/Oceania (smallest). **5 oceans:** Pacific (largest), Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic. About **71% of the Earth's surface is water**.
- **Highest peak:** **Mount Everest, 8,849 m** (joint Nepal-China measurement, 2020). **Longest river:** the **Nile** (about 6,650 km; the Amazon is the largest by volume). **Largest hot desert:** the **Sahara**. **Largest country by area:** **Russia**; **smallest:** **Vatican City**. **Most populous countries:** India and China. The UN has **193 member states**.
- **Lines:** Equator 0°, Tropic of Cancer 23.5°N, Tropic of Capricorn 23.5°S, Prime Meridian 0° (Greenwich), International Date Line about 180°.
- **Earth facts:** rotation about 24 hours (day and night), revolution 365.25 days (leap year every 4 years); 15° of longitude = 1 hour.

> Time rule: every 15° of longitude equals 1 hour, or 1° = 4 minutes. | 1° देशांतर = 4 मिनट।
