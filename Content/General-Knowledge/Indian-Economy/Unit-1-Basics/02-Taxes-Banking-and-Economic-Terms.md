# Taxes, banking and economic terms | कर, बैंकिंग और आर्थिक शब्दावली
minutes: 15
yt: indian economy terms tax banking
@study
- **Direct taxes** (income tax, corporate tax) are paid by the person bearing them; **indirect taxes** (GST, customs) are passed on to consumers.
- **Banking:** commercial banks accept deposits and lend; **NABARD** (1982) serves agriculture and rural credit; **Jan Dhan Yojana** (launched **28 August 2014**) aims at universal bank accounts; **UPI** (2016) enables instant payments.
- **Fiscal deficit** = total expenditure - total receipts excluding borrowing. **Monetary policy** is RBI's tool (interest rates, money supply); **fiscal policy** is the government's (taxes, spending).
- **Simple formulas:** per capita income = national income/population. Example: income Rs 3,00,000 crore and population 120 crore -> **Rs 2,500** per person.
- **Poverty and employment** schemes: MGNREGA (rural work guarantee, 2005), PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat.
