# Economy basics, RBI and the Budget | अर्थव्यवस्था के मूल तत्व
minutes: 15

@study
- **GDP** = total value of final goods and services produced in a year. **Sectors:** primary (agriculture), secondary (industry), tertiary (services). India is a **mixed economy**.
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI):** established **1 April 1935**, nationalised **1949**, headquartered in **Mumbai**. It issues currency notes (except the Re 1 note, issued by the Finance Ministry), controls credit through the **repo rate** (rate at which RBI lends to banks) and **CRR/SLR**, and regulates banks.
- **NITI Aayog** replaced the Planning Commission on **1 January 2015**. The First Five-Year Plan ran **1951-56**.
- **Budget:** presented every year, normally on **1 February**; **financial year = 1 April to 31 March**. **Finance Commission** (Art. 280) is constituted every 5 years to share taxes between Centre and States.
- **GST** (Goods and Services Tax) started on **1 July 2017**, replacing many indirect taxes. **SEBI** regulates securities markets (statutory body since 1992).
- **Inflation** = rise in prices. Example: if the CPI rises from 150 to 159, inflation = (159 - 150)/150 = **6%**.

## Advanced
P: Explain how raising the repo rate controls inflation.
A: Banks borrow at a higher cost, so they raise loan rates; borrowing and spending fall, reducing demand and price pressure.
