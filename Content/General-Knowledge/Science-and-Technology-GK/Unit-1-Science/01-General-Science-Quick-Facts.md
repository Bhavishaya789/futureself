# General science quick facts | सामान्य विज्ञान के तथ्य
minutes: 15
yt: general science facts for competitive exams
@study
- **Human body:** 206 bones (adult), 32 teeth, normal body temperature 37°C (98.6°F), heart ~72 beats a minute, largest organ: skin, largest gland: liver, blood groups A, B, AB, O (universal donor O negative; universal recipient AB positive).
- **Vitamins:** A (night blindness), B1 (beri-beri), C (scurvy), D (rickets), K (blood clotting).
- **Physics:** speed of light about **3 x 10^8 m/s**; SI units: force newton, energy joule, power watt, pressure pascal, frequency hertz; sound needs a medium; acceleration due to gravity about 9.8 m/s².
- **Chemistry:** water H2O; common salt NaCl; pH below 7 acidic, 7 neutral, above 7 basic; atomic number = number of protons; lightest element hydrogen.
- **Biology:** photosynthesis in chlorophyll makes glucose and oxygen; DNA carries genetic information; plants take in CO2.
- **Nobel Prize (Indians):** Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913), C. V. Raman (Physics, 1930), Mother Teresa (Peace, 1979), Amartya Sen (Economics, 1998), Kailash Satyarthi (Peace, 2014).
