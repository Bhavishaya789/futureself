- **ISRO** (Indian Space Research Organisation) was formed in **1969**; headquarters **Bengaluru**; launch site **Sriharikota**.
- **Aryabhata** (1975) was India's first satellite. **Rohini** (1980) was launched by India's own SLV-3.
- **Chandrayaan-1** (2008) found evidence of water molecules on the Moon. **Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission)** was launched **5 November 2013** and entered Mars orbit on **24 September 2014**, making India the first country to reach Mars orbit in its first attempt.
- **Chandrayaan-3** landed near the lunar south pole on **23 August 2023**, making India the fourth country to land on the Moon. **Aditya-L1**, India's first solar mission, launched **2 September 2023**.
- **Digital India:** UPI, Aadhaar, DigiLocker; **5G** launched in India in October 2022. **AI, semiconductors and quantum technology** are national priorities (National Quantum Mission, approved 2023).
- Verify recent missions on the official ISRO website; this lesson lists settled historical facts.
