# Ancient India: Indus to the Guptas | प्राचीन भारत
minutes: 15
yt: ancient india history indus valley mauryan gupta
@study
- **Indus Valley Civilisation** (mature phase about **2600-1900 BCE**): planned cities such as **Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal**; grid streets, drains; script not yet deciphered.
- **Vedic period:** Rigveda (early), later Vedic texts; Mahajanapadas (16 states) around **6th century BCE**; Buddha and Mahavira lived in this age.
- **Mauryan Empire (about 321-185 BCE):** founded by **Chandragupta Maurya** with **Chanakya (Kautilya)**, author of the Arthashastra. **Ashoka** fought the **Kalinga war (261 BCE)**, then promoted dhamma; his edicts and the Lion Capital of Sarnath (national emblem) are famous.
- **Gupta period (about 4th-6th century CE):** often called a golden age: **Chandragupta II**, Kalidasa, **Aryabhata** (born 476 CE), decimal system and the concept of zero in mathematics, Nalanda's rise.
- Southern kingdoms: Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas; the Chola temple at Thanjavur.
