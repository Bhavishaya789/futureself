- **Delhi Sultanate (1206-1526):** five dynasties: Mamluk (Slave), Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi. Qutb-ud-din Aibak began the Qutub Minar; Alauddin Khalji's market reforms; Muhammad bin Tughlaq moved the capital to Daulatabad.
- **Mughal Empire:** **Babur** won the **First Battle of Panipat (1526)** over Ibrahim Lodi. **Humayun** lost to Sher Shah Suri. **Akbar** (reigned from **1556**) expanded the empire, with the **Second Battle of Panipat (1556)**, and followed religious tolerance (Din-i-Ilahi, Sulh-i-kul). **Shah Jahan** built the **Taj Mahal** (Agra); **Aurangzeb** (died 1707) ruled the largest extent.
- **Vijayanagara Empire** (1336, Hampi) and the **Marathas** under **Shivaji** (coronation 1674).
- **Bhakti and Sufi movements:** Kabir, Guru Nanak, Mirabai, Tulsidas, Chishti saints.
