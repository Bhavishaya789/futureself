# Making of the Constitution and the Preamble | संविधान निर्माण और प्रस्तावना
minutes: 15
yt: making of indian constitution preamble
@study
- The Constituent Assembly first met on **9 December 1946**. The Drafting Committee was chaired by **Dr B. R. Ambedkar**; **Dr Rajendra Prasad** presided over the Assembly.
- The Constitution was **adopted on 26 November 1949** (Constitution Day) and **came into force on 26 January 1950** (Republic Day). It took about **2 years, 11 months and 18 days**.
- It is the longest written constitution of any sovereign country; it began with 395 articles, 22 parts and 8 schedules and now has about 450 articles and **12 schedules**.
- **Preamble:** "We, the people of India" resolve to make India a **Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic**, securing **Justice, Liberty, Equality** and **Fraternity**. The words *Socialist* and *Secular* were added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)**.
- Sources: Government of India Act 1935 (federal scheme), Britain (parliamentary system), USA (Fundamental Rights, judicial review), Ireland (Directive Principles), Canada (strong Centre), Weimar Germany (emergency).

> Constitution Day is 26 November (adopted); Republic Day is 26 January (came into force). | संविधान दिवस 26 नवंबर, गणतंत्र दिवस 26 जनवरी।
