Early India, empires, medieval India and world events.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Indus Valley civilisation**: flourished around 2500 BCE
- **Harappa and Mohenjo-daro**: are major Indus sites
- **Mauryan empire**: was founded by Chandragupta Maurya
- **Ashoka**: adopted Buddhism after the Kalinga war
- **Gupta period**: is called the golden age of India
- **Harshavardhana**: ruled from Kannauj

**Facts to remember**
- Indus Valley civilisation flourished around 2500 BCE.
- Harappa and Mohenjo-daro are major Indus sites.
- Mauryan empire was founded by Chandragupta Maurya.
- Ashoka adopted Buddhism after the Kalinga war.
- Gupta period is called the golden age of India.
- Harshavardhana ruled from Kannauj.
- Delhi Sultanate was founded in 1206 by Qutbuddin Aibak.
- Battle of Panipat 1526 established the Mughal empire under Babur.
- Akbar introduced the Mansabdari system.
- Battle of Plassey took place in 1757.
- French Revolution began in 1789.
- First World War lasted from 1914 to 1918.
- Second World War lasted from 1939 to 1945.
- Russian Revolution took place in 1917.
