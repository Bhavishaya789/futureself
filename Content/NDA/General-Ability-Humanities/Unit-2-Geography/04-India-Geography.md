**Key formulas and rules**
- **Himalayas**: are young fold mountains
- **Western Ghats**: are older than the Himalayas
- **Ganga**: is the longest river of India
- **Brahmaputra**: is a major river of the northeast
- **Godavari**: is the longest peninsular river
- **Narmada and Tapi**: flow westwards into the Arabian Sea

**Facts to remember**
- Himalayas are young fold mountains.
