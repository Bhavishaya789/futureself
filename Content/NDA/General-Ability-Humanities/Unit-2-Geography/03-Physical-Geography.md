Earth, atmosphere, oceans and landforms.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Earth**: rotates on its axis from west to east
- **Latitudes**: are parallel circles around the earth
- **Equator**: is at 0 degree latitude
- **Prime meridian**: passes through Greenwich
- **Earth's layers**: are crust, mantle and core
- **Troposphere**: is the lowest layer of the atmosphere

**Facts to remember**
- Earth rotates on its axis from west to east.
- Latitudes are parallel circles around the earth.
- Equator is at 0 degree latitude.
- Prime meridian passes through Greenwich.
- Earth's layers are crust, mantle and core.
- Troposphere is the lowest layer of the atmosphere.
- Ozone is found in the stratosphere.
- Monsoon is a seasonal reversal of winds.
- Mount Everest is the highest peak at 8849 m.
- Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean.
- Dead Sea is the lowest point on land.
- Igneous rocks form from cooled magma.
- Sedimentary rocks contain fossils.
- Tropic of Cancer passes through eight Indian states.
