**Key formulas and rules**
- **President of India**: is the Supreme Commander of the armed forces
- **Chief of Defence Staff**: post was created in 2019
- **Indian Army day**: is celebrated on 15 January
- **Indian Air Force day**: is celebrated on 8 October
- **Indian Navy day**: is celebrated on 4 December
- **Param Vir Chakra**: is the highest gallantry award

**Facts to remember**
- President of India is the Supreme Commander of the armed forces.
- Chief of Defence Staff post was created in 2019.
- Indian Army day is celebrated on 15 January.
- Indian Air Force day is celebrated on 8 October.
- Indian Navy day is celebrated on 4 December.
- Param Vir Chakra is the highest gallantry award.
- Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime gallantry award.
- National Defence Academy is at Khadakwasla, Pune.
- Indian Military Academy is at Dehradun.
- Officers Training Academy is at Chennai.
- Rank of General is the highest in the Army.
- Agnipath is the scheme for recruitment of Agniveers.
- INS Vikrant is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier.
- Brahmos is a supersonic cruise missile.
