Set operations, functions and complex numbers.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Union of sets**: n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A∩B)
- **Subsets of an n element set**: number 2^n
- **Equivalence relation**: is reflexive, symmetric and transitive
- **One-one onto function**: is a bijection and has an inverse
- **Cube roots of unity**: sum to zero
- **Modulus of a complex number**: is √(a² + b²)

**Facts to remember**
- Union of sets n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A∩B).
- Subsets of an n element set number 2^n.
- Equivalence relation is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.
- One-one onto function is a bijection and has an inverse.
- Cube roots of unity sum to zero.
- Modulus of a complex number is √(a² + b²).
- Argument of a complex number is the angle with the positive real axis.
- I raised to 4 equals 1.
- Conjugate product z times z-bar equals the modulus squared.
- Number of relations from A to B is 2^(mn).
