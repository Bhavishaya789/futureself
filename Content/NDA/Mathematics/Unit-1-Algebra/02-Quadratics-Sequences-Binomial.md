Roots, AP, GP, nPr, nCr and the binomial theorem.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Discriminant**: is b² - 4ac
- **Roots are real and equal**: when the discriminant is zero
- **Sum of roots**: is -b/a and product is c/a
- **Sum of n terms of AP**: is n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]
- **Sum of n terms of GP**: is a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1)
- **Sum to infinity of GP**: is a/(1 - r) for |r| less than 1

**Facts to remember**
- Discriminant is b² - 4ac.
- Roots are real and equal when the discriminant is zero.
- Sum of roots is -b/a and product is c/a.
- Sum of n terms of AP is n/2 [2a + (n-1)d].
- Sum of n terms of GP is a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1).
- Sum to infinity of GP is a/(1 - r) for |r| less than 1.
- AM ≥ GM ≥ HM for positive numbers.
- NCr equals nC(n-r).
- Number of terms in (a + b)^n is n + 1.
- Sum of binomial coefficients is 2^n.
