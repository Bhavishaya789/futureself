Ratios, identities, equations, inverse functions and applications.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Sin² + cos²**: equals 1
- **Sin 2θ**: equals 2 sinθ cosθ
- **Cos 2θ**: equals 1 - 2sin²θ
- **Tan(A + B)**: equals (tanA + tanB)/(1 - tanA tanB)
- **Sine rule**: states a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC
- **Cosine rule**: states c² = a² + b² - 2ab cosC

**Facts to remember**
- Sin² + cos² equals 1.
- Sin 2θ equals 2 sinθ cosθ.
- Cos 2θ equals 1 - 2sin²θ.
- Tan(A + B) equals (tanA + tanB)/(1 - tanA tanB).
- Sine rule states a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC.
- Cosine rule states c² = a² + b² - 2ab cosC.
- Principal value of sin inverse lies in [-π/2, π/2].
- Principal value of cos inverse lies in [0, π].
- Period of tan x is π.
- Angle of elevation is measured above the horizontal.
