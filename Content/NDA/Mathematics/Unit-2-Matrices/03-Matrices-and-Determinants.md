Matrix algebra, determinants, inverse and linear equations.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Matrix product AB**: is defined when columns of A equal rows of B
- **Determinant of a product**: equals the product of determinants
- **Inverse of a matrix**: exists when the determinant is non-zero
- **Adjoint formula**: gives A inverse as adj A over |A|
- **Transpose of a product**: reverses the order
- **Symmetric matrix**: equals its transpose

**Facts to remember**
- Matrix product AB is defined when columns of A equal rows of B.
- Determinant of a product equals the product of determinants.
- Inverse of a matrix exists when the determinant is non-zero.
- Adjoint formula gives A inverse as adj A over |A|.
- Transpose of a product reverses the order.
- Symmetric matrix equals its transpose.
- Skew symmetric matrix has zero diagonal.
- Cramer's rule solves linear equations using determinants.
- Homogeneous system always has the trivial solution.
- Rank of a matrix is the order of its largest non-zero minor.
