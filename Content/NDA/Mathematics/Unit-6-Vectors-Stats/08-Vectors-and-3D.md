Dot and cross products, direction cosines, lines and planes.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Dot product**: equals |a||b| cosθ
- **Cross product magnitude**: equals |a||b| sinθ
- **Perpendicular vectors**: have zero dot product
- **Parallel vectors**: have zero cross product
- **Direction cosines**: satisfy l² + m² + n² = 1
- **Scalar triple product**: gives volume of a parallelepiped

**Facts to remember**
- Dot product equals |a||b| cosθ.
- Cross product magnitude equals |a||b| sinθ.
- Perpendicular vectors have zero dot product.
- Parallel vectors have zero cross product.
- Direction cosines satisfy l² + m² + n² = 1.
- Scalar triple product gives volume of a parallelepiped.
- Unit vector is a/|a|.
- Equation of a plane is ax + by + cz = d.
- Distance of origin from plane is d/√(a² + b² + c²).
- Angle between two lines uses the dot product of direction vectors.
