Mean, median, mode, variance and probability rules.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Mean**: is the sum of values divided by their number
- **Median**: is the middle value of sorted data
- **Mode**: is the most frequent value
- **Variance**: is the average of squared deviations
- **Standard deviation**: is the square root of variance
- **Adding a constant to data**: does not change the standard deviation

**Facts to remember**
- Mean is the sum of values divided by their number.
- Median is the middle value of sorted data.
- Mode is the most frequent value.
- Variance is the average of squared deviations.
- Standard deviation is the square root of variance.
- Adding a constant to data does not change the standard deviation.
- P(A∪B) equals P(A) + P(B) - P(A∩B).
- Independent events have P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B).
- Bayes' theorem gives conditional probability of causes.
- Binomial distribution has mean np and variance npq.
