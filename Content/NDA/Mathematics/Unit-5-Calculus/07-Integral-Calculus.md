Indefinite and definite integrals, area and ODEs.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Integral of x^n**: is x^(n+1)/(n+1) for n not equal to minus one
- **Integral of 1/x**: is ln|x|
- **Integral of e^x**: is e^x
- **Integral of sin x**: is minus cos x
- **Area under a curve**: is the definite integral of y dx
- **Integral of an odd function over a symmetric interval**: is zero

**Facts to remember**
- Integral of x^n is x^(n+1)/(n+1) for n not equal to minus one.
- Integral of 1/x is ln|x|.
- Integral of e^x is e^x.
- Integral of sin x is minus cos x.
- Area under a curve is the definite integral of y dx.
- Integral of an odd function over a symmetric interval is zero.
- Order of a differential equation is its highest derivative.
- Variable separable equations are integrated after separating variables.
- Integrating factor is e raised to the integral of P.
- Definite integral is the limit of a sum.
