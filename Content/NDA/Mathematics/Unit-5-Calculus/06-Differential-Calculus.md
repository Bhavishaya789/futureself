Limits, derivatives, tangents and maxima minima.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Lim sin x/x as x tends to 0**: equals 1
- **Derivative of x^n**: is n x^(n-1)
- **Derivative of sin x**: is cos x
- **Derivative of e^x**: is e^x
- **Derivative of ln x**: is 1/x
- **Chain rule**: differentiates composite functions

**Facts to remember**
- Lim sin x/x as x tends to 0 equals 1.
- Derivative of x^n is n x^(n-1).
- Derivative of sin x is cos x.
- Derivative of e^x is e^x.
- Derivative of ln x is 1/x.
- Chain rule differentiates composite functions.
- Local maximum has f' zero and f'' negative.
- Differentiable implies continuous but not conversely.
- Slope of tangent equals the derivative at the point.
- Rolle's theorem needs f(a) = f(b).
