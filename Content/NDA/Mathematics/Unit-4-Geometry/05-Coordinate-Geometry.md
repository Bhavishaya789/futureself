Straight lines, circle, parabola, ellipse and hyperbola.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Slope of a line**: is (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1)
- **Perpendicular lines**: have product of slopes equal to minus one
- **Distance of a point from a line**: is |ax1 + by1 + c|/√(a² + b²)
- **Circle with centre (h,k)**: is (x-h)² + (y-k)² = r²
- **Eccentricity of a parabola**: is 1
- **Eccentricity of an ellipse**: is less than 1

**Facts to remember**
- Slope of a line is (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1).
- Perpendicular lines have product of slopes equal to minus one.
- Distance of a point from a line is |ax1 + by1 + c|/√(a² + b²).
- Circle with centre (h,k) is (x-h)² + (y-k)² = r².
- Eccentricity of a parabola is 1.
- Eccentricity of an ellipse is less than 1.
- Eccentricity of a hyperbola is greater than 1.
- Latus rectum of y² = 4ax is 4a.
- Focus of y² = 4ax is at (a, 0).
- Section formula divides a segment in the ratio m:n.
