**Key formulas and rules**
- **Sodium**: is stored in kerosene
- **Gold**: is the most malleable and ductile metal
- **Mercury**: is the only metal liquid at room temperature
- **Rusting**: needs both oxygen and water
- **Alloy brass**: is copper and zinc
- **Alloy bronze**: is copper and tin

**Facts to remember**
- Sodium is stored in kerosene.
- Gold is the most malleable and ductile metal.
- Mercury is the only metal liquid at room temperature.
- Rusting needs both oxygen and water.
- Alloy brass is copper and zinc.
- Alloy bronze is copper and tin.
- Steel is iron with carbon.
- Methane is the main constituent of natural gas.
- LPG is mainly butane and propane.
- Soap is a sodium or potassium salt of fatty acids.
- Hardness of water is caused by calcium and magnesium salts.
- Vinegar contains acetic acid.
- Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate.
- Plaster of Paris is calcium sulphate hemihydrate.
