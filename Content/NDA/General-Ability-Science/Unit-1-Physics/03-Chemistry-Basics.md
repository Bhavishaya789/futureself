Basic chemistry for NDA.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Atomic number**: equals the number of protons
- **Mass number**: equals protons plus neutrons
- **Isotopes**: have the same atomic number but different mass number
- **Ionic bond**: forms by transfer of electrons
- **Covalent bond**: forms by sharing of electrons
- **PH of pure water**: is 7 at 25 degrees Celsius

**Facts to remember**
- Atomic number equals the number of protons.
- Mass number equals protons plus neutrons.
- Isotopes have the same atomic number but different mass number.
- Ionic bond forms by transfer of electrons.
- Covalent bond forms by sharing of electrons.
- PH of pure water is 7 at 25 degrees Celsius.
- Acids turn blue litmus red.
- Bases turn red litmus blue.
- Oxidation is loss of electrons.
- Reduction is gain of electrons.
- Noble gases are in group 18.
- Halogens are in group 17.
- Alkali metals are in group 1.
- Avogadro number is 6.022 × 10^23.
