Circuits, magnets, optics and atoms.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Ohm's law**: states V = IR
- **Resistances in series**: add directly
- **Resistances in parallel**: add as reciprocals
- **Electric power**: equals VI
- **Unit of electrical energy**: is the kilowatt hour
- **Fuse**: is a safety device with a low melting point wire

**Facts to remember**
- Ohm's law states V = IR.
- Resistances in series add directly.
- Resistances in parallel add as reciprocals.
- Electric power equals VI.
- Unit of electrical energy is the kilowatt hour.
- Fuse is a safety device with a low melting point wire.
- Fleming's left hand rule gives the direction of force on a current carrying conductor.
- Electromagnetic induction was discovered by Faraday.
- Transformer works on mutual induction and changes AC voltage.
- Concave mirror is used as a shaving mirror and in headlights.
- Convex mirror is used as a rear view mirror.
- Total internal reflection is used in optical fibres.
- Photoelectric effect was explained by Einstein.
- X-rays were discovered by Roentgen.
