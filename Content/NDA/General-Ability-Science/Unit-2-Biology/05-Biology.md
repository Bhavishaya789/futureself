Biology for the general science paper.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Cell**: is the structural and functional unit of life
- **Mitochondria**: release energy in the cell
- **Chloroplast**: carries out photosynthesis
- **DNA**: carries genetic information
- **Haemoglobin**: carries oxygen in red blood cells
- **Human heart**: has four chambers

**Facts to remember**
- Cell is the structural and functional unit of life.
- Mitochondria release energy in the cell.
- Chloroplast carries out photosynthesis.
- DNA carries genetic information.
- Haemoglobin carries oxygen in red blood cells.
- Human heart has four chambers.
- Normal blood pressure is about 120 over 80 mm Hg.
- Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy.
- Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets.
- Vitamin A deficiency causes night blindness.
- Iodine deficiency causes goitre.
- Malaria is spread by female Anopheles mosquito.
- Tuberculosis is caused by a bacterium.
- Insulin is secreted by the pancreas.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Attempt the generated questions in sets of ten, mark each answer, and note the reason for every miss. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
