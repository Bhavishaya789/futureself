Ecosystems, pollution and technology basics.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Ozone layer**: protects the earth from ultraviolet rays
- **Greenhouse gases**: include carbon dioxide and methane
- **Acid rain**: is caused by sulphur and nitrogen oxides
- **Biodegradable waste**: is broken down by microbes
- **Renewable energy**: includes solar and wind
- **ISRO**: is India's space agency

**Facts to remember**
- Ozone layer protects the earth from ultraviolet rays.
- Greenhouse gases include carbon dioxide and methane.
- Acid rain is caused by sulphur and nitrogen oxides.
- Biodegradable waste is broken down by microbes.
- Renewable energy includes solar and wind.
- ISRO is India's space agency.
- Chandrayaan is India's moon mission series.
- GSAT are communication satellites.
- DRDO develops defence technology.
- Agni and Prithvi are Indian missiles.
- Laser stands for light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation.
- Radar uses radio waves to detect objects.
- Sonar uses sound waves underwater.
- Global Positioning System uses satellites for location.
