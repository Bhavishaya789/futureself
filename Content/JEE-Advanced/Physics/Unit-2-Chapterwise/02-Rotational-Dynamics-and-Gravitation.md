@study
Torque tau = I alpha, angular momentum L = I omega, rotational kinetic energy (1/2) I omega^2. Moments of inertia: ring M R^2, disc (1/2) M R^2, solid sphere (2/5) M R^2, rod ML^2/12 about the centre. Rolling without slipping: v = omega R, total K = (1/2) M v^2 (1 + k^2/R^2), a = g sin theta/(1 + k^2/R^2). Gravitation: F = G m1 m2/r^2, g = G M/R^2, orbital speed sqrt(GM/r), escape speed sqrt(2GM/R), Kepler T^2 proportional to r^3.
## Deep dive: moment of inertia and rotation
Moment of inertia I = sum m r^2. About the axis through the centre: thin ring M R^2; disc (1/2) M R^2; solid sphere (2/5) M R^2; hollow sphere (2/3) M R^2; rod M L^2/12 (centre, perpendicular) and M L^2/3 (end); rectangular plate M (a^2 + b^2)/12. Parallel axis theorem I = I_cm + M d^2; perpendicular axis theorem (plane lamina) I_z = I_x + I_y. Radius of gyration k = sqrt (I/M). Rotational equations: omega = omega0 + alpha t, theta = omega0 t + (1/2) alpha t^2, omega^2 = omega0^2 + 2 alpha theta. Torque tau = r x F = I alpha; work W = tau theta; power P = tau omega; rotational kinetic energy (1/2) I omega^2.
## Deep dive: angular momentum and rolling
Angular momentum L = I omega = r x p; torque = dL/dt, so L is conserved when no external torque acts (ice-skater, diver, planetary motion: Kepler second law equal areas in equal times, areal velocity L/2m constant). Rolling without slipping: v_cm = omega R, point of contact instantaneously at rest; total kinetic energy = (1/2) M v^2 + (1/2) I omega^2 = (1/2) M v^2 (1 + k^2/R^2). On an incline of angle theta the acceleration is a = g sin theta/(1 + k^2/R^2) (ring 1/2 g sin theta, disc 2/3 g sin theta, solid sphere 5/7 g sin theta); required friction f = M a k^2/R^2; minimum mu for pure rolling = (k^2/R^2) tan theta/(1 + k^2/R^2). Order of arrival: sphere before disc before ring. Toppling and sliding conditions for blocks use torque balance about the corner.
## Deep dive: gravitation
Newton law F = G m1 m2/r^2 with G = 6.67 x 10^-11. Field g = G M/r^2 outside and g = G M r/R^3 inside a uniform solid sphere; g at depth d is g (1 - d/R); at height h it is g (R/(R + h))^2 (approximately g (1 - 2h/R)); variation with latitude g' = g - omega^2 R cos^2 lambda. Potential V = -G M/r; potential energy U = -G M m/r; escape velocity v_e = sqrt (2 G M/R) = sqrt (2 g R) = 11.2 km/s for Earth; orbital velocity v_o = sqrt (G M/r) = v_e/sqrt 2. Satellite energy: K = G M m/(2r), U = -G M m/r, total E = -G M m/(2r). Kepler laws: orbits are ellipses with the Sun at one focus; areal velocity constant; T^2 proportional to a^3 (T = 2 pi sqrt (r^3/(G M))). Geostationary satellite: T = 24 h, height about 36000 km, orbit in the equatorial plane. Binding energy of a satellite is +G M m/(2r).
