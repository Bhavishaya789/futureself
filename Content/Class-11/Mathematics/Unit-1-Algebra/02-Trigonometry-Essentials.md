Angles are measured in degrees or radians; **pi radians = 180 degrees**. Arc length s = r t (t in radians).

## Basic identities
sin^2 t + cos^2 t = 1; 1 + tan^2 t = sec^2 t; 1 + cot^2 t = cosec^2 t.

## Sum and difference
- sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B
- cos(A + B) = cos A cos B - sin A sin B
- tan(A + B) = (tan A + tan B) / (1 - tan A tan B)

## Double angle
sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A; cos 2A = cos^2 A - sin^2 A = 1 - 2 sin^2 A = 2 cos^2 A - 1.

## Example
sin 15 = sin(45 - 30) = (sqrt 2/2)(sqrt 3/2) - (sqrt 2/2)(1/2) = (sqrt 6 - sqrt 2)/4 = **0.2588**.

## General solutions
sin x = sin a gives x = n pi + (-1)^n a; cos x = cos a gives x = 2 n pi +/- a; tan x = tan a gives x = n pi + a.

## Quadrant signs (ASTC)
All positive in Q1, Sine in Q2, Tangent in Q3, Cosine in Q4.

## Moderate
P: Prove sin^2 t + cos^2 t = 1 using a right triangle.
A: With opposite a, adjacent b and hypotenuse c: sin t = a/c, cos t = b/c; so sin^2 + cos^2 = (a^2 + b^2)/c^2 = 1 by Pythagoras.

## Advanced
P: Solve 2 sin^2 x - 3 sin x + 1 = 0 for x in [0, 2 pi].
A: (2 sin x - 1)(sin x - 1) = 0, so sin x = 1/2 or 1. x = pi/6, 5 pi/6, pi/2.
