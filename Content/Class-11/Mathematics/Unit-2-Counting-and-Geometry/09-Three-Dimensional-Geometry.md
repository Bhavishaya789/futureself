A point in space is (x, y, z) in three mutually perpendicular axes forming eight octants. The distance between (x1, y1, z1) and (x2, y2, z2) is sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2 + (z2 - z1)^2). The section formula divides a segment in the ratio m:n.
## Deep dive: coordinate system in space
Three mutually perpendicular axes X, Y, Z meet at the origin O. The coordinate planes XY, YZ, ZX divide space into eight octants. A point P (x, y, z) has distances |x|, |y|, |z| from the YZ, ZX and XY planes. Points on the x-axis are (x, 0, 0), on the y-axis (0, y, 0), on the z-axis (0, 0, z). Points in the XY plane have z = 0, in YZ plane x = 0, in ZX plane y = 0. The octant of a point is decided by the signs of x, y, z.
## Deep dive: distance and section formulae
Distance between P (x1, y1, z1) and Q (x2, y2, z2) is PQ = sqrt ((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2 + (z2 - z1)^2). The distance of P from the origin is sqrt (x^2 + y^2 + z^2). The point dividing PQ internally in the ratio m : n is ((m x2 + n x1)/(m + n), (m y2 + n y1)/(m + n), (m z2 + n z1)/(m + n)); the mid-point is the average of the coordinates, and for external division change n to -n. The centroid of a triangle is the mean of the three vertices.
## Deep dive: applications
Three points are collinear if the sum of two of the distances equals the third. Triangle shape: if all three sides are equal it is equilateral; two equal, isosceles; a^2 + b^2 = c^2, right-angled. The locus of points at a fixed distance r from a given point is a sphere.
