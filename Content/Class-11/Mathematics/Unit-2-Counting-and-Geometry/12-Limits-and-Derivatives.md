# Limits and Derivatives
minutes: 45

@study
The limit of f(x) as x approaches a is L, written lim f(x) = L, if f(x) comes arbitrarily close to L as x approaches a from both sides. The limit exists if the left-hand limit equals the right-hand limit. Algebra of limits: the limit of a sum, difference, product and quotient (denominator limit not zero) equals the sum, difference, product and quotient of limits; the limit of a constant is the constant. Evaluating limits: direct substitution; factorising to remove a common factor when the form is 0/0; rationalising when roots appear; for polynomials as x tends to infinity divide by the highest power. Standard limits: lim (x^n - a^n)/(x - a) = n a^(n-1); lim sin x / x = 1 as x tends to 0; lim (1 - cos x)/x = 0; lim tan x / x = 1; lim (e^x - 1)/x = 1; lim log(1 + x)/x = 1; lim (a^x - 1)/x = log a. The derivative of f at x is f'(x) = lim h tends to 0 of [f(x + h) - f(x)]/h, called the first principle. It gives the slope of the tangent and the rate of change. Rules: derivative of a constant is 0; (c f)' = c f'; sum and difference rules; product rule (uv)' = u'v + uv'; quotient rule (u/v)' = (u'v - uv')/v^2. Standard derivatives: d/dx x^n = n x^(n-1); sin x gives cos x; cos x gives -sin x; tan x gives sec^2 x; cot x gives -cosec^2 x; sec x gives sec x tan x; cosec x gives -cosec x cot x; e^x gives e^x; log x gives 1/x. Derivatives are used for slope of tangent, velocity as ds/dt and rate problems. A function that is differentiable at a point is continuous there, but the converse is false.
## Deep dive: Limits
Indeterminate forms such as 0/0 need algebra. Left and right limits are checked for piecewise functions. Limits at infinity compare the highest powers. Standard limits are used with substitution to adjust forms.
## Deep dive: Derivative from first principles
Write f(x + h) - f(x), divide by h and take the limit. For x^n the binomial expansion gives n x^(n-1). The derivative is slope of tangent at the point.
## Deep dive: Rules and meaning
Product and quotient rules combine standard derivatives. Velocity is the derivative of position. A sharp corner like |x| at 0 is continuous but not differentiable.
