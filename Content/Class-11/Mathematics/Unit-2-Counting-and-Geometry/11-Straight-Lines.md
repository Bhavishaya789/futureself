@study
Coordinate geometry studies figures using coordinates. The distance between (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) is sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2). The section formula for a point dividing the join in the ratio m:n internally is ((m x2 + n x1)/(m + n), (m y2 + n y1)/(m + n)); the midpoint is the average of coordinates. The centroid of a triangle is ((x1 + x2 + x3)/3, (y1 + y2 + y3)/3). The area of a triangle is half the modulus of x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2); three points are collinear if this is zero. The slope of a line is m = tan(theta) = (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1), where theta is the angle with the positive x-axis; the x-axis has slope 0 and a vertical line has undefined slope. Parallel lines have equal slopes m1 = m2; perpendicular lines satisfy m1 m2 = -1. The angle between two lines: tan(theta) = |(m1 - m2)/(1 + m1 m2)|. Equations: horizontal line y = b; vertical line x = a; point-slope y - y1 = m(x - x1); two-point form (y - y1) = (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1) (x - x1); slope-intercept y = mx + c; intercept form x/a + y/b = 1; normal form x cos(alpha) + y sin(alpha) = p. The general equation is Ax + By + C = 0 with slope -A/B, x-intercept -C/A and y-intercept -C/B. Distance of the point (x1, y1) from the line Ax + By + C = 0 is |A x1 + B y1 + C|/sqrt(A^2 + B^2). Distance between parallel lines Ax + By + C1 = 0 and Ax + By + C2 = 0 is |C1 - C2|/sqrt(A^2 + B^2). The family of lines through the intersection of L1 = 0 and L2 = 0 is L1 + k L2 = 0. Shifting the origin to (h, k) replaces x by x + h and y by y + k.
## Deep dive: Basics
Slope measures steepness. Angles of inclination lie between 0 and 180 degrees. Collinear points have equal slopes between pairs. A line through the origin has no constant term.
## Deep dive: Equations
Choose the form from the given data: point and slope, two points, or intercepts. Convert to the general form to compute slope and intercepts. Parallel and perpendicular lines need only a change of the slope or constant.
## Deep dive: Distances and families
The perpendicular distance formula is used for heights and for tangents. Parallel lines have a fixed distance. L1 + k L2 = 0 gives all lines through a point of intersection.
