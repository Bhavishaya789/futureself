## Straight lines
Slope m = (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1) = tan t. Forms: slope-intercept y = m x + c; point-slope y - y1 = m (x - x1); intercept x/a + y/b = 1. Distance from point (x1, y1) to line A x + B y + C = 0: |A x1 + B y1 + C| / sqrt(A^2 + B^2). Example: distance of (1, 2) from 3x + 4y - 5 = 0 is |3 + 8 - 5| / 5 = **1.2**.
Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have m1 m2 = -1.

## Limits
lim x->0 sin x / x = 1; lim x->0 (1 + x)^(1/x) = e; lim x->a (x^n - a^n)/(x - a) = n a^(n-1).

## Derivatives
d/dx of x^n = n x^(n-1); sin x -> cos x; cos x -> -sin x; product rule (uv)' = u'v + uv'; quotient rule (u/v)' = (u'v - uv')/v^2.
Example: derivative of x^2 at x = 1 from the definition: lim h->0 ((1 + h)^2 - 1)/h = lim (2 + h) = **2**.
