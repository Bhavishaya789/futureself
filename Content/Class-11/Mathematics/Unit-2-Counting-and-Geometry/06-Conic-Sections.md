# Conic sections
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 conic sections
@study
A conic is the section of a double cone by a plane: a circle, ellipse, parabola or hyperbola. Standard forms: circle x^2 + y^2 = r^2; parabola y^2 = 4ax; ellipse x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1 with b^2 = a^2(1 - e^2); hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1 with b^2 = a^2(e^2 - 1).
## Deep dive: how conics arise
Cutting a double-napped cone by a plane gives: a circle (plane perpendicular to the axis), an ellipse (tilted plane cutting one nappe), a parabola (plane parallel to a generator) and a hyperbola (plane cutting both nappes). Every conic is the locus of a point whose distance from a fixed point (the focus) is e times its distance from a fixed line (the directrix): e = 0 circle, e < 1 ellipse, e = 1 parabola, e > 1 hyperbola.
## Deep dive: circle and parabola
Circle with centre (h, k) and radius r: (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2; the general form x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0 has centre (-g, -f) and radius sqrt (g^2 + f^2 - c). Parabola y^2 = 4ax: vertex (0, 0), focus (a, 0), directrix x = -a, axis the x-axis, length of latus rectum 4a. Other forms: y^2 = -4ax (opens left), x^2 = 4ay (opens up), x^2 = -4ay (opens down).
## Deep dive: ellipse and hyperbola
Ellipse x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1 (a > b): centre origin, vertices (+-a, 0), foci (+-ae, 0), major axis length 2a, minor axis 2b, eccentricity e = sqrt (1 - b^2/a^2), latus rectum 2b^2/a, directrices x = +-a/e. Hyperbola x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1: vertices (+-a, 0), foci (+-ae, 0), transverse axis 2a, conjugate axis 2b, e = sqrt (1 + b^2/a^2), latus rectum 2b^2/a, asymptotes y = +- (b/a) x. For the ellipse the sum of focal distances is 2a; for the hyperbola their difference is 2a.

Q: What is a conic section?
A: The curve of intersection of a plane with a double right circular cone.
Q: What is eccentricity?
A: The constant ratio of the distance from the focus to the distance from the directrix.
Q: What is the eccentricity of a circle?
A: 0.
Q: What is the eccentricity of a parabola?
A: 1.
Q: What is the eccentricity of an ellipse?
A: Less than 1.
Q: What is the eccentricity of a hyperbola?
A: Greater than 1.
Q: What is the latus rectum?
A: The chord through the focus perpendicular to the axis.
Q: What is the length of the latus rectum of y^2 = 4ax?
A: 4a.
Q: What is the directrix of y^2 = 4ax?
A: x = -a.

A: (a, 0).
Q: What is the focal property of an ellipse?
A: The sum of the distances from the two foci to any point on it is 2a.
Q: What is the focal property of a hyperbola?
A: The difference of the distances from the two foci is 2a.
