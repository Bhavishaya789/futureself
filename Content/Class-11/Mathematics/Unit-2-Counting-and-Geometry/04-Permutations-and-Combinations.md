# Permutations and combinations
minutes: 20

@study
**Fundamental principle:** if one task can be done in m ways and another in n ways, together they can be done in m x n ways (multiplication) or m + n ways (either one, addition).

**Permutations (order matters):** nPr = n! / (n - r)!. **Combinations (order does not matter):** nCr = n! / (r! (n - r)!). nCr = nC(n-r).

### Examples
- Arrange 5 distinct books: 5! = **120**.
- Choose 3 from 10: 10C3 = **120**.
- Choose 2 men from 8 and 3 women from 6: 8C2 x 6C3 = 28 x 20 = **560**.
- Arrangements of the letters of MISSISSIPPI: 11! / (4! 4! 2!) = 34650.
- Circular arrangements of n objects: (n - 1)!.
