# What is Psychology?
minutes: 40

@study
Psychology is the scientific study of mental processes, experiences and behaviour in different contexts. The word comes from the Greek psyche (soul or mind) and logos (study). Mental processes include thinking, remembering and feeling; experiences are internal and subjective; behaviour includes responses and activities that can be observed. Psychology began as a formal science in 1879 when Wilhelm Wundt set up the first psychological laboratory at Leipzig, Germany. Early schools: Structuralism (Wundt and Titchener) analysed consciousness into elements by introspection; Functionalism (William James, who wrote Principles of Psychology in 1890) studied the functions of mind in adapting to the environment; Behaviourism (J B Watson, later B F Skinner and Pavlov) studied observable behaviour; Gestalt psychology (Wertheimer, Koffka and Kohler) emphasised that the whole is more than the sum of its parts; Psychoanalysis (Sigmund Freud) emphasised the unconscious; Humanistic psychology (Carl Rogers and Abraham Maslow) emphasised growth and self-actualisation; Cognitive psychology studies mental processes like memory and thinking. Goals: to describe, explain, predict and control behaviour. Branches: clinical, counselling, developmental, educational, social, industrial and organisational, health, sports, forensic and environmental psychology. Psychology as a discipline relates to biology, sociology, anthropology, economics and political science. Applications: schools, hospitals, workplaces, sports, military and the community. Psychology in India: the first department was set up at Calcutta University in 1915 under Narendra Nath Sengupta; Girindrasekhar Bose founded the Indian Psychoanalytic Society in 1922; important institutions include NIMHANS, the Indian Council of Social Science Research and the National Council of Educational Research and Training. Indian thought (yoga, Buddhism, the Gita) contributed to ideas on consciousness. Psychologists include counsellors, researchers and therapists; a psychiatrist is a medical doctor who treats mental disorders with medicine, whereas a psychologist studies behaviour and gives therapy. Psychology is a science because it uses systematic methods, but also deals with subjective experience. Unlike common sense, it relies on evidence.
## Deep dive: Evolution of psychology
Psychology grew from philosophy and physiology. Wundt established it as a science. Schools offered different views: structuralism on elements, functionalism on purposes, behaviourism on behaviour, psychoanalysis on the unconscious.
## Deep dive: Goals and fields
Psychologists aim to describe, explain, predict and influence behaviour. Fields include clinical, educational, social and industrial psychology. Applications improve life and work.
## Deep dive: Psychology in India
Indian psychology developed from philosophy and modern science. Calcutta University started the first department in 1915. Concerns include mental health, education and community.
