# Introducing Sociology
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 sociology introducing sociology sociological imagination mills
@study
Sociology is the systematic study of human society and social behaviour. The word comes from the Latin socius (companion) and the Greek logos (study); it was coined by Auguste Comte in 1838. Sociology emerged in Europe from the Enlightenment, the Industrial Revolution and the French Revolution, which created questions about social order and change. Founders: Auguste Comte (the father of sociology, positivism), Emile Durkheim (suicide, division of labour, social facts), Karl Marx (class conflict) and Max Weber (verstehen, ideal types). C Wright Mills introduced the sociological imagination, the ability to link personal troubles with public issues and to see the interplay of biography and history. Personal troubles are private matters felt by the individual, whereas public issues are matters of social structure; for example, unemployment of one person is a personal trouble but mass unemployment is a public issue. Sociology and common sense differ: sociology uses systematic evidence and methods, whereas common sense is based on unexamined belief. Sociology differs from other social sciences: economics studies production and consumption, political science studies power and the state, psychology studies the individual mind, history studies the past, and anthropology studies cultures and early societies; sociology studies society as a whole and interactions between its parts. Scope of sociology: the study of institutions such as family, religion, education and economy, as well as groups, class and change. Sociology in India: it began in Bombay University in 1919 with Patrick Geddes, then Lucknow (G S Ghurye, D P Mukerji, A K Saran) and Delhi (M N Srinivas). Indian sociology was shaped by colonial rule, British social anthropology and nationalism. Key Indian terms: caste, family, village studies and tribe. Sociology applies to careers in social work, research, teaching, policy and administration. Sociology is a science of society but is not like natural science because human beings have consciousness and meanings. Perspectives: functionalism, conflict and interactionism. Micro sociology studies small-scale interaction and macro sociology studies large structures. Pure sociology builds knowledge while applied sociology solves problems. Sociology promotes critical and objective understanding, making the familiar strange.
## Deep dive: Origins
Sociology emerged in Europe in response to rapid changes. Comte named it. Durkheim, Marx and Weber shaped its main traditions.
## Deep dive: Sociological imagination
Mills asks us to connect private troubles with public issues. This lets us see that individual lives are shaped by social forces.
## Deep dive: Sociology in India
Indian sociology developed from universities in Bombay, Lucknow and Delhi. Scholars studied caste, tribe, village and family.
