# Structural Organisation in Animals
minutes: 40

@study
Animal tissues: epithelial, connective, muscular, neural. Epithelium: simple (squamous, cuboidal, columnar, ciliated) and compound; junctions: tight, adhering, gap. Connective: loose (areolar, adipose), dense (regular, irregular), specialised (cartilage, bone, blood). Muscle: skeletal, smooth, cardiac. Neural: neurons and neuroglia. Earthworm (Pheretima): 100-120 segments, clitellum on 14-16, setae, nephridia, 4 pairs of spermathecae. Cockroach (Periplaneta): head, thorax, abdomen, mosaic vision, Malpighian tubules, open circulation, 10 pairs of spiracles. Frog (Rana tigrina): three-chambered heart, tadpole metamorphosis.
## Deep dive: Epithelial and connective tissue
Squamous epithelium: lining of blood vessels and alveoli; cuboidal: ducts of glands, tubules of nephrons; columnar: stomach and intestine lining; ciliated: trachea, oviducts. Glandular: unicellular (goblet cells) or multicellular (salivary glands). Tight junctions prevent leakage, adhering junctions cement cells, gap junctions allow communication. Areolar tissue has fibroblasts, macrophages and mast cells; adipose stores fat; tendons join muscle to bone, ligaments join bone to bone. Cartilage has chondrocytes in lacunae; bone has osteocytes with Haversian canals; blood is a fluid connective tissue.
## Deep dive: Muscle, neural and organ systems
Skeletal muscle is striated and voluntary; smooth muscle is non-striated, involuntary and spindle-shaped; cardiac muscle is striated, involuntary and branched with intercalated discs. A neuron has dendrites, cell body (cyton) and axon; neuroglia support neurons. Earthworm: digestive tract with pharynx, oesophagus, gizzard, stomach, intestine with typhlosole; closed circulation; hermaphrodite; fertilisation external to the body in cocoon. Cockroach: crop, gizzard with chitinous teeth, hepatic caecae, tracheal respiration, nocturnal; males have anal styles.
## Deep dive: Frog
Rana tigrina: smooth moist skin, buccal cavity, cloaca, 3-chambered heart (2 atria, 1 ventricle), sinus venosus, renal portal system, hepatic portal system, kidneys (mesonephric), male has vocal sacs and nuptial pads, fertilisation external, tadpole with gills develops into adult by metamorphosis. Respiration: cutaneous, buccopharyngeal and pulmonary.
