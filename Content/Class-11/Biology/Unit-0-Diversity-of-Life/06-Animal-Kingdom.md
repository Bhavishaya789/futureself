@study
Animals are classified on levels of organisation (cellular, tissue, organ, organ system), symmetry (asymmetrical, radial, bilateral), germ layers (diploblastic, triploblastic), coelom (acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate), segmentation and notochord. Phyla: Porifera, Coelenterata (Cnidaria), Ctenophora, Platyhelminthes, Aschelminthes, Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Hemichordata, Chordata. Chordata has Urochordata, Cephalochordata and Vertebrata (Cyclostomata, Gnathostomata: Pisces and Tetrapoda). Chondrichthyes have cartilaginous skeleton; Osteichthyes bony. Amphibia, Reptilia, Aves, Mammalia.
## Deep dive: Basis of classification
Levels of organisation: cellular (sponges), tissue (coelenterates), organ (flatworms), organ system (higher). Symmetry: asymmetrical (sponges), radial (coelenterates, echinoderm adults), bilateral (annelids to chordates). Diploblastic have ectoderm and endoderm with mesoglea; triploblastic add mesoderm. Coelom is the body cavity lined by mesoderm: acoelomate (flatworms), pseudocoelomate (roundworms), coelomate (annelids onwards). Notochord is a mesodermal rod; animals are non-chordates or chordates.
## Deep dive: Non-chordate phyla
Porifera: pores, canal system, choanocytes, spicules, spongin (Sycon, Spongilla, Euspongia). Coelenterata: cnidoblasts, polyp and medusa (Hydra, Aurelia, Physalia, Adamsia, Pennatula). Ctenophora: comb plates, bioluminescence (Pleurobrachia). Platyhelminthes: flame cells, hooks and suckers (Taenia, Fasciola, Planaria). Aschelminthes: roundworms, sexes separate (Ascaris, Wuchereria, Ancylostoma). Annelida: metameric segmentation (Nereis, Pheretima, Hirudinaria). Arthropoda: jointed appendages, chitinous exoskeleton, largest phylum (Apis, Bombyx, Laccifer, Anopheles, Locusta). Mollusca: soft body, mantle, radula, shell (Pila, Octopus, Pinctada). Echinodermata: water vascular system, spiny skin (Asterias, Echinus). Hemichordata: Balanoglossus.
## Deep dive: Chordates
Three features: notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits. Urochordata (Ascidia, Salpa), Cephalochordata (Branchiostoma), Vertebrata with vertebral column. Cyclostomata: jawless (Petromyzon, Myxine). Chondrichthyes: Scoliodon, Pristis, Trygon, Torpedo. Osteichthyes: Exocoetus, Hippocampus, Labeo, Catla. Amphibia: Rana, Bufo, Hyla, Salamandra. Reptilia: Chelone, Testudo, Chameleon, Calotes, Crocodilus, Naja. Aves: Corvus, Columba, Psittacula, Pavo. Mammalia: mammary glands, hair; Ornithorhynchus, Macropus, Pteropus, Panthera.
## Deep dive: Comparison of vertebrates
Fishes: two-chambered heart, cold-blooded, gills. Amphibians: three-chambered heart, moist skin. Reptiles: scales, mostly three-chambered heart (crocodile four). Birds and mammals: four-chambered heart, warm-blooded (homeotherms). Birds: feathers, pneumatic bones. Mammals: viviparous mostly, mammary glands, diaphragm.

## Advanced
P: Compare Chondrichthyes and Osteichthyes, and Amphibia, Reptilia and Aves.
A: Chondrichthyes: cartilaginous skeleton, placoid scales, no air bladder. Osteichthyes: bony skeleton, air bladder, operculum. Amphibia: moist skin, 3-chambered heart; Reptiles: dry scaly skin; Aves: feathers, 4-chambered heart.
