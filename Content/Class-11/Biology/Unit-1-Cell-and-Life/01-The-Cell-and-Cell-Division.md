**Cell theory:** all organisms are made of cells; the cell is the basic unit; all cells arise from pre-existing cells.
## Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells
Prokaryotes (bacteria) lack a membrane-bound nucleus and organelles; DNA lies in a nucleoid; ribosomes are 70S. Eukaryotes have a nucleus, membrane-bound organelles and 80S ribosomes.
## Organelles
- **Mitochondria:** ATP production, own DNA. **Chloroplast:** photosynthesis. **ER:** protein/lipid synthesis. **Golgi:** packaging and secretion. **Lysosomes:** digestion. **Ribosomes:** protein synthesis. **Nucleus:** DNA, controls cell.
## Cell cycle
Interphase (G1, S, G2) then M phase. **Mitosis** phases: prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase; gives two identical diploid daughter cells. **Meiosis** has two divisions and produces four haploid cells with crossing over in prophase I.

## Moderate
P: List four differences between mitosis and meiosis.
A: Divisions (1 vs 2), daughter cells (2 vs 4), ploidy (diploid vs haploid), crossing over (absent vs present).
R: 2 | Two differences

## High
P: Describe the stages of mitosis.
A: Prophase: chromosomes condense; metaphase: line up at equator; anaphase: sister chromatids separate; telophase: nuclei re-form and cytokinesis follows.
R: 2 | Prophase and metaphase
R: 2 | Anaphase and telophase
## Advanced
P: If a cell with 2n = 46 undergoes meiosis, state chromosome numbers at the end of meiosis I and II.
A: After meiosis I: two cells with 23 chromosomes (each with two chromatids). After meiosis II: four cells with 23 single chromatids.
R: 2 | Meiosis I
R: 2 | Meiosis II
