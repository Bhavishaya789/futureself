# Cell: The Unit of Life
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 biology cell the unit of life organelles
@study
Cell theory (Schleiden and Schwann, Virchow): all organisms are made of cells, cells are the basic units, and all cells arise from pre-existing cells. Prokaryotic cells lack a membrane-bound nucleus (bacteria, 70S ribosomes, nucleoid, plasmids, mesosomes). Eukaryotic cells have membrane-bound organelles. Fluid mosaic model (Singer and Nicolson, 1972). Cell wall: cellulose in plants, peptidoglycan in bacteria. Endomembrane system: ER, Golgi, lysosomes, vacuoles. Mitochondria (cristae, F1 particles), plastids (chloroplast with grana and stroma), ribosomes (80S), cytoskeleton, cilia, flagella, centrosome, nucleus (nuclear envelope, nucleolus, chromatin).
## Deep dive: Cell theory and types
Robert Hooke discovered cells (1665) in cork. Leeuwenhoek saw living cells. Robert Brown discovered the nucleus. Schleiden (plants) and Schwann (animals) proposed cell theory; Virchow added Omnis cellula e cellula. Prokaryotes: size 0.1-0.5 µm for mycoplasma; bacteria 3-5 µm. Cell envelope: glycocalyx, cell wall, plasma membrane. Flagella of bacteria, pili, fimbriae. Ribosomes 70S (50S + 30S). Eukaryotic ribosomes 80S (60S + 40S).
## Deep dive: Membrane and organelles
Plasma membrane: phospholipid bilayer with proteins; integral and peripheral; cholesterol in animals. Transport: passive (simple diffusion, facilitated), active (against gradient with ATP, Na+-K+ pump), osmosis. ER: RER with ribosomes, SER makes lipids. Golgi (Camillo Golgi): packaging and secretion. Lysosomes: hydrolytic enzymes, suicide bags. Vacuoles: tonoplast in plants. Mitochondria: double membrane, matrix, own DNA and 70S ribosomes, ATP synthesis. Plastids: chloroplast, chromoplast, leucoplast (amyloplast, elaioplast, aleuroplast).
## Deep dive: Nucleus and cytoskeleton
Nucleus: double membrane with pores, nucleoplasm, nucleolus (rRNA synthesis), chromatin. Chromosomes classified by centromere position: metacentric, sub-metacentric, acrocentric, telocentric. Microbodies: peroxisomes and glyoxysomes. Cytoskeleton: microtubules, microfilaments, intermediate filaments. Cilia and flagella have 9+2 axoneme structure; centrioles have 9+0 triplet structure. Centrosome forms spindle.
