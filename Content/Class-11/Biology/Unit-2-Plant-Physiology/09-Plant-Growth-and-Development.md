# Plant Growth and Development
minutes: 40

@study
Growth is an irreversible permanent increase in size. Phases: meristematic, elongation, maturation; sigmoid curve; arithmetic and geometric growth. Differentiation, dedifferentiation, redifferentiation. Plant growth regulators: auxins (IAA, IBA, NAA, 2,4-D), gibberellins (GA3), cytokinins (zeatin, kinetin), ethylene, abscisic acid (ABA). Photoperiodism: short-day, long-day, day-neutral plants; phytochrome. Vernalisation. Seed dormancy and germination.
## Deep dive: Growth and development
Growth rate: arithmetic (linear), geometric (exponential, Wt = W0 e^rt). Relative growth rate. Conditions: water, oxygen, nutrients, temperature. Development includes growth and differentiation. Plasticity: heterophylly (cotton, coriander, larkspur). Open form of growth: meristems throughout life.
## Deep dive: Plant growth regulators
Auxin: discovered by Darwin and Went; apical dominance, root initiation, parthenocarpy, weed killer (2,4-D). Gibberellins: bolting in cabbage, elongation of sugarcane stem, seedless grapes, malting. Cytokinins: cell division, delay senescence, overcome apical dominance, Skoog and Miller. Ethylene: fruit ripening, abscission, in Cranberry; ethephon. ABA: stress hormone, stomatal closure, seed dormancy.
## Deep dive: Photoperiodism and vernalisation
Short day plants: Xanthium, rice, chrysanthemum flower when night exceeds a critical length; long day plants: wheat, spinach. Phytochrome Pr and Pfr senses red/far-red light. Vernalisation: low temperature promotes flowering (winter wheat, barley). Florigen was proposed as a flowering hormone. Seed dormancy broken by scarification, stratification, GA.
