# Respiration in Plants
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 biology respiration in plants glycolysis krebs cycle ETS
@study
Cellular respiration oxidises organic food to release energy stored as ATP. Glycolysis (EMP pathway) in cytoplasm: glucose to 2 pyruvate, net 2 ATP and 2 NADH. Aerobic: pyruvate to acetyl CoA (link reaction), Krebs cycle in the matrix (3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 GTP per turn), electron transport system on the inner mitochondrial membrane with oxidative phosphorylation. Anaerobic: fermentation (alcohol by yeast, lactic acid in muscles). Respiratory quotient RQ = CO2 released / O2 consumed; 1 for carbohydrates, <1 for fats, >1 for organic acids. Net gain from glucose about 30-38 ATP.
## Deep dive: Glycolysis and fermentation
Glycolysis occurs in all living cells. Steps: phosphorylation to glucose-6-phosphate, fructose 1,6-bisphosphate, splitting into DHAP and PGAL, oxidation to 1,3-BPGA with NADH, substrate level phosphorylation making ATP, finally pyruvate. Net 2 ATP, 2 NADH. Fermentation in yeast gives ethanol and CO2 (pyruvate decarboxylase, alcohol dehydrogenase), 2 ATP only; in muscles lactic acid (lactate dehydrogenase). Fermentation releases less than 7% of energy of glucose.
## Deep dive: Krebs cycle and ETS
Pyruvate enters mitochondria; pyruvate dehydrogenase gives acetyl CoA, NADH and CO2. Krebs cycle: acetyl CoA + oxaloacetic acid -> citric acid; steps release 2 CO2, 3 NADH, 1 FADH2 and 1 ATP/GTP per turn. ETS: complexes I to IV, ubiquinone, cytochrome c; oxygen is the final acceptor forming water; ATP synthase (F0F1) by chemiosmosis. NADH gives about 2.5 ATP, FADH2 about 1.5 ATP.
## Deep dive: Respiratory balance sheet and RQ
Per glucose: glycolysis 2 ATP (+2 NADH), link 2 NADH, Krebs 2 ATP + 6 NADH + 2 FADH2; total about 30 ATP (modern) or 38 (older). Amphibolic pathway: respiration involves both catabolism and anabolism (fatty acid breakdown and synthesis). RQ: glucose 1.0; fats about 0.7; proteins about 0.9; tripalmitin 0.7; oxalic acid 4.0.
