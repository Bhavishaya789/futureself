# Photosynthesis and respiration
minutes: 25
yt: photosynthesis and cellular respiration class 11
@study
**Photosynthesis:** 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6 O2. Light reaction (thylakoid membranes): water splits, O2 released, ATP and NADPH made. Calvin cycle (stroma): CO2 fixed by RuBisCO into 3-PGA and then sugars. C4 plants (maize, sugarcane) use PEP carboxylase and show Kranz anatomy.
**Respiration:** C6H12O6 + 6 O2 -> 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + energy. Glycolysis (cytoplasm) gives 2 ATP, 2 NADH and 2 pyruvate; Krebs cycle (mitochondrial matrix); electron transport chain (inner membrane) makes most ATP. Net about **30-32 ATP** per glucose in eukaryotes (older textbooks say 36-38).

## High
P: Compare the light reaction and the Calvin cycle.
A: Light reaction: thylakoid, needs light, makes ATP, NADPH and O2. Calvin cycle: stroma, uses ATP and NADPH to fix CO2 into sugar.
R: 2 | Light reaction
R: 2 | Calvin cycle
## Advanced
P: Explain why C4 plants are efficient in hot dry climates.
A: PEP carboxylase fixes CO2 in mesophyll and delivers it to bundle sheath cells, concentrating CO2 around RuBisCO and reducing photorespiration.
R: 2 | PEP carboxylase and Kranz anatomy
R: 2 | Reduced photorespiration
