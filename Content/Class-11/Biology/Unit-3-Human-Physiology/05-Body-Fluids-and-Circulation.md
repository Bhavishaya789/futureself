@study
Blood is a fluid connective tissue: plasma (55%, water 90-92%, proteins fibrinogen, globulin, albumin) and formed elements (RBC, WBC, platelets). RBC (5-5.5 million/mm3) lack nucleus and contain Hb (12-16 g/100 mL). WBC: granulocytes (neutrophils, eosinophils, basophils) and agranulocytes (lymphocytes, monocytes). Blood groups ABO and Rh; Landsteiner. Coagulation: thromboplastin, prothrombin to thrombin, fibrinogen to fibrin. Lymph. Heart: four chambers, SA node (pacemaker), AV node, bundle of His, Purkinje fibres. Cardiac output = stroke volume x heart rate (5 L/min). ECG waves P, QRS, T. Double circulation. Disorders: hypertension (120/80 normal), coronary artery disease, angina, heart failure.
## Deep dive: Blood and groups
Plasma without clotting factors is serum. Albumin maintains osmotic balance; globulins defence; fibrinogen clotting. Neutrophils (60-65%) are phagocytic; lymphocytes (20-25%) are B and T cells. Platelets 1.5-3.5 lakh/mm3. ABO: A antigen/anti-B, B/anti-A, AB universal recipient, O universal donor. Rh factor: Rh-positive 80%; erythroblastosis foetalis when Rh-negative mother carries Rh-positive foetus. Coagulation cascade involves calcium ions and factors.
## Deep dive: Heart and cardiac cycle
Heart is mesodermal, in the thoracic cavity, enclosed in the pericardium. Right atrium receives deoxygenated blood via superior and inferior vena cava; tricuspid valve; right ventricle to pulmonary artery. Left atrium receives pulmonary veins; bicuspid (mitral) valve; left ventricle to aorta. Nodal tissue: SA node generates 70-75 impulses/min; AV node; AV bundle; Purkinje fibres. Cardiac cycle about 0.8 s: atrial systole 0.1 s, ventricular systole 0.3 s, joint diastole 0.4 s. Heart sounds: lub (closure of AV valves) and dub (closure of semilunar valves). ECG: P wave atrial depolarisation, QRS ventricular depolarisation, T ventricular repolarisation.
## Deep dive: Circulation and disorders
Double circulation: pulmonary and systemic; hepatic portal circulation; coronary circulation. Open circulation in arthropods, closed in annelids and vertebrates. Fish have single circulation. Lymph: colourless, lymphocytes, drains tissue fluid, carries fats absorbed (lacteals). Regulation: neural (medulla cardiac centres, sympathetic increases rate, parasympathetic decreases) and hormonal (adrenaline). Disorders: hypertension above 140/90; atherosclerosis; angina; heart failure (congestive); cardiac arrest.
