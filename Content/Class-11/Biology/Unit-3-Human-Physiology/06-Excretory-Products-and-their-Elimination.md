# Excretory Products and their Elimination
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 biology excretory products kidney nephron urine formation
@study
Ammonotelic (aquatic animals), ureotelic (mammals) and uricotelic (birds, reptiles, insects) excretion. Human excretory system: kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra. Nephron: Malpighian body (glomerulus + Bowman capsule) and renal tubule (PCT, loop of Henle, DCT, collecting duct). Cortical (85%) and juxtamedullary nephrons. Urine formation: glomerular filtration (GFR 125 mL/min, 180 L/day), reabsorption, secretion. Counter-current mechanism concentrates urine (osmolarity 1200 mOsm/L). Regulation: ADH, RAAS (JGA, renin, angiotensin II, aldosterone), ANF. Micturition. Disorders: uremia, renal calculi, glomerulonephritis; haemodialysis; kidney transplant.
## Deep dive: Nephron and urine formation
Each kidney has about one million nephrons. Podocytes in Bowman capsule form filtration slits; filtration is ultrafiltration through three layers: endothelium, basement membrane, podocytes. PCT reabsorbs nearly all glucose, amino acids, 70-80% of electrolytes and water, and secretes H+, K+ and ammonia. Descending limb permeable to water; ascending limb impermeable to water but transports electrolytes. DCT conditionally reabsorbs Na+ and water, reabsorbs HCO3-. Collecting duct reabsorbs water, urea. Urine is 1-1.5 L/day, pH 6.0, with urea 25-30 g/day.
## Deep dive: Concentration and regulation
Counter-current mechanism: loop of Henle (counter-current flow) and vasa recta; NaCl and urea create the osmotic gradient in medullary interstitium up to 1200 mOsm/L. ADH from the neurohypophysis increases water permeability; low ADH gives diabetes insipidus. JG cells release renin when GFR falls: renin converts angiotensinogen to angiotensin I and then II, which constricts vessels, stimulates aldosterone and Na+ reabsorption. ANF from the atria causes vasodilation and decreases RAAS.
## Deep dive: Other organs and disorders
Lungs remove CO2 (200 mL/min) and water; liver secretes bilirubin, cholesterol, degraded steroid hormones; skin sweat glands remove NaCl, urea, lactic acid; sebaceous glands sterols. Micturition is a reflex: stretch receptors send signals to the CNS, smooth muscle contraction. Uremia (urea accumulation) may cause kidney failure; haemodialysis removes urea using cellophane tubing, heparin as an anticoagulant. Glomerulonephritis inflames glomeruli. Renal calculi are stone masses of crystallised oxalates. Kidney transplant is the best long-term remedy.

## High
P: Explain the functions of PCT, loop of Henle and DCT.
A: PCT reabsorbs most solutes; loop of Henle creates gradient; DCT conditionally reabsorbs Na+ and water.
