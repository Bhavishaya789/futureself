# Neural Control and Coordination
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 biology neural control and coordination neuron brain reflex action potential
@study
Neuron: dendrites, cell body, axon; myelinated and non-myelinated; unipolar, bipolar, multipolar. Resting potential -70 mV maintained by Na+-K+ pump. Action potential: depolarisation, repolarisation, hyperpolarisation; all-or-none. Synapse: electrical and chemical; neurotransmitters acetylcholine, dopamine. Human brain: forebrain (cerebrum, thalamus, hypothalamus), midbrain, hindbrain (pons, cerebellum, medulla). Spinal cord. Reflex arc: receptor, afferent, CNS, efferent, effector. Sensory organs: eye (retina, rods and cones, rhodopsin), ear (cochlea, organ of Corti, semicircular canals for balance). Cerebrospinal fluid.
## Deep dive: Neuron and impulse
Resting membrane: K+ leak channels, Na+-K+ ATPase pumps 3 Na+ out, 2 K+ in. Stimulus opens Na+ channels; Na+ influx reverses polarity (depolarisation) to +30 mV; K+ efflux repolarises. Conduction in myelinated fibres is saltatory (Nodes of Ranvier) and faster. Chemical synapse: presynaptic neuron releases neurotransmitter into the synaptic cleft; binds receptors on the postsynaptic membrane generating EPSP or IPSP. Electrical synapses have gap junctions and faster transmission.
## Deep dive: Brain and spinal cord
Meninges: dura mater, arachnoid, pia mater. Cerebrum divided by a longitudinal fissure into hemispheres connected by the corpus callosum; cerebral cortex (grey matter) has sensory, motor and association areas; Broca and Wernicke areas for speech. Thalamus: relay centre; hypothalamus: body temperature, hunger, thirst, hormones; limbic system for emotions. Midbrain: corpora quadrigemina. Pons: respiration regulation. Cerebellum: balance and coordination. Medulla: cardiovascular and respiratory reflexes, vomiting. Reflex action: involuntary, rapid, via spinal cord (knee jerk).
## Deep dive: Sensory organs
Eye: sclera, cornea, choroid, iris, pupil, lens, retina; fovea (cones highest), blind spot (no photoreceptors); aqueous humor, vitreous humor. Rods are for dim light with rhodopsin (opsin + retinal from vitamin A); cones for colour and bright vision (red, green, blue). Ear: outer (pinna, canal, tympanum), middle (malleus, incus, stapes, Eustachian tube), inner (cochlea with organ of Corti, vestibular apparatus: semicircular canals and otolith organs). Hearing: sound waves move tympanum, ossicles, oval window, cochlear fluid, hair cells, nerve impulses.
