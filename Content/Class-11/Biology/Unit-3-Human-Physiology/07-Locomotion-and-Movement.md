# Locomotion and Movement
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 biology locomotion and movement muscle skeleton joints
@study
Types of movement: amoeboid, ciliary, muscular. Muscle: skeletal (striated, voluntary), visceral (smooth), cardiac. Skeletal muscle ultrastructure: sarcomere (between two Z lines), A band (myosin), I band (actin), H zone, M line. Sliding filament theory (Huxley): actin slides over myosin; cross-bridges; ATP and Ca2+; troponin, tropomyosin. Neuromuscular junction, acetylcholine. Red fibres (myoglobin) and white fibres. Human skeleton: 206 bones; axial 80, appendicular 126; skull 29, vertebral column 26, ribs 12 pairs, sternum, girdles. Joints: fibrous, cartilaginous, synovial (ball and socket, hinge, pivot, gliding, saddle). Disorders: myasthenia gravis, tetany, muscular dystrophy, arthritis, osteoporosis, gout.
## Deep dive: Muscle structure and contraction
Muscle fibre has sarcolemma, sarcoplasm, sarcoplasmic reticulum (stores Ca2+), myofibrils. Thick filaments of myosin with head and tail (meromyosins, ATPase); thin filaments of actin with tropomyosin and troponin. Contraction: signal from CNS releases ACh at the motor end plate, action potential, Ca2+ release, binds troponin, exposes myosin binding sites, cross-bridge forms, ATP hydrolysed, actin pulled to centre, I bands shorten, A band stays same. Relaxation: Ca2+ pumped back. Rigor mortis, muscle fatigue due to lactic acid. Red fibres have more mitochondria and myoglobin.
## Deep dive: Skeletal system
Axial skeleton 80 bones: skull (cranial 8 and facial 14, hyoid 1, ear ossicles 6), vertebral column (7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral fused, 4 coccygeal fused), sternum 1, ribs 24. Appendicular 126: pectoral girdle (clavicle and scapula), upper limb 30 each, pelvic girdle, lower limb 30 each. Ribs: true (7 pairs), false (3 pairs), floating (2 pairs). Glenoid cavity and acetabulum articulate with humerus and femur. Patella is the knee cap.
## Deep dive: Joints and disorders
Fibrous joints: sutures of skull. Cartilaginous: between vertebrae. Synovial: synovial fluid; ball and socket (shoulder, hip), hinge (knee, elbow), pivot (atlas-axis), gliding (carpals), saddle (carpo-metacarpal of thumb). Myasthenia gravis: autoimmune, neuromuscular junction; tetany: low Ca2+; muscular dystrophy: genetic progressive degeneration; arthritis: joint inflammation; osteoporosis: reduced bone mass with age and oestrogen decline; gout: uric acid crystals in joints.
