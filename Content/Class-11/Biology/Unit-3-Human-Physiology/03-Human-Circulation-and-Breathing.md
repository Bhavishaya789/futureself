# Human circulation and breathing
minutes: 20
yt: human heart circulation class 11
@study
The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. Blood flows right atrium -> right ventricle -> lungs (pulmonary circulation) -> left atrium -> left ventricle -> body (systemic circulation). The **SA node** is the pacemaker. Normal heart rate about 72 beats/min; cardiac output = heart rate x stroke volume = 72 x 70 mL = about **5 L/min**.
**ECG:** P wave (atrial depolarisation), QRS complex (ventricular depolarisation), T wave (ventricular repolarisation).
**Breathing:** inspiration uses the diaphragm and external intercostal muscles; tidal volume 500 mL; vital capacity about 3.5 to 4.5 L. O2 is carried by haemoglobin; most CO2 is carried as bicarbonate.
