# Breathing and Exchange of Gases

@study
Respiratory system: nostrils, nasal chamber, pharynx, larynx (sound box), trachea with C-shaped cartilage rings, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli. Lungs enclosed in double pleura. Inspiration: diaphragm contracts and external intercostals contract, thoracic volume increases and pressure falls. Expiration reverses. Volumes: TV 500 mL, IRV 2500-3000 mL, ERV 1000-1100 mL, RV 1100-1200 mL; VC = IRV + TV + ERV about 3500-4500 mL; TLC about 5800-6000 mL. Gas exchange by diffusion across the alveolar membrane; pO2 alveoli 104 mm Hg, pCO2 40. Oxygen carried as oxyhaemoglobin (97%); CO2 as bicarbonate (70%), carbamino-haemoglobin (20-25%), dissolved (7%). Bohr effect. Respiratory regulation by the medulla rhythm centre, pneumotaxic centre. Disorders: asthma, emphysema, occupational lung disease.
## Deep dive: Mechanism of breathing
Boyle law: volume and pressure are inversely related. Inspiration: diaphragm flattens, ribs and sternum move up and out, lung volume increases, intra-pulmonary pressure falls below atmosphere and air enters. Expiration: relaxation of muscles; pressure rises. About 12-16 breaths per minute. Pulmonary ventilation = TV x rate = about 6000-8000 mL/min. Spirometer measures volumes. Functional residual capacity = ERV + RV. Inspiratory capacity = TV + IRV. Expiratory capacity = TV + ERV.
## Deep dive: Gas exchange and transport
Partial pressures: atmosphere pO2 159, alveoli 104, deoxygenated blood 40, oxygenated 95; pCO2: alveoli 40, deoxygenated blood 45, tissues 45. CO2 solubility is 20-25 times that of O2. Diffusion membrane: squamous epithelium of alveoli, endothelium of capillaries and basement substance; thickness less than 1 mm. Oxygen dissociation curve is sigmoid; low pCO2, high pH, lower temperature, fewer 2,3-BPG shift curve left; opposite shifts right (Bohr effect). Carbonic anhydrase makes CO2 + H2O -> H2CO3 -> H+ + HCO3-; chloride shift.
## Deep dive: Regulation and disorders
Rhythm centre in medulla; pneumotaxic centre in pons moderates; chemosensitive area adjacent to rhythm centre is sensitive to CO2 and H+; receptors in aortic arch and carotid artery. Asthma: inflammation of bronchi; emphysema: damaged alveolar walls, major cause cigarette smoking; occupational lung disease from dust (silicosis, asbestosis). Hypoxia and carbon monoxide poisoning (CO binds Hb 200 times more strongly).
