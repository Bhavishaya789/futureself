# Societal Impact
minutes: 35

@study
Digital footprint (active and passive). Netiquette. Cyber safety: protecting identity, passwords, secure websites (https), avoiding scams. Cyber crime: hacking, phishing, identity theft, cyber bullying, cyber stalking, ransomware, spam. Cyber law: IT Act 2000 (amended 2008). Intellectual property rights: copyright, patent, trademark; plagiarism; licensing (GPL, Creative Commons). Digital property. Open source and free software. E-waste management: reduce, reuse, recycle; hazardous substances. Technology and health: ergonomics, screen time, addiction. Digital society: e-governance (Digilocker, UPI), e-commerce, e-learning, e-banking. Technology and gender and accessibility; digital divide; net neutrality.
## Deep dive: Digital footprint and cyber safety
Active footprint: posts, comments, forms you fill. Passive footprint: cookies, IP address, browsing history collected without your action. Strong password: 8+ characters with mixed letters, numbers, symbols; two-factor authentication; use https; avoid public Wi-Fi for banking; update software. Phishing and social engineering; cyber bullying (harassment through digital means); cyber grooming; reporting at cybercrime.gov.in.
## Deep dive: IPR and law
Copyright protects original literary, artistic, musical works automatically; a patent protects inventions for 20 years; trademarks protect brand names and logos. Plagiarism is presenting others' work as one's own. Licences: GPL (copyleft), Creative Commons (attribution, share-alike, non-commercial). IT Act 2000 recognises electronic records and digital signatures and punishes hacking (Section 66), identity theft (66C), cyber terrorism (66F). Data protection and privacy.
## Deep dive: E-waste, health, society
E-waste: discarded electronics with lead, mercury, cadmium. Management: reuse, recycle, buy-back schemes, authorised recyclers; E-Waste Management Rules. Ergonomics: posture, 20-20-20 rule. Digital addiction, screen time. E-governance services: Aadhaar, DigiLocker, UMANG, e-Hospital. Digital divide, assistive technology, inclusive design. Technology for gender equality.
