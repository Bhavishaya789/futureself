# Nomadic Empires
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 history nomadic empires mongols genghis khan
@study
Nomadic empires were created by pastoral peoples of the Central Asian steppe. The best known are the Mongols. Temujin was born about 1162 and was proclaimed Genghis Khan (Chinggis Khan), the universal ruler, at a kuriltai in 1206. Mongol society was organised in clans and tribes bound by oaths of loyalty (the anda) and the yasa, a code of laws. Their army was disciplined and fast, based on horse archers and a decimal organisation of units of ten, a hundred, a thousand and ten thousand. Genghis Khan conquered northern China, Central Asia (Khwarezm), parts of Persia and Russia; he died in 1227. His sons and grandsons continued the conquests: Ogodei, Mongke and Kublai Khan, who founded the Yuan dynasty in China. Hulagu destroyed Baghdad in 1258. The empire was divided into khanates: the Golden Horde in Russia, the Il-Khanate in Persia and the Chagatai Khanate in Central Asia. The Mongols created the yam, a postal relay system, and protected trade along the Silk Road, the Pax Mongolica. Baghdad's fall and the destruction of Central Asian cities had enormous costs, but the Mongols also promoted trade and exchange of knowledge. Later Timur (Tamerlane) and the Mughal founder Babur claimed Mongol descent.
## Deep dive: Society and army
Mongol society was pastoral nomadic. The army used speed, discipline and feigned retreats. Conquered skilled people were often used by the Mongols. Religious tolerance was usual. The yasa was the common law.
## Deep dive: Conquests and rule
Genghis Khan united the tribes, defeated the Jin and Khwarezm, and attacked Persia. The empire was divided among his sons after his death. Kublai Khan became emperor of China as the Yuan dynasty. Mongols ruled Russia as the Golden Horde for about two centuries.
## Deep dive: Effects
Destruction of cities such as Merv and Baghdad, but also trade, the yam post and movement of ideas. The Black Death travelled along these trade routes. Mongol rule left a lasting political and cultural legacy.
