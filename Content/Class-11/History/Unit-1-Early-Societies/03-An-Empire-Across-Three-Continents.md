# An Empire Across Three Continents
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 history roman empire augustus constantine republic
@study
The Roman Empire stretched across Europe, North Africa and West Asia around the Mediterranean. Rome began as a city-state on the Tiber, ruled by kings, then became a Republic in 509 BCE governed by consuls, the Senate and assemblies. The Roman Republic expanded through wars, including the Punic Wars against Carthage. Julius Caesar conquered Gaul and was assassinated in 44 BCE. His grand-nephew Octavian became Augustus in 27 BCE and began the Principate, the first Roman emperor and founder of the Pax Romana. Later emperors included Trajan (the empire at its greatest extent, 117 CE), Hadrian, Marcus Aurelius and Diocletian, who reorganised the empire into a Tetrarchy. Constantine made Christianity legal by the Edict of Milan in 313 CE and founded Constantinople in 330 CE. In 476 CE the last Western emperor, Romulus Augustulus, was deposed by Odoacer, while the Eastern Roman or Byzantine Empire continued until 1453. Roman society had patricians and plebeians, slaves and coloni. Rome's achievements include roads, aqueducts, law, Latin and amphitheatres such as the Colosseum.
## Deep dive: Republic to empire
The Republic had Senate, consuls and tribunes. Civil wars and the rise of generals like Pompey and Caesar ended it. Augustus kept republican forms but held real power through the army and the Senate. The empire was divided into provinces governed by governors.
## Deep dive: Economy and society
Agriculture, trade across the Mediterranean, slavery and large estates (latifundia) supported the economy. Cities had forums, baths and amphitheatres. Roman women had limited rights. Slaves worked on farms and mines. Roman law developed ideas of citizenship and property.
## Deep dive: Crisis and decline
The third century saw civil war, plague, inflation and barbarian invasions. Diocletian and Constantine restored order. The Western empire fell to Germanic tribes. The Eastern empire survived with Constantinople as the capital.
