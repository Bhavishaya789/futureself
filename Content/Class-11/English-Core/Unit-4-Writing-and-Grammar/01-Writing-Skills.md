# Writing Skills: Notice, Poster, Letter, Article, Speech and Debate
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 english core writing skills notice poster letter article speech debate format
@study
Writing skills in Class 11 English Core cover notices, posters, letters (to the editor, business and job application), articles, speeches and debates. Each format has its own layout, tone and length. A notice has the name of the issuing body, the word NOTICE, date, heading, body in 50 words, and the name and designation of the writer. A poster has a catchy heading, brief matter, visuals and details of the issuing authority. A formal letter has the sender address, date, receiver address, subject, salutation, body, complimentary close and signature. An article has a title, byline and body of introduction, development and conclusion. A speech opens by greeting the audience and closes with thanks. A debate presents the arguments for or against a motion with evidence and rebuttal.
## Deep dive: Notices and posters
Notice: issuing body, NOTICE, date, heading, matter (who, what, when, where, contact), signature with name and designation. Poster: bold title, slogan, concise matter, contact details, a visual idea, and the issuing authority. Both must be brief and to the point.
## Deep dive: Letters
Formal letter: sender address, date, receiver, subject line, salutation (Sir or Madam), body (introduction, details, request), close (Yours faithfully) and signature. Letters to the editor express opinion on public issues. Job application letters give qualifications and attach a resume. Business letters make orders or complaints.
## Deep dive: Articles, speeches and debates
Article: title, byline, introduction, body, conclusion. Speech: greeting, topic, arguments, appeal, thanks. Debate: opening, points for or against, counter-arguments and conclusion. Use formal vocabulary, linking words and facts; keep to the word limit.
