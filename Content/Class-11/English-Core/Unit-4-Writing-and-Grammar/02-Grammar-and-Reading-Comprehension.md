# Grammar and Reading Comprehension
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 english core grammar tenses modals determiners reported speech passive
@study
Grammar and reading comprehension skills tested in Class 11 include determiners, tenses, modals, subject-verb agreement, reported speech, the active and passive voice, gap filling, editing and omission. Reading comprehension passages test understanding, inference, vocabulary and summarising. Rules: a singular subject takes a singular verb; modals such as can, could, may, might, must, should express ability, permission, possibility or obligation; reported speech shifts tense back and changes pronouns and time words; the passive voice uses the form of be plus the past participle. For reading, read the questions first, scan the passage, locate answers and use own words where required.
## Deep dive: Tenses and agreement
Simple present for habits and facts; present continuous for ongoing actions; present perfect for results linked to now; simple past for finished actions; past perfect for an earlier past action. Subject-verb agreement: each, every, either, neither take a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb; two subjects joined by and take a plural verb.
## Deep dive: Modals and determiners
Can shows ability; may shows permission or possibility; must shows necessity; should shows advice; would shows a polite request. Determiners: a, an, the, some, any, much, many, few, little, each, every.
## Deep dive: Reported speech and passive
Direct to indirect: say becomes said, present becomes past, here becomes there, today becomes that day, now becomes then. Passive: object becomes subject, be plus past participle, by the agent. Reading: skim, scan, infer.
