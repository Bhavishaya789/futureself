# The Address
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots the address marga minco
@study
The Address by Marga Minco is a story of a girl who survives the Second World War and returns to find her mother's belongings. During the war, her mother gave valuables to Mrs Dorling and gave the girl the address. After the war, the narrator visits the house and sees that Mrs Dorling has her mother's things. Mrs Dorling pretends not to know her and is cold; the narrator is taken round to see the objects but receives nothing. She is uneasy and leaves. Years later she visits again, but the sight of her mother's belongings in a stranger's house is painful. She feels that the things now belong to the past and abandons the search. Themes: loss, memory, war's effect on people, greed and the pain of lost belongings.
## Deep dive: Plot
The narrator's mother gave her the address of Mrs Dorling, an acquaintance with whom she left valuables. After the war the narrator finds the house and sees familiar items: a cloth, a bowl and other items. Mrs Dorling invites her in but behaves like a stranger. The narrator is uncomfortable, takes nothing and leaves. She later tries again but cannot go through with it.
## Deep dive: Characters
The narrator is sensitive, quiet and mourns her mother. Mrs Dorling is guilty, selfish and uneasy. The mother is only remembered through objects. The girl in the house is Mrs Dorling's daughter.
## Deep dive: Themes
The war's emotional damage; the power of objects to carry memory; the selfishness of people who took advantage; the narrator's inability to claim her mother's things; loss and survival.
