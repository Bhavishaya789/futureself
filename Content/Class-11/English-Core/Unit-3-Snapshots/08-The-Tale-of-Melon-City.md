# The Tale of Melon City
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots the tale of melon city vikram seth
@study
The Tale of Melon City by Vikram Seth is a humorous poem in a narrative form and a satire. A king builds an arch across a road. When the king passes under it, his crown is knocked off because the arch is too low. The king orders the chief builder to be hanged. The chief builder blames the workmen, who blame the architect, who blames the king himself. Finally the blame reaches the king, who is too proud to accept it. The wise men then say that the noose must hang whoever it fits, and since it fits only the king, he is spared by a trick: to avoid hanging, the king insists that the man who is hanged must be the one worthy of the throne. The ministers decide that the next person to pass by will be made king; the passer-by is an idiot who says he wants to be a melon, and the melon is crowned. The city is called Melon City. The poem satirises political folly and arbitrary rulers. Themes: absurdity of authority, satire, justice.
## Deep dive: Plot
The king builds a royal arch. His crown is knocked off. He orders the chief builder to be hanged. Each person passes blame until it comes to the king. The courtiers say the noose fits the king. The king is made to realise that justice is blind; the king asks the wise men. The ministers decide to choose a king by chance: the next person to pass is an idiot. The idiot says that he wants a melon. The melon is crowned. The city is called Melon City.
## Deep dive: Satire
The poem mocks absurd laws and the arbitrary power of rulers, the foolishness of officials and the way of passing blame. It shows an unjust system in comic form and that rulers can be as foolish as their subjects.
## Deep dive: Style
Simple rhyming couplets, fable-like, humorous, an ironic tone; the language is light and the story builds to an absurd ending.
