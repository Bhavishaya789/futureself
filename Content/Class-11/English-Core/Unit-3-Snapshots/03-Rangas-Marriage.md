# Ranga’s Marriage
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots rangas marriage masti venkatesha iyengar
@study
Ranga's Marriage by Masti Venkatesha Iyengar is a humorous story set in the village of Hosahalli in Karnataka. Ranga, a young man, returns from Bangalore after studying and has become modern in his ways. Shyama Joyisa, the village astrologer and matchmaker, is determined to marry him to Ratna, a village girl. The astrologer predicts a marriage match and by clever manoeuvres involves Ranga and Ratna until they marry. The story is narrated by the writer, who is amused by the astrologer's plans. The tale highlights village life, the role of astrology and matchmaking and the playful trick that leads to marriage. Themes: tradition and modernity, village life, humour, marriage customs.
## Deep dive: Characters
Ranga: educated, modern and well-mannered. Shyama Joyisa: the astrologer, shrewd and humorous. Ratna: the village girl, young and quiet. Rama Rao: the writer's friend or the narrator. The villagers: curious and traditional.
## Deep dive: Plot
Ranga returns to Hosahalli. The astrologer sees him as a suitable match for Ratna. Without openly saying, he tells Ranga that Ratna is a good girl. He gradually brings them together through a visit and a mild trick. After a proposal, they marry. The story ends happily.
## Deep dive: Themes
Village life; arranged marriage; humour and irony; tradition versus modern education; the matchmaker as a figure of village society; the charm of the story.
