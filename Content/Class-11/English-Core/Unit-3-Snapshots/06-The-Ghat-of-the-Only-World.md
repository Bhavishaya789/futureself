# The Ghat of the Only World
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots the ghat of the only world amitav ghosh agha shahid ali
@study
The Ghat of the Only World by Amitav Ghosh is a tribute to his friend, the Kashmiri-American poet Agha Shahid Ali, who died of brain cancer in December 2001. The essay recalls how the writer learned of the illness, the poet's courage in the face of death and his love of poetry, Kashmir, Delhi and Begum Akhtar's music. Shahid's poem about the Ganga, the ghat and the idea of a world in which one rests, gave the essay its title. The writer reflects on friendship, mortality and the way Shahid lived. Shahid wanted his ashes scattered in the Ganga and Kashmir. Themes: friendship, memory, death, poetry and exile.
## Deep dive: Content
Ghosh recalls meeting Shahid in Delhi in the 1970s and later in America. When Shahid fell ill and underwent treatment, he kept writing. Shahid loved Kashmir, the Ganga and Begum Akhtar's music, and wrote in English with Urdu forms such as the ghazal. In the last days, friends gathered. Shahid died on 8 December 2001 at Amherst. The ghat of the only world is where Ganga flows and where the poet would have wished to be remembered.
## Deep dive: Style
Personal essay, elegiac and reflective; mixes memories with poetic ideas. The writer is both friend and storyteller. Cultural references: Kashmiri culture, Hindustani music and Urdu poetry.
## Deep dive: Themes
Friendship and loss; the dignity of the poet facing death; exile and belonging; poetry as a way of life; the cultural unity of India and Kashmir.
