# The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots summer of the beautiful white horse william saroyan
@study
The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse by William Saroyan is told by Aram, a nine-year-old Armenian boy in California. His cousin Mourad, who is thirteen and considered mad in the family, wakes him early one morning with a beautiful white horse. Aram knows the family is poor and honest, and the horse must be stolen. He is afraid but loves riding. For about two weeks they ride the horse in secret, hiding it in a vineyard barn. Then they return it to its owner John Byro, who recognises that his horse looks better. The boys return the horse. Byro says that the horse was well kept and lets the matter go. Themes: honesty, innocence of childhood, poverty, family honour and the beauty of the horse.
## Deep dive: Characters
Aram: narrator, nine, honest and innocent. Mourad: cousin, thirteen, adventurous and passionate about horses. Uncle Khosrove: a loud, impatient uncle who says do not mind; famous for his temper. John Byro: the owner of the horse, a farmer. The Garoghlanian family: known for honesty and poverty.
## Deep dive: Plot and setting
The story is set in Fresno, California, among Armenian immigrants during the early twentieth century. Mourad brings a horse. Aram worries about the family's reputation. They keep the horse in a shed in the vineyard and ride in the early mornings. The horse becomes better with feeding. When they return the horse, Byro recognises it and says it is his, but accepts. The title suggests the beauty and the magic of the summer.
## Deep dive: Themes
Honesty and family honour; the tension between poverty and pride; the joy of childhood adventure; Armenian cultural background; humour in the way the boys treat the horse; moral dilemma of the boys.
