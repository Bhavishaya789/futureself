# Birth
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots birth a j cronin
@study
Birth by A. J. Cronin is an extract from The Citadel. Dr Andrew Manson, a young doctor in a Welsh mining town, attends a difficult delivery at the home of Susan Mutch, a poor woman. After a long labour he delivers the child, which appears stillborn. He gives priority to the mother, who is dangerously ill, and then hands the baby to the nurse, thinking it is dead. But he decides to make an effort to revive the child. He puts the baby alternately in hot and cold water, massages, and finally the baby gasps and cries. The mother survives too. The story shows the doctor's commitment, hope and the miracle of life. Themes: duty, perseverance, hope and the value of life.
## Deep dive: Plot
Manson is called in the night to help Mrs Mutch. After a long wait, he delivers the baby, which is not breathing. Dr Hannah is not there. The mother is in danger. The nurse hands the baby to Manson who tries to revive it. He alternates between hot and cold water and rubs the child. After a long effort, the baby cries. The mother recovers.
## Deep dive: Characters
Dr Andrew Manson: young, dedicated, thoughtful. Mrs Susan Mutch: young, brave mother. Hannah: the experienced doctor who later helps. The nurse: practical and caring. Mr Joe Mutch: father who waits.
## Deep dive: Themes
Medical dedication; hope and persistence; the sanctity of life; the importance of not giving up; compassion.
