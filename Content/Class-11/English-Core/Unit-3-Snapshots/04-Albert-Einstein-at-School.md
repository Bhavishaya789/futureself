# Albert Einstein at School
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots albert einstein at school patrick pringle
@study
Albert Einstein at School by Patrick Pringle describes the young Einstein's experience in a German school. Einstein was a thoughtful pupil who did not like the rote learning and harsh discipline of the Luitpold Gymnasium in Munich. His teachers thought him slow and the Greek teacher, who disliked his questioning attitude, told him that he would never be successful. A teacher told him that his mere presence spoiled the respect of the class. Einstein said he could not stay and left school, taking a certificate from a doctor to prove a need for rest. He left for Italy to join his family. Later he developed his ideas on relativity. Themes: rote learning versus questioning, creativity, authority and genius.
## Deep dive: Episodes
Einstein, aged about fifteen, attends the Luitpold Gymnasium. He dislikes the teaching, which stresses memory. He asks questions that the teachers cannot answer. The Greek teacher tells him he will not make anything of his life. Einstein decides to leave the school with a medical certificate. He goes to Italy, and later studies in Zurich.
## Deep dive: Characters
Einstein: thoughtful, independent and brilliant. The Greek teacher: strict and unhappy with questioning. The family doctor: understanding. Einstein's parents: moved to Italy and supported his talents.
## Deep dive: Themes
The education system that stifles creativity; a questioning mind; the difference between knowledge and memory; the importance of freedom in learning; the later success of a boy who was told he would fail.
