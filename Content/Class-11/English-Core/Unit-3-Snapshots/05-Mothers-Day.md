# Mother’s Day
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english snapshots mothers day j b priestley
@study
Mother's Day is a one-act comedy by J. B. Priestley. Mrs Annie Pearson is a mild woman who is taken for granted by her husband George and her children Doris and Cyril. Her friend Mrs Fitzgerald, a fortune-teller and neighbour, suggests that she should assert herself. Mrs Fitzgerald uses a magic spell to exchange their personalities, so that Mrs Pearson speaks like Mrs Fitzgerald. When the family comes home, Mrs Pearson (in Mrs Fitzgerald's manner) refuses to do their work and tells them to look after themselves. The family is shocked and begs her to return to normal. Finally the spell is reversed, but Mrs Pearson now speaks firmly, showing she has gained confidence. Themes: respect for the mother, the role of housewife, humour and assertion.
## Deep dive: Characters
Mrs Pearson: gentle, passive and weak at the start; turns firm. Mrs Fitzgerald: strong, practical and outspoken. Doris and Cyril: self-centred, lazy, demanding. George Pearson: selfish and authoritarian. The play uses humour and contrast.
## Deep dive: Plot
Mrs Pearson complains to Mrs Fitzgerald about her family's lack of respect. Mrs Fitzgerald says she should change her ways. They exchange personalities. Doris, Cyril and George arrive; they find Mrs Pearson (as Mrs Fitzgerald) unusual. She refuses to serve them, goes to play cards and leaves them to prepare supper. The family is in shock. Eventually the exchange is reversed.
## Deep dive: Themes
The family should respect and share work with a mother; assertiveness; humour as social comment; gender roles in households; the title is ironic as the family does not honour mothers.
