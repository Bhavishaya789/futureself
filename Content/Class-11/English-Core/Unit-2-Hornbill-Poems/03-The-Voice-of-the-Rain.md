# The Voice of the Rain
minutes: 30
yt: class 11 english hornbill poem the voice of the rain walt whitman summary
@study
The Voice of the Rain by Walt Whitman is a conversation between the poet and the rain. The poet asks who the rain is. The rain answers that it is the poem of the earth, rising as vapour from the land and sea, ascending to the sky and then falling back to refresh the earth. The rain compares itself to a song that begins in the heart, goes into the air and returns to the heart that composed it. The poem shows the water cycle and compares it with the cycle of poetry. Themes: the cycle of nature, transformation, renewal and the relationship between nature and poetry.
## Deep dive: Structure
The poet asks who the rain is. The rain describes itself as the endless cycle of water; it rises from earth as vapour, reaches the clouds, returns as rain and gives life to the earth. The poem ends with a comparison with a song: a song begins in the poet's heart and returns to the heart. It is written in free verse with a question-answer form.
## Deep dive: Devices
Personification of the rain; dialogue; metaphor of the rain as the poem of the earth; imagery of rising and falling; simile of the rain to a song. The cyclic structure echoes the water cycle.
## Deep dive: Themes
The unity of nature and art; the cycle of life; transformation in nature; renewal; the generosity of rain; the cycle of creation. Walt Whitman was an American poet known for free verse and Leaves of Grass.
