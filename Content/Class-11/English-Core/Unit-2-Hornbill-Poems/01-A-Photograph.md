# A Photograph
minutes: 30
yt: class 11 english hornbill poem a photograph shirley toulson summary
@study
A Photograph by Shirley Toulson describes the poet looking at an old photograph of her mother as a young girl, at the seaside with two cousins, taken by an unknown relative. The photograph recalls a happy moment when the girl was laughing at the sea. Twenty years after the photograph, the poet recalls her mother's laughter; now the mother is dead and the picture remains, with the poet's silence. The poem shows how photographs preserve memory and how the passing of time and death leave a silent grief. Themes: memory, loss, change and the permanence of the photograph against the impermanence of life.
## Deep dive: Stanzas
First: the photograph of the mother with cousins, a sunny day at the sea, with the paddling girls. Second: the sea and the mother's laughter in the picture; the poet remembers her mother's smile. Third: the poet in the present, the mother now dead; the photograph holds a moment of joy; the poet's unspoken grief. The poem has free verse and simple language.
## Deep dive: Devices
Imagery of the sea; contrast between the carefree past and silent present; the tone is nostalgic and sorrowful. The photograph is a symbol of memory. Repetition of time ideas. Poetic effect: understatement of grief.
## Deep dive: Themes
Time passes and takes loved ones; memory and photographs preserve the past; death and silence; family bonds across generations. The poet understands her mother's joy and life through the picture.
