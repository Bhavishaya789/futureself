# Childhood
minutes: 30
yt: class 11 english hornbill poem childhood markus natten summary
@study
Childhood by Markus Natten reflects on the loss of childhood. The poet asks when his childhood went, whether it was when he found that Hell is not a place of fire but the cruelty and pain of the world, or when he realised that adults do not always mean what they say. He asks whether childhood was lost when he began to understand that thoughts and words are not the same, or when he started to imitate adults and hide his real self. The poet concludes that childhood has been replaced by adult cleverness. He wonders where his innocence went and suspects it is somewhere else, perhaps in a child. Themes: loss of innocence, growing up, hypocrisy of adults, the search for the lost self.
## Deep dive: Content
The poem opens with a question: when did my childhood go. He recalls the time he first learned that Hell is not an actual place of fire but the suffering in the world. He realises that adults are often insincere. He thinks of the day he began to separate thought from speech and to imitate others. His innocence is replaced by cleverness. At the end he asks where childhood is now, perhaps in other children.
## Deep dive: Devices
Rhetorical questions; contrast between innocence and experience; personal reflective tone; irony of adults' behaviour; simple language. The structure is a series of questions leading to an uncertain answer.
## Deep dive: Themes
Loss of innocence; growing up; hypocrisy and artificiality of adult life; nostalgia for childhood; the search for identity. The poem speaks about the process of growing up as a gradual loss.
