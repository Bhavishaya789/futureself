# Father to Son
minutes: 30
yt: class 11 english hornbill poem father to son elizabeth jennings summary
@study
Father to Son by Elizabeth Jennings describes the gap between a father and his son. The father says he does not understand his son, and the son does not understand him; they live in the same house but there is a lack of communication. The father wants to reach out and bridge the distance, to walk with his son and share his life, but does not know how, and the son too is silent. The poem expresses regret, love and helplessness. It ends with the father's wish for the son to be near him, though he feels he cannot reach him. Themes: generation gap, communication, love and regret in family relations.
## Deep dive: Content
The father says he cannot understand his son, who is a stranger even though he is of his blood. The son is not a stranger but not understood. The father recalls that the son once walked beside him and wishes to build the bond again. He feels that he has failed to communicate. The poem expresses a desire for reconciliation and shows the emotional distance in the family.
## Deep dive: Devices
Plain, direct language; rhetorical question; contrast between the physical closeness and emotional distance; a reflective and sad tone; the father's voice as the speaker. The repetition of ideas of strangeness and the use of conversational rhythm.
## Deep dive: Themes
Generation gap; breakdown in communication; the father's love and helplessness; the wish for reconciliation. The poet Elizabeth Jennings is an English poet known for clear, controlled verse.
