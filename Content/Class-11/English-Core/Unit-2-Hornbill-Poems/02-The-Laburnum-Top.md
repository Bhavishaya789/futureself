# The Laburnum Top
minutes: 30
yt: class 11 english hornbill poem the laburnum top ted hughes summary
@study
The Laburnum Top by Ted Hughes describes a laburnum tree in early spring, still and silent, until a goldfinch arrives. The bird enters the tree and the whole tree seems to come alive with a vibrant sound and activity as the bird calls and the chicks answer from the nest in the tree. The goldfinch moves quickly with its mate or chicks, and the tree seems to rattle with life and movement like a machine. When the bird flies away, the tree returns to quiet. The poem shows the vitality of nature and the way a living creature transforms the world around it. Themes: energy of nature, harmony between bird and tree, cycle of life and renewal.
## Deep dive: Stages
The tree is quiet and still in the early part of the year. A goldfinch lands and the tree comes alive with a sharp, loud sound. The bird's mate or chicks respond from the nest, producing a lively chorus. The bird's sudden departure leaves the tree in stillness again. The poem is of four stanzas of free verse.
## Deep dive: Imagery and devices
Metaphors comparing the tree to a machine, a source of electric sound and a being with a pulse; contrast of stillness and activity; sound imagery; personification of the tree. Movement and colour, with the bird's bright plumage and the laburnum's yellow flowers.
## Deep dive: Themes
Nature's vitality; interdependence of tree and bird; sudden change from stillness to energy; the poet's keen observation of nature. Ted Hughes is known for his animal poetry.
