# Silk Road
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 english hornbill silk road nick middleton mount kailash tibet
@study
Silk Road by Nick Middleton is a travelogue of his journey in Tibet to Mount Kailash, the sacred mountain. Middleton travels with a driver, Tsetan, a guide and a cook, in a Land Cruiser across the Tibetan plateau, along an old branch of the ancient Silk Road, a trade route that carried silk and other goods between China and Europe. He suffers from altitude sickness and fatigue and the group faces rough terrain, rain, cold and difficulties with the vehicle. He meets locals, a nomad, passes small towns such as Zhongba and Darchen and sees that Tibetan pilgrims walk around the mountain in a circuit (kora) believing it cleanses sins. Mount Kailash is sacred to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Bon followers; Lake Manasarovar is nearby. Themes: adventure, human endurance, faith and the pull of the sacred.
## Deep dive: The journey
The writer sets out with the aim to see Mount Kailash. The journey goes through remote high-altitude terrain at over 4000 metres. They face a snowstorm, a broken-down vehicle and rough roads. The writer has a headache and nausea because of altitude. The writer describes the people: nomads, shopkeepers, pilgrims. The mountain is a symbol of faith.
## Deep dive: Silk Road and context
The Silk Road was a network of trade routes linking China to the Mediterranean, carrying silk, spices, ideas and religions. In the story the writer travels on a southern route through Tibet. The pilgrims believe that circling Kailash gives merit. The title links history with the present journey.
## Deep dive: Themes and style
Travel writing combines description, humour and observation. The writer is an outsider who observes with curiosity. Themes: endurance, belief and the unity of cultures; nature's vastness; contrasts between modern vehicles and traditional pilgrim walkers. Vocabulary: pilgrimage, terrain, altitude, plateau, nomad.
