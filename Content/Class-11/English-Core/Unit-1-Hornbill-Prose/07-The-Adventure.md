# The Adventure
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 english hornbill the adventure jayant narlikar summary
@study
The Adventure by Jayant Narlikar is a science fiction story about parallel universes. Professor Gangadharpant Gaitonde, a historian, is travelling by train and is about to go to Pune. In a mix-up of time and space he finds himself in a world where the Marathas won the Battle of Panipat in 1761, and the British never ruled India. The world is different: the Maratha empire stretched across, the cities are not as he knew. In this alternate history he finds that the places bear different names. He returns to his familiar world and is unsure whether he dreamed it. The story explores the idea that the universe may split at critical events (the branching of history) and that a person can cross from one branch to another. Themes: history and chance, the many-world hypothesis, science fiction and identity.
## Deep dive: Plot
Professor Gaitonde is a history teacher who enjoys discussing the effect of chance on history. He boards a train, has a strange experience when the train changes direction and he steps into a different Bombay in which Victoria Terminus is replaced by something else; Indian people have different names and the British are not present. The professor meets a friend and a policeman, then realises that he has crossed into a parallel world where history diverged at Panipat. After reading a newspaper he learns that the Marathas defeated the Afghan invaders.
## Deep dive: Ideas
The story relates to quantum mechanics and the many-worlds idea: when an event has more than one outcome, the universe may branch. It shows that small events like a battle can change the entire future, making history contingent. The professor's curiosity and scientific reading of the situation drive the plot. Style: first-person narration within a frame of discussion; science explained simply.
## Deep dive: Themes and evaluation
Themes: chance in history, the idea of parallel universes, the importance of imagination and science fiction as a genre. Humour: his reaction to the new world. The story introduces students to alternate history and to the work of Jayant Narlikar, a leading astrophysicist and writer.
