# The Portrait of a Lady
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english hornbill the portrait of a lady khushwant singh summary
@study
The Portrait of a Lady by Khushwant Singh is a memoir of the writer's grandmother. She was old, fair, wrinkled and walked with a hunched back, always in white, with her silver hair. In the village she took the boy to school, taught him the alphabet and the morning prayer, and spent her time at the temple. When the family moved to the city she lost her role as his teacher and spent time spinning, feeding the sparrows and sitting by the window. The boy went abroad for higher studies; she greeted him at the station and, for the first time, sang to the beat of a drum, a day or two before she died. The sparrows sat around her body in silence. The lesson: love and memory, ageing, the generation gap, and how simple loving goodness lingers.
## Deep dive: Character of the grandmother
She is portrayed as a loving, religious, simple woman. She spent most of the day with prayer and with rosary. She walked the boy to school with a wooden slate and fed stray dogs with stale chapattis. In the city, the boy attended an English school and learned science, and the grandmother could not help. She did not complain. She sought companionship in feeding sparrows.
## Deep dive: Themes and technique
The memoir uses simple, affectionate description and understatement. Themes: the generation gap, the changing relationship between the two as the boy grows, tradition versus modernity, death and the continuity of love. The contrast between her sorrow at his departure and the unusual joy at his return. The sparrows as a symbol of her compassion. The narrator is detached but warm.
## Deep dive: Words and phrases to understand
Hunched, spartan, rosary, serene, quarrelsome, mundane, nostalgic. Simile: she looked like the winter landscape in the mountains. Comparisons: the white clad figure like a winter landscape. Irony and understatement in the description of the death scene.
