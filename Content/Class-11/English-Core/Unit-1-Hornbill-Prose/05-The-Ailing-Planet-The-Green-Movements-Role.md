# The Ailing Planet: the Green Movement’s Role
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english hornbill the ailing planet green movement nani palkhivala
@study
The Ailing Planet by Nani Palkhivala argues that the Earth is sick because of human activities and that the green movement is humanity's attempt to heal it. Early societies lived in harmony with nature; the Industrial Revolution and modern technology led to over-exploitation of resources. The essay speaks of population explosion, pollution, the greenhouse effect, depletion of the ozone layer, loss of forests and species. The green movement began when people recognised that economic growth was damaging the planet and that natural resources are limited. It calls for the idea of sustainable use and preserving the earth for future generations, underlining that the planet is not inherited from ancestors but borrowed from children. It stresses that nature has her limits; the Earth can be saved by individual action, stronger laws and international cooperation. Themes: environmental degradation, responsibility, hope and action.
## Deep dive: Problem of the ailing planet
The earth is under stress from population growth, industrialisation, deforestation, depletion of fossil fuels, pollution of air and water and global warming. The author warns that if the damage continues, future generations will inherit a poorer world. Human greed and short-term gains are the root of the problem. Economic growth without ecological sense is harmful.
## Deep dive: The green movement
The green movement is a worldwide movement that aims to protect the environment. It spread through scientists, writers, activists and common citizens. Its principles: respect for nature, sustainable development, reducing consumption and waste, conserving energy and protecting biodiversity. Recognition came with conferences such as the Earth Summit. Governments introduced laws. The movement connects ecology with economics and ethics.
## Deep dive: Message and style
The essay argues persuasively, using facts, rhetorical statements and an urgent tone. It ends on hope: individuals can contribute through simple lifestyles and cooperation. Key words: ecology, sustainable, degradation, biodiversity, conservation. The idea of stewardship: humans are trustees of the earth.
