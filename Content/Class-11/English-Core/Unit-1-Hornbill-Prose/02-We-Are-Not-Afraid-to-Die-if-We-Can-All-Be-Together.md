# We’re Not Afraid to Die... if We Can All Be Together
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 english hornbill we are not afraid to die if we can all be together summary
@study
This is a true account of a family's round-the-world sailing voyage by Gordon Cook and Alan East. The Cook family sailed on the small boat Wavewalker with their young children, aiming to experience nature and teamwork. In the southern Indian Ocean, the family was hit by a terrible storm with huge waves. The boat was knocked about by gigantic waves; the family endured, using a drogue and careful sailing, and the boat survived. Their courage, unity and attitude: the family chose to face fear together rather than to be afraid of death. Themes: courage, family unity, the power of nature, human endurance, the importance of preparation and faith in each other.
## Deep dive: The voyage
The family's adventure began as a long sea journey. Preparation included knowledge, training and support. They had a sailing boat Wavewalker, and the journey took them across oceans. The sea changed from calm to terrifying. Descriptions show the helplessness of humans before nature and the importance of keeping calm.
## Deep dive: The storm and survival
The storm in the Indian Ocean produced waves as tall as a building and a very strong wind. The family tried different measures, used a drogue to slow the boat, and held together. They passed days of fear but the parents encouraged the children. Eventually the sea calmed and the family continued the journey with a deeper bond.
## Deep dive: Lessons and style
Lessons: be prepared, stay together, do not lose hope, courage is not absence of fear but mastery of fear. The account uses first-person narration, vivid description and short sentences to create tension and suspense. The title expresses the strength drawn from togetherness.
