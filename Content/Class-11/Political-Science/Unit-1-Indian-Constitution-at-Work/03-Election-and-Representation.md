# Election and Representation
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 political science election and representation fptp pr election commission
@study
Elections are the means of choosing representatives in a democracy. Two main systems exist: First Past the Post (FPTP), in which the candidate with the most votes in a constituency wins (used in India for Lok Sabha and state assemblies), and Proportional Representation (PR), in which parties get seats in proportion to their votes. India adopted FPTP because it is simple and gives stable governments. PR is used in India through the single transferable vote for elections to the Rajya Sabha, the President and the Vice President. Universal adult franchise is given by Article 326; the voting age was reduced from 21 to 18 by the 61st Amendment in 1988. The Election Commission of India (Article 324) is an independent body that supervises elections; it was set up on 25 January 1950 (observed as National Voters Day). It consists of the Chief Election Commissioner and other commissioners. Sukumar Sen was the first Chief Election Commissioner. The first general election was held in 1951-52. Electoral laws are the Representation of the People Acts of 1950 and 1951. The Model Code of Conduct guides parties and candidates during elections. Constituencies are demarcated by the Delimitation Commission; seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The Lok Sabha has 543 elected members; the Rajya Sabha has up to 250 members (238 elected by states and 12 nominated by the President). Anglo-Indian nomination was ended by the 104th Amendment (2019). Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) were first used in Kerala in 1982 and in all of India from 2004; VVPAT slips give verification; NOTA (none of the above) was introduced after a 2013 Supreme Court order. Reforms: free and fair elections, curbing money power and criminalisation, disclosure of assets and criminal records, and the anti-defection law. The Women's Reservation Act (106th Amendment, 2023) reserves one-third of seats in the Lok Sabha and state assemblies for women, to take effect after delimitation. Representation means that elected members speak for the people; reservation ensures inclusion of weaker groups. Panchayati Raj institutions have one-third reservation for women (73rd and 74th Amendments).
## Deep dive: FPTP and PR
FPTP is easy to understand and often gives clear winners but a candidate may win without a majority of votes. PR gives a fairer share of seats to parties but may lead to coalitions. India chose FPTP.
## Deep dive: Election Commission
It prepares electoral rolls, schedules elections, enforces the code of conduct, recognises parties and allots symbols. It is independent of the government.
## Deep dive: Reservation and reforms
Reserved seats ensure that disadvantaged groups are represented. Reforms deal with money power, criminals in politics and voter awareness.
