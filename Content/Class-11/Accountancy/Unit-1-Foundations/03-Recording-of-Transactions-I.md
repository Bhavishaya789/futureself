# Recording of Transactions I
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 accountancy journal ledger recording of transactions
@study
Recording begins with the source documents: cash memo, invoice, vouchers, debit note, credit note. Transactions are first recorded in the journal (book of original entry) in chronological order with date, particulars (Dr and Cr accounts), ledger folio (L.F.), debit and credit amount and a narration. Then they are posted to the ledger (book of final entry): each account has a debit and credit side, balanced at the end of the period. Compound journal entries have more than two accounts. Opening entry brings forward balances. Journal proper is for transactions that do not fit special books. Posting from journal to ledger completes the cycle, and the balance of each account is carried down. Capital and revenue items are distinguished; GST is shown as input tax credit and output tax liability.
## Deep dive: Journal
Format: Date | Particulars | L.F. | Dr (Rs) | Cr (Rs). Debit account is written first with Dr, the credit account next with To. Narration explains. Rules: Personal (receiver Dr, giver Cr); Real (comes in Dr, goes out Cr); Nominal (expenses/losses Dr, incomes/gains Cr). Opening entry: Assets A/c Dr. To Liabilities A/c; To Capital A/c. Closing entries transfer nominal accounts to Profit and Loss.
## Deep dive: Ledger and posting
Ledger folio L.F. cross-references the journal and ledger. For the debit account, write the credit account on the debit side as "To" in the particulars, and vice versa with "By". Balance: if debit total exceeds credit, the balance is a debit balance (carried down on the credit side as By balance c/d and brought down on the debit side as To balance b/d). Real and personal accounts are balanced; nominal accounts are closed by transfer.
## Deep dive: Documents and vouchers
Cash memo is the basis for cash sale; invoice is issued by the seller and the same is bill for the buyer; debit note is sent by the buyer to the seller on return of goods; credit note is sent by the seller to the buyer; voucher is the written evidence of cash payment and receipt. Receipts, pay-in slips, cheque counterfoils are also source documents.
## Deep dive: Goods and Services Tax
GST replaced several indirect taxes. CGST and SGST are levied on intra-state supplies; IGST on inter-state supplies. Input tax credit is deducted from output tax. Purchases are recorded net of GST and input GST is a debit to an asset account (Input CGST, Input SGST); sales are credited net with Output GST as a liability. The net amount is paid to the government.
