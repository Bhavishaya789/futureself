# Bills of Exchange
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 accountancy bills of exchange
@study
A bill of exchange is a written, signed, unconditional order from the drawer to the drawee to pay a fixed sum on demand or at a fixed future date to the payee. It has three parties: drawer, drawee, payee; the drawer and payee can be the same. A promissory note is an unconditional promise by the maker to pay; it has two parties. Terms: acceptance, term, maturity date (due date plus three days of grace), endorsement, discounting, retirement, dishonour, noting, renewal. Accounting: the drawer records Bills Receivable (asset); the drawee records Bills Payable (liability). A bill may be retained until maturity, discounted, endorsed, or sent for collection. If dishonoured, the drawee remains liable and the drawer debits the drawee, together with noting charges.
## Deep dive: Features and parties
Bill of exchange: in writing, signed by the drawer, unconditional order, certain sum, to a certain person, stamped. Parties: drawer (creditor who draws), drawee (debtor who accepts), payee. Promissory note: made by the debtor, two parties (maker and payee), no acceptance needed. Difference: promissory note is a promise, a bill is an order; in a note the maker is liable primarily; the note cannot be drawn payable to bearer on demand.
## Deep dive: Maturity and discounting
The due date is the date of maturity plus 3 days of grace. For a bill drawn on 1 January for 3 months, the term ends on 1 April, and the maturity is 4 April. The discount charged by the bank is calculated on the face value for the unexpired period at the bank's rate: Discount = Face value x rate x time. Cash received = face value - discount. A bill can be endorsed to a creditor in settlement of a debt.
## Deep dive: Accounting treatment
In the books of the drawer: Bills Receivable A/c Dr To Debtor on receipt; Bank Dr To Bills Receivable on maturity; if discounted: Bank Dr, Discount Dr To Bills Receivable. In the books of the drawee: Creditor Dr To Bills Payable on acceptance; Bills Payable Dr To Bank on payment. On dishonour, the drawer debits the drawee with the bill amount and noting charges; the drawee reverses the entry. On renewal, interest is added and a new bill is accepted.
## Deep dive: Dishonour, noting and renewal
When the drawee fails to pay on maturity, the bill is dishonoured. Noting by a notary public records the fact and protests the bill. A dishonoured bill can be renewed by cancellation of the old bill and acceptance of a new bill with interest, often after part payment. Retirement: payment before the due date with rebate.
