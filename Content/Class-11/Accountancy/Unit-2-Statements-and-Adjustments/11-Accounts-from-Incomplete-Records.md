# Accounts from Incomplete Records
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 accountancy incomplete records single entry statement of affairs
@study
Accounts from incomplete records (single entry system) are kept by small traders who do not maintain full double entry. The profit is ascertained by the statement of affairs (net worth) method or by the conversion method. Statement of affairs lists assets and liabilities at a date; capital = assets - liabilities. Profit = closing capital - opening capital + drawings - additional capital. In the conversion method, books are converted into double entry: cash book summary, total debtors account, total creditors account, bills accounts, then trading and profit and loss account and balance sheet. Credit purchases = payments to creditors + closing creditors - opening creditors. Credit sales = receipts from debtors + closing debtors - opening debtors. Margin and mark-up: cost + mark-up = selling price; gross profit on sales is margin.
## Deep dive: Features and limitations
Single entry system is a hybrid: personal accounts maintained, real and nominal accounts partly or not at all. Features: no uniform record, cash book usually kept, personal accounts maintained. Limitations: no trial balance, true profit not known, difficult to detect errors and frauds, not accepted by tax authorities. Statement of affairs is not a balance sheet since it is prepared from incomplete records and may include estimates.
## Deep dive: Net worth method
Opening capital = opening assets - opening liabilities. Closing capital = closing assets - closing liabilities. Profit = (closing capital - opening capital) + drawings - fresh capital introduced. Adjust for interest on drawings and any other adjustments after this. Example: opening capital 2,00,000; closing capital 2,60,000; drawings 30,000; additional capital 10,000: profit = 60,000 + 30,000 - 10,000 = 80,000.
## Deep dive: Conversion method
Prepare a summary cash book to find missing figures (cash sales, cash purchases or expenses). Prepare total debtors account: opening debtors + credit sales - cash received - discount - bad debts - returns = closing debtors. Total creditors account: opening creditors + credit purchases - payments - discount - returns = closing creditors. Find credit sales and purchases as balancing figures. Then prepare trading and P and L and the balance sheet.
## Deep dive: Ratios and margin
Mark-up = profit as percentage of cost: selling price = cost x (1 + mark-up). Margin = profit as percentage of sales. If margin is 20% then mark-up is 25%. Using these, find missing sales or cost of goods sold. Stock destroyed or missing can be computed by trading account approach.
