# Financial Statements I
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 accountancy financial statements trading profit and loss account
@study
Financial statements are the final accounts prepared at the end of the accounting period: trading account, profit and loss account and balance sheet. The trading account finds gross profit (net sales - cost of goods sold). Cost of goods sold = opening stock + net purchases + direct expenses - closing stock. The profit and loss account finds net profit = gross profit + other incomes - indirect expenses. Operating profit and net profit are important. Capital and revenue items are separated. Closing entries transfer nominal accounts. Adjustments: closing stock, outstanding and prepaid expenses, accrued and received in advance incomes, depreciation, bad debts, provision for doubtful debts, interest on capital and drawings, and manager commission.
## Deep dive: Trading account
Debit side: opening stock, purchases (less returns), direct expenses (wages, carriage inwards, freight, fuel, power), gross loss. Credit side: sales (less returns), closing stock, gross profit. Gross profit = sales - cost of goods sold. Gross profit ratio = GP/net sales x 100. Closing stock is valued at cost or net realisable value, whichever is lower.
## Deep dive: Profit and loss account
Debit side: indirect expenses (salaries, rent, advertising, depreciation, bad debts, interest paid, carriage outwards, discount allowed, loss on sale of assets). Credit side: gross profit, other incomes (rent received, interest received, commission, discount received). Net profit goes to capital. Operating profit = gross profit - operating expenses; excludes non-operating items.
## Deep dive: Adjustments
Outstanding expenses are added to the expense and shown as a liability. Prepaid expenses are deducted from the expense and shown as an asset. Accrued income is added to income and shown as an asset. Income received in advance is deducted from income and shown as a liability. Depreciation is debited to P and L. Bad debts are deducted from debtors; provision is made on remaining debtors. Interest on capital is an expense for the business and income for the owner; interest on drawings is an income for the business.
## Deep dive: Closing entries
Transfer purchases and expenses to trading account: Trading A/c Dr To Purchases A/c. Sales are transferred: Sales A/c Dr To Trading A/c. Gross profit is transferred to P and L: Trading A/c Dr To P and L A/c. Net profit goes to Capital: P and L A/c Dr To Capital A/c. Drawings are closed to capital.

## Formulas

Gross profit | Net sales - COGS | Trading A/c
Net profit | GP + Other income - Indirect exp | P and L
Gross profit ratio | GP / Net sales x 100 | %
Net profit ratio | NP / Net sales x 100 | %
Operating profit | GP - Operating exp | Excludes non-operating
