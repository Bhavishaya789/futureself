# Depreciation, Provisions and Reserves
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 accountancy depreciation provisions reserves
@study
Depreciation is the decrease in the value of a fixed asset due to use, passage of time or obsolescence. Causes: wear and tear, expiry of legal rights, obsolescence, abnormal factors. Methods: straight line method (SLM) — fixed amount each year: (Cost - Scrap)/Life; written down value method (WDV) — a fixed percentage on the reducing balance. Depreciation is a non-cash expense debited to Profit and Loss and credited to the asset account (or provision for depreciation account). Provision is a charge against profit for a known liability whose amount is uncertain (bad debts, discount, depreciation). Reserves are appropriations of profit for general or specific purposes: general reserve, capital reserve, revenue reserve, secret reserve, specific reserve.
## Deep dive: Straight line and WDV
SLM: annual depreciation = (Cost - Residual value)/Useful life; rate = depreciation/cost x 100. Book value falls evenly. WDV: depreciation each year = rate x opening book value; it declines year to year; scrap value is not deducted. Example: machinery Rs 1,00,000, scrap Rs 10,000, life 5 years: SLM = 18,000 a year. WDV at 10 per cent: year 1: 10,000; year 2: 9,000; book value year 2 = 81,000. Change in method requires adjustments with prospective effect (AS-6).
## Deep dive: Accounting treatment
Method 1 (charge to asset): Depreciation A/c Dr To Machinery A/c; asset appears at reduced value. Method 2 (provision for depreciation): Depreciation A/c Dr To Provision for Depreciation A/c; the asset stays at cost. At year end, Depreciation A/c is closed by transfer to Profit and Loss A/c. On sale of an asset: calculate profit or loss as sale price - book value. Profit on sale credited to P and L; loss debited. Transfer from the asset account or sale account appears via Asset Disposal A/c.
## Deep dive: Provisions
Provision for doubtful debts: P&L Dr; Provision A/c Cr; presented as a deduction from debtors. Provision for discount on debtors is made on (debtors - bad debts - provision for doubtful debts). Provision for depreciation, repairs, taxation, and discounts. A provision is a charge against profit, whereas a reserve is an appropriation of profit. Provisions are created for known liabilities; reserves are for strengthening the financial position.
## Deep dive: Reserves
General reserve strengthens finances; specific reserves have a purpose (dividend equalisation, workmen compensation). Capital reserve comes from capital profits (not distributable as dividend); revenue reserve comes from revenue profits. Secret reserve is not shown on the balance sheet. Reserve fund and sinking fund. Creating reserve: Profit and Loss Appropriation A/c Dr To Reserve A/c.
