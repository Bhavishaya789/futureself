# Trial Balance and Rectification of Errors
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 accountancy trial balance rectification errors
@study
A trial balance is a statement of debit and credit balances of ledger accounts at a date to check arithmetical accuracy. Total debits equal total credits. Accounts with debit balances: assets, expenses, losses, drawings. Credit balances: liabilities, capital, incomes, gains. Prepared by the total method, balance method or total-cum-balance. Errors not disclosed by the trial balance: errors of omission, commission (same account type), principle, compensating errors. Disclosed errors: one-sided errors, wrong totals, wrong balances. A suspense account holds the difference until errors are found. Rectification is done by one-day or journal entries.
## Deep dive: Preparing a trial balance
Take the balances from the ledger; place debit balances in the debit column and credit in the credit column; total each. Items: Capital, Drawings, Sales, Purchases, Returns, Debtors, Creditors, Stock (opening), Expenses and incomes, Loans, Fixed assets, Cash and bank. Closing stock is not shown in the trial balance since it appears in the adjustments.
## Deep dive: Types of errors
Errors of omission (complete or partial), commission (wrong posting, wrong total), principle (capital/revenue confusion), compensating (offsetting errors). Errors affecting the trial balance: one-sided posting, wrong balancing, wrong totalling, mismatch between debit and credit amounts. These are disclosed. Errors not affecting the agreement: omission, principle, commission with correct double entry, compensating.
## Deep dive: Suspense account
If the trial balance does not tally, the difference is put in the suspense account on the shorter side. After finding errors, rectify through suspense: for one-sided errors an entry with Suspense A/c; for two-sided errors a single journal entry that corrects the accounts. When all are found, the suspense account is closed.
## Deep dive: Rectification of errors
Before preparing final accounts: rectify with Suspense as needed. After the final accounts are prepared: affect profit, so use Profit and Loss Adjustment A/c. Steps: find the wrong entry, the correct entry, and pass the corrective entry. Example: Furniture purchased Rs 5,000 debited to purchases: Furniture A/c Dr 5,000 To Purchases A/c 5,000.
## Hindi saransh
Saaranshpatra (trial balance) mein debit aur credit shesh ka milan hota hai. Ashuddhiyan: chhoot, kamishan, siddhant, pratikarak. Antar hone par suspense khata. Shodhan prvishti dwara.
> Errors of principle and omission do not affect the trial balance agreement.
