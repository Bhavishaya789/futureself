# Bank Reconciliation Statement
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 accountancy bank reconciliation statement
@study
A bank reconciliation statement (BRS) reconciles the balance as per the cash book (bank column) with the balance as per the pass book (bank statement) on a given date. Differences arise from items recorded in one but not in the other. Causes: cheques issued but not yet presented for payment, cheques deposited but not yet collected, direct deposits by customers, direct payments by bank (standing instructions, insurance premium), interest and bank charges, dishonour of cheques, and errors. Start from the favourable or overdraft balance and add/subtract each item to get the other. The BRS is prepared periodically to detect errors and frauds and is not a part of the ledger.
## Deep dive: Reasons for difference
Timing differences: cheques issued but not presented (cash book credit, not in the pass book); cheques deposited but not yet credited by the bank; bank charges, interest on overdraft, collection charges; direct credits like dividend and interest; direct debits like insurance or loan instalments; cheques dishonoured (bank debits the account); errors by the bank or business.
## Deep dive: Preparing the statement
Starting with balance as per cash book (debit balance): Add: cheques issued but not presented; interest credited by bank. Less: cheques deposited but not collected; bank charges; direct payments. Result: balance as per pass book. If starting from an overdraft as per cash book (credit balance), reverse the adding and subtracting. When starting from the pass book, the signs are opposite to those starting from the cash book.
## Deep dive: Adjusted cash book
Items already known to the bank but not recorded in the cash book (interest, charges, dishonour, direct payments) require corrections in the cash book first (Adjusted cash book) and then the remaining items form the BRS. The balance of the adjusted cash book reconciles with the passbook after timing items.
## Deep dive: Why BRS is needed
Detects errors, frauds, delays and bank charges, and ensures the correct bank balance; it is internal control and gives true cash position. It is not a ledger account and does not alter the books.

## Advanced
P: Explain the causes of difference between cash book and pass book and the need for BRS.
A: Timing items (cheques not presented/collected), bank charges/interest, direct credit/debit, dishonour and errors. BRS detects errors and frauds, shows the true balance, gives internal control and reduces delay.
