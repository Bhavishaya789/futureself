# Emerging Modes of Business
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies emerging modes of business e-business outsourcing
@study
E-business covers all business activities through electronic means; e-commerce is online buying and selling. Scope: B2B, B2C, C2C, intra-B, P2P. Benefits: speed, low costs, wider access, convenience. Limitations: low personal touch, security, system failure. Online transactions: registration, placing order, payment (cash on delivery, cheque, credit/debit card, net banking, digital cash). Security and safety concerns: privacy, phishing, hacking. Outsourcing: contracting out non-core activities (BPO, KPO). Scope: contract manufacturing, contract research, contract sales. Need: focus on core, cost, skill, speed. Concerns: loss of confidentiality, lack of control, ethical concerns.
## Deep dive: E-business and e-commerce
E-business has wider scope than e-commerce: includes buying, selling, customer service, logistics, supply chain and collaborating with partners. Types: B2B (suppliers and manufacturers), B2C (retailers to customers), C2C (OLX), B2G, intra-B. Differences with traditional business: ease of formation, low investment, convenience, speed, global reach, impersonal nature, resource requirements. Online transactions steps: register, place order, make payment, delivery, post-sale service.
## Deep dive: Payment and security
Payment: cash on delivery, cheque, net banking, credit or debit cards, digital cash (e-wallet), UPI. Security: encryption, firewalls, passwords, digital signatures, SSL. Risks: transaction risks (non-delivery), data storage and transmission risk, risk of threat to intellectual property, privacy risks. Safeguards: secure websites, digital certificates, authentication. IT Act 2000 provides legal recognition.
## Deep dive: Outsourcing
Outsourcing means entrusting non-core activities to outside agencies. Features: long-term contract, reduces cost, focus on core competence. Forms: BPO (call centres, data entry), KPO (research, analytics), contract manufacturing. Need: focus on core, reduce costs, access to skills, speed. Concerns: lack of confidentiality, dependence on vendor, ethical considerations, loss of jobs in developed countries.
