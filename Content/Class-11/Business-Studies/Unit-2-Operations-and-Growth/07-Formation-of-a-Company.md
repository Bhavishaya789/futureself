# Formation of a Company
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 business studies formation of a company promotion incorporation prospectus
@study
Stages of company formation: promotion, incorporation, subscription of capital (public company), commencement of business (earlier certificate of commencement). Promotion: identification of business opportunity, feasibility studies, name approval, fixing of signatories to the memorandum, appointment of professionals, preparation of documents. Incorporation: documents to Registrar of Companies: memorandum of association (name, registered office, objects, liability, capital clauses), articles of association, consent of directors, agreement, declaration, address proof, fee payment. Certificate of incorporation is conclusive evidence. Subscription of capital: SEBI approval, prospectus, application, allotment, minimum subscription (90%), share certificates. Prospectus content and statement in lieu of prospectus. Private company does not issue a prospectus.
## Deep dive: Promotion and incorporation
Promoter conducts promotional functions: opportunity, feasibility (technical, financial, economic), name (reserved with Registrar), signatories, professionals (mercantile bankers, auditors), documents. Memorandum clauses: name, registered office, objects, liability, capital, association (subscription). Articles: internal rules about directors, shares, meetings, dividends. Incorporation documents: memorandum, articles, agreement, declaration, address proof. Registrar issues certificate of incorporation, and Corporate Identity Number (CIN).
## Deep dive: Capital subscription
Public company: SEBI guidelines; prospectus invites public; contents: name of company, issue details, capital, objects, directors; Letter of allotment, application money, allotment, calls. Minimum subscription is 90% of issue. Allotment within 60 days. Money received kept in escrow with a scheduled bank. Private company can start after incorporation; public company after complying subscription rules.
## Deep dive: Documents
Memorandum of association: charter, defines relationship with the outside world, can be altered only per the Act. Articles of association: internal rules. Prospectus: document inviting the public (any notice, circular). Statement in lieu of prospectus: filed by a public company not issuing a prospectus. Red herring prospectus. Differences: memorandum is subordinate to the Act and articles to the memorandum. Doctrine of ultra vires and constructive notice.
