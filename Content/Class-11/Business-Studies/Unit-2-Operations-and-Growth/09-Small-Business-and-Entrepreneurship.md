# Small Business and Entrepreneurship
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies small business and entrepreneurship MSME start-up
@study
MSMED Act 2006 classified enterprises by investment in plant and machinery; revised in 2020 with composite criteria: micro (investment up to Rs 1 crore, turnover up to Rs 5 crore), small (up to Rs 10 crore, Rs 50 crore), medium (up to Rs 50 crore, Rs 250 crore). Role: employment, balanced regional development, equitable distribution, mobilisation of resources, export, self-reliance. Problems: finance, raw material, managerial skills, labour, marketing, infrastructure, technology, competition. Government assistance: Entrepreneurship Development Programme, NSIC, SIDBI, DIC, Khadi and Village Industries Commission, Mudra, Start-up India, Stand-up India. Entrepreneurship: process of seeking opportunities, taking risks, managing resources. Entrepreneur vs manager; characteristics: initiative, risk taking, innovation, vision; process of entrepreneurial development.
## Deep dive: Small business
Cottage industries (household), village industries, small-scale industries. Enterprises can be manufacturing or service. Role: employment generation (second to agriculture), contribution to GDP and exports, dispersal of industries, use of local resources. Problems: lack of funds, raw materials, finding labour, marketing, outdated technology.
## Deep dive: Government support
Schemes: Credit Guarantee Fund, Mudra loans (Shishu, Kishor, Tarun), Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme, Start-up India (tax relief, simplified compliance), Make in India. Institutions: NSIC (marketing support, raw material), SIDBI (finance), KVIC, DICs (district level), technology development centres, export promotion councils, SISI. Policies: reservation of items, tax holidays, subsidised power, priority sector lending.
## Deep dive: Entrepreneurship
Entrepreneur: initiative, risk taking, innovation, vision, hardworking. Entrepreneurship is a process; entrepreneurs vs managers: motive, status, risk, rewards. Steps: idea, opportunity, resources, plan, launch. Entrepreneurial development process: identification of opportunity, project report, resource mobilisation, implementation. Theories: Schumpeter innovation theory. Women entrepreneurs: SHGs, Mahila Udyam. Intrapreneurship.
