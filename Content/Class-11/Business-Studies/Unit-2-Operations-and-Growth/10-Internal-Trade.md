# Internal Trade
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies internal trade wholesale retail GST
@study
Internal trade is the buying and selling of goods and services within the boundaries of a country. Wholesale trade: purchase in bulk from producers and sale to retailers; services to producers (risk bearing, financing, storage, grading, market information) and retailers (credit, goods availability, small lots, specialised knowledge, transport). Retail trade: sale to ultimate consumers. Types: itinerant (hawkers, peddlers, street traders), fixed shop (general stores, speciality stores, supermarkets, department stores, chain stores, mail order houses, consumer cooperative stores, vending machines, online). Chambers of commerce (FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM). Goods and Services Tax (GST) 2017 replaced multiple indirect taxes; components CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST. Documents: invoice, debit and credit note, lorry receipt, railway receipt, bill of lading. Terms of trade: cash on delivery, FOB, C.I.F., E.O.E., and 3 other terms.
## Deep dive: Wholesale and retail
Services of wholesaler to manufacturer: bulk purchase, risk bearing, financing, warehousing, expert advice, marketing. To retailers: availability, marketing support, credit, specialised knowledge. Retailer to consumer: availability of goods, new products, after-sales service, credit. Retailer to wholesaler/manufacturer: distribution, personal selling, enabling large-scale operations, market information, risk. Large-scale retailers: department stores (one roof), chain stores (multiple shops), supermarkets (self-service), mail order, vending machine, hypermarket.
## Deep dive: Types of retailers and GST
Itinerant: hawkers, cheap jacks, peddlers, market traders. Fixed shop: general stores, speciality shops, street stalls, second-hand goods shops, consumer cooperative stores. GST: destination-based tax on supply of goods and services; one nation one tax; input tax credit; slabs 5, 12, 18 and 28 percent; GSTN; registration for turnover above the threshold. Benefits: removes cascading effect, simpler compliance, uniform prices.
## Deep dive: Documents and terms
Documents: invoice, debit note, credit note, lorry receipt (LR), railway receipt (RR), way bill (e-way bill), bill of lading. Terms: Cash on delivery (COD), cash discount, trade discount, quantity discount, FOB (free on board), CIF (cost, insurance, freight), E and O.E. (errors and omissions excepted), payment, delivery, return, advice. Chambers: FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM represent business interests.
