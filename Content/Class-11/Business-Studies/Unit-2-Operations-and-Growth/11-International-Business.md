# International Business
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 business studies international business export import WTO documents
@study
International business covers commercial transactions across national borders: exports, imports, FDI, licensing, franchising, joint ventures. Differences from domestic business: nationality of buyers and sellers, mobility of factors, customer heterogeneity, differences in business practices, political factors, currency, trade regulations. Benefits to nations: earning foreign exchange, more efficient use of resources, improving growth prospects, increased standard of living. Benefits to firms: higher profits, increased capacity utilisation, prospects for growth, way out to intense domestic competition, improved business vision. Modes of entry: exporting and importing, contract manufacturing, licensing and franchising, joint ventures, wholly owned subsidiaries. Export-import procedure: export (enquiry, quotation, order, letter of credit, production, pre-shipment inspection, excise clearance, insurance, customs, bill of lading, invoice, payment); import (trade enquiry, procurement of licence, obtaining foreign exchange, placing order, shipment, customs clearance, payment). Documents: commercial invoice, certificate of origin, bill of lading, mate’s receipt, packing list. WTO: formed 1995, principles of non-discrimination.
## Deep dive: Nature and benefits
International business includes merchandise exports and imports, service exports and imports, licensing and franchising, foreign investments. Differences: nationalities, factor mobility, markets, business systems, political and legal environment, currency, documentation. Reasons: availability of resources, comparative advantage, trade surplus. Barriers: tariffs, quotas, subsidies, non-tariff barriers.
## Deep dive: Export and import procedure
Export: enquiry, receipt of order, assessing importer’s creditworthiness, obtaining export licence, obtaining pre-shipment finance, production, pre-shipment inspection, excise clearance, obtaining certificate of origin, reservation of shipping space, packing and forwarding, insurance, customs clearance, mate’s receipt, payment of freight, issue of invoice, preparing of bill of exchange and presentation to bank. Import: trade enquiry, procurement of import licence, obtaining foreign exchange, placing order, letter of credit, arrangement of finance, receipt of shipment advice, retirement of import documents, arrival of goods, customs clearance, payment of customs duty, warehousing and release of goods.
## Deep dive: Documents and WTO
Export documents: invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, certificate of inspection, mate’s receipt, shipping bill, airway bill. Import: bill of entry, import licence, letter of credit. Letter of credit: bank’s guarantee of payment. WTO: replaced GATT in 1995, headquartered in Geneva, objectives: trade liberalisation, dispute settlement; principles: non-discrimination, MFN, national treatment. Trade policy bodies and export promotion: EXIM Bank, Export Promotion Councils, Special Economic Zones (SEZ), EPZ, 100% EOUs.
