# Social Responsibilities of Business and Business Ethics
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies social responsibilities of business and business ethics
@study
Social responsibility is the obligation of businessmen to pursue policies and make decisions that are desirable in terms of the values and objectives of society. Arguments for: justification for existence, long-term interest of the firm, avoidance of government regulation, maintenance of society, availability of resources, better environment. Against: violation of profit maximisation, burden on consumers, lack of social skills, lack of broad public support. Social responsibility towards shareholders, workers, consumers, government and community. Business and environmental protection: pollution (air, water, land, noise); causes, need for protection. Business ethics: values and principles guiding conduct; elements: top management commitment, publication of a code, establishment of compliance mechanisms, involving everyone, measuring results.
## Deep dive: Social responsibility
Concept: obligation to society; Bowen and Davis defined it. Levels of responsibility: economic, legal, ethical, discretionary (Carroll). Towards shareholders (fair return), workers (fair wages, safety), consumers (quality, fair price), government (taxes), community (health, education, environment). Kinds: economic, legal, ethical, discretionary responsibilities. Reasons: justification for existence, long-term interest, avoidance of government regulation, maintenance of society, availability of resources.
## Deep dive: Environmental protection
Pollution: air (industries, vehicles), water (effluents, sewage), land (solid waste, pesticides), noise. Need: reduction of health hazards, lower costs, avoid legal action, public image, ecological balance. Role of business: Central Pollution Control Board, Environment Protection Act 1986, polluter pays principle, corporate social responsibility (CSR) under Companies Act 2013 (2% of average net profit for companies with net worth Rs 500 crore, turnover Rs 1000 crore or profit Rs 5 crore).
## Deep dive: Business ethics
Ethics deal with right and wrong. Elements: top management commitment, publication of code, compliance mechanism, involving everyone at all levels, measuring results. Concept: honesty, fairness, respect. Ethics pay in the long run: goodwill, customer loyalty, employee morale. Unethical practices: adulteration, hoarding, misleading advertisement, pollution, child labour.
