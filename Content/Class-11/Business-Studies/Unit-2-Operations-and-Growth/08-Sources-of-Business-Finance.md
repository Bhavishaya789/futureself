# Sources of Business Finance
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 business studies sources of business finance equity shares debentures bank loan
@study
Business finance is the money required for carrying out business activities. Classification: by period (long-term, medium-term, short-term), by ownership (owner funds, borrowed funds), by source of generation (internal, external). Sources: retained earnings, trade credit, factoring, lease financing, public deposits, commercial paper, issue of shares (equity and preference), debentures, loans from commercial banks, financial institutions, international financing (commercial banks, ADR, GDR, FCCB, FDI). Equity shares: ownership, voting rights, dividends. Preference shares: preferential dividend and repayment; types cumulative, non-cumulative, participating, convertible, redeemable. Debentures: creditorship, interest, types secured, unsecured, convertible, non-convertible, registered, bearer. Factoring: bills receivable sold at discount. Trade credit and bank overdraft for working capital.
## Deep dive: Classification and owner funds
Owner funds: equity share capital, preference share capital, retained earnings (ploughing back of profits), ordinary shares. Equity shares: permanent capital, residual claim, voting rights, no fixed dividend, more risk but potential gains. Preference shares: fixed rate, priority over equity at winding up, no voting rights (except in certain cases). Retained earnings: no cost of floatation, no dilution, may cause dissatisfaction among shareholders.
## Deep dive: Borrowed funds
Loans from commercial banks: short-term (cash credit, overdraft), term loans. Public deposits (companies invite deposits from the public for 1 to 3 years, RBI regulations). Trade credit: credit by suppliers (credit period 30-60 days). Commercial paper: unsecured promissory note by large firms, 15 days to 1 year, discounted. Factoring: sale of receivables (factor pays 80%), with or without recourse. Lease: lessor and lessee; operating and finance lease. Debentures: interest fixed, credit risk; redemption.
## Deep dive: Choice of source
Factors: cost (interest vs dividend), financial strength, tax benefits (interest is deductible), flexibility, risk profile, control (equity dilutes), nature of the enterprise, purpose and time period. Global sources: ADR (American Depository Receipts), GDR, FCCB, ECB, FDI, FII. Long-term finance should match long-term assets (matching principle). Trading on equity: borrowing at lower cost than return on capital.
