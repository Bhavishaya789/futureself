# Forms of Business Organisation
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 business studies forms of business organisation sole proprietorship partnership HUF cooperative company
@study
Forms: sole proprietorship, joint Hindu family business (HUF), partnership (Indian Partnership Act 1932), cooperative society (Cooperative Societies Act 1912), joint stock company (Companies Act 2013). Sole proprietor: single owner, unlimited liability, no separate legal entity. HUF: karta, coparceners, Hindu Law, unlimited liability of karta. Partnership: minimum 2, maximum 50 (Companies Act), partnership deed, kinds of partners (active, sleeping, secret, nominal, partner by estoppel), unlimited joint liability. LLP: limited liability, separate legal entity. Cooperative society: minimum 10 members, one member one vote. Company: private (min 2, max 200 members) and public (min 7, no limit), separate legal entity, perpetual succession, limited liability. Choice depends on cost, liability, continuity, control, capital, legal formalities.
## Deep dive: Sole proprietorship, HUF, partnership
Sole proprietorship merits: quick decisions, secrecy, direct incentive, easy formation; limits: limited resources, limited life, unlimited liability. HUF: formation by birth in the family, minor coparceners, continuity unaffected by the death of karta. Partnership: agreement oral or written; profit-sharing; mutual agency; types by duration (at will, particular), liability (general, limited). Registration optional. Merits: easy formation, balanced judgement, more funds, sharing of risks. Limits: unlimited liability, instability, lack of public confidence, limited resources.
## Deep dive: Cooperative society and company
Cooperative: voluntary association, democratic management, service motive, limited liability; types: consumer, producer, marketing, farmers, credit, cooperative housing. Company: formation by incorporation; features: artificial legal person, separate legal entity, formation, perpetual succession, control, liability, common seal, risk bearing, ownership and management separation. Private company: restrictions on transfer of shares, minimum paid-up capital not mandatory, cannot invite public. Public company: free transfer, invites public subscription. One Person Company (OPC) permitted by the 2013 Act.
## Deep dive: Choice of form
Factors: cost and ease of formation, liability, continuity, management and control, capital considerations, nature of business. Sole proprietorship suits small retail; partnership suits professions; company suits large capital. Comparative merits: company has the largest funds and stability, but most legal formalities and least secrecy.
