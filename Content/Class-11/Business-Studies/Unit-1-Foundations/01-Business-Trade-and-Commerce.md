# Business, Trade and Commerce
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies business trade and commerce
@study
Business is an economic activity involving regular production or purchase and sale of goods and services to earn profit. Features: economic activity, production or procurement, sale or exchange, dealings in goods and services, profit earning, recurring transactions, uncertainty of return, risk. Business, profession and employment differ in qualification, reward, risk, capital and transfer of interest. Industry: primary (extractive, genetic), secondary (manufacturing, construction), tertiary. Commerce = trade + auxiliaries to trade (transport, banking, insurance, warehousing, communication, advertising). Trade: internal (wholesale, retail) and external (import, export, entrepot). Objectives: profit, market standing, innovation, productivity, social responsibility. Business risks arise from natural, human, economic causes.
## Deep dive: Business, profession, employment
Business: no minimum qualification, profit as reward, risk, capital needed, transfer of interest with consent. Profession: specialised knowledge, professional fees, code of conduct, membership of an association (doctors, lawyers, chartered accountants). Employment: contract of service, wages or salary, little risk, no or little capital. Objectives of business: profit is necessary but not the only one; market standing, innovation, productivity, social responsibility.
## Deep dive: Industry and commerce
Primary: extractive (farming, mining, fishing, hunting) and genetic (nurseries, cattle breeding). Secondary: manufacturing (analytic, synthetic, processing, assembling) and construction. Tertiary: banking, transport, insurance, communication. Auxiliaries to trade: transport removes hindrance of place, banking of finance, insurance of risk, warehousing of time, advertising of knowledge. Trade: wholesale and retail. Hindrances to trade: person (trade), place, time, finance, risk, knowledge.
## Deep dive: Business risk
Risk is the possibility of loss. Causes: natural (flood, fire, earthquake), human (dishonest employees, strikes), economic (price fluctuations, competition), other (government policy, technological change). Nature: uncertainty, essential part of business, degree depends on nature and size, profit is the reward for risk. Insurable and uninsurable risks. Business is a part of the larger concept of economic activities including non-economic activities done for love and sentiments.
