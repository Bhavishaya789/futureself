# Private, Public and Global Enterprises
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies private public and global enterprises PSU MNC joint venture
@study
Private sector: owned by private individuals. Public sector: owned by government; forms: departmental undertakings (railways, post and telegraph), statutory corporations (LIC, ONGC, Air India earlier), government companies (BHEL, SAIL, HAL). Features of public enterprise: ownership, finance, management, public welfare. Changing role: liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation (LPG, 1991); disinvestment, navratna and maharatna status, MOU. Global enterprises: multinational corporations (MNCs) with branches in many countries, large size, foreign collaborations, advanced technology. Joint ventures: equity-based and contractual. Public private partnership (PPP): BOT and build-own-operate-transfer.
## Deep dive: Public sector forms
Departmental undertaking: part of a ministry, financed through the budget, audited by the CAG, no separate legal entity. Statutory corporation: set up by Act of Parliament (LIC, RBI, FCI), separate legal entity, autonomous, own funds. Government company: at least 51% paid-up capital with government under Companies Act (BHEL, SAIL, GAIL). Merits: financial resources, social benefit, balanced regional development. Limits: bureaucracy, delayed decisions, political interference, lack of accountability.
## Deep dive: Changing role and PSU reforms
New industrial policy 1991: reduced reserved industries (from 17 to 3), disinvestment, restructuring of sick units, MOU between government and PSU for performance targets. Navratna (nine) and Maharatna (larger) PSUs given greater autonomy. Privatisation: denationalisation, franchising, contracting out. Disinvestment of shares to improve efficiency and revenue.
## Deep dive: Global enterprises and PPP
MNCs features: huge capital and assets, foreign collaboration, advanced technology, product innovation and marketing strategies, expansion of markets, centralised control. Examples: Coca-Cola, Unilever, Nestle. Joint venture: Maruti Suzuki. Benefits to India: technology, capital, employment, exports. Public private partnership: BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer), BOOT; applied in roads and airports.
