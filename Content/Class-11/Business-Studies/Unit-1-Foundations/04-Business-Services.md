# Business Services
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 business studies business services banking insurance communication transport warehousing
@study
Services are intangible, inseparable, perishable, involve the consumer, lack ownership and are heterogeneous. Types: business services (banking, insurance, transportation, warehousing, communication), social services, personal services. Banking: commercial banks (public, private, foreign, regional rural), cooperative banks, specialised banks, central bank (RBI). Services: ATM, debit card, credit card, internet banking, RTGS, NEFT, UPI. Insurance: principles (utmost good faith, insurable interest, indemnity, contribution, subrogation, causa proxima); types: life, health, fire, marine, motor. Postal services (speed post, e-post) and telecom (cellular, broadband). Transport modes, warehousing types (private, public, bonded, cooperative, government), functions (consolidation, break-bulk, stock holding, pricing).
## Deep dive: Banking and insurance
Bank accounts: savings, current, fixed deposit, recurring. E-banking: ATM, debit and credit cards, RTGS (above Rs 2 lakh), NEFT, UPI, mobile banking. Insurance: contract in which insurer promises to indemnify insured for loss against premium. Principles: utmost good faith (uberrimae fidei), insurable interest, indemnity (not for life), contribution, subrogation, causa proxima. Types: life (whole life, endowment, term, money-back), health, fire, marine (hull, cargo, freight), motor.
## Deep dive: Communication and transport
Postal: mail (letters), parcel, money order, speed post, e-post, banking and insurance via post offices, PIN. Telecommunication: cellular, radio paging, fixed line, cable, VSAT, DTH, internet. Transport: road, rail, air, water. Advantages of rail: heavy goods, long distances; air: speed; road: door-to-door; water: low cost. Freight and passenger types.
## Deep dive: Warehousing
Warehouse is a building to store goods. Types: private, public, bonded, cooperative, government (FCI, CWC, SWC). Functions: consolidation, break-bulk, stock holding, value addition, price stabilisation, transit, financing (warehouse receipt as collateral), risk bearing. Importance: removes hindrance of time; helps price stability; supports exports.
