# Societal Impacts
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 informatics practices societal impacts digital footprint cyber safety ewaste
@study
Information technology affects society in many ways, and responsible use matters. A digital footprint is the trail of data we leave online; an active footprint is data shared deliberately (posts, emails) and a passive footprint is data collected without our active input (browsing history, IP address, cookies). Net etiquette (netiquette) means polite behaviour online: be respectful, avoid sharing false news, respect privacy and copyright. Communication etiquette covers email, chat and social media. Cyberbullying is harassment online. Cyber crime includes hacking, phishing (fake messages to steal details), identity theft, ransomware, spam, trolling and cyberstalking. Malware includes viruses, worms, Trojan horses, spyware and ransomware; antivirus software, firewalls and updates protect systems. Safe practices include strong passwords, two-factor authentication, not clicking unknown links and using secure HTTPS websites. Intellectual property rights protect creations: copyright for creative works, patents for inventions and trademarks for brand names. Plagiarism is presenting another person's work as your own. Licences: open source and Creative Commons allow sharing with conditions; free and open source software (FOSS) is widely used. The Information Technology Act, 2000 is the main Indian law on cyber crime and electronic records, amended in 2008. Digital property, cyber law, privacy and data protection are key issues; India passed the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in 2023. E-waste is discarded electronic goods such as computers and phones; it contains toxic metals like lead and mercury, and should be recycled, reused or disposed of properly. Green computing reduces energy use. Technology also raises gender and disability issues, so assistive technologies and accessibility are important, and the digital divide is the gap between those with and without access. Health issues include eye strain, repetitive strain injury, poor posture and addiction. Indian initiatives include Digital India, DigiLocker, UPI and CERT-In.
## Deep dive: Digital footprint and etiquette
Everything we post can be traced. Active footprints are deliberate; passive ones are collected silently. Privacy settings reduce exposure. Think before posting and respect others online.
## Deep dive: Cyber crime and law
Phishing tricks people with fake sites. Hacking is unauthorised access. Identity theft misuses personal data. The IT Act, 2000 defines offences and penalties. Report cyber crime at cybercrime.gov.in or the national helpline 1930.
## Deep dive: E-waste and health
E-waste contains harmful substances; recycling recovers metals. The 3 Rs are reduce, reuse and recycle. Good ergonomics and breaks protect health. Accessibility helps persons with disabilities.
