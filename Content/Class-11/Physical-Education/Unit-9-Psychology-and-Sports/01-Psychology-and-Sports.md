# Psychology and Sports
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 physical education psychology sports personality motivation adherence
@study
Psychology is the scientific study of behaviour and mental processes. Sports psychology applies psychological principles to sports to improve performance and well-being. Its aims are to understand how psychological factors affect performance, to teach mental skills, to handle stress and to enhance enjoyment. Personality is the sum of the characteristics that make a person unique. Carl Jung classified people as introverts (inward looking, quiet) and extroverts (outgoing, sociable); an ambivert has both. Sheldon's somatotypes link body build to temperament: endomorph (round, relaxed), mesomorph (muscular, energetic) and ectomorph (thin, introverted). The Big Five traits are openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness and neuroticism. Personality and sports: team sport players are often extroverts; individual sport players are often more introverted, and personality affects choice of sport. Motivation is the force that energises and directs behaviour toward a goal. Intrinsic motivation comes from within (enjoyment, satisfaction); extrinsic motivation comes from outside (rewards, medals, praise). Techniques to motivate: set clear goals, give feedback and rewards, use praise, create a positive environment, vary training and involve athletes in decisions. Achievement motivation is the drive to succeed. Exercise adherence is continuing a programme of exercise; factors that support it include enjoyment, social support, convenience, goal setting and seeing results; barriers include lack of time, injury and lack of facilities. Strategies to improve adherence: set realistic goals, choose enjoyable activities, exercise with friends, keep records and reward progress. Mental skills include goal setting, imagery, relaxation, self-talk and concentration. Anxiety and arousal affect performance; the inverted-U hypothesis says moderate arousal gives best performance. Aggression in sports can be hostile or instrumental. Team cohesion improves performance. Sportspersons should learn to handle pressure and failure and develop confidence.
## Deep dive: Personality
Personality is shaped by heredity and environment. Jung's types and Sheldon's body types are early theories. Traits are stable patterns. The Big Five model is widely used.
## Deep dive: Motivation
Intrinsic motivation is long lasting; extrinsic can help but may reduce interest if overused. Goals should be specific, measurable and achievable. Coaches use feedback and encouragement.
## Deep dive: Exercise adherence
Many people stop exercise within months. Enjoyment, support and planning improve adherence. Time management and variety help. Health benefits motivate long-term participation.
