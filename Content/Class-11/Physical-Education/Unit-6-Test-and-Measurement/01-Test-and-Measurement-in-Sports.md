# Test and Measurement in Sports
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 physical education test measurement evaluation harvard step test rockport barrow
@study
A test is a tool or procedure used to collect information about a quality; measurement is the process of collecting numerical data using a test; evaluation is interpreting the data to judge progress or worth. Tests should be valid (measure what they intend to), reliable (give consistent results), objective (free of personal bias), norms-based and practical. Importance: to select talent, classify students, assess progress, motivate and plan training. Fitness tests include the 50 m dash for speed, the standing broad jump for explosive leg power, sit and reach for flexibility, sit-ups or partial curl-ups for abdominal strength, push-ups for upper body endurance and the 600 m run or walk for cardiovascular endurance. The Harvard Step Test measures cardiovascular fitness: a subject steps up and down on a bench (20 inches high for boys and 18 inches for girls) 30 times per minute for 5 minutes to a metronome set at 120 beats per minute, then sits and pulse is counted at 1 to 1.5, 2 to 2.5 and 3 to 3.5 minutes. Fitness Index = duration of exercise in seconds x 100 divided by 2 x sum of the three pulse counts. The Rockport One-Mile Walk test estimates VO2 max by timing a one-mile walk and measuring heart rate at the end. The Barrow Three-Item General Motor Ability test uses standing broad jump, zig-zag run and medicine ball put. The Johnson-Metheny test measures motor ability. The Rikli and Jones Senior Citizen Fitness Test has chair stand, arm curl, 6-minute walk or 2-minute step, chair sit-and-reach, back scratch and 8-foot up-and-go. Kraus-Weber tests measure minimum muscular fitness. Khelo India Fitness assessment includes BMI, flexibility, strength, endurance and speed tests. Evaluation uses norms, percentiles and ratings. Test administration needs warm-up, clear instructions, safety, trained testers and recorded results.
## Deep dive: Concepts
Test, measurement and evaluation are linked. A good test is valid, reliable and objective. Norms help compare scores. Records help track progress.
## Deep dive: Harvard step test
It measures the recovery of the pulse after stepping. A higher fitness index means better cardiovascular fitness. Rating: below 55 poor; 55 to 64 low average; 65 to 79 high average; 80 to 89 good; 90 and above excellent.
## Deep dive: Other tests
Barrow tests general motor ability. Rockport estimates aerobic capacity. Rikli and Jones tests help the elderly. Kraus-Weber checks minimum fitness. Each has specific items and scoring.
