# Physical Fitness, Wellness and Lifestyle
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 physical education physical fitness wellness lifestyle components nutrition posture
@study
Physical fitness is the ability to carry out daily tasks vigorously and alertly without undue fatigue and with energy left for leisure. Health-related fitness components are cardiovascular endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility and body composition. Skill-related components are agility, balance, coordination, power, speed and reaction time. Wellness is a state of positive health involving physical, mental, social, emotional, spiritual, environmental, occupational and intellectual dimensions; health is not only the absence of disease but complete physical, mental and social well-being (WHO). Lifestyle is the way a person lives. A sedentary lifestyle leads to hypokinetic diseases such as obesity, diabetes, hypertension, heart disease and back pain. Active lifestyle includes regular exercise, balanced diet, adequate sleep, stress management and avoiding tobacco, alcohol and drugs. Body composition is measured by Body Mass Index (BMI = weight in kg divided by height in m squared); normal BMI is about 18.5 to 24.9. Nutrition: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water and fibre; a balanced diet gives all in right proportion. Carbohydrates and fats provide energy, proteins build tissues, vitamins and minerals regulate body functions. Postural deformities include kyphosis (rounded upper back), lordosis (inward curve of lower back), scoliosis (sideways curve), flat foot, knock knees, bow legs and round shoulders; corrective exercises and good posture help. Sports and games develop fitness and self-confidence. Yoga and relaxation reduce stress. Obesity is excess body fat causing health problems; regular exercise and diet control help. Sports persons need more energy and protein. Physical activity guidelines recommend at least 60 minutes of moderate to vigorous activity daily for children.
## Deep dive: Fitness
Health-related fitness is needed by everyone; skill-related fitness by athletes. Cardiovascular endurance is the capacity of the heart and lungs to supply oxygen. Strength is the force a muscle can exert. Flexibility is the range of motion at a joint.
## Deep dive: Wellness and lifestyle
Wellness is a multi-dimensional balance. Lifestyle diseases are caused by inactivity, poor diet and stress. Healthy habits prevent them. Sleep of 7 to 9 hours helps recovery.
## Deep dive: Nutrition and posture
Balanced diet provides all nutrients. Water is vital. Postural deformities can be corrected by exercise and awareness. Good posture reduces strain on muscles and bones.
