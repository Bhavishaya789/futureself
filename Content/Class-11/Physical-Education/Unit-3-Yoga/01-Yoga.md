@study
The word yoga comes from the Sanskrit root yuj, meaning to join or unite the individual self with the universal self. Patanjali, in his Yoga Sutras, defined yoga as the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind. The eight limbs (Ashtanga Yoga) of Patanjali are Yama (restraints), Niyama (observances), Asana (postures), Pranayama (breath control), Pratyahara (withdrawal of senses), Dharana (concentration), Dhyana (meditation) and Samadhi (absorption). The five yamas are ahimsa, satya, asteya, brahmacharya and aparigraha; the five niyamas are shaucha, santosha, tapas, svadhyaya and ishvara pranidhana. Types of yoga include Jnana yoga (knowledge), Bhakti yoga (devotion), Karma yoga (selfless action), Raja yoga (mind control) and Hatha yoga. Asanas are classified as standing (Tadasana, Vrikshasana, Trikonasana, Ardha Chakrasana, Padahastasana), sitting (Vajrasana, Padmasana, Sukhasana, Ardha Matsyendrasana, Paschimottanasana, Gomukhasana), prone (Bhujangasana, Shalabhasana, Dhanurasana, Makarasana) and supine (Shavasana, Setubandhasana, Halasana, Sarvangasana, Matsyasana, Naukasana). Benefits: Tadasana improves posture; Bhujangasana strengthens the back; Vajrasana helps digestion and can be done after meals; Shavasana relaxes. Pranayama has three stages: puraka (inhalation), kumbhaka (retention) and rechaka (exhalation). Types: Anulom-Vilom (alternate nostril breathing), Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Bhramari, Ujjayi, Sheetali and Sheetkari. The six cleansing techniques (Shatkarma) are Neti, Dhauti, Nauli, Basti, Kapalabhati and Trataka. Meditation is concentration of the mind. Guidelines: practise on an empty stomach, in a clean airy place, on a mat, in comfortable clothes, and not when ill. International Day of Yoga is observed on 21 June, proposed by India's Prime Minister and adopted by the UN General Assembly in 2014; the first was observed in 2015. Yoga helps reduce stress and improves flexibility and concentration. The Ministry of AYUSH promotes yoga and the common yoga protocol is practised by many.
## Deep dive: Yoga and its limbs
Yoga unites body, mind and spirit. Patanjali's eight limbs form a path from ethics to liberation. Yama and niyama are the moral foundation. Asana and pranayama train body and breath.
## Deep dive: Asanas
Each asana has a method, benefit and precaution. Standing asanas improve balance and posture. Sitting asanas help flexibility and meditation. Prone and supine asanas strengthen the back and relax the body.
## Deep dive: Pranayama and cleansing
Breath control calms the mind. Anulom-Vilom balances the breath through both nostrils. Kapalabhati is rapid exhalation. Shatkarma purify the body. Precautions: avoid strain and practise under guidance.
## Hindi saransh
Yoga ka arth jodna hai; Patanjali ke ashtanga yoga mein yama se samadhi tak aath ang hain.
> Ashtanga order: Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi.
