# Changing Trends and Career in Physical Education
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 physical education changing trends career meaning aims khelo india fit india
@study
Physical Education is education through physical activity for the all-round development of the individual: physical, mental, social and emotional. Its aims are to develop physical fitness, skills, healthy attitudes and character, and its objectives include physical development, mental development, social development, emotional development, and development of leadership and fair play. Evolution: in ancient Greece physical training was central, and the first ancient Olympic Games were held in 776 BCE at Olympia; the modern Olympic Games began in 1896 at Athens through the efforts of Pierre de Coubertin. In India the Asian Games were first held at New Delhi in 1951 and the Commonwealth Games at Delhi in 2010. Sports in India are promoted by the Sports Authority of India (SAI), the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the Indian Olympic Association (founded 1927) and national federations. Government initiatives include Khelo India (launched 2018 to build a strong sports culture), the Fit India Movement (launched on 29 August 2019 on National Sports Day) and the Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS). National Sports Day is observed on 29 August, the birthday of hockey wizard Major Dhyan Chand. Awards include the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award, Arjuna Award, Dronacharya Award and Dhyan Chand Award. Career options include physical education teacher, coach, sports administrator, sports journalist, sports psychologist, sports medicine doctor, physiotherapist, fitness trainer, referee or umpire, sports nutritionist and sports manager. Changing trends include use of technology, wearable devices, sports science, professional leagues, inclusive sports, health and wellness focus, and global competition. Adaptive PE and recreation expanded; yoga and fitness became part of the school curriculum, and National Education Policy 2020 stresses sports integration.
## Deep dive: Aims and objectives
Aims are the general purposes; objectives are specific results. Physical education builds the body, mind and character. It teaches co-operation, discipline and sportsmanship. It promotes healthy living and leisure use.
## Deep dive: Trends
Technology, analytics and sports science now guide training. Professional leagues and private academies have grown. Women participation has increased. Fitness is linked to well-being and prevention of lifestyle disease.
## Deep dive: Careers
Careers are in teaching, coaching, sports medicine, media, management and fitness industry. Qualifications include B.P.Ed, M.P.Ed and NIS diplomas from Patiala. SAI centres train athletes.
