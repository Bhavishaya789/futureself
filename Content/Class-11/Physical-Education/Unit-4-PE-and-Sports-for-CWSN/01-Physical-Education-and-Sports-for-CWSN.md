# Physical Education and Sports for CWSN
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 physical education sports for children with special needs disability inclusion paralympics
@study
CWSN means Children With Special Needs. Disability is a condition that limits a person's physical, sensory or mental functions. The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (RPwD Act) recognises 21 disabilities and replaced the 1995 Act. Types of disability: locomotor disability (limited movement of limbs, as in cerebral palsy or amputation), visual impairment (blindness and low vision), hearing impairment (deaf and hard of hearing), intellectual disability (low intelligence quotient and adaptive skills), autism spectrum disorder (ASD), specific learning disabilities (dyslexia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia), attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and cerebral palsy. Causes include heredity, birth complications, accidents, diseases and malnutrition. Concepts: inclusion (all children learn together with support), integration, segregation and adaptive physical education, which modifies activities for the needs of the child. Aims of adaptive PE: improve fitness, build confidence, develop social skills, reduce dependence and give a sense of achievement. Strategies: use simple clear instructions, modify rules, equipment and space, provide assistive devices, use peer help, offer individual attention, set achievable goals and give positive reinforcement. Sports organisations: the Paralympic Games (first held in Rome, 1960; Sir Ludwig Guttmann), Special Olympics (founded 1968 by Eunice Kennedy Shriver; for intellectual disability) and the Deaflympics (first held 1924 in Paris). In India the Paralympic Committee of India and Special Olympics Bharat promote these sports. Indian para-athletes include Devendra Jhajharia (javelin), Deepa Malik (shot put, silver in 2016), Mariyappan Thangavelu (high jump, gold 2016) and Avani Lekhara (shooting, gold 2020). Role of the teacher: identify the need, plan activities, ensure safety, motivate and encourage participation. Barriers: negative attitudes, lack of facilities, lack of trained teachers and inaccessible buildings. Children with special needs have the right to education and sports under the Right to Education Act and the RPwD Act. Posture and fitness of CWSN can be improved by exercise, and physical activity helps their mental health.
## Deep dive: Types and causes
Locomotor, visual, hearing, intellectual, autism, learning disabilities and ADHD each need different support. Early identification helps. Causes may be before, during or after birth.
## Deep dive: Inclusion and adaptive PE
Inclusion gives equal opportunity. Adaptive PE modifies rules, equipment and goals. The teacher uses demonstration, touch cues, signs and extra time as needed. Safety and encouragement are vital.
## Deep dive: Sports and law
The Paralympics are for physical disabilities; Special Olympics for intellectual disabilities; Deaflympics for hearing impaired athletes. The RPwD Act 2016 recognises 21 disabilities and promotes inclusive education and sports.
