# Olympic Value Education

@study
Olympism is a philosophy of life that blends sport with culture and education, promoting harmony and respect. Olympic Value Education (OVE) teaches the values of the Olympic Movement: joy of effort, fair play, respect for others, pursuit of excellence, and balance between body, will and mind. The International Olympic Committee (IOC) was founded in 1894 by Pierre de Coubertin and is headquartered at Lausanne, Switzerland. The ancient Olympic Games were held every four years at Olympia in honour of Zeus and were banned about 393 CE by Emperor Theodosius. The first modern Games were held in 1896 at Athens. The Olympic motto is Citius, Altius, Fortius (faster, higher, stronger), to which Communiter (together) was added in 2021. The Olympic symbol consists of five interlocking rings (blue, yellow, black, green and red) on a white background, representing the union of five continents; the Olympic flag was introduced in 1914 and first flown in 1920 at Antwerp. The Olympic flame is lit at Olympia and carried by a torch relay to the host city; the torch relay began in 1936. The Olympic oath is taken by an athlete at the opening ceremony. The Olympic Charter is the codification of fundamental principles. The Winter Olympics began in 1924 at Chamonix. The Youth Olympic Games began in 2010 at Singapore. The Paralympic Games, begun by Ludwig Guttmann, were first held in Rome in 1960; the Special Olympics were founded by Eunice Kennedy Shriver in 1968; the Deaflympics began in Paris in 1924. India's Olympic history: Norman Pritchard won two silver medals in 1900, the Indian hockey team won gold in 1928 at Amsterdam, K D Jadhav won independent India's first individual medal (bronze) in 1952, Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual gold in shooting in 2008 at Beijing, and Neeraj Chopra won gold in javelin in 2021 at Tokyo. Values are applied in school: fair play, respect and excellence in sports. The Olympic Games are held every four years, and the cycle is called an Olympiad.
## Deep dive: Olympism and values
Olympism puts sport at the service of humanity. OVE educates through joy of effort, fair play, respect, excellence and balance. It builds character, tolerance and international understanding. Olympic ideals fight discrimination.
## Deep dive: Symbols and ceremonies
Rings, flag, flame, motto, oath, anthem and mascots are Olympic symbols. Ceremonies include the opening with the parade of nations and the closing ceremony. Medals are gold, silver and bronze.
## Deep dive: Other Games and India
The Paralympics, Special Olympics and Deaflympics give opportunities to persons with disabilities. The IOA organises India's participation. The Olympic Games are the biggest sports event in the world.
