# India: Climate
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 geography india climate monsoon seasons rainfall
@study
India has a tropical monsoon climate. The word monsoon comes from the Arabic mausim, meaning season. The factors affecting climate are latitude, altitude, pressure and winds, distance from the sea, ocean currents and relief. The monsoon is caused by differential heating of land and sea, the shift of the Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ), the high pressure area over Madagascar, the Tibetan plateau heating, the Tropical Easterly Jet and Somali Jet, and the El Nino and Southern Oscillation. The Himalaya acts as a climatic divide protecting India from cold winds of central Asia. There are four seasons: the cold weather season (winter, December to February), the hot weather season (March to May), the advancing southwest monsoon (June to September) and the retreating monsoon (October to November). In winter the north-east trade winds blow and western disturbances bring rainfall to north-west India. In summer there are local storms such as loo, Kalbaisakhi (Nor Westers), mango showers (Kerala) and Andhi. The southwest monsoon bursts in Kerala around 1 June, reaches Delhi by about 29 June and covers India by 15 July. The Arabian Sea branch brings heavy rain to the Western Ghats; the Bay of Bengal branch brings rain to the north-east and the Ganga plain. Mawsynram receives the highest rainfall. Rajasthan is dry. Tamil Nadu gets rain from the retreating monsoon, the north-east monsoon, in October-November. Climatic regions of India, according to Koppen, include Amw, As, Aw, BShw, Cwg, Dfc, ET, and E. Monsoon is the unifying bond for Indian life and the economy; rainfall variability brings floods and droughts.
## Deep dive: Controls
Latitude: tropical in the south, subtropical in the north. Altitude: temperature falls 6.5 C per km. Pressure and winds: seasonal reversal. Relief: windward and leeward areas. Continental location creates extremes. Ocean currents have little effect in India. The Himalaya and the Thar affect rainfall.
## Deep dive: Seasons and storms
Winter: clear skies, high pressure in the north, western disturbances bring rabi rainfall. Summer: highest temperature in Rajasthan, loo, Kalbaisakhi in Bengal, Bardoichila in Assam, mango showers in Kerala. Monsoon: break in monsoon, onset, burst. Retreating monsoon: October heat in the north, cyclones in the Bay of Bengal.
## Deep dive: Distribution and importance
Rainfall: over 200 cm in the Western Ghats and north-east; 100 to 200 in the east and Ganga plain; 50 to 100 in the Deccan; under 50 in Rajasthan and Ladakh. The monsoon controls agriculture, power, water and life; variability causes floods and droughts. Rain-fed farming dominates.
