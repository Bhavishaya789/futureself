# India: Location, Structure and Physiography

@study
India lies entirely in the northern and eastern hemispheres, between 8 degrees 4 minutes N and 37 degrees 6 minutes N latitudes and 68 degrees 7 minutes E and 97 degrees 25 minutes E longitudes. The Tropic of Cancer (23.5 degrees N) divides the country into almost two halves. The mainland extends 3214 km north to south and 2933 km east to west; the total area is 3.28 million sq km, 2.4 percent of the world. The land boundary is about 15,200 km and the coastline, including islands, is 7516.6 km. The Standard Meridian is 82.5 degrees E, passing through Mirzapur, giving Indian Standard Time, 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT. The Suez Canal shortened the route to Europe. India has 28 states and 8 union territories. Geologically, the Peninsular block is the oldest, the Himalaya are young fold mountains formed by the collision of the Indian and Eurasian plates, and the plains were formed by alluvial deposition. The physiographic divisions are: the Northern Mountains (Himalaya: Himadri, Himachal, Shiwalik, and the Purvachal), the Northern Plains (Punjab, Haryana, Ganga, Brahmaputra plains; Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar), the Peninsular Plateau (Central Highlands, Deccan Plateau, Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats), the Indian Desert, the Coastal Plains and the Islands. Andaman and Nicobar islands in the Bay of Bengal and Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea are the island groups.
## Deep dive: Location and extent
Latitude 8 4 N to 37 6 N (Kanyakumari to Kashmir; Indira Point at 6 45 N is the southernmost tip of the territory). Longitude 68 7 E to 97 25 E. The time difference between Gujarat and Arunachal Pradesh is about two hours. Neighbours: Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar and Bangladesh; Sri Lanka and Maldives across the sea. Palk Strait separates India from Sri Lanka.
## Deep dive: Structure
Three geological divisions: the Peninsular block (Gondwana remnants, Archaean rocks), the Himalaya and other peninsular mountains, and the Indo-Ganga-Brahmaputra plain. Himalaya: Himadri (Greater), Himachal (Lesser), Shiwalik (Outer). Highest peak: Kanchenjunga in India (8598 m), Mount Everest in Nepal. The Purvachal include Patkai, Naga, Manipur and Mizo hills.
## Deep dive: Plains, plateau and islands
Plains: Bhabar (pebbles), Terai (marshy), Bhangar (old alluvium), Khadar (new alluvium). Peninsular plateau: Malwa, Chotanagpur, Deccan, Western Ghats (Anai Mudi 2695 m), Eastern Ghats. Thar desert; coastal plains: western (Konkan, Kannad, Malabar) and eastern (Northern Circars, Coromandel). Islands: Andaman and Nicobar (about 572), Lakshadweep (coral atolls).
