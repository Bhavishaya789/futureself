# Geomorphic Processes and Landforms
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 geography geomorphic processes weathering erosion deposition landforms
@study
Geomorphic processes are the forces that shape the earth's surface. They are of two types: endogenic processes (from within the earth, such as diastrophism and volcanism) and exogenic processes (from outside, such as weathering, mass movement, erosion and deposition). Weathering is the in-situ breakdown of rocks by physical, chemical and biological means. Physical weathering includes frost action, exfoliation and thermal expansion; chemical weathering includes solution, carbonation, hydration, oxidation and reduction. Mass movements include creep, flow, slide and fall. Erosion and deposition are carried out by running water, groundwater, glaciers, waves and wind, which produce characteristic landforms. Running water forms V-shaped valleys, waterfalls, meanders, ox-bow lakes, floodplains and deltas. Glaciers produce cirques, U-shaped valleys, moraines and eskers. Groundwater forms karst topography with sinkholes, caves, stalactites and stalagmites. Waves create cliffs, beaches and sea stacks; winds create deflation hollows, mushroom rocks, barchans and loess.
## Deep dive: Processes
Endogenic: diastrophism (folding, faulting), volcanism, earthquakes. Exogenic: weathering, mass wasting, erosion and transport, deposition. Gradation: degradation (lowering of relief) and aggradation (raising by deposition). Soil formation is a result of weathering; its factors are parent material, topography, climate, organisms and time.
## Deep dive: Weathering and mass movement
Physical: frost wedging, salt weathering, exfoliation (onion skin), block disintegration. Chemical: carbonation of limestone, oxidation of iron. Biological: roots, burrowing animals. Mass movements: soil creep, mudflow, landslide, rockfall and slump, triggered by gravity and water.
## Deep dive: Landforms
River: gorges, canyons, meanders, ox-bow lakes, levees, deltas. Glacier: cirques, arêtes, horns, hanging valleys, fjords, drumlins. Karst: sinkholes, uvalas, dolines, stalactites. Coast: stacks, arches, spits. Wind: barchans, seif dunes, yardangs.
