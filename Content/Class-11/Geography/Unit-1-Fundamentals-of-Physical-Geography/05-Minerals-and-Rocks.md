@study
A mineral is a naturally occurring, inorganic substance with a definite chemical composition and physical properties. Rocks are aggregates of minerals. Eight elements make up most of the crust: oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron, calcium, sodium, potassium and magnesium. Major minerals include feldspar (most abundant), quartz, pyroxene, amphibole, mica and olivine. Physical properties: crystal form, cleavage, fracture, lustre, colour, streak, transparency, structure, hardness (the Mohs scale) and specific gravity. Rocks are of three types: igneous (formed by cooling of magma), sedimentary (formed by deposition and compaction of sediments) and metamorphic (formed by changes due to heat and pressure). Igneous rocks are classified as intrusive (granite, gabbro) and extrusive (basalt). Sedimentary rocks include sandstone, limestone and shale. Metamorphic rocks include slate, marble, quartzite and gneiss. The rock cycle explains the transformation of one rock type into another.
## Deep dive: Minerals
Silicates (quartz, feldspar, mica) are the most abundant; metallic minerals (ferrous, non-ferrous, precious) and non-metallic minerals. Properties: lustre, hardness, cleavage. Mohs scale from 1 (talc) to 10 (diamond).
## Deep dive: Rocks
Igneous: primary rocks, no fossils, crystalline. Intrusive: granite (plutonic), dolerite. Extrusive: basalt. Sedimentary: layered, may contain fossils; mechanical (sandstone, conglomerate), organic (coal, limestone) and chemical (gypsum, rock salt). Metamorphic: changed by heat, pressure or fluids; shale to slate, limestone to marble, sandstone to quartzite, granite to gneiss, coal to graphite.
## Deep dive: Rock cycle
Igneous rocks weather to sediments; sediments form sedimentary rocks; heat and pressure make metamorphic rocks; melting returns them to magma. The cycle is continuous and has no fixed start.
