# The Origin and Evolution of the Earth
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 geography origin and evolution of the earth big bang nebular hypothesis
@study
Theories of the origin of the earth range from early ideas of Kant and Laplace's nebular hypothesis to the modern Big Bang theory. Kant proposed a cold, slowly rotating nebula; Laplace modified this into a hot, rotating nebula that threw off rings that condensed into planets. Chamberlin and Moulton proposed the planetesimal hypothesis, in which a passing star pulled material from the sun. Jeans and Jeffreys proposed the tidal hypothesis. Later, Otto Schmidt and Carl Weizsacker proposed a dust cloud around the sun. The modern theory is the Big Bang, proposed by Georges Lemaitre and supported by Edwin Hubble's expanding universe, in which the universe began about 13.7 billion years ago from a tiny, extremely dense point. Stars formed within galaxies from nebulae; planets formed by accretion within the solar nebula. The earth formed about 4.6 billion years ago, differentiated into core, mantle and crust, and its atmosphere evolved in three stages with the help of volcanic outgassing; life began about 3.8 billion years ago.
## Deep dive: Early theories
Kant (1755) proposed a gaseous nebula. Laplace (1796) described a hot rotating nebula that shed rings. Chamberlin and Moulton proposed the planetesimal hypothesis. Jeans and Jeffreys proposed the tidal hypothesis. Schmidt, Weizsacker and Kuiper developed dust-cloud models. Hoyle proposed the steady-state hypothesis; Lemaitre proposed the primeval atom, and Hubble showed the galaxies were receding.
## Deep dive: Big Bang and solar system
About 13.7 billion years ago the universe was in a single dense point; it expanded. Stars formed from clouds of hydrogen; within the solar nebula, planetesimals formed planets by accretion. The inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are terrestrial; the outer planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) are Jovian. The moon is thought to have formed from a giant impact with a Mars-sized body.
## Deep dive: Evolution of the earth
Earth became layered by density differentiation; heavier iron sank to the core. The early atmosphere of hydrogen and helium was lost; volcanic outgassing added water vapour, carbon dioxide, nitrogen and ammonia. Cooling led to rain and oceans. Life arose in the oceans; photosynthesis added oxygen.
