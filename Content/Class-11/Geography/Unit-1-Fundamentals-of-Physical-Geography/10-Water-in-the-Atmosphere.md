# Water in the Atmosphere
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 geography water in the atmosphere humidity clouds precipitation
@study
Water vapour enters the atmosphere by evaporation, transpiration and sublimation. Humidity is the amount of water vapour in the air. Absolute humidity is the weight of water vapour per unit volume of air in grams per cubic metre; relative humidity is the percentage of moisture the air holds relative to its capacity at that temperature; specific humidity is the mass of vapour per kg of air. Saturation occurs when the air holds the maximum vapour; the temperature at which it occurs is the dew point. Condensation occurs when air cools to dew point. Forms of condensation include dew, frost, fog, mist and clouds. Clouds are classified as high clouds (cirrus, cirrostratus, cirrocumulus), middle clouds (altocumulus, altostratus), low clouds (stratus, stratocumulus, nimbostratus) and clouds of vertical development (cumulus and cumulonimbus). Precipitation takes the forms of rain, snow, sleet and hail. Types of rainfall are convectional, orographic and cyclonic or frontal. World rainfall is highest near the equator and on windward slopes, and low in the deserts.
## Deep dive: Humidity
Absolute, specific and relative. Relative humidity = actual vapour divided by capacity times 100. Warm air holds more vapour. Evaporation depends on temperature, wind, humidity and the surface area. The dew point is the temperature at which air becomes saturated.
## Deep dive: Condensation and clouds
Condensation needs cooling and nuclei. Dew forms on cool surfaces; frost when the dew point is below freezing; fog is a cloud at ground level, and mist is thinner. Clouds: cirrus (wispy), cumulus (puffy), stratus (layered), nimbus (rain-bearing).
## Deep dive: Rainfall
Convectional: heated ground, rising air, daily afternoon rain in the equatorial belt. Orographic: moist wind forced up a mountain; the leeward side is a rain shadow. Cyclonic: along fronts. Distribution: Equatorial belt over 250 cm; windward coasts; deserts under 25 cm. Isohyets join equal rainfall.
