# Life on the Earth and Biodiversity
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 geography biosphere ecosystem biodiversity conservation
@study
The biosphere is the zone of life on the earth, formed by the interaction of the lithosphere, atmosphere and hydrosphere. An ecosystem is a functional unit of living organisms and their environment. The components are abiotic (soil, water, air, sunlight) and biotic (producers, consumers and decomposers). Energy flows through food chains and food webs in trophic levels; the 10 percent law states that only about 10 percent of energy passes to the next level. Ecological pyramids show numbers, biomass or energy. Biogeochemical cycles include the carbon, nitrogen, water, oxygen and phosphorus cycles. Biomes are large ecological regions: forest, grassland, desert, tundra and aquatic. Biodiversity is the variety of life at the level of genes, species and ecosystems. About 1.7 million species are described, and tropical regions have the highest diversity. Hotspots are areas with high endemism and threat; India has four hotspots: the Himalaya, the Western Ghats, Indo-Burma and the Sundaland (Nicobar). Threats include habitat loss, over-exploitation, invasive species and climate change. Conservation can be in situ (national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves) or ex situ (zoos, botanical gardens, seed banks). The IUCN Red List classifies species as extinct, critically endangered, endangered, vulnerable and so on. International efforts include the Convention on Biological Diversity (Rio, 1992), CITES and the Ramsar Convention.
## Deep dive: Ecosystem
Producers are green plants; primary consumers are herbivores; secondary and tertiary consumers are carnivores; decomposers are bacteria and fungi. Food chains: grazing and detritus. Ecological pyramids are upright (grassland) or inverted (number in a tree ecosystem). Biomes: tropical rainforest, savanna, temperate grassland, taiga, tundra and desert.
## Deep dive: Cycles
Carbon cycle: photosynthesis, respiration, combustion, decomposition. Nitrogen cycle: fixation by bacteria and lightning, nitrification, assimilation, denitrification. Water cycle. Phosphorus cycle is sedimentary.
## Deep dive: Biodiversity and conservation
Genetic, species and ecosystem diversity. Hotspots, endemism, extinction. Protected areas: national parks, wildlife sanctuaries and biosphere reserves such as Nilgiri. Project Tiger (1973), Project Elephant. Conservation helps for ecological balance, resources and ethics.
