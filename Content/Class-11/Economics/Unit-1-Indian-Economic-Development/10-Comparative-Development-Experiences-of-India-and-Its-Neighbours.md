# Comparative Development Experiences of India and Its Neighbours
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 economics comparative development India China Pakistan
@study
India, China and Pakistan: common starting points in the late 1940s. China: 1949 revolution, Great Leap Forward (1958, industrialisation, communes), Cultural Revolution (1966-76), reforms of 1978 (Household Responsibility System, Township and Village Enterprises, Special Economic Zones, dual pricing). Pakistan: 1947; import substitution; Green Revolution in 1960s; reforms in the 1980s; economic problems; low investment in social sectors. Demographic indicators: population (China 1.4 billion, India 1.4 billion, Pakistan 0.2 billion); sex ratio; fertility; one-child policy (1979 to 2015). GDP growth: China about 9-10 percent (1978 on); India 6-7 percent; Pakistan 4-5 percent. Sectoral shares. Human development indicators: HDI rank, life expectancy, literacy, access to water and sanitation. Poverty. Lessons: India's democratic planning; China's manufacturing export-led growth; Pakistan's lack of diversification and dependence on remittances.
## Deep dive: China and Pakistan's development strategies
China: land reforms, commune system, Great Leap Forward (rapid industrialisation), Proletarian Cultural Revolution (students to rural), 1978 reforms in stages: phase 1 agriculture (communes dismantled, household responsibility), phase 2 industry (TVEs, foreign investment, SEZs, dual pricing), open door policy. Pakistan: mixed economy, import substitution, Green Revolution, nationalisation 1970s, deregulation in 1988, remittances, borrowing; politically unstable; low savings.
## Deep dive: Comparative indicators
Population: China 1.4 billion, growth rate 0.5 percent; India 1.4 billion, 1.0; Pakistan 0.2 billion, 2.0. Sex ratio: India 943 females per 1000 males (2011), China about 940, Pakistan about 940. Fertility rate: China 1.6, India 2.4, Pakistan 3.6. Urbanisation: China 60 percent. GDP growth in 1980s: China 10 percent; India 5.7 (1980-90). Sector shares: China manufacturing and services; India services share high; Pakistan agriculture. HDI 2018: China 85th, India 129th, Pakistan 150th (approx). Life expectancy: China 76, India 69, Pakistan 67.
## Deep dive: Appraisal
Neighbours share colonial history. Lessons: India's growth is service-led; China's manufacturing-led with large investment in education and health; Pakistan had slower diversification. Democracy versus authoritarianism; role of the state; agriculture-to-industry shift; gender gaps; poverty. Common issues: unemployment, regional disparity and environmental costs. SAARC and regional cooperation.
