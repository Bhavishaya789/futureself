# Rural Development
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 economics rural development credit marketing diversification organic farming
@study
Rural development is the action plan for the economic regeneration of villages. Key issues: credit, marketing, diversification of activities, productive employment, sustainable development. Rural credit: sources non-institutional (moneylenders, traders) and institutional (cooperatives, commercial banks, RRBs, NABARD, self-help groups, microfinance); NABARD set up in 1982; Kisan Credit Card. Agricultural marketing: regulated markets, cooperative marketing, e-NAM, minimum support price, warehouses, grading, standardisation; problems: middlemen, lack of storage. Diversification: crop and non-farm productive activities: animal husbandry (Operation Flood, White Revolution, Verghese Kurien, Amul), fisheries (Blue Revolution), horticulture (Golden Revolution), non-farm employment (agro-processing, handicrafts, tourism). Organic farming: no chemical inputs; benefits: health, soil fertility, export; problems: lower yields initially. Sustainable development of rural areas.
## Deep dive: Rural credit
Rural indebtedness and need for credit for seeds, fertilisers, social functions. Non-institutional: moneylenders charging high interest, landlords. Institutional: Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), commercial banks (nationalised in 1969), Regional Rural Banks (1975), NABARD (apex), SHGs (microcredit with 10-20 members), Kisan Credit Card. Problems: inadequate coverage, collateral requirement, farm loan waiver issues, regional disparity.
## Deep dive: Marketing and diversification
Agricultural marketing: gathering, storage, grading, transport and sale. Government measures: regulated market (APMC), infrastructure (roads, storage), cooperative marketing societies, MSP, buffer stock, PDS, e-NAM. Alternative marketing channels: apni mandi (Punjab), hadaspar mandi (Pune), rythu bazars (Andhra), uzhavar sandies (Tamil Nadu). Diversification: change in cropping pattern; allied activities: animal husbandry (milk, meat, eggs), fisheries, horticulture; Operation Flood.
## Deep dive: Organic farming and sustainability
Organic farming uses organic manures, biopesticides, crop rotation; benefits: higher price in export markets, healthy food, less pollution, employment; problems: lower yields, certification, awareness, small producer. Sustainable development: better infrastructure, education, health, women empowerment, rural industries, agro-based industries, tourism and IT. Role of cooperatives, SHGs, Sikkim as an organic state.
