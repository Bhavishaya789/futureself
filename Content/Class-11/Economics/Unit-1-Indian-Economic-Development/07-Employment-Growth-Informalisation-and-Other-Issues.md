# Employment: Growth, Informalisation and Other Issues
minutes: 35
yt: class 11 economics employment growth informalisation unemployment
@study
Worker: a person who works to earn. Workforce participation rate (WPR) = (workers / population) x 100. Types of workers: self-employed, hired (regular salaried and casual wage labour). Employment in India: primary 45 percent, secondary 25, tertiary 30 (2018). Formal and informal sector: formal workers get social security; informal (unorganised) about 90 percent of workforce. Growth and employment: jobless growth; casualisation; informalisation. Unemployment: open, disguised (agriculture), seasonal, underemployment; educated unemployment; structural; cyclical. Government initiatives: MGNREGA, PMEGP, SGSY, NRLM, Skill India, Make in India, Start-up India. Employment generation. Rural, urban and female participation. Sector changes: agriculture workforce shifting to services.
## Deep dive: Basic concepts
Gainful employment, WPR for males and females in rural and urban areas (urban lower). Self-employed form about 52 percent of workforce; regular salaried about 24 percent; casual labour 24 percent. Rural women WPR is higher because of poverty. Employment by status and sector. Employment by region: Himachal and Kerala differ. Informal sector workers are not protected by laws.
## Deep dive: Growth, informalisation and unemployment
Jobless growth: GDP grows without matching jobs; informalisation: shift to informal work with poor conditions; casualisation. Unemployment types: disguised (more workers than needed), seasonal (agriculture off-season), open, educated, structural. Underemployment. Causes: population growth, low growth in manufacturing, rural-urban migration, mismatched skills. Consequences: poverty, wasted human resources, social unrest.
## Deep dive: Government and issues
Government as an employer: public sector employs about 6 percent. Schemes: MGNREGA, PMEGP, SGSY. Policies for the informal sector: social security (e-Shram, PM-SYM). Issues: child labour, wage inequality, gender gap, insecure jobs, lack of skills. Remedies: labour-intensive industries, small and medium enterprises, skill development, education, rural infrastructure, agro-based industries.
