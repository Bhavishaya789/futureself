# Statistics: Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion
minutes: 50
yt: class 11 economics measures of central tendency mean median mode dispersion standard deviation
@study
Central tendency: arithmetic mean (simple, weighted, grouped: direct, short-cut, step deviation), median (middle value; for continuous series Median = l + ((N/2 - cf)/f) x h), mode (most frequent; Mode = l + ((f1 - f0)/(2f1 - f0 - f2)) x h), empirical relation Mode = 3 Median - 2 Mean. Properties of mean: sum of deviations is zero; affected by extreme values. Quartiles, deciles, percentiles. Dispersion: range, quartile deviation, mean deviation, standard deviation (sigma), variance, coefficient of variation = (sigma/mean) x 100, Lorenz curve. Absolute and relative measures. Example: mean of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 is 30; standard deviation about 14.14. Grouped example: classes 0-10, 10-20, 20-30, 30-40 with frequencies 5, 8, 12, 5 give mean 20.67, median 21.67 and mode 23.64.
## Deep dive: Mean, median and mode
Simple mean = sum x / n. Weighted mean = sum wx / sum w. Grouped mean = sum fx / sum f with class marks. Step deviation: mean = A + (sum fd / sum f) x h. Median: arrange in order; odd n median is the (n+1)/2 th; even is the average of two middle items. Worked grouped example: N = 30; cumulative frequencies 5, 13, 25, 30; N/2 = 15 lies in 20-30; median = 20 + (15 - 13)/12 x 10 = 21.67. Mode: highest frequency class 20-30 with f1 = 12, f0 = 8, f2 = 5; mode = 20 + (12 - 8)/(24 - 8 - 5) x 10 = 23.64. Mean = 620/30 = 20.67. Check: 3 median - 2 mean = 65 - 41.33 = 23.67, near the mode.
## Deep dive: Dispersion
Range = L - S; coefficient of range = (L - S)/(L + S). Quartile deviation = (Q3 - Q1)/2; coefficient = (Q3 - Q1)/(Q3 + Q1). Mean deviation = sum |x - mean| / n. Standard deviation: sigma = sqrt( sum (x - mean)^2 / n ). For 10, 20, 30, 40, 50: deviations -20, -10, 0, 10, 20; squares 400, 100, 0, 100, 400 sum 1000; variance 200; sigma = 14.14; coefficient of variation = 14.14/30 x 100 = 47.1 percent. Lower CV means greater consistency. If every value is increased by a constant, mean changes but sigma does not; if multiplied by k, both multiply by k.
## Deep dive: Uses and Lorenz curve
Mean for continuous variables, median for skewed data or open-ended classes, mode for the most common size (shoes). Merits and demerits of each. Lorenz curve shows income inequality: the line of equality is the diagonal; the farther the curve, the greater the inequality; Gini coefficient is the area ratio. Quartiles divide data into four equal parts. Comparison of two series with CV.
