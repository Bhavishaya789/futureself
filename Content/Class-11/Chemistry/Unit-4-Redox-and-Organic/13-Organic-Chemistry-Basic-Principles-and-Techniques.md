# Organic Chemistry: Some Basic Principles and Techniques
minutes: 50

@study
Tetravalence of carbon, shapes of organic molecules (sp3 tetrahedral, sp2 planar, sp linear). Structural representations: Lewis, condensed, bond-line. Classification: acyclic, alicyclic, aromatic; functional groups; homologous series. IUPAC nomenclature: longest chain, lowest locants, alphabetical order, prefixes and suffixes. Isomerism: structural (chain, position, functional group, metamerism, tautomerism) and stereoisomerism (geometrical, optical). Fundamental concepts: fission (homolytic gives free radicals, heterolytic gives carbocations and carbanions), electrophiles and nucleophiles, electron displacement effects (inductive, electromeric, resonance, hyperconjugation). Types of reactions: substitution, addition, elimination, rearrangement. Purification: crystallisation, sublimation, distillation (simple, fractional, steam, reduced pressure), differential extraction, chromatography (column, TLC, paper). Qualitative analysis: Lassaigne test (Na fusion), Duma and Kjeldahl method for nitrogen, Carius method for halogens and sulphur. Quantitative estimation of C and H by combustion.
## Deep dive: Nomenclature and isomerism
IUPAC rules: choose the longest chain with the principal functional group, number to give the lowest locant, name substituents alphabetically. Priority: acid > ester > amide > aldehyde > ketone > alcohol > amine. Examples: CH3CH(CH3)CH2CH3 is 2-methylbutane; CH3COCH2CH3 is butan-2-one. Chain isomers: butane/isobutane; position: 1- and 2-propanol; functional: ethanol/dimethyl ether; metamerism: diethyl ether/methyl propyl ether; tautomerism: keto-enol.
## Deep dive: Electronic effects and reaction intermediates
Inductive effect: permanent polarisation of sigma bonds; -I groups: NO2 > CN > COOH > F > Cl > Br > I; +I: alkyl groups. Resonance (mesomeric) effect: +R (OH, NH2, halogens) and -R (NO2, C=O). Hyperconjugation: sigma-pi delocalisation; more alpha H means more stability (CH3+ < 1 < 2 < 3 carbocation). Carbocation stability: 3 > 2 > 1 > methyl; benzyl and allyl are stable. Carbanion stability: methyl > 1 > 2 > 3. Free radicals stability 3 > 2 > 1. Electrophiles: H+, NO2+, carbocations, AlCl3; nucleophiles: OH-, CN-, NH3, H2O.
## Deep dive: Purification and analysis
Crystallisation separates solid mixtures by solubility; sublimation for camphor, naphthalene; distillation for liquids with different bp; fractional for close bp; steam for liquids immiscible with water (aniline); distillation under reduced pressure (glycerol). Chromatography: adsorption (silica or alumina stationary phase), Rf = distance moved by substance / solvent front. Lassaigne extract: N gives Prussian blue, S gives violet with nitroprusside or black PbS, halogens give AgX precipitate. Kjeldahl: percentage N = 1.4 x M x V / mass. Duma: N2 volume at STP. Carius: AgX and BaSO4.
