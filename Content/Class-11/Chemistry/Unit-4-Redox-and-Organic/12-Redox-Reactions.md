# Redox Reactions
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 chemistry redox reactions oxidation number balancing
@study
Classical idea: oxidation is addition of oxygen/electronegative element or removal of hydrogen; reduction is the reverse. Electron concept: oxidation is loss of electrons, reduction is gain (OIL RIG). Oxidation number rules: free element 0; monoatomic ion equals charge; H is +1 (except metal hydrides -1); O is -2 (peroxides -1, OF2 +2, superoxides -1/2); F always -1; sum equals charge. Oxidising agent gets reduced; reducing agent gets oxidised. Types: combination, decomposition, displacement, disproportionation (2H2O2 -> 2H2O + O2; Cl2 in alkali), comproportionation. Balancing: oxidation number method and half-reaction (ion-electron) method in acidic and basic media. Redox couples; electrode potential; electrochemical series; Daniell cell. Corrosion; Fe2+/Fe3+.
## Deep dive: Oxidation numbers
Examples: S in H2SO4 is +6; Cr in K2Cr2O7 is +6; Mn in KMnO4 is +7; N in HNO3 is +5; C in glucose 0; Cl in HClO4 +7; S in Na2S2O3 average +2; fractional oxidation number in Fe3O4 (+8/3), Na2S4O6 (+2.5). Maximum oxidation state equals group number (up to 8), minimum equals group number minus 8 for non-metals. A species in an intermediate state can act as both oxidant and reductant (H2O2, SO2).
## Deep dive: Balancing redox equations
Oxidation number method: identify changes, balance electrons, balance O with H2O, H with H+ (acidic) or OH- (basic). Half-reaction method: split into oxidation and reduction half-reactions, balance atoms other than H and O, balance O with water, H with H+, charge with electrons, equalise electrons, add. MnO4- + 5Fe2+ + 8H+ -> Mn2+ + 5Fe3+ + 4H2O. Cr2O7^2- + 14H+ + 6e- -> 2Cr3+ + 7H2O. In basic medium add OH- to neutralise H+. Disproportionation: P4 + 3NaOH + 3H2O -> PH3 + 3NaH2PO2.
## Deep dive: Electrode processes
Redox couple: oxidised and reduced forms. Electrode potential, standard hydrogen electrode (0 V). Higher reduction potential means better oxidising agent (F2 +2.87 V, Li -3.05 V). Daniell cell: Zn anode, Cu cathode, E cell = 1.1 V. Metals above hydrogen displace H2 from acids. Corrosion of iron as an electrochemical process. Redox in titrations: permanganate, dichromate, iodometric.
