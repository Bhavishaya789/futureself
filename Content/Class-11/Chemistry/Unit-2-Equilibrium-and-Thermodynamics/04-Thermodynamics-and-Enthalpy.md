# Thermodynamics and enthalpy
minutes: 20
yt: thermodynamics class 11 chemistry
@study
**First law:** dU = q + w (internal energy). **Enthalpy** H = U + PV; at constant pressure q_p = dH. Exothermic: dH < 0.
**Hess's law:** total enthalpy change is independent of the path.
**Standard enthalpy of reaction** = sum of formation enthalpies of products minus reactants.
Example (methane combustion): CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O(l). dH = (-393.5) + 2 (-285.8) - (-74.8) = **-890.3 kJ/mol**.
**Entropy** S measures disorder. **Gibbs free energy** dG = dH - T dS; reaction is spontaneous if dG < 0. At equilibrium dG = 0.

## Advanced
P: A reaction has dH = +40 kJ and dS = +120 J/K. Above what temperature is it spontaneous?
A: dG = 0 at T = dH / dS = 40000/120 = 333 K. Above 333 K dG is negative.
