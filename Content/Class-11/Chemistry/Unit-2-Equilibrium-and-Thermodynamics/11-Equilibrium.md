@study
Physical equilibrium (solid-liquid, liquid-vapour, solid-vapour, dissolution) and chemical equilibrium. Law of mass action: for aA + bB <-> cC + dD, Kc = [C]^c [D]^d / [A]^a [B]^b. Kp = Kc (RT)^delta n. Reaction quotient Q. Le Chatelier principle: concentration, pressure, temperature, catalyst (no effect on position). Ionic equilibrium: strong and weak electrolytes; Arrhenius, Bronsted-Lowry, Lewis acids and bases; conjugate pairs. Ionisation: Ka, Kb, Ka x Kb = Kw = 10^-14 at 298 K. pH = -log[H+]; pH + pOH = 14. Degree of dissociation alpha = sqrt(Ka/C). Buffer: Henderson-Hasselbalch pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]). Salt hydrolysis. Solubility product Ksp. Common ion effect.
## Deep dive: Chemical equilibrium
Equilibrium is dynamic; rates of forward and reverse reactions are equal. Characteristics: constant concentrations, attained from either side, catalyst does not change Kc. Kc units depend on delta n. If Q < K reaction goes forward; Q > K reverse. Examples: N2 + 3H2 <-> 2NH3 (delta n = -2), PCl5 <-> PCl3 + Cl2, H2 + I2 <-> 2HI (Kc independent of volume). Le Chatelier: increasing pressure shifts to fewer moles of gas; exothermic reaction favoured by low temperature; Haber process 450 C, 200 atm, Fe catalyst. K changes only with temperature.
## Deep dive: Acids, bases, pH
Arrhenius: acid gives H+, base gives OH-. Bronsted-Lowry: acid is a proton donor, base a proton acceptor; strong acid has weak conjugate base. Lewis: acid accepts an electron pair. Water autoprotolysis Kw = 1 x 10^-14. pH of 0.01 M HCl = 2. Weak acid: [H+] = sqrt(Ka C). Weak base: [OH-] = sqrt(Kb C). Polyprotic acids ionise stepwise (Ka1 > Ka2). Ostwald dilution law: alpha increases with dilution.
## Deep dive: Buffers, hydrolysis, solubility
Buffer resists pH change: acidic buffer (CH3COOH + CH3COONa), basic buffer (NH4OH + NH4Cl). pH = pKa + log [salt]/[acid]; buffer capacity highest when ratio is 1. Salt hydrolysis: salt of strong acid and weak base is acidic; weak acid and strong base basic; weak acid and weak base depends. Solubility product: for AxBy, Ksp = (xs)^x (ys)^y; ionic product Q > Ksp precipitates. Common ion effect lowers solubility. Examples: AgCl, BaSO4.
