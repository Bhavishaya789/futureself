# Thermodynamics
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 chemistry thermodynamics enthalpy entropy Gibbs energy Hess law
@study
System, surroundings, boundary; open, closed, isolated systems. State functions (U, H, S, G, P, V, T) and path functions (q, w). First law: delta U = q + w; work of expansion w = -P_ext delta V; isothermal reversible w = -2.303 nRT log(V2/V1). Enthalpy H = U + PV; delta H = delta U + delta n_g RT. Heat capacity Cp - Cv = R. Enthalpies of formation, combustion, neutralisation (-57.1 kJ/mol), atomisation, bond, lattice, solution. Hess law. Spontaneity: entropy S (second law), delta S_universe > 0; Gibbs energy delta G = delta H - T delta S; delta G < 0 spontaneous; delta G standard = -RT ln K = -2.303 RT log K. Third law: entropy of a perfect crystal at 0 K is zero. Calorimetry: bomb calorimeter (delta U), constant pressure (delta H).
## Deep dive: First law and enthalpy
Internal energy changes via heat and work. Sign convention: work done on system positive, heat absorbed positive. Free expansion: w = 0. Adiabatic: q = 0. Isochoric: w = 0, delta U = q_v. Isobaric: delta H = q_p. For ideal gas delta U depends only on T. Enthalpy of formation of elements in their standard state is zero. delta_r H = sum delta_f H(products) - sum delta_f H(reactants). Bond enthalpy: delta_r H = sum bonds broken - sum bonds formed.
## Deep dive: Hess law and enthalpy types
Hess law: total enthalpy change is independent of the path. Combustion: complete oxidation. Neutralisation of strong acid with strong base: -57.1 kJ/mol. Lattice enthalpy via Born-Haber cycle. Enthalpy of solution. Enthalpy of fusion of ice 6.01 kJ/mol; vaporisation of water 40.7 kJ/mol. Phase transitions: delta S = delta H / T.
## Deep dive: Entropy and Gibbs energy
Entropy S is a measure of randomness; units J/K/mol; S(gas) > S(liquid) > S(solid). delta S_system + delta S_surroundings > 0 for spontaneous process; delta S_surr = -delta H / T. delta G = delta H - T delta S; four cases: exothermic and entropy increase always spontaneous; endothermic and entropy decrease never; others depend on T. At equilibrium delta G = 0 and T = delta H / delta S. delta G standard = -RT ln K.
