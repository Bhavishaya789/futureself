# Classification of Elements and Periodicity in Properties
minutes: 40
yt: class 11 chemistry periodic table periodicity ionisation enthalpy electronegativity
@study
Dobereiner triads, Newlands octaves, Mendeleev periodic law (atomic mass), Moseley (atomic number); modern periodic law. Long form: 18 groups, 7 periods; s, p, d, f blocks. IUPAC names for Z above 100 (unnilunium etc.; now Nh, Mc, Ts, Og). Periodic trends: atomic radius (decreases across a period, increases down a group), ionic radius, isoelectronic species, ionisation enthalpy (increases across, decreases down, with exceptions Be/B, N/O), electron gain enthalpy (Cl highest), electronegativity (Pauling, F = 4.0), valence, oxidation states. Diagonal relationship. Anomalous properties of the second period elements.
## Deep dive: Development and structure
Mendeleev left gaps and predicted eka-aluminium (gallium) and eka-silicon (germanium). Modern table: periods 1 (2 elements), 2 (8), 3 (8), 4 (18), 5 (18), 6 (32), 7 (incomplete). s-block: group 1 and 2; p-block: groups 13 to 18; d-block: groups 3 to 12 (transition); f-block: lanthanoids and actinoids. Representative elements; noble gases; halogens; chalcogens (group 16); pnictogens (15).
## Deep dive: Periodic trends
Atomic radius: covalent, metallic, van der Waals; decreases across a period due to increasing effective nuclear charge; increases down due to extra shells. Cation smaller than parent atom; anion larger. Isoelectronic ions: size decreases with increasing nuclear charge (N3- > O2- > F- > Na+ > Mg2+). Ionisation enthalpy: first IE of Mg > Al (s vs p), P > S (half-filled). Electron gain enthalpy more negative for Cl than F (small size of F). Electronegativity: Pauling scale; increases across period; decreases down. Metallic character decreases across a period.
## Deep dive: Oxidation states and anomalies
Group valence; transition metals show multiple oxidation states. Anomalous behaviour of Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F due to small size, high electronegativity and absence of d orbitals. Diagonal relationships: Li-Mg, Be-Al, B-Si. Basic oxides on left, acidic on right, amphoteric Al2O3. Reactivity of metals decreases across; nonmetals increases.
