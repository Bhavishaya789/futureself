**Subatomic particles:** electron (-1), proton (+1), neutron (0). **Atomic number Z** = protons; **mass number A** = protons + neutrons.

## Bohr model (hydrogen-like)
Electrons move in fixed orbits; energy E_n = -13.6 Z^2 / n^2 eV. Radius r_n = 0.529 n^2 / Z angstrom. Transition energy dE = 13.6 Z^2 (1/n1^2 - 1/n2^2) eV. Wavelength lambda (nm) = 1240 / dE (eV).
Example: n = 3 to n = 2 in hydrogen: dE = 13.6 (1/4 - 1/9) = 1.889 eV, lambda = **656 nm** (red H-alpha line of the Balmer series).

## Quantum numbers
n (shell), l (subshell: 0 s, 1 p, 2 d, 3 f), m_l (-l to +l), m_s (+/- 1/2). Each shell holds 2n^2 electrons.

## Electron configuration rules
**Aufbau** (fill lowest energy first), **Pauli exclusion** (no two electrons share all four quantum numbers), **Hund's rule** (fill degenerate orbitals singly first). Exceptions: Cr is [Ar] 3d5 4s1, Cu is [Ar] 3d10 4s1.

## de Broglie and uncertainty
lambda = h / (m v). Heisenberg: dx x dp >= h / 4 pi.
