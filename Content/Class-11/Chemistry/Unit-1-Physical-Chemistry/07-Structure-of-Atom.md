# Structure of Atom
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 chemistry structure of atom quantum numbers orbitals Bohr model
@study
Discovery of electron (Thomson, e/m = 1.76 x 10^8 C/g), proton (Goldstein), neutron (Chadwick). Thomson plum pudding. Rutherford nuclear model from alpha scattering. Atomic number Z, mass number A. Isotopes, isobars, isotones. Electromagnetic radiation: wavelength, frequency, wave number, c = nu x lambda; Planck E = h nu (h = 6.626 x 10^-34 J s); photoelectric effect; hydrogen spectrum (Lyman, Balmer, Paschen, Brackett, Pfund). Bohr model: En = -2.18 x 10^-18 / n^2 J = -13.6/n^2 eV, r_n = 52.9 n^2/Z pm. de Broglie lambda = h/mv; Heisenberg uncertainty principle. Quantum numbers n, l, m_l, m_s. Shapes of orbitals. Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion, Hund rule; electronic configuration; stability of half-filled and fully filled orbitals (Cr, Cu).
## Deep dive: Early models and radiation
Cathode rays are electrons. Rutherford: most alpha particles pass through; few deflect at large angles; nucleus is small, dense, positive. Limitations: stability and line spectra. Planck quantum theory: energy emitted in quanta. Photoelectric effect (Einstein): h nu = W0 + KE; threshold frequency. Hydrogen spectrum: 1/lambda = R_H (1/n1^2 - 1/n2^2), R_H = 1.097 x 10^7 m^-1; Lyman (UV, n1=1), Balmer (visible, n1=2), Paschen (IR, n1=3).
## Deep dive: Bohr and quantum mechanics
Bohr postulates: stationary orbits, L = n h / 2 pi, energy change on jumping. Limitations: fails for multi-electron atoms, Zeeman and Stark effects. de Broglie: matter waves. Heisenberg: delta x . delta p >= h / 4 pi. Schrodinger equation, psi, psi^2 as probability density. Radial nodes = n - l - 1; angular nodes = l; total nodes = n - 1.
## Deep dive: Quantum numbers and configuration
Principal n = 1, 2, 3 (shell size); azimuthal l = 0 to n-1 (s, p, d, f shapes); magnetic m_l = -l to +l (orientation); spin m_s = +1/2 or -1/2. Number of orbitals in a shell is n^2; electrons 2n^2. Aufbau: filling in order of increasing (n + l), 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p 5s. Pauli: no two electrons have the same four quantum numbers. Hund: pairing after each orbital is singly occupied. Cr is [Ar] 3d5 4s1; Cu is [Ar] 3d10 4s1.
