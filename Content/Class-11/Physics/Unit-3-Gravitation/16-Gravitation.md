# Gravitation

@study
Newton's law of universal gravitation: every particle attracts every other with a force F = G m1 m2/r^2, directed along the line joining them, where G = 6.67 x 10^-11 N m^2 kg^-2 (found by Cavendish). Gravitation is always attractive, a central and conservative force, and G is a universal constant. Acceleration due to gravity at the surface g = G M/R^2 = 9.8 m s^-2. Variation of g: with height h, g' = g (1 - 2h/R) for small h and g' = g R^2/(R + h)^2 in general; with depth d, g' = g (1 - d/R), and g is zero at the centre; with latitude, g is minimum at the equator and maximum at the poles; due to rotation, g' = g - R omega^2 cos^2 (latitude). Kepler's laws: (1) planets move in elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus; (2) the line joining a planet and the Sun sweeps equal areas in equal times (conservation of angular momentum); (3) T^2 is proportional to a^3 (T^2/a^3 = constant). Gravitational potential energy U = -G M m/r; gravitational potential V = -G M/r; potential energy near the surface = m g h. Escape velocity v_e = sqrt(2 G M/R) = sqrt(2 g R) = 11.2 km/s for the earth; it does not depend on the mass of the body. Orbital velocity of a satellite near the earth v_o = sqrt(g R) = 7.9 km/s, and v_e = sqrt(2) v_o. For a satellite at height h, v_o = sqrt(G M/(R + h)); time period T = 2 pi sqrt((R + h)^3/(G M)); about 84 minutes near the earth's surface. Energy of a satellite: kinetic energy = G M m/(2r), potential energy = -G M m/r, total = -G M m/(2r); the binding energy is G M m/(2r). A geostationary satellite orbits in the equatorial plane in the same direction as the earth rotates with a period of 24 hours at a height of about 36,000 km; it appears fixed in the sky and is used for communication. Polar satellites have low orbits passing over poles and are used for remote sensing. Weightlessness occurs in a satellite because both satellite and astronaut are in free fall. Gravitational field intensity E = F/m = G M/r^2 and has the same units as acceleration. Inside a hollow shell the field is zero. Mass of the earth M = g R^2/G = 6 x 10^24 kg.
## Deep dive: Law of gravitation
Force varies inversely with the square of distance. Gravitational force is weakest of the four fundamental forces but acts over long distance. Mass determines the force; weight is the gravitational force on a body.
## Deep dive: Kepler and orbits
Planets move faster near the Sun (perihelion) and slower far away (aphelion). Orbital period depends only on the semi-major axis. Satellites move in circles or ellipses under gravity.
## Deep dive: Escape and satellites
Escape velocity is the minimum speed to leave the earth's gravity. It is independent of direction and mass. Satellites have negative total energy, meaning they are bound.
