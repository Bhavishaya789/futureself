**Newton's law of gravitation:** F = G m1 m2 / r^2 with G = 6.67 x 10^-11 N m^2/kg^2.
**Acceleration due to gravity:** g = G M / R^2 (about 9.8 m/s^2 on Earth). Variation: g decreases with height (g' = g (R/(R+h))^2), decreases with depth (g' = g (1 - d/R)), and varies with latitude.
**Gravitational potential energy** of two masses U = - G M m / r.

## Escape and orbital velocity
- Orbital velocity near the surface v_o = sqrt(g R) = sqrt(G M / R) = about **7.9 km/s**.
- Escape velocity v_e = sqrt(2 g R) = sqrt(2) v_o = about **11.2 km/s**.
Computed with G = 6.67e-11, M = 5.97e24 kg, R = 6.371e6 m: v_e = 11.18 km/s and v_o = 7.91 km/s.

## Kepler's laws
1. Planets move in ellipses with the Sun at one focus.
2. The line joining a planet to the Sun sweeps equal areas in equal times (angular momentum conserved).
3. T^2 is proportional to a^3.

## Satellites
Time period T = 2 pi sqrt(r^3 / G M). A **geostationary** satellite has period 24 h, orbits in the equatorial plane at about 36,000 km above the surface.

Q: State Kepler's third law.
A: The square of the orbital period is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis, T^2 proportional to a^3.
Q: Why is the weight zero in a satellite?
A: The satellite and its contents are in free fall; there is no normal reaction, so apparent weight is zero though gravity acts.
Q: Relation between escape and orbital velocity?
A: v_e = sqrt(2) v_o.
Q: Value of g at height R above the Earth's surface?
A: g/4, because the distance from the centre is 2R.
