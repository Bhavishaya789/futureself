# System of Particles and Rotational Motion
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 physics rotational motion centre of mass torque moment of inertia angular momentum
@study
A rigid body is one in which the distance between any two particles remains constant. The centre of mass of a system of particles is x_cm = (m1 x1 + m2 x2 + ...)/(m1 + m2 + ...); the centre of mass of a uniform rod is at its midpoint, of a ring at its centre, and of a triangular lamina at its centroid. The motion of the centre of mass is as if all mass is concentrated there and all external forces act there; if no external force acts, the centre of mass moves with constant velocity. Rotational motion: angular displacement theta (radian), angular velocity omega = d theta/dt, angular acceleration alpha = d omega/dt, with v = r omega and a_t = r alpha. Equations of rotational motion are omega = omega_0 + alpha t; theta = omega_0 t + (1/2) alpha t^2; omega^2 = omega_0^2 + 2 alpha theta. Torque tau = r x F = r F sin theta is the turning effect of a force, with unit N m. Moment of inertia I = sum m r^2 is the rotational analogue of mass. Torque = I alpha. Rotational kinetic energy = (1/2) I omega^2. Angular momentum L = I omega = r x p; torque is the rate of change of angular momentum. Conservation of angular momentum: if net external torque is zero, L is constant (ice skater spinning faster by pulling arms in; diver). Moment of inertia of common bodies about the axis through the centre: ring M R^2; disc (1/2) M R^2; solid sphere (2/5) M R^2; hollow sphere (2/3) M R^2; rod about its centre (1/12) M L^2; rod about an end (1/3) M L^2; solid cylinder (1/2) M R^2. Radius of gyration k is given by I = M k^2. Theorem of parallel axes: I = I_cm + M d^2. Theorem of perpendicular axes (for planar bodies): I_z = I_x + I_y. Rolling motion without slipping has v = R omega, and the total kinetic energy = (1/2) M v^2 (1 + k^2/R^2). A body rolling down an incline has acceleration a = g sin theta/(1 + k^2/R^2). Equilibrium of a rigid body requires net force zero and net torque zero. Couple is a pair of equal and opposite forces with torque equal to force times perpendicular distance. Principle of moments: in equilibrium sum of clockwise moments equals sum of anticlockwise moments. Centre of gravity coincides with the centre of mass in a uniform field.
## Deep dive: Centre of mass
Position is the mass-weighted average. It may lie outside the body (a ring). For two particles the centre of mass lies closer to the heavier one. Explosion in flight does not change centre of mass motion.
## Deep dive: Rotation
Angular quantities mirror linear ones: omega like v, alpha like a, I like m, torque like F, L like p. Moment of inertia depends on mass distribution and the axis.
## Deep dive: Angular momentum and rolling
Without external torque L is conserved. Rolling combines translation and rotation. At the point of contact the velocity is zero. A solid sphere reaches the bottom of an incline before a hollow one.
