## Vectors
A vector has magnitude and direction. Add by the **triangle/parallelogram law**; resolve into components A_x = A cos t, A_y = A sin t. Resultant of two vectors with angle t: R = sqrt(A^2 + B^2 + 2AB cos t). Dot product A.B = AB cos t (scalar); cross product has magnitude AB sin t (vector).

## Projectile motion
A body thrown with speed u at angle t above the horizontal moves under gravity only. Horizontal velocity is constant; vertical motion has acceleration -g.
- Time of flight T = 2 u sin t / g
- Maximum height H = u^2 sin^2 t / (2 g)
- Range R = u^2 sin 2t / g (maximum at 45 degrees, equal for t and 90 - t)
- Path is a parabola.

### Worked example (u = 20 m/s, t = 30 degrees, g = 10)
T = 2(20)(0.5)/10 = **2 s**; H = 400(0.25)/20 = **5 m**; R = 400(0.866)/10 = **34.64 m**.

## Circular motion
Uniform circular motion: speed constant, direction changes. Centripetal acceleration a = v^2 / r = w^2 r directed to the centre.

A: The horizontal component, because no horizontal force acts (ignoring air).

A: 45 degrees, where sin 2t = 1.
Q: Write the centripetal acceleration formula.
A: a = v^2 / r = w^2 r, towards the centre.
Q: Range for u = 20 m/s at 30 degrees?
A: R = 400 x sin 60 / 10 = 34.64 m.

## Advanced
P: Show that the range equals 4 H / tan t for a projectile.
A: R = 2 u^2 sin t cos t / g and H = u^2 sin^2 t / 2g. So 4H / tan t = 2 u^2 sin^2 t / g x cos t / sin t = 2 u^2 sin t cos t / g = R.
