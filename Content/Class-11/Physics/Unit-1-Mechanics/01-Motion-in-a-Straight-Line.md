@study
**Kinematics** describes motion without asking what causes it.

## Key quantities
- **Distance** is the total path length (scalar). **Displacement** is the change in position (vector).
- **Speed** = distance / time. **Velocity** = displacement / time. **Acceleration** = change of velocity / time.
- Uniform acceleration: area under a v-t graph = displacement; slope of v-t = acceleration; slope of x-t = velocity.

## Equations of motion (constant acceleration a)
1. v = u + a t
2. s = u t + (1/2) a t^2
3. v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s
4. s(nth second) = u + a (2n - 1) / 2

### Worked examples (g = 10 m/s^2)
- u = 10 m/s, a = 2 m/s^2, t = 5 s: v = 20 m/s and s = 10(5) + (1/2)(2)(25) = **75 m**.
- A car at 20 m/s brakes with a = -4 m/s^2: stopping distance = 20^2 / (2 x 4) = **50 m**, time = 20/4 = **5 s**.
- A stone dropped from 80 m: t = sqrt(2 x 80 / 10) = **4 s**, landing speed = 10 x 4 = **40 m/s**.

## Relative velocity in one dimension
v(A relative to B) = v(A) - v(B). Two trains moving towards each other at 30 and 20 m/s approach at 50 m/s.

@qa
Q: Difference between distance and displacement?
A: Distance is the total length of the path travelled (scalar); displacement is the straight-line change in position with direction (vector).
Q: What does the slope of a velocity-time graph give?
A: The acceleration. The area under it gives displacement.
Q: Why can displacement be zero when distance is not?
A: If the body returns to its starting point the net change in position is zero, though it has travelled a path.
Q: Stopping distance of a car at 20 m/s with deceleration 4 m/s^2?
A: s = v^2 / (2a) = 400 / 8 = 50 m.

## Moderate
P: A car moving at 20 m/s brakes uniformly at 4 m/s^2. Find the stopping time and distance.
A: t = 20/4 = 5 s. s = 20^2/(2 x 4) = 50 m.

## High
P: A ball is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s (g = 10). Find the maximum height and the total time of flight.
A: At the top v = 0: h = u^2/(2g) = 400/20 = 20 m. Time up = u/g = 2 s, so total time = 4 s.
R: 2 | h = 20 m
R: 2 | Total time 4 s
## Advanced
P: A body falls from rest for n seconds. Show that the distance covered in the nth second is g(2n-1)/2 and evaluate it for n = 4 with g = 10.
A: s(n) - s(n-1) = (1/2)g n^2 - (1/2)g (n-1)^2 = g(2n - 1)/2. For n = 4: 10 x 7 / 2 = 35 m.
