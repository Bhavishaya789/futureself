# Motion in a Straight Line
minutes: 45
yt: class 11 physics motion in a straight line kinematics equations graphs
@study
Kinematics describes motion without considering its causes. An object is at rest or in motion relative to a frame of reference. Motion in one dimension is along a straight line. Distance is the total path length (scalar); displacement is the shortest change in position with direction (vector). Speed is distance divided by time (scalar); velocity is displacement divided by time (vector). Average speed = total distance/total time; average velocity = displacement/time. Instantaneous velocity is the limit of displacement per unit time as the time interval tends to zero, v = dx/dt. Acceleration a = dv/dt = change in velocity per unit time; retardation (deceleration) is negative acceleration. For uniform acceleration the equations of motion are v = u + at; s = ut + (1/2) a t^2; v^2 = u^2 + 2as; and the distance in the nth second s_n = u + a(2n - 1)/2. Free fall: an object released from rest falls with acceleration g = 9.8 m s^-2 (about 10) toward the earth if air resistance is neglected; for upward motion use a = -g. The time of ascent equals the time of descent, the maximum height is u^2/(2g) and the time of flight is 2u/g. Graphs: in a position-time graph the slope gives velocity; in a velocity-time graph the slope gives acceleration and the area under the graph gives displacement; in an acceleration-time graph the area gives change in velocity. A straight horizontal line in an x-t graph means rest; a straight inclined line means uniform velocity; a curve means acceleration. Relative velocity: velocity of A relative to B is v_AB = v_A - v_B; for objects moving in opposite directions speeds add. Stopping distance = v^2/(2a). Calculus form: v = dx/dt, a = dv/dt = v dv/dx; integrate to find x and v. Reaction time and braking distance are important in road safety.
## Deep dive: Basic quantities
Position is relative to an origin. Distance is never negative; displacement may be zero for a round trip. Speed is the magnitude of velocity only for instantaneous values. Units: m/s and m/s^2.
## Deep dive: Equations of motion
These apply only for constant acceleration. Choose the sign convention first. Pick the equation that does not contain the unknown you do not need. For free fall take upward as positive and a = -g.
## Deep dive: Graphs
Slopes and areas on graphs give velocity, acceleration and displacement. A velocity-time graph with a negative slope shows deceleration. Area below the axis is negative displacement.
