@study
Scalars have magnitude only (mass, time, speed) and vectors have magnitude and direction (displacement, velocity, force). Vectors are added by the triangle law or parallelogram law. For two vectors A and B at angle theta, the magnitude of the resultant R = sqrt(A^2 + B^2 + 2AB cos theta) and the direction tan(alpha) = B sin theta/(A + B cos theta). Subtraction is addition of the negative vector. A vector can be resolved into rectangular components: A_x = A cos theta and A_y = A sin theta, with magnitude sqrt(A_x^2 + A_y^2). Unit vectors i, j, k point along x, y, z. The scalar (dot) product A.B = AB cos theta is a scalar, commutative; i.i = 1 and i.j = 0. The vector (cross) product A x B = AB sin theta n is a vector perpendicular to both; i x j = k and it is not commutative. Projectile motion: an object thrown at angle theta with speed u follows a parabola. Horizontal component u cos theta is constant; vertical component u sin theta changes with acceleration -g. Time of flight T = 2u sin theta/g; maximum height H = u^2 sin^2 theta/(2g); horizontal range R = u^2 sin 2 theta/g. Maximum range occurs at 45 degrees, equal ranges for angles theta and 90 - theta, and R = 4H/tan theta. The equation of the path is y = x tan theta - g x^2/(2u^2 cos^2 theta). For a horizontal projection from a height h, the time of fall is sqrt(2h/g). Uniform circular motion: speed is constant but velocity changes direction, so there is acceleration directed toward the centre, a_c = v^2/r = omega^2 r; the period T = 2 pi r/v, frequency f = 1/T, angular velocity omega = 2 pi/T = 2 pi f and v = r omega. Angular displacement is in radians. Centripetal force F = m v^2/r. Relative velocity in two dimensions uses vector subtraction, as in rain-man and river-boat problems.
## Deep dive: Vectors
The parallelogram law finds the resultant. Component method is easier for many vectors. Dot product gives work; cross product gives torque. Zero vector has zero magnitude.
## Deep dive: Projectile
Horizontal and vertical motions are independent. At the highest point the vertical velocity is zero but horizontal velocity remains. Range is symmetric about 45 degrees. Air resistance is ignored.
## Deep dive: Circular motion
Centripetal acceleration points to the centre. Speed is constant in uniform circular motion. Angular speed omega = v/r. Examples: planets, stones whirled on strings.
