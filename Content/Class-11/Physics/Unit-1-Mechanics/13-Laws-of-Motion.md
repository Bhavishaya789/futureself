# Laws of Motion
minutes: 45

@study
Newton's laws of motion explain the cause of motion. First law (law of inertia): a body remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced external force. Inertia is the property of resisting change in the state of motion; mass is its measure. Second law: the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force, F = dp/dt = m a (for constant mass). Linear momentum p = m v with unit kg m s^-1. The SI unit of force is the newton: 1 N = 1 kg m s^-2. Third law: to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction; action and reaction act on different bodies. Impulse J = F x dt = change in momentum; the unit is N s. The law of conservation of linear momentum states that if no external force acts, total momentum remains constant, and it is applied in recoil of a gun, rocket propulsion and collisions. Equilibrium of concurrent forces needs the vector sum to be zero; Lami's theorem relates three forces. Friction opposes relative motion: static friction f_s <= mu_s N, kinetic friction f_k = mu_k N with mu_k less than mu_s, and rolling friction is smaller. The angle of friction is tan^-1 mu; the angle of repose equals the angle of friction. On an incline of angle theta the component along the plane is mg sin theta and perpendicular is mg cos theta; a body just slides when tan theta = mu. Circular motion needs a centripetal force F = m v^2/r provided by tension, friction or gravity; for a vehicle on a level road v_max = sqrt(mu r g); on a banked road v = sqrt(r g tan theta) with no friction; for a vertical circle the minimum speed at the lowest point is sqrt(5 g r) and at the top sqrt(g r). Weight is mg; apparent weight in a lift is m(g plus or minus a). Free body diagrams show all forces on a body. Tension in a string and normal reaction are contact forces. For two blocks connected by a string, acceleration a = (m1 - m2) g/(m1 + m2) in an Atwood machine. Pseudo force acts in non-inertial frames.
## Deep dive: Newton's laws
The first law defines inertia and force. The second law gives the measure of force. The third law explains pairs of forces. Forces of action and reaction never cancel because they act on different bodies.
## Deep dive: Momentum and impulse
Conservation of momentum holds in collisions and explosions. Impulse equals change in momentum. A cricketer draws the hands back to increase the time and lower the force.
## Deep dive: Friction and circular motion
Friction depends on the nature of surfaces and normal reaction, not on area. Kinetic friction is less than limiting static friction. Banking reduces the need for friction. Free body diagrams make problems simple.
