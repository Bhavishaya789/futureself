@study
Closing stock at lower of cost and NRV; trading account, profit and loss account and balance sheet.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Gross profit = Sales - COGS**: rule
- **Net profit = GP + other income - expenses**: rule
- **COGS = Opening stock + Purchases - Closing stock**: rule
- **Capital = Assets - Liabilities**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Closing stock appears on the credit side of the trading account and as a current asset.
- Carriage inwards is debited to the trading account.
- Carriage outwards is debited to the profit and loss account.
- Outstanding expenses are added to the expense and shown as a liability.
- Prepaid expenses are deducted from the expense and shown as an asset.
- Bad debts are written off to the profit and loss account.
- Provision for doubtful debts is created on closing debtors.
- Drawings reduce the capital of the proprietor.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In CA Foundation, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Revise this chapter in three passes: the formula sheet, the facts above, then the generated numerical questions with a timer.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Attempt the generated questions in sets of ten, mark each answer, and note the reason for every miss. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
