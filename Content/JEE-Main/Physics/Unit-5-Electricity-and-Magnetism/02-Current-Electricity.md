# Current Electricity
minutes: 40
yt: JEE Main Current Electricity
@study
Ohm's law, resistivity, Kirchhoff's rules, meter bridge, potentiometer and heating effect of current.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **V = IR**: Ohm
- **R = ρl/A**: resistance
- **P = VI = I^2R**: power
- **Rs = R1 + R2; 1/Rp = 1/R1 + 1/R2**: rule
- **E = V + Ir**: cell

**Facts to remember**
- Drift velocity is of the order of 10^-4 m/s.
- Resistivity of a metal increases with temperature.
- Resistivity of a semiconductor decreases with temperature.
- Kirchhoff's junction rule expresses conservation of charge.
- Kirchhoff's loop rule expresses conservation of energy.
- Potentiometer measures emf without drawing current at balance.
- A voltmeter should have very high resistance.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In JEE Main, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
