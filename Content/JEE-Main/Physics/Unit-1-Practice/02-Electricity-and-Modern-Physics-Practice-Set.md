## Formula sheet
- RC time constant tau = R C; charging V = V0 (1 - e^(-t/tau)).
- Photoelectric: K_max = h f - phi; E(eV) = 1240 / lambda(nm).
- Lens: 1/v - 1/u = 1/f.
- Heat: Q = m c dT (c of water 4200 J/kg K).
- Radioactivity: N = N0 / 2^(t/T).
## Strategy
Units first: convert uF, kohm, nm before substituting. Numerical-value questions (no options) need exact arithmetic, so write each step.
@qa
Q: What is the time constant of an RC circuit?
A: tau = R C, the time to reach about 63% of the final voltage.
Q: Energy needed to heat 2 kg of water by 50 K?
A: 2 x 4200 x 50 = 420,000 J.
Q: Photon energy for 400 nm light?
A: 3.1 eV.
