## Formula sheet
- Free fall: v = sqrt(2 g h); pendulum T = 2 pi sqrt(L/g); spring-mass T = 2 pi sqrt(m/k).
- Doppler (source approaching): f' = f v / (v - v_s).
- Isothermal work: W = n R T ln(V2/V1); adiabatic: P V^gamma = constant.
- Carnot efficiency: eta = 1 - T_cold / T_hot (kelvin).
- Heat: Q = m c dT.

## Strategy
Read the question twice, list givens in SI units, check the dimensions of the answer, eliminate options that are out by obvious factors. In JEE Main each correct MCQ gives +4 and a wrong one costs -1, so skip only when you cannot narrow to two options.
@qa
Q: Why use kelvin in Carnot efficiency?
A: The formula follows from the ratio of absolute temperatures; using Celsius gives wrong results.
Q: Period of a simple pendulum of length 1 m?
A: 2 pi sqrt(1/9.8) = 2.007 s.
Q: What is the change in internal energy in an isothermal process of an ideal gas?
A: Zero, since U depends on temperature only.

## Moderate
P: A body falls from 20 m. Find the speed on reaching the ground and the time taken (g = 10).
A: v = 20 m/s and t = 2 s.
R: 1 | v
R: 1 | t
## High
P: Find the efficiency of a Carnot engine between 500 K and 300 K and the heat rejected for 1000 J absorbed.
A: eta = 40%; Q_out = 600 J.
R: 2 | eta
R: 1 | Q_out
## Advanced
P: A source of frequency 500 Hz moves towards a stationary observer at 20 m/s and then away at 20 m/s. Find both observed frequencies (v = 340).
A: Approaching 531.25 Hz; receding 500 x 340/360 = 472.2 Hz.
R: 2 | Approaching
R: 2 | Receding
