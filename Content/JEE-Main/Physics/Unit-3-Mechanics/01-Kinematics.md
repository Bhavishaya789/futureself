# Kinematics

@study
Kinematics describes motion without asking its cause. Master the three equations of uniform acceleration, projectile motion and relative velocity.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **v = u + at**: v = u + a t (uniform acceleration)
- **s = ut + at^2/2**: s = u t + a t squared over 2 (displacement)
- **v^2 = u^2 + 2as**: v squared = u squared + 2 a s (no time given)
- **Range**: R = u^2 sin2θ / g (projectile)

**Facts to remember**
- Slope of a position-time graph gives velocity.
- Area under a velocity-time graph gives displacement.
- Slope of a velocity-time graph gives acceleration.
- At the top of a vertical throw the velocity is zero but acceleration is still g.
- Two projectiles with complementary angles have the same range.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In JEE Main, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic.
