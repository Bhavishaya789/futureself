# Work, Energy and Power
minutes: 40
yt: JEE Main Work, Energy and Power
@study
Work is force along displacement; energy is the capacity for work. Conservation of mechanical energy solves most problems quickly.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **W = F s cosθ**: work (constant force)
- **KE = mv^2/2**: kinetic energy
- **PE = mgh**: gravitational potential energy
- **P = W/t = Fv**: power
- **PE = kx^2/2**: spring energy

**Facts to remember**
- Work done by a conservative force is path independent.
- Work done by friction is negative and dissipates energy.
- Work-energy theorem says net work equals change in kinetic energy.
- Power is the rate of doing work.
- In an elastic collision kinetic energy is conserved.
- In a perfectly inelastic collision the bodies move together after impact.
- Area under a force-displacement graph gives work done.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude.
