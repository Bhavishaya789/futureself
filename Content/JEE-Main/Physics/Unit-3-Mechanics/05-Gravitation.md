@study
Newton's law of gravitation gives orbital motion, escape speed and satellite energy. g changes with height and depth.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **F = Gm1m2/r^2**: gravitational force
- **g = GM/R^2**: surface gravity
- **ve = √(2gR)**: escape speed 11.2 km/s
- **vo = √(gR)**: orbital speed near surface
- **T^2 ∝ r^3**: Kepler III

**Facts to remember**
- Escape speed does not depend on the mass of the projectile.
- Satellite orbital speed decreases as orbit radius increases.
- Geostationary satellite has a period of 24 hours and orbits above the equator.
- Kepler's second law expresses conservation of angular momentum.
- Weight at the centre of the Earth is zero.
- Gravitational potential energy is negative for bound systems.
- G is least at the equator and greatest at the poles.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In JEE Main, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Revise this chapter in three passes: the formula sheet, the facts above, then the generated numerical questions with a timer.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Attempt the generated questions in sets of ten, mark each answer, and note the reason for every miss. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
