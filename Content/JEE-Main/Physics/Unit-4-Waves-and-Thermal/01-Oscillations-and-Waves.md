# Oscillations and Waves
minutes: 40

@study
Simple harmonic motion, wave speed, superposition, standing waves, Doppler effect and beats.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **T = 2π√(m/k)**: spring
- **T = 2π√(l/g)**: simple pendulum
- **v = fλ**: wave speed
- **f' = f(v ± vo)/(v ∓ vs)**: Doppler
- **fbeat =**: f1 - f2

**Facts to remember**
- In SHM acceleration is proportional to displacement and directed to the mean.
- Total energy in SHM is constant and equals kA^2/2.
- Sound waves in air are longitudinal.
- Resonance occurs when driving frequency equals natural frequency.
- Closed organ pipe supports only odd harmonics.
- Open organ pipe supports all harmonics.
- Speed of a transverse wave on a string equals √(T/μ).

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In JEE Main, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Revise this chapter in three passes: the formula sheet, the facts above, then the generated numerical questions with a timer.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
