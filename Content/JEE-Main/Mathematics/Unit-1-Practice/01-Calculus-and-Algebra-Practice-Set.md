## Formula sheet
- Integration by parts: integral u dv = uv - integral v du. Integral of x e^x dx = (x - 1) e^x.
- Integral of sin^2 x over 0 to pi = pi/2.
- Limit: (e^x - 1 - x)/x^2 tends to 1/2 as x tends to 0.
- Determinant by expansion along a row; complex numbers (1 + i)^2 = 2i.
## Strategy
Use standard limits and symmetry (even/odd) to shorten integrals; check answers by substituting simple values.
