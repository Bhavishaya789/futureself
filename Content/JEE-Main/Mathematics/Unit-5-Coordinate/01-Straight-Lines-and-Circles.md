Distance, section formula, slope, line forms, circle equations and tangents.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **d = √((x2-x1)² + (y2-y1)²)**: rule
- **m = (y2-y1)/(x2-x1)**: rule
- **Distance from point to line =**: ax1+by1+c (/√(a²+b²))
- **Circle: (x-h)² + (y-k)² = r²**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Slopes of perpendicular lines have product -1.
- Parallel lines have equal slopes.
- Radical axis of two circles is a straight line.
- A tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
- Condition for y = mx + c to touch x² + y² = a² is c² = a²(1 + m²).
- Centroid divides each median in the ratio 2:1.
