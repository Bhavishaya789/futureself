@study
Mean, median, mode, variance and standard deviation.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Mean = Σx/n**: rule
- **Variance = Σ(x - mean)²/n**: rule
- **SD = √variance**: rule
- **Coefficient of variation = SD/mean × 100**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Adding a constant to all observations changes the mean but not the standard deviation.
- Multiplying all observations by k multiplies the standard deviation by |k|.
- Median is not affected by extreme values.
- Mode is the most frequent value.
- Mean deviation can be taken about the mean or median.
- Variance is the square of standard deviation.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude.
