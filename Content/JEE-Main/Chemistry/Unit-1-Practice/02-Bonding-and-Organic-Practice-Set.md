## Key facts
- Hybridisation: CH4 sp3 (109.5 degrees), C2H4 sp2 (120), C2H2 sp (180). Bond order: N2 = 3, O2 = 2, O2^- = 1.5, O2^2- = 1.
- SN1: tertiary, racemisation. SN2: primary, inversion.
- Saytzeff: the more substituted alkene is major.
## Strategy
Draw structures; count sigma bonds and lone pairs to decide hybridisation.

## Moderate
P: Give the hybridisation and shape of CH4, C2H4 and C2H2.
A: sp3 tetrahedral; sp2 planar; sp linear.

## High
P: Compare the bond orders of N2, O2 and O2^-.
A: 3, 2 and 1.5; stability and bond strength order N2 > O2 > O2^-.
R: 2 | Values
R: 1 | Order
## Advanced
P: Explain why O2 is paramagnetic using molecular orbital theory.
A: The last two electrons occupy the degenerate pi* orbitals singly with parallel spins, giving two unpaired electrons.
