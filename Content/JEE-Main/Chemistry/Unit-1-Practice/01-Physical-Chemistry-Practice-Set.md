## Formula sheet
- n = m/M; M = n/V (litres); gas volume at STP 22.4 L/mol.
- Henderson-Hasselbalch: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]).
- dG = -n F E; first-order t1/2 = 0.693/k.
## Strategy
Write the unit with every number; for pH use logs of ratios: log 2 = 0.301.
