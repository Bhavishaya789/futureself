## Key facts
Mole concept: 1 mol = 6.022 x 10^23; molar mass H2O 18 g/mol. Atomic structure: E_n = -13.6/n^2 eV. pH = -log[H+]. Thermochemistry: dG = dH - T dS. NCERT organic reaction trends: SN1/SN2, Lucas test.
## Strategy
In NEET chemistry, NCERT lines (periodic trends, coordination compounds, biomolecules) are worth more than long numericals.

## Advanced
P: Differentiate primary, secondary and tertiary alcohols using the Lucas test.
A: Tertiary turbid at once; secondary in about 5 min; primary no turbidity at room temperature.
