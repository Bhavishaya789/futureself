@study
Cell potential, Nernst equation, Faraday laws, rate laws and half-lives.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode**: cell
- **ΔG = -nFE**: work
- **m = ZIt**: Faraday
- **k = 0.693/t1/2**: first order
- **k = A e^(-Ea/RT)**: Arrhenius

**Facts to remember**
- Salt bridge maintains electrical neutrality in a galvanic cell.
- Oxidation occurs at the anode.
- Reduction occurs at the cathode.
- Half life of a first order reaction is independent of initial concentration.
- Order of reaction is found experimentally.
- Molecularity is always a whole number.
- Rate constant increases with temperature.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude.
