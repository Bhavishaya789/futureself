## Key facts
- Heart: SA node pacemaker; cardiac output = heart rate x stroke volume (72 x 70 mL is about 5 L/min). ECG: P atrial depolarisation, QRS ventricular depolarisation, T repolarisation.
- Photosynthesis: Calvin cycle in stroma; RuBisCO fixes CO2; C4 plants (maize) use PEP carboxylase.
- Ecology: 10 percent law of energy transfer; food chains; pyramids of numbers, biomass, energy.
## Strategy
For ecology and environment questions, remember named examples and definitions from NCERT.
