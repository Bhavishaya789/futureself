**Exam-style practice (original questions in the NEET pattern; not an official past paper).** Biology carries 90 of the 180 NEET questions, mostly NCERT-based.
## Key facts
- Cell: mitochondria (ATP), ribosomes 70S in prokaryotes, 80S in eukaryotes; meiosis gives four haploid cells.
- Genetics: dihybrid ratio 9:3:3:1; Hardy-Weinberg p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1; ABO codominance.
- DNA: A-T 2 bonds, G-C 3 bonds; start codon AUG.
## Strategy
Read every NCERT line and box; learn tables of differences; revise diagrams.

R: 1 | 0.32
## Advanced
P: Explain why colour blindness is more common in males.
A: It is X-linked recessive; males have one X so one defective allele is expressed, females need two.
R: 2 | X-linked recessive
R: 2 | Hemizygous males
