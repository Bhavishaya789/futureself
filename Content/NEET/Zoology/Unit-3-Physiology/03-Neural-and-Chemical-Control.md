# Neural Control and Chemical Coordination

Nervous system, action potential, sense organs and endocrine glands.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Neuron**: is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system
- **Resting membrane potential**: is about -70 mV
- **Sodium-potassium pump**: moves 3 Na out and 2 K in
- **Synapse**: transmits impulses between neurons
- **Acetylcholine**: is a neurotransmitter at neuromuscular junctions
- **Myelin sheath**: speeds up conduction

**Facts to remember**
- Neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
- Resting membrane potential is about -70 mV.
- Sodium-potassium pump moves 3 Na out and 2 K in.
- Synapse transmits impulses between neurons.
- Acetylcholine is a neurotransmitter at neuromuscular junctions.
- Myelin sheath speeds up conduction.
- Cerebrum controls intelligence and memory.
- Hypothalamus controls body temperature and hunger.
- Medulla controls heart beat and breathing.
- Cerebellum maintains balance.
- Reflex arc includes receptor, sensory neuron, interneuron, motor neuron and effector.
- Rods are for dim light vision.
- Cones are for colour vision.
- Cochlea is the organ of hearing.
