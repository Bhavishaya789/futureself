# Digestion, Absorption and Breathing
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Digestion, Absorption and Breathing
@study
Human digestive enzymes, absorption, and mechanism of respiration.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Salivary amylase**: begins starch digestion in the mouth
- **Pepsin**: digests proteins in the stomach
- **Hydrochloric acid**: activates pepsinogen
- **Trypsin**: is secreted as inactive trypsinogen
- **Bile**: emulsifies fats and is made in the liver
- **Pancreatic lipase**: digests fats

**Facts to remember**
- Salivary amylase begins starch digestion in the mouth.
- Pepsin digests proteins in the stomach.
- Hydrochloric acid activates pepsinogen.
- Trypsin is secreted as inactive trypsinogen.
- Bile emulsifies fats and is made in the liver.
- Pancreatic lipase digests fats.
- Enterokinase activates trypsinogen.
- Villi increase the surface area for absorption.
- Lacteals absorb fats.
- Peristalsis moves food in the alimentary canal.
- Kwashiorkor is caused by protein deficiency.
- Marasmus is caused by both protein and calorie deficiency.
- Vital capacity is the maximum air exhaled after a deep inhalation.
- Tidal volume is about 500 mL.
