# Locomotion and Movement
minutes: 40

Muscle structure, sliding filament theory and the skeletal system.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Skeletal muscle**: is striated and voluntary
- **Smooth muscle**: is involuntary and unstriated
- **Cardiac muscle**: is striated and involuntary
- **Sarcomere**: is the functional unit of contraction
- **Actin**: forms the thin filament
- **Myosin**: forms the thick filament

**Facts to remember**
- Skeletal muscle is striated and voluntary.
- Smooth muscle is involuntary and unstriated.
- Cardiac muscle is striated and involuntary.
- Sarcomere is the functional unit of contraction.
- Actin forms the thin filament.
- Myosin forms the thick filament.
- Sliding filament theory was proposed by Huxley and Hanson.
- Troponin binds calcium during contraction.
- Calcium ions are released from the sarcoplasmic reticulum.
- Myoglobin gives red muscle its colour.
- Human skeleton has 206 bones.
- Skull has 22 bones.
- Vertebral column has 33 vertebrae in the embryo and 26 in the adult.
- Ribs are 12 pairs.
