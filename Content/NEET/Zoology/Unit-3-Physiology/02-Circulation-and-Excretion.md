Blood, the heart and cardiac cycle, urine formation and osmoregulation.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Erythrocytes**: lack a nucleus in mammals
- **Leucocytes**: defend the body
- **Platelets**: help in blood clotting
- **Blood group A**: has antigen A and anti-B antibodies
- **Blood group O**: is the universal donor
- **Blood group AB**: is the universal recipient

**Facts to remember**
- Erythrocytes lack a nucleus in mammals.
- Leucocytes defend the body.
- Platelets help in blood clotting.
- Blood group A has antigen A and anti-B antibodies.
- Blood group O is the universal donor.
- Blood group AB is the universal recipient.
- Rh incompatibility causes erythroblastosis foetalis.
- Human heart is four chambered.
- SA node is the pacemaker.
- Systole is contraction and diastole is relaxation.
- Cardiac output equals stroke volume times heart rate.
- ECG records electrical activity of the heart.
- QRS complex represents ventricular depolarisation.
- Double circulation occurs in mammals.
