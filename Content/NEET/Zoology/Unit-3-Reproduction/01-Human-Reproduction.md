Gametogenesis, the menstrual cycle, fertilisation, development, contraception and ART.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Spermatogenesis**: occurs in the seminiferous tubules
- **Sertoli cells**: nourish the sperms
- **Leydig cells**: secrete androgens
- **Oogenesis**: begins during foetal life
- **Menstrual cycle**: is about 28 days
- **Ovulation**: occurs on about the 14th day

**Facts to remember**
- Spermatogenesis occurs in the seminiferous tubules.
- Sertoli cells nourish the sperms.
- Leydig cells secrete androgens.
- Oogenesis begins during foetal life.
- Menstrual cycle is about 28 days.
- Ovulation occurs on about the 14th day.
- LH surge triggers ovulation.
- Corpus luteum secretes progesterone.
- Fertilisation occurs in the ampulla of the fallopian tube.
- Implantation occurs at the blastocyst stage.
- Placenta secretes hCG hPL and relaxin.
- Gestation period in humans is about nine months.
- Colostrum is the first milk rich in antibodies.
- Copper-T is an intrauterine device.
