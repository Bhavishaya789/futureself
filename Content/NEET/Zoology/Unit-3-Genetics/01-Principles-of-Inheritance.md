# Principles of Inheritance and Variation
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Principles of Inheritance and Variation
@study
Mendelian inheritance, sex determination, linkage and genetic disorders.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Incomplete dominance**: gives 1:2:1 in the F2 of Antirrhinum
- **Codominance**: is shown by AB blood group
- **Multiple alleles**: control ABO blood groups
- **Epistasis**: is interaction between non-allelic genes
- **Linkage**: was studied by Morgan in Drosophila
- **XX-XY system**: is found in humans

**Facts to remember**
- Incomplete dominance gives 1:2:1 in the F2 of Antirrhinum.
- Codominance is shown by AB blood group.
- Multiple alleles control ABO blood groups.
- Epistasis is interaction between non-allelic genes.
- Linkage was studied by Morgan in Drosophila.
- XX-XY system is found in humans.
- XX-XO system is found in grasshoppers.
- ZW-ZZ system is found in birds.
- Haemophilia is an X linked recessive disorder.
- Colour blindness is an X linked recessive disorder.
- Sickle cell anaemia is an autosomal recessive disorder.
- Phenylketonuria is caused by lack of phenylalanine hydroxylase.
- Down syndrome is trisomy 21.
- Klinefelter syndrome has the karyotype 47 XXY.
