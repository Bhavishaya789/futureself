# Animal Kingdom and Microbes in Human Welfare
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Animal Kingdom and Microbes in Human Welfare
@study
Classification of animals and the use of microbes in food, industry and sewage treatment.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Porifera**: are sponges with canal systems and choanocytes
- **Cnidaria**: have cnidoblasts and are diploblastic
- **Platyhelminthes**: are flatworms with flame cells
- **Aschelminthes**: are roundworms with a pseudocoelom
- **Annelida**: show true metamerism
- **Arthropoda**: is the largest phylum

**Facts to remember**
- Porifera are sponges with canal systems and choanocytes.
- Cnidaria have cnidoblasts and are diploblastic.
- Platyhelminthes are flatworms with flame cells.
- Aschelminthes are roundworms with a pseudocoelom.
- Annelida show true metamerism.
- Arthropoda is the largest phylum.
- Mollusca have a calcareous shell and radula.
- Echinodermata have a water vascular system.
- Chordata have a notochord and dorsal hollow nerve cord.
- Cyclostomes are jawless vertebrates.
- Aves have pneumatic bones.
- Mammalia have mammary glands and hair.
- Lactobacillus converts milk to curd.
- Saccharomyces cerevisiae is used in bread and beer.
