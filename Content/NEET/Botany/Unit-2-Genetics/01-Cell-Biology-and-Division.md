# Cell: the Unit of Life and Cell Division
minutes: 40

Cell organelles and the cell cycle with mitosis and meiosis.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Cell theory**: was proposed by Schleiden and Schwann
- **Omnis cellula e cellula**: was added by Rudolf Virchow
- **Mitochondria**: are the powerhouse of the cell
- **Ribosomes**: are the site of protein synthesis
- **Lysosomes**: contain hydrolytic enzymes
- **Golgi apparatus**: packages and secretes materials

**Facts to remember**
- Cell theory was proposed by Schleiden and Schwann.
- Omnis cellula e cellula was added by Rudolf Virchow.
- Mitochondria are the powerhouse of the cell.
- Ribosomes are the site of protein synthesis.
- Lysosomes contain hydrolytic enzymes.
- Golgi apparatus packages and secretes materials.
- Plastids are present only in plant cells.
- Cell wall of plants is made of cellulose.
- Plasma membrane is described by the fluid mosaic model of Singer and Nicolson.
- Nucleolus is the site of rRNA synthesis.
- Interphase has G1, S and G2 phases.
- DNA replication occurs in the S phase.
- Mitosis keeps the chromosome number the same.
- Meiosis halves the chromosome number.
