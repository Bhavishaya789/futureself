# Molecular Basis of Inheritance

@study
DNA is the genetic material; replication, transcription and translation express the genetic code.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Griffith**: discovered transformation in Streptococcus pneumoniae
- **Hershey and Chase**: proved DNA is the genetic material using bacteriophages
- **Watson and Crick**: proposed the double helix model
- **Chargaff's rule**: says A equals T and G equals C
- **DNA replication**: is semiconservative as shown by Meselson and Stahl
- **DNA polymerase**: synthesises DNA in the 5 to 3 direction

**Facts to remember**
- Griffith discovered transformation in Streptococcus pneumoniae.
- Hershey and Chase proved DNA is the genetic material using bacteriophages.
- Watson and Crick proposed the double helix model.
- Chargaff's rule says A equals T and G equals C.
- DNA replication is semiconservative as shown by Meselson and Stahl.
- DNA polymerase synthesises DNA in the 5 to 3 direction.
- Okazaki fragments are formed on the lagging strand.
- Transcription makes RNA from DNA using RNA polymerase.
- Promoter is the site where RNA polymerase binds.
- Splicing removes introns in eukaryotes.
- Genetic code is triplet, degenerate and universal.
- AUG is the start codon and codes for methionine.
- UAA, UAG and UGA are stop codons.
- TRNA is the adapter molecule.
