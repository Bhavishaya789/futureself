# Photosynthesis and Respiration in Plants
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Photosynthesis and Respiration in Plants
@study
Light reactions make ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle fixes CO2. Respiration releases energy through glycolysis, Krebs cycle and the electron transport chain.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Photosynthesis**: occurs in chloroplasts
- **Light reaction**: occurs in the thylakoid membranes
- **Calvin cycle**: occurs in the stroma
- **Photolysis of water**: releases oxygen
- **RuBisCO**: is the most abundant enzyme on earth
- **First stable product in C3 plants**: is 3-phosphoglyceric acid

**Facts to remember**
- Photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts.
- Light reaction occurs in the thylakoid membranes.
- Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma.
- Photolysis of water releases oxygen.
- RuBisCO is the most abundant enzyme on earth.
- First stable product in C3 plants is 3-phosphoglyceric acid.
- First stable product in C4 plants is oxaloacetic acid.
- C4 plants show Kranz anatomy.
- Photorespiration is absent in C4 plants.
- Cyclic photophosphorylation involves only PS I and produces ATP.
- Chlorophyll a is the primary pigment of the reaction centre.
- Glycolysis occurs in the cytoplasm.
- Krebs cycle occurs in the mitochondrial matrix.
- Electron transport chain occurs in the inner mitochondrial membrane.
