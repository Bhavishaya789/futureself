# Plant Growth, Hormones and Mineral Nutrition
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Plant Growth, Hormones and Mineral Nutrition
@study
Hormones coordinate growth. Plants need macro and micronutrients; deficiency gives characteristic symptoms.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Auxin**: promotes apical dominance and root initiation
- **Gibberellin**: promotes stem elongation and bolting
- **Cytokinin**: promotes cell division and delays senescence
- **Ethylene**: is a gaseous hormone that ripens fruits
- **Abscisic acid**: is the stress hormone that closes stomata
- **Photoperiodism**: is the response to day length

**Facts to remember**
- Auxin promotes apical dominance and root initiation.
- Gibberellin promotes stem elongation and bolting.
- Cytokinin promotes cell division and delays senescence.
- Ethylene is a gaseous hormone that ripens fruits.
- Abscisic acid is the stress hormone that closes stomata.
- Photoperiodism is the response to day length.
- Vernalisation is the promotion of flowering by cold treatment.
- Phytochrome is the pigment for photoperiodic responses.
- Nitrogen fixation is done by Rhizobium in root nodules.
- Leghaemoglobin protects nitrogenase from oxygen.
- Nitrogenase converts N2 to ammonia.
- Nitrification converts ammonia to nitrate by Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter.
- Macronutrients include nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, calcium, magnesium and sulphur.
- Magnesium is a component of chlorophyll.
