# Ecology, Biodiversity and Environmental Issues

Ecosystems, energy flow, biodiversity and conservation.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Ecosystem**: includes biotic and abiotic components
- **Primary productivity**: is the rate of production of organic matter by producers
- **Ten percent law**: was proposed by Lindeman
- **Pyramid of energy**: is always upright
- **Pyramid of numbers**: in a tree ecosystem can be inverted
- **Detritus food chain**: begins with dead organic matter

**Facts to remember**
- Ecosystem includes biotic and abiotic components.
- Primary productivity is the rate of production of organic matter by producers.
- Ten percent law was proposed by Lindeman.
- Pyramid of energy is always upright.
- Pyramid of numbers in a tree ecosystem can be inverted.
- Detritus food chain begins with dead organic matter.
- Decomposition involves fragmentation, leaching, catabolism, humification and mineralisation.
- Ecological succession on bare rock is called primary succession.
- Pioneer species on bare rock are lichens.
- Climax community is the final stable stage.
- Mutualism benefits both species.
- Commensalism benefits one species and does not affect the other.
- Biodiversity hotspots are regions of high endemism and habitat loss.
- Western Ghats and Himalaya are biodiversity hotspots in India.
