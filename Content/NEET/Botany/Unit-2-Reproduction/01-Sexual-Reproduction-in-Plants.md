# Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
@study
Flower structure, microsporogenesis, megasporogenesis, pollination, fertilisation and seed development.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Stamen**: consists of a filament and anther
- **Pollen grain**: has an outer exine of sporopollenin
- **Tapetum**: nourishes the developing pollen grains
- **Microsporogenesis**: is the formation of pollen grains
- **Megaspore mother cell**: divides meiotically to give four megaspores
- **Embryo sac**: is the female gametophyte with seven cells and eight nuclei

**Facts to remember**
- Stamen consists of a filament and anther.
- Pollen grain has an outer exine of sporopollenin.
- Tapetum nourishes the developing pollen grains.
- Microsporogenesis is the formation of pollen grains.
- Megaspore mother cell divides meiotically to give four megaspores.
- Embryo sac is the female gametophyte with seven cells and eight nuclei.
- Synergids have filiform apparatus.
- Autogamy is self pollination in the same flower.
- Geitonogamy is pollination between flowers of the same plant.
- Xenogamy brings genetically different pollen.
- Cleistogamous flowers never open and ensure self pollination.
- Double fertilisation gives a zygote and a triploid primary endosperm nucleus.
- Endosperm is triploid in angiosperms.
- Perisperm is the persistent nucellus in black pepper.
