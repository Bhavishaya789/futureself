# Plant Kingdom
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Plant Kingdom
@study
Algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms show increasing complexity of the plant body and life cycle.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Algae**: are chlorophyll bearing thalloid autotrophs mostly aquatic
- **Chlorophyceae**: are green algae with chlorophyll a and b
- **Phaeophyceae**: are brown algae containing fucoxanthin
- **Rhodophyceae**: are red algae containing phycoerythrin
- **Bryophytes**: are called amphibians of the plant kingdom
- **Liverworts and mosses**: are the two main bryophyte classes

**Facts to remember**
- Algae are chlorophyll bearing thalloid autotrophs mostly aquatic.
- Chlorophyceae are green algae with chlorophyll a and b.
- Phaeophyceae are brown algae containing fucoxanthin.
- Rhodophyceae are red algae containing phycoerythrin.
- Bryophytes are called amphibians of the plant kingdom.
- Liverworts and mosses are the two main bryophyte classes.
- Pteridophytes are the first terrestrial plants with vascular tissue.
- Sporophyte is the dominant phase in pteridophytes.
- Gymnosperms have naked seeds.
- Cycas has coralloid roots associated with cyanobacteria.
- Pinus has mycorrhizal roots.
- Angiosperms are divided into monocots and dicots.
- Double fertilisation is a feature of angiosperms.
- Haplontic life cycle has a dominant haploid gametophyte.
