# Anatomy of Flowering Plants
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Anatomy of Flowering Plants
@study
Tissues form the plant body: meristematic, simple permanent and complex permanent tissues.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Meristem**: is a tissue of dividing cells
- **Apical meristem**: increases the length of the plant
- **Lateral meristem**: increases the girth
- **Intercalary meristem**: is found at the base of internodes in grasses
- **Parenchyma**: is thin walled living tissue
- **Collenchyma**: gives mechanical support to growing parts

**Facts to remember**
- Meristem is a tissue of dividing cells.
- Apical meristem increases the length of the plant.
- Lateral meristem increases the girth.
- Intercalary meristem is found at the base of internodes in grasses.
- Parenchyma is thin walled living tissue.
- Collenchyma gives mechanical support to growing parts.
- Sclerenchyma has dead cells with lignified walls.
- Xylem conducts water and minerals.
- Phloem conducts food.
- Companion cells are associated with sieve tube elements.
- Vascular bundles in dicot stem are open and arranged in a ring.
- Vascular bundles in monocot stem are closed and scattered.
- Cambium is absent in monocots.
- Endodermis has Casparian strips.
