# Biotechnology: Principles, Processes and Applications
minutes: 40

Recombinant DNA technology, tools, PCR and applications in agriculture and medicine.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **Restriction endonuclease**: cuts DNA at specific palindromic sequences
- **EcoRI**: was isolated from Escherichia coli RY13
- **DNA ligase**: joins DNA fragments
- **PBR322**: is a popular cloning vector
- **Ori**: is the origin of replication
- **Selectable marker**: identifies transformants

**Facts to remember**
- Restriction endonuclease cuts DNA at specific palindromic sequences.
- EcoRI was isolated from Escherichia coli RY13.
- DNA ligase joins DNA fragments.
- PBR322 is a popular cloning vector.
- Ori is the origin of replication.
- Selectable marker identifies transformants.
- Gel electrophoresis separates DNA fragments by size.
- Ethidium bromide stains DNA orange under UV.
- PCR amplifies DNA using Taq polymerase.
- Taq polymerase is heat stable and comes from Thermus aquaticus.
- Bioreactor produces large quantities of recombinant products.
- Bt toxin is produced by Bacillus thuringiensis and kills insects.
- Bt cotton resists bollworms.
- RNA interference silences mRNA and protects tobacco from nematodes.
