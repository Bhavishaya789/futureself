# Units, Dimensions and Errors
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Units, Dimensions and Errors
@study
Dimensional analysis checks formulas; error propagation and significant figures decide the precision of an answer.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **[F] = MLT^-2**: force
- **[E] = ML^2T^-2**: energy
- **ΔZ/Z = aΔA/A + bΔB/B**: product errors
- **Least count of vernier = 1 MSD - 1 VSD**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Dimensional analysis cannot determine dimensionless constants.
- Principle of homogeneity requires equal dimensions on both sides of an equation.
- Angle and strain are dimensionless.
- Systematic error always acts in the same direction.
- Random error is reduced by taking many readings.
- Significant figures in a product follow the least precise factor.
- Absolute error has the same unit as the quantity.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In NEET, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Revise this chapter in three passes: the formula sheet, the facts above, then the generated numerical questions with a timer.

**How to revise.** Read the formula list and the facts once, then close the page and write them from memory. Attempt the generated questions in sets of ten, mark each answer, and note the reason for every miss. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
