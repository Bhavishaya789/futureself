# Semiconductors and Communication

@study
Band theory, p-n junction, diodes, transistors, logic gates and basic modulation.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **IE = IB + IC**: transistor
- **β = IC/IB**: current gain
- **AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR**: gates
- **m = Am/Ac**: modulation index

**Facts to remember**
- In an n-type semiconductor electrons are the majority carriers.
- In a p-type semiconductor holes are the majority carriers.
- A p-n junction in forward bias has a narrow depletion layer.
- A diode as a rectifier converts AC to DC.
- NAND and NOR gates are universal gates.
- Zener diode is used for voltage regulation in reverse breakdown.
- Amplitude modulation varies the carrier amplitude with the signal.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In NEET, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
