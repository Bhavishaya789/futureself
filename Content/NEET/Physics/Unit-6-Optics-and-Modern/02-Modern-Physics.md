@study
Photoelectric effect, de Broglie waves, Bohr model, radioactivity and binding energy.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **E = hf = hc/λ**: photon energy
- **Kmax = hf - φ**: Einstein
- **λ = h/p**: de Broglie
- **En = -13.6/n^2 eV**: hydrogen
- **N = N0 e^(-λt)**: decay

**Facts to remember**
- Stopping potential depends on frequency and not on intensity.
- Threshold frequency is the minimum frequency for photoemission.
- The Bohr model quantises angular momentum as nh/2π.
- Binding energy per nucleon is maximum near iron.
- Alpha particle is a helium nucleus.
- Half life is independent of the amount of the sample.
- Mass defect is converted to binding energy by E = mc^2.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude. In NEET, speed comes from recognising the standard pattern of each chapter, so practise the numerical types below until they are automatic. Questions in this chapter repeat a small number of patterns with changed numbers or wording, so after two or three sets you should recognise each pattern at a glance. Keep a short error log of units, signs and exceptions, and read it again the day before the test.
