# Thermodynamics and Kinetic Theory
minutes: 40
yt: NEET Thermodynamics and Kinetic Theory
@study
First law, heat engines, entropy and kinetic theory of gases connect heat, work and molecular motion.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **ΔU = Q - W**: first law
- **PV = nRT**: ideal gas
- **W = nRT ln(V2/V1)**: isothermal
- **η = 1 - T2/T1**: Carnot
- **vrms = √(3RT/M)**: rule

**Facts to remember**
- Isothermal process has constant temperature and ΔU = 0.
- Adiabatic process has Q = 0.
- Isochoric process does no work.
- Carnot engine has the maximum efficiency between two temperatures.
- Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.
- Degrees of freedom of a diatomic gas at room temperature equal 5.
- Mean kinetic energy per molecule equals 3kT/2.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude.
