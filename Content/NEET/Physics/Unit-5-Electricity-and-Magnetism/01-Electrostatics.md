# Electrostatics
minutes: 40

@study
Coulomb's law, fields, Gauss's law, potential and capacitors with dielectrics.

**Key formulas and rules**
- **F = kq1q2/r^2**: k = 9×10^9
- **E = kq/r^2**: field
- **V = kq/r**: potential
- **C = ε0A/d**: parallel plate
- **U = CV^2/2**: stored energy

**Facts to remember**
- Electric field inside a conductor is zero in electrostatic equilibrium.
- Electric field lines never cross each other.
- Gauss's law relates flux to enclosed charge.
- Dielectric between capacitor plates increases the capacitance by K.
- Capacitors in series have the same charge on each.
- Capacitors in parallel have the same potential difference.
- Equipotential surfaces are perpendicular to field lines.

**Exam approach.** Write the formula first, substitute with units, then check the order of magnitude.
