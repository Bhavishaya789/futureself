**Exam-style practice (original questions written in the NEET pattern; not an official past paper).** NEET gives +4 for a correct answer and -1 for a wrong one, 180 questions in 3 hours 20 minutes.
## Formula sheet
Newton: F = ma; work-energy: W = change in KE; Ohm: V = IR; lens: 1/v - 1/u = 1/f; Photon energy E(eV) = 1240/lambda(nm); half-life N = N0/2^(t/T).
## Strategy
NCERT lines and standard numericals form most of the paper; revise units and dimensions thoroughly.

Q: SI unit of the electric field?

A: 60 cm.
Q: After how many half-lives does 1/8 of a sample remain?
A: 3.

## Advanced
P: A sample has an activity of 800 counts/min. After 30 minutes it is 100 counts/min. Find the half-life.
A: 800 to 100 is 3 half-lives, so T = 10 min.
