# Lifelines of National Economy
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 geography lifelines of national economy
@study
Transport, communication and trade are the lifelines of the economy. India has one of the largest road networks in the world; roads are classified as Golden Quadrilateral, National Highways, State Highways, District roads and Border roads (BRO). Railways are the principal mode of transport; the first train ran in 1853 between Mumbai and Thane. Pipelines carry crude oil and gas; waterways include National Waterway 1 (Prayagraj-Haldia), 2 (Sadiya-Dhubri) and 3 (West Coast Canal). Major ports are Kandla, Mumbai, JNPT, Marmagao, New Mangalore, Kochi, Chennai, Tuticorin, Visakhapatnam, Paradwip, Kolkata and Haldia. Airways are the fastest mode. Communication includes post, telecom and mass media. International trade is the exchange of goods between countries; tourism is a major trade; the government promotes it as an industry.
## Deep dive: roadways
Golden Quadrilateral (Delhi-Kolkata-Chennai-Mumbai), NS-EW corridors, national and state highways, district roads and BRO roads. Roads are cheaper for short distances and give door-to-door service.
## Deep dive: railways, pipelines, waterways
Railways: 17 zones; first train 1853. Pipelines carry oil and gas. Waterways: NW1, NW2, NW3. Ports: Kandla, Mumbai, JNPT, Marmagao, New Mangalore, Kochi, Chennai, Tuticorin, Visakhapatnam, Paradwip, Kolkata-Haldia.
## Deep dive: airways and communication
Air transport is fastest; Pawan Hans serves remote areas. India Post has the world's largest network; telecom, radio, TV and the internet link the country.
## Deep dive: trade and tourism
International trade: exports, imports and balance of trade. Tourism creates jobs, foreign exchange and national integration; types: cultural, medical, eco, adventure.
