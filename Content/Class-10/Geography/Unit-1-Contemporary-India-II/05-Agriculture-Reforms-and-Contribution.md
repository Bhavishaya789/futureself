# Agriculture: Reforms, Technology and Contribution
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 geography agriculture: reforms, technology and contribution
@study
Agriculture contributes to national income and employment but faces problems of small holdings, dependence on rain and low investment. Technological and institutional reforms include land reforms (consolidation of holdings, abolition of zamindari, Bhoodan-Gramdan of Vinoba Bhave), the Green Revolution (HYV seeds, fertilisers, irrigation in the 1960s), White Revolution (Operation Flood), crop insurance, Kisan Credit Card, minimum support price, Grameen banks, and Kisan call centres. Challenges: fragmented holdings, over-use of fertilisers, shrinking land, WTO competition and fluctuations in prices. Globalisation exposes farmers to competition; organic farming and diversification are encouraged. Food security is essential, and the government supports it through procurement and public distribution.
## Deep dive: institutional reforms
Land reforms: abolition of zamindari, land ceilings, consolidation, Bhoodan-Gramdan. Credit: Grameen banks, Kisan Credit Card. Insurance: PMFBY. Price support: MSP and procurement. Information: Kisan call centres.
## Deep dive: technological reforms
Green Revolution (1960s): HYV seeds, fertilisers, irrigation, pesticides; raised wheat and rice output in Punjab, Haryana. White Revolution (Operation Flood): increased milk production.
## Deep dive: challenges
Fragmentation, rain dependence, soil damage, price instability, competition from imports. Remedies: organic farming, diversification, cooperative farming, irrigation and market support.

A: Joining scattered plots into a compact area.

A: Vinoba Bhave's appeal for rich landowners to donate land to the poor.
Q: What is Gramdan?
A: Donation of an entire village's land to a community.

A: The use of HYV seeds, fertilisers and irrigation to increase food grain output.

A: The 1960s.

A: A price guaranteed by the government for certain crops.
Q: What is the Kisan Credit Card?
A: A scheme giving farmers easy credit.
Q: What is crop insurance?
A: Insurance to protect farmers against crop failure.
Q: What is a PMFBY?
A: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, a crop insurance scheme.
Q: What are Grameen banks?
A: Banks to give loans to rural people.
Q: What is a Kisan call centre?
A: A helpline giving farmers advice.

A: It increased regional inequality, soil damage and chemical use.

Q: What is crop rotation?
A: Growing different crops in sequence on the same land.
Q: What is mixed farming?
A: Growing crops and rearing animals together.
Q: What is multiple cropping?
A: Growing more than one crop on a piece of land in a year.
Q: What is irrigation?
A: Artificial supply of water to crops.
