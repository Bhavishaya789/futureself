# Agriculture: Types, Seasons and Crops
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 geography agriculture: types, seasons and crops
@study
Agriculture is the primary activity employing about half of India's workforce. Farming types include primitive subsistence (slash and burn, called jhumming in the North-East), intensive subsistence, commercial farming (Punjab, Haryana) and plantation farming (tea, coffee, rubber, sugarcane, banana). India has three cropping seasons: Rabi (sown October to December, harvested April to June: wheat, barley, peas, gram, mustard), Kharif (sown with monsoon onset, harvested September-October: rice, maize, jowar, bajra, tur, moong, urad, cotton, jute, groundnut, soybean) and Zaid (summer: watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder). Major crops are rice, wheat, millets, maize, pulses, sugarcane, oilseeds, tea, coffee, rubber, cotton, jute and horticulture crops.
## Deep dive: farming types
Primitive subsistence (slash and burn), intensive subsistence (high labour inputs in densely populated areas), commercial (modern inputs, high yield per hectare) and plantation (single cash crop, large estates, migrant labour, capital).
## Deep dive: cropping seasons
Rabi: sown winter, harvested summer. Kharif: sown with monsoon, harvested autumn. Zaid: between the two, short summer season.
## Deep dive: major crops
Rice: hot, humid, 100+ cm rain; alluvial plains. Wheat: cool, 50-75 cm; Ganga-Satluj plains. Millets: poor soils; Maize: food and fodder. Pulses: legumes restore nitrogen. Sugarcane: tropical and subtropical, second in the world. Oilseeds: groundnut leads. Tea, coffee, rubber, cotton, jute: cash and fibre crops.
## Deep dive: horticulture
India is among the top producers of fruits and vegetables: mangoes, bananas, oranges, grapes, peas, tomatoes, onions.
