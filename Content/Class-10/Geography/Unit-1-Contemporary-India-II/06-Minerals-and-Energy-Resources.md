# Minerals and Energy Resources
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 geography minerals and energy resources
@study
A mineral is a homogeneous naturally occurring substance with a definable internal structure. Minerals are classified as metallic (ferrous: iron ore, manganese, nickel, cobalt; non-ferrous: copper, lead, zinc, bauxite), non-metallic (mica, salt, limestone) and energy minerals (coal, petroleum, natural gas). Iron ore (magnetite is the best) is mainly found in Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur, Bellary-Chitradurga-Chikmagaluru-Tumakuru, and Maharashtra-Goa belts. Conventional energy: coal, petroleum, natural gas, electricity (thermal, hydro, nuclear). Non-conventional: solar, wind, biogas, tidal, geothermal and biomass. India's Gondwana coal fields are in Damodar valley (Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro), Mahanadi, Godavari and Son valleys; lignite at Neyveli. Petroleum comes from Mumbai High, Gujarat and Assam (Digboi). Conservation of minerals and energy is vital.
## Deep dive: minerals
Metallic ferrous: iron ore, manganese. Non-ferrous: copper, bauxite. Non-metallic: mica, limestone. Minerals occur in igneous and metamorphic rocks (veins), sedimentary rocks (layers), alluvial deposits and ocean waters.
## Deep dive: iron ore and other minerals
Magnetite (70% iron) is best; hematite is most important. Belts: Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur, Bellary-Chitradurga-Tumakuru, Maharashtra-Goa. Manganese: Odisha. Copper: Balaghat, Khetri. Bauxite: Koraput, Amarkantak. Mica: Koderma.
## Deep dive: conventional energy
Coal: peat, lignite, bituminous, anthracite; Gondwana coal in Damodar valley; Neyveli lignite. Petroleum: Mumbai High, Gujarat, Assam. Gas: Krishna-Godavari, HVJ pipeline. Thermal, hydro and nuclear electricity.
## Deep dive: non-conventional energy
Solar (Bhuj), wind (Nagercoil, Jaisalmer), biogas, tidal, geothermal (Manikaran, Puga), biomass.
## Deep dive: conservation
Use minerals judiciously, recycle metals, use substitutes and efficient technology; save energy.

Q: What is a mineral?
A: A naturally occurring homogeneous substance with a definable internal structure.
Q: How are minerals classified?
A: Metallic, non-metallic and energy minerals.
Q: What are ferrous minerals?
A: Minerals containing iron, such as iron ore and manganese.
Q: What are non-ferrous minerals?
A: Minerals without iron, such as copper, lead and bauxite.
Q: Name non-metallic minerals.
A: Mica, salt, potash, sulphur and limestone.
Q: Which is the best quality iron ore?
A: Magnetite.
Q: What is hematite?
A: Iron ore with about 60-70 per cent iron.
Q: Where are the iron ore belts?
A: Odisha-Jharkhand, Durg-Bastar-Chandrapur, Bellary-Chitradurga-Tumakuru and Maharashtra-Goa.
Q: Which state is the largest producer of manganese?
A: Odisha.
Q: Which mineral is used in making steel alloys?
A: Manganese.
Q: Which state produces most copper?
A: Madhya Pradesh (Balaghat).
Q: Where else is copper mined?
A: Khetri in Rajasthan and Singhbhum in Jharkhand.
Q: What is the ore of aluminium?
A: Bauxite.
Q: Where is bauxite found?
A: Odisha (Koraput), Amarkantak plateau and Gujarat.
Q: Which mineral is used in electrical industries?
A: Mica.
Q: Where is mica found?
A: Jharkhand (Koderma), Andhra Pradesh and Rajasthan.
Q: What is limestone used for?
A: Cement production and iron smelting.
Q: What are rat-hole mines?
A: Narrow tunnels dug by hand in Meghalaya.
Q: What are the conventional sources of energy?
A: Coal, petroleum, natural gas and electricity.
Q: What are non-conventional sources?
A: Solar, wind, tidal, biomass, geothermal and nuclear.
Q: What is coal?
A: A fossil fuel formed from buried plant matter.
Q: What are the types of coal?
A: Peat, lignite, bituminous and anthracite.
Q: Which is the best quality coal?
A: Anthracite.
Q: Which coal is the most abundant?
A: Bituminous.
Q: Where does Gondwana coal occur?
A: Damodar, Son, Mahanadi and Godavari valleys.
Q: Name Gondwana coalfields.
A: Jharia, Raniganj and Bokaro.
Q: Where is lignite found in India?
A: Neyveli in Tamil Nadu.
Q: Which are the tertiary coalfields?
A: Assam, Meghalaya and Nagaland.
Q: What is petroleum?
A: A fossil fuel obtained from sedimentary rocks.
Q: Where are the oil fields of India?
A: Mumbai High, Gujarat (Ankleshwar) and Assam (Digboi).
Q: Where is the oldest oil refinery in Assam?
A: Digboi.
Q: Which is the biggest oil producing region?
A: Mumbai High.
Q: What is natural gas?
A: A clean fossil fuel found with petroleum.
Q: Where is gas found in India?
A: Krishna-Godavari basin, Mumbai High and Gujarat.
Q: What is the HVJ pipeline?
A: Hazira-Vijaipur-Jagdishpur gas pipeline.
Q: What is CNG?
A: Compressed natural gas, used as vehicle fuel.
Q: What is thermal power?
A: Electricity from burning coal, oil or gas.
Q: What is hydro power?
A: Electricity from falling water.
Q: What is nuclear power?
A: Electricity from uranium or thorium fission.
Q: Where are the nuclear plants?
A: Tarapur, Kalpakkam, Kaiga, Narora and Rawatbhata.
Q: What is solar energy?
A: Energy from the Sun using photovoltaic cells.
Q: Where is the largest solar plant in Gujarat?
A: Bhuj (Madhapur).
Q: What is wind energy?
A: Power from moving air.
Q: Where are wind farms?
A: Nagercoil, Jaisalmer and Tamil Nadu coast.
Q: What is biogas?
A: Gas from animal and plant waste.
Q: What is tidal energy?
A: Energy from sea tides.
Q: Where is geothermal energy used?
A: Manikaran (Himachal) and Puga valley (Ladakh).
Q: What is energy conservation?
A: Using energy efficiently and saving it.
