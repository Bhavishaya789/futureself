# Digital Documentation (Advanced)
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it digital documentation (advanced)
@study
Advanced word processing (Writer/Word) covers styles, headers and footers, page numbers, table of contents, mail merge, track changes, comments, tables, images, hyperlinks, page layout, columns, templates and printing. Styles keep formatting consistent; mail merge combines a main document with a data source to make personalised letters. Cross-references, footnotes and bookmarks help in long documents.
## Deep dive: styles and structure
Apply Heading 1, Heading 2 etc. to create a table of contents automatically. Use headers, footers, page numbers and section breaks for professional layout.
## Deep dive: mail merge
Prepare a main letter, connect a data source (spreadsheet or database), insert merge fields such as «Name», preview and merge to a new document or print.
## Deep dive: collaboration
Track changes records edits; comments carry reviewer notes; accept or reject changes. Protect with a password and share as PDF.
## Deep dive: references
Footnotes, endnotes, bookmarks, hyperlinks and cross-references make long documents easier to navigate.
