# Green Skills II
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it green skills ii
@study
Green skills are the knowledge, abilities and attitudes needed to live in and support a sustainable society. Sustainable development meets present needs without harming future generations. The United Nations set 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) for 2030. Key ideas are renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro), the 3 Rs (reduce, reuse, recycle), water conservation, waste management, e-waste, circular economy, carbon footprint and green jobs. International agreements include the Kyoto Protocol, Montreal Protocol and Paris Agreement.
## Deep dive: sustainability and SDGs
Sustainable development balances economic growth, social well-being and environmental protection. The UN's 17 SDGs (2015, to be achieved by 2030) include no poverty, zero hunger, clean water, clean energy, climate action and life on land.
## Deep dive: energy and waste
Use renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro) instead of fossil fuels. Follow the 3 Rs, compost biodegradable waste and recycle e-waste through authorised centres. Choose BEE five-star appliances and LED lights.
## Deep dive: climate and agreements
Greenhouse gases cause global warming and climate change. The Montreal Protocol (1987) protects the ozone layer, the Kyoto Protocol (1997) cut emissions and the Paris Agreement (2015) aims to limit warming to below 2°C.
## Deep dive: green careers
Green jobs include solar technician, waste manager, environmental engineer and organic farmer. Green skills involve energy saving, resource efficiency and eco-friendly design.
