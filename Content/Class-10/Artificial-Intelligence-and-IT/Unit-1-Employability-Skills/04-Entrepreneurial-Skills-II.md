# Entrepreneurial Skills II
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it entrepreneurial skills ii
@study
An entrepreneur is a person who identifies a business opportunity, takes risks and sets up an enterprise. Qualities include creativity, risk-taking, leadership, decision-making and perseverance. The steps are idea generation, market research, business plan, funding and marketing. Government schemes such as Startup India, Make in India, Digital India and Mudra loans support entrepreneurs. A business plan covers the idea, market, finance, operations and marketing mix (4 Ps: product, price, place, promotion). Ethical practices and customer satisfaction are essential.
## Deep dive: entrepreneurial qualities
Successful entrepreneurs are creative, take calculated risks, lead teams, make timely decisions and persevere through failures. They spot needs in the market and offer solutions.
## Deep dive: business steps
Generate an idea, research the market, write a business plan, arrange capital (savings, loans, investors, crowdfunding), choose a legal form (sole proprietorship, partnership, company), launch and market. The 4 Ps of marketing are product, price, place and promotion.
## Deep dive: finance basics
Profit = revenue - cost. Profit % = (profit/cost price) × 100. Break-even units = fixed cost ÷ (selling price - variable cost per unit).
## Deep dive: government support
Startup India, Make in India, Digital India and Mudra loans help new businesses with funding, skills and infrastructure. Ethical practices build trust and long-term success.
