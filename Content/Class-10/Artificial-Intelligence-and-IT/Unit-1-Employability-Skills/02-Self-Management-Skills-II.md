# Self-Management Skills II
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it self-management skills ii
@study
Self-management skills help a person stay in control of emotions, time and goals. They include self-awareness (knowing strengths and weaknesses through SWOT), self-confidence, positive attitude, goal setting using the SMART method, time management, stress management, work ethics, teamwork, resilience and personal grooming. A person with good self-management is punctual, disciplined, motivated and able to work with others.
## Deep dive: self-awareness and confidence
A SWOT analysis lists strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats. Knowing them helps a student choose a career and improve. Confidence grows through preparation, practice and positive self-talk.
## Deep dive: goals and time
SMART goals are specific, measurable, achievable, realistic and time-bound. Time management uses to-do lists, priorities and schedules and avoids procrastination.
## Deep dive: stress and resilience
Stress passes through alarm, resistance and exhaustion stages. Exercise, sleep, hobbies and support from family reduce stress. Resilience helps people recover from setbacks.
## Deep dive: workplace behaviour
Work ethics include honesty, punctuality, discipline and accountability. Teamwork needs communication, respect and shared goals. Grooming and hygiene create a good impression.

A: Areas where a person needs improvement.

A: Belief in one's own abilities.
Q: What is a positive attitude?
A: Looking at the bright side and expecting good results.
Q: What is self-motivation?
A: Inner drive to do something without being pushed.
Q: What is a goal?
A: A desired result a person plans to achieve.
Q: What are short-term goals?
A: Goals that can be achieved in a short time.
Q: What are long-term goals?
A: Goals that take a long time to achieve.

A: Realistic.

A: Time-bound.
Q: What is time management?
A: Planning and using time effectively.
Q: What is prioritising?
A: Deciding the order of tasks by importance and urgency.

A: Putting off tasks.

A: Belief that abilities improve with effort.
