# Communication Skills II
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it communication skills ii
@study
Communication is the exchange of information between a sender and a receiver through a channel with feedback. Methods are verbal (oral and written), non-verbal (body language) and visual (charts, signs). Effective communication follows the 7 Cs: clear, concise, correct, complete, courteous, concrete and considerate. Barriers such as noise, language, emotion and culture can distort the message. Good writing skills include correct sentences, parts of speech, paragraphs, e-mails and letters. Active listening and feedback complete the cycle.
## Deep dive: communication cycle
The sender encodes an idea into a message and sends it through a channel. The receiver decodes it and gives feedback. Noise can disturb any stage. Two-way communication includes feedback; one-way does not.
## Deep dive: methods and the 7 Cs
Verbal (oral, written), non-verbal (gesture, posture, eye contact, tone) and visual (charts, symbols). The 7 Cs are clear, concise, correct, complete, courteous, concrete and considerate.
## Deep dive: barriers and listening
Physical, language, emotional, cultural and attitudinal barriers reduce effectiveness. Active listening needs attention, eye contact, no interruption, questions and summarising.
## Deep dive: writing skills
Use all four sentence types appropriately, choose the correct part of speech, write paragraphs with a topic sentence, and follow formats for e-mails (subject, salutation, body, closing) and letters.

Q: What is communication?
A: The exchange of information, ideas or feelings between two or more persons.
Q: Name the elements of the communication cycle.
A: Sender, message, encoding, channel, decoding, receiver and feedback.
Q: What is encoding?
A: Converting an idea into words or symbols.
Q: What is decoding?
A: Understanding the meaning of the received message.
Q: What is feedback?
A: The receiver's response that shows the message was understood.
Q: What is a channel?
A: The medium that carries the message.
Q: What is noise in communication?
A: Any disturbance that distorts the message.
Q: What are the three methods of communication?
A: Verbal, non-verbal and visual.
Q: What is verbal communication?
A: Communication using words, spoken or written.
Q: What is oral communication?
A: Spoken communication such as a phone call or meeting.
Q: What is written communication?
A: Communication through letters, e-mails and reports.
Q: What is non-verbal communication?
A: Communication through body language, gestures and expressions.
Q: What is visual communication?
A: Communication using pictures, charts, maps and signs.
Q: What is meant by clarity in a message?
A: Using simple words to convey one main idea.
Q: What does concise mean?
A: Short but complete.
Q: What does courteous mean?
A: Polite, respectful and friendly.
Q: What is a concrete message?
A: A message with specific facts and figures.
Q: What is a considerate message?
A: A message that keeps the receiver's needs in mind.
Q: Name four types of barriers to communication.
A: Physical, language, emotional and cultural barriers.
Q: What is a language barrier?
A: Difficulty because of different languages or jargon.
Q: What is a physical barrier?
A: Difficulty caused by noise, distance or poor equipment.
Q: What is an emotional barrier?
A: Difficulty caused by anger, fear or stress.
Q: What is jargon?
A: Special words used by a group that others may not understand.
Q: What is active listening?
A: Listening with full attention, without interruption, and giving feedback.
Q: How can active listening be shown?
A: By eye contact, nodding, asking questions and summarising.
Q: What is body language?
A: Messages sent through posture, gestures and facial expression.
Q: What is the purpose of the subject line in an e-mail?
A: To state the purpose of the message briefly.
Q: Which salutation is formal in an e-mail?
A: Dear Sir or Madam.
Q: What are the four types of sentences by function?
A: Assertive, interrogative, imperative and exclamatory.
Q: What is an assertive sentence?
A: A sentence that makes a statement.
Q: What is an interrogative sentence?
A: A sentence that asks a question.
Q: What is an imperative sentence?
A: A sentence that gives a command or request.
Q: What is an exclamatory sentence?
A: A sentence that expresses strong feeling.
Q: What are the parts of speech?
A: Noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction and interjection.
Q: What is a paragraph?
A: A group of sentences on one main idea.
Q: What is a topic sentence?
A: The sentence that states the main idea of a paragraph.
Q: What is the difference between formal and informal communication?
A: Formal follows official rules; informal is casual.
Q: What is telephone etiquette?
A: Polite and professional behaviour on phone calls.
Q: What is public speaking?
A: Delivering a speech to an audience.
Q: Why is feedback important?
A: It confirms that the message has been understood.
