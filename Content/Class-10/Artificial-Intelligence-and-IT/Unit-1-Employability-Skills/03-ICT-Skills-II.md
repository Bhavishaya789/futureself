# ICT Skills II
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 ai and it ict skills ii
@study
ICT (Information and Communication Technology) covers devices and tools used to create, store and share information: computers, mobiles, the internet, e-mail and cloud services. Important skills are keyboarding and data entry (touch typing on the home row, ergonomics), using word processors and spreadsheets, safe internet use (netiquette, digital footprint, strong passwords), avoiding plagiarism and respecting copyright. Cyber safety protects users from cyberbullying, phishing and malware.
## Deep dive: keyboarding and ergonomics
Touch typing uses the home row keys ASDF and JKL; as base positions. Sit upright, keep wrists straight, screen at eye level and take breaks to avoid strain.
## Deep dive: productivity tools
Word processors format documents; spreadsheets calculate with formulas; presentation software shows ideas; cloud tools let people share and edit files together. Shortcuts: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+S.
## Deep dive: cyber safety
Use strong passwords, two-factor authentication, updated antivirus and a firewall. Do not click unknown links (phishing). Report cyberbullying. Your digital footprint is permanent, so think before posting.
## Deep dive: ethics and law
Plagiarism is copying without credit. Copyright protects original works; open-source and creative-commons licences permit sharing under conditions. The IT Act 2000 deals with cybercrime in India.
