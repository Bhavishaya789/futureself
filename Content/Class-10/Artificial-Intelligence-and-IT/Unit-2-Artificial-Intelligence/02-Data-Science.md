@study
Data science extracts insight from data using statistics, programming and domain knowledge. Data may be structured (tables) or unstructured (text, images). Data is gathered, cleaned, analysed and visualised. Basic statistics include mean, median, mode and range; K-Nearest Neighbour (KNN) classifies a new point by looking at the nearest labelled points. Data science is applied in finance, healthcare, agriculture, sports and e-commerce. Python libraries NumPy, Pandas and Matplotlib support analysis.
## Deep dive: types of data
Structured data fit tables; unstructured data (text, images, audio) do not. Big data is described by volume, velocity and variety.
## Deep dive: statistics
Mean = sum/n, median = middle of ordered data, mode = most frequent, range = max - min. Outliers can distort the mean more than the median.
## Deep dive: KNN
To classify a new point, compute the distance to each labelled point (Euclidean: √((x2-x1)²+(y2-y1)²)), pick the K nearest and choose the majority class. An odd K avoids ties.
## Deep dive: tools
NumPy handles arrays, Pandas handles DataFrames, Matplotlib plots graphs. Data science helps healthcare, agriculture, finance, sports and e-commerce.
