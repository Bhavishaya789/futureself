# Chemical reactions, acids and bases, and heredity
minutes: 25

@study
**Balanced equation:** atoms of each element equal on both sides. Example: 2 Mg + O2 -> 2 MgO; Fe + CuSO4 -> FeSO4 + Cu (displacement). Types: combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement, redox.
**Acids and bases:** acids turn blue litmus red; pH below 7 acidic, 7 neutral, above 7 basic. Neutralisation: acid + base -> salt + water; e.g. HCl + NaOH -> NaCl + H2O. Common salt, baking soda (NaHCO3) and washing soda (Na2CO3.10H2O).
**Heredity:** Mendel's monohybrid cross gives a 3:1 phenotypic ratio in the F2 generation; human sex is determined by XY (male) and XX (female); the sex of the child depends on the father's chromosome.
