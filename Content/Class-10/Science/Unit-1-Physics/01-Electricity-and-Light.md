# Electricity and light (Class 10)
minutes: 25

@study
**Ohm's law** V = IR; power P = VI = I^2 R. For V = 5 V and R = 10 ohm: I = **0.5 A**, P = **2.5 W**. Resistors in series add; in parallel 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2: 2 ohm and 3 ohm give **1.2 ohm**. Heating effect H = I^2 R t.
**Light:** mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; magnification m = -v/u. Concave mirror f = -15 cm, object at u = -30 cm: v = **-30 cm**, m = **-1** (real, inverted, same size). **Refraction:** Snell's law; refractive index n = c / v: if light travels at 2 x 10^8 m/s in glass, n = **1.5**. Lens power P = 1/f (metres), in dioptres.
**Magnetic effects:** field around a current carrying wire; motor, generator, electromagnetic induction.
