# How do Organisms Reproduce?
minutes: 40
yt: class 10 science how do organisms reproduce
@study
Reproduction produces new individuals and maintains continuity of species through DNA copying with variation. Asexual methods: fission (binary in Amoeba, multiple in Plasmodium), fragmentation (Spirogyra), regeneration (Hydra, Planaria), budding (Hydra, yeast), vegetative propagation (cutting, layering, grafting, tissue culture) and spore formation (Rhizopus). Sexual reproduction in flowering plants involves pollination, fertilisation, seed and fruit formation. In humans, the male system has testes, vas deferens, urethra; the female system has ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus. Fertilisation forms a zygote that implants in the uterus; menstruation occurs if no fertilisation. Contraceptive methods (barrier, hormonal, IUCD, surgical) control pregnancy; STDs include AIDS, gonorrhoea, syphilis and HPV.
## Deep dive: Asexual reproduction
Binary fission: Amoeba divides into two after nuclear division; Leishmania has a whip-like structure at one end. Multiple fission: Plasmodium divides into many daughter cells. Fragmentation: Spirogyra breaks into pieces that grow. Regeneration: Hydra and Planaria grow from cut pieces using specialised cells. Budding: a bud develops on parent Hydra and detaches. Vegetative propagation: roots, stems and leaves produce new plants as in Bryophyllum leaf buds, potato eyes, rose cutting, mango grafting; advantages: fruit and flower early, genetically identical, seedless plants like banana and orange grown. Spore formation: Rhizopus hyphae form sporangia with spores that germinate in moisture. Tissue culture grows plantlets from a small tissue in nutrient medium, e.g. orchids.
## Deep dive: Sexual reproduction in plants
Flower: sepals, petals, stamens (anther with pollen, filament) and carpel (stigma, style, ovary with ovules). Pollination transfers pollen from anther to stigma (self or cross, by wind, water, insects). Pollen germinates to form a pollen tube that carries male gametes to the ovule; fertilisation produces a zygote; ovule becomes seed; ovary becomes fruit. Unisexual flowers: papaya, watermelon; bisexual: Hibiscus, mustard.
## Deep dive: Reproduction in humans
Male: testes produce sperms and testosterone in the scrotum (lower temperature), vas deferens, seminal vesicle and prostate add fluid forming semen; urethra. Female: ovaries produce one egg every month, fallopian tube (fertilisation site), uterus (implantation and growth), cervix, vagina. Puberty: sex hormones; menarche at about 10-12 years in girls. Fertilisation forms a zygote that divides into an embryo; the placenta provides nutrition, oxygen and removes wastes. If no fertilisation, the thickened uterine lining breaks about every 28 days: menstruation.
## Deep dive: Reproductive health
Contraception: barrier methods (condom, diaphragm), chemical (oral pills changing hormonal balance), IUCD (copper-T), and surgical methods (vasectomy, tubectomy). Abortion and sex determination misuse reduce the female ratio. STDs: bacterial gonorrhoea and syphilis; viral warts and HIV-AIDS; condoms offer protection. Population growth matters because of resources.

## Moderate
P: Distinguish between binary fission and multiple fission.
A: Binary fission: parent divides into two (Amoeba). Multiple fission: parent divides into many daughter cells (Plasmodium) under unfavourable conditions by forming a cyst.

## High
P: Describe the process of pollination and fertilisation in flowering plants.
A: Pollen transfer from anther to stigma by wind, water or insects; pollen grain germinates forming a pollen tube through style to the ovule; male gamete fuses with the egg to form a zygote; ovule becomes seed and ovary a fruit.

## Advanced
P: Explain the roles of the placenta and the menstrual cycle, and compare contraceptive methods.
A: Placenta, embedded in the uterus wall, supplies oxygen and nutrients to the embryo and removes wastes. If no zygote implants the uterine lining is shed about every 28 days. Barrier methods prevent sperm meeting egg and protect against STDs; pills change hormones; IUCD prevents implantation; surgery blocks the tubes.
