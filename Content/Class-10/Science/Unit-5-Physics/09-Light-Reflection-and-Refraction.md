# Light: Reflection and Refraction
minutes: 45
yt: class 10 science light reflection and refraction
@study
Light travels in straight lines at 3 x 10^8 m/s in vacuum. Reflection follows two laws: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, and the incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane. Spherical mirrors: concave converges, convex diverges. Focal length f = R/2. Mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; magnification m = -v/u = h2/h1. Refraction is bending of light as it passes between media; Snell's law n = sin i / sin r; refractive index n = c/v. Lens formula 1/v - 1/u = 1/f, magnification m = v/u, power P = 1/f in metres (dioptre). Convex lens converges (f positive) and concave diverges (f negative). Sign convention: distances measured from the pole or optical centre; those in the direction of the incident light are positive.
## Deep dive: Spherical mirrors
Terms: pole P, centre of curvature C, radius of curvature R, principal focus F, aperture. Concave mirror images: object at infinity -> real, at F, very small; beyond C -> between F and C, real, inverted, diminished; at C -> at C, real, inverted, same size; between C and F -> beyond C, real, inverted, enlarged; at F -> at infinity, highly enlarged; between P and F -> virtual, erect, enlarged behind mirror. Convex mirror always forms virtual, erect, diminished images between P and F; used as rear view mirror for wide field of view. Concave mirrors are used in shaving mirrors, headlights, solar furnaces, torches and by dentists.
## Deep dive: Refraction
Light going from rarer to denser bends towards the normal; denser to rarer bends away. Rectangular glass slab: emergent ray parallel to incident ray, with lateral displacement. n = c/v: refractive index of water 1.33, glass 1.5, diamond 2.42; higher n means optically denser and slower light. Absolute n of medium; relative n of 2 w.r.t. 1 = v1/v2 = n2/n1. Apparent depth, twinkling, advanced sunrise arise from refraction.
## Deep dive: Lenses
Convex lens images: object at infinity -> at F2, real, point sized; beyond 2F1 -> between F2 and 2F2, real, inverted, diminished; at 2F1 -> at 2F2, same size; between F1 and 2F1 -> beyond 2F2, enlarged; at F1 -> infinity; between F1 and O -> virtual, erect, enlarged on the same side (magnifying glass). Concave lens: always virtual, erect, diminished between O and F1. Power P = 1/f(m); +ve for convex, -ve for concave; combination P = P1 + P2. 1 dioptre is the power of a lens of focal length 1 m.
## Deep dive: Sign convention and ray diagrams
Cartesian convention: pole as origin, principal axis as x-axis, incident light from left. Object distance u is negative. Concave mirror f negative, convex mirror f positive; convex lens f positive, concave lens f negative. Ray diagrams: ray parallel to axis passes through the focus; ray through the focus goes parallel; ray through the centre of curvature retraces; ray to the pole reflects with equal angle; for lenses a ray through the optical centre goes undeviated.

## Moderate
P: An object is placed 20 cm from a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. Find the image distance and nature.
A: u = -20, f = -10: 1/v = 1/f - 1/u = -1/10 + 1/20 = -1/20, so v = -20 cm. Image is real, inverted, at C, same size (m = -1).
