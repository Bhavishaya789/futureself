# Areas related to circles
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 areas related to circles
@study
Circumference of a circle is 2 pi r and area is pi r^2. A sector with angle theta has arc length (theta/360) x 2 pi r and area (theta/360) x pi r^2. Area of segment = area of sector - area of triangle. Use pi = 22/7 unless told otherwise.
## Deep dive: circle, sector, segment
Circumference C = 2 pi r = pi d. Area A = pi r^2. For a sector of angle theta at the centre: arc length l = (theta/360) x 2 pi r and area = (theta/360) x pi r^2 = (1/2) l r. A minor segment is the region between a chord and its arc: area = area of sector - area of triangle formed by the radii and the chord = (theta/360) pi r^2 - (1/2) r^2 sin theta. The major segment area = pi r^2 - minor segment area.
## Deep dive: special figures
Ring (annulus) between radii R and r: area = pi (R^2 - r^2). Distance moved by a wheel in one rotation = circumference; in n rotations = n x 2 pi r. A minute hand of length l sweeps an angle of 6 degrees per minute, so in t minutes it sweeps area (6t/360) pi l^2. An equilateral triangle of side a has area (sqrt 3 / 4) a^2; a square of side a inscribed in a circle has diagonal a sqrt 2 = 2r. Combined figures: find each area separately, then add or subtract.
**Common mistakes:** using diameter instead of radius, forgetting to subtract the triangle for a segment, mixing units (cm and m).
