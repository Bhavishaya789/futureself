# Trigonometry and heights and distances

@study
In a right triangle: sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent. Standard values: sin 30 = 1/2, sin 45 = 1/sqrt 2, sin 60 = sqrt 3/2; cos 60 = 1/2; tan 45 = 1; tan 30 = 1/sqrt 3. Identity sin^2 + cos^2 = 1.
**Heights and distances:** angle of elevation is measured upward from the horizontal. Example: standing 20 m from a tower with an angle of elevation 30 degrees: height = 20 tan 30 = 20/sqrt 3 = **11.55 m**.
**Probability:** P(E) = favourable / total. A card from 52: P(heart) = 13/52 = **1/4**.
