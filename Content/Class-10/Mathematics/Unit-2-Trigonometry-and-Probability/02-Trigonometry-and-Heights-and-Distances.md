In a right triangle: sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent. Standard values: sin 30 = 1/2, sin 45 = 1/sqrt 2, sin 60 = sqrt 3/2; cos 60 = 1/2; tan 45 = 1; tan 30 = 1/sqrt 3. Identity sin^2 + cos^2 = 1.
**Heights and distances:** angle of elevation is measured upward from the horizontal. Example: standing 20 m from a tower with an angle of elevation 30 degrees: height = 20 tan 30 = 20/sqrt 3 = **11.55 m**.
**Probability:** P(E) = favourable / total. A card from 52: P(heart) = 13/52 = **1/4**.
## Deep dive: trigonometric ratios
**Definitions.** In a right triangle, for an acute angle A: sin A = opposite / hypotenuse, cos A = adjacent / hypotenuse, tan A = opposite / adjacent. Reciprocals: cosec A = 1/sin A, sec A = 1/cos A, cot A = 1/tan A. Also tan A = sin A / cos A and cot A = cos A / sin A.
**Standard angles.** sin: 0, 1/2, 1/sqrt 2, sqrt 3/2, 1 for 0, 30, 45, 60, 90 degrees. cos is the same list reversed. tan: 0, 1/sqrt 3, 1, sqrt 3, not defined. A memory trick: sin = sqrt(0/4), sqrt(1/4), sqrt(2/4), sqrt(3/4), sqrt(4/4).
**Complementary angles.** sin (90 - A) = cos A; cos (90 - A) = sin A; tan (90 - A) = cot A; cot (90 - A) = tan A; sec (90 - A) = cosec A; cosec (90 - A) = sec A. So sin 35 = cos 55 and tan 20 x tan 70 = 1.
**Identities.** sin^2 A + cos^2 A = 1; 1 + tan^2 A = sec^2 A; 1 + cot^2 A = cosec^2 A. To prove an identity, work on the more complicated side, write everything in sin and cos, and simplify.
**Behaviour.** As A rises from 0 to 90 degrees sin A rises from 0 to 1, cos A falls from 1 to 0, tan A rises from 0 without limit. The value of sin A and cos A never exceeds 1; sec A and cosec A are never below 1.
## Deep dive: heights and distances
**Angles.** The angle of elevation is the angle between the horizontal and the line of sight upward to an object. The angle of depression is measured downward from the horizontal to an object below. The angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the bottom (alternate angles).
**Method.** Draw a clear figure; mark the right angle, the known side and the angle; choose the ratio that links the known and unknown sides: height and distance use tan, height and line of sight use sin, distance and line of sight use cos. Typical forms: height = distance x tan (angle); two observation points give two equations that share the height.
**Common values.** At 45 degrees height equals distance. At 30 degrees height = distance / sqrt 3. At 60 degrees height = distance x sqrt 3. Take sqrt 3 = 1.732 and sqrt 2 = 1.414 for numerical answers.
## Deep dive: probability
**Definition.** P(E) = number of favourable outcomes / total number of equally likely outcomes. 0 <= P(E) <= 1; P(sure event) = 1; P(impossible event) = 0; P(E) + P(not E) = 1.
**Standard sample spaces.** A coin: 2 outcomes. A die: 6. Two coins: 4. Two dice: 36. A pack of cards: 52 cards, 4 suits of 13, 12 face cards, 26 red, 26 black, 4 aces, 4 kings.
