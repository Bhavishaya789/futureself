## Deep dive: coordinate geometry
**Cartesian plane.** Two perpendicular axes meet at the origin O (0, 0). A point is (x, y): x is the abscissa and y the ordinate. The quadrants are I (+,+), II (-,+), III (-,-), IV (+,-). Points on the x-axis have y = 0; points on the y-axis have x = 0.
**Distance formula.** PQ = sqrt ((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2). Distance from the origin to (x, y) is sqrt (x^2 + y^2). Use it to test shapes: an isosceles triangle has two equal sides; a right triangle satisfies Pythagoras; a parallelogram has equal opposite sides and bisecting diagonals; a rhombus has four equal sides; a rectangle has equal diagonals; three points are collinear when the sum of two distances equals the third.
**Section formula.** The point dividing A (x1, y1) and B (x2, y2) internally in the ratio m : n is ((m x2 + n x1)/(m + n), (m y2 + n y1)/(m + n)). The midpoint is ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2). The centroid of a triangle is ((x1 + x2 + x3)/3, (y1 + y2 + y3)/3). To find the ratio in which a line divides a segment, let the ratio be k : 1 and solve.
**Area of a triangle.** Area = (1/2) |x1 (y2 - y3) + x2 (y3 - y1) + x3 (y1 - y2)|. Three points are collinear when this area is 0. The area of a quadrilateral is found by splitting it into two triangles.
## Deep dive: similar triangles and circles
**Similar triangles.** Two triangles are similar if their angles are equal; then corresponding sides are proportional. Criteria: AAA (or AA), SSS (sides in the same ratio) and SAS (two sides in the same ratio and the included angle equal). The ratio of areas is the square of the ratio of corresponding sides. The basic proportionality (Thales) theorem: a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
**Tangents.** A tangent touches a circle at exactly one point, the point of contact. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact. From an external point exactly two tangents can be drawn and they are equal in length. If the distance from the centre to the external point is d and the radius r then the tangent length is sqrt (d^2 - r^2). A circle has infinitely many tangents; from a point inside it none.
