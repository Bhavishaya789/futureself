**Distance formula:** d = sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2); e.g. (0, 0) to (3, 4) is **5**. **Section formula:** point dividing (x1, y1), (x2, y2) in ratio m:n = ((m x2 + n x1)/(m + n), (m y2 + n y1)/(m + n)); midpoint is the case m = n. Area of triangle = (1/2)|x1(y2 - y3) + x2(y3 - y1) + x3(y1 - y2)|.
**Similar triangles:** corresponding sides in the same ratio; areas in the ratio of the squares of sides. **Circle:** tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact; tangents from an external point are equal. If tangent length is 12 and radius 5, the distance from the centre is sqrt(144 + 25) = **13**.

## Advanced
P: Prove that tangents from an external point are equal.
A: In triangles OPA and OPB, OA = OB (radii), angles OAP and OBP are right angles, and OP is common, so RHS congruence gives PA = PB.
