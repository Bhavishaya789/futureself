# Pair of linear equations in two variables
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 pair of linear equations
@study
A pair of linear equations a1 x + b1 y + c1 = 0 and a2 x + b2 y + c2 = 0 is solved graphically (two lines) or algebraically by substitution, elimination, or cross-multiplication. The nature of the solution depends on a1/a2, b1/b2 and c1/c2.
## Deep dive: nature of solutions
For a1 x + b1 y + c1 = 0 and a2 x + b2 y + c2 = 0: if a1/a2 != b1/b2 the lines intersect at one point, so the pair is consistent with a unique solution. If a1/a2 = b1/b2 != c1/c2 the lines are parallel: no solution (inconsistent). If a1/a2 = b1/b2 = c1/c2 the lines coincide: infinitely many solutions (dependent, consistent).
## Deep dive: methods
**Substitution:** express one variable from one equation and substitute into the other. **Elimination:** multiply to make the coefficients of one variable equal in size, then add or subtract. **Cross-multiplication:** x/(b1 c2 - b2 c1) = y/(c1 a2 - c2 a1) = 1/(a1 b2 - a2 b1). **Graphical:** plot both lines; the intersection is the solution. Each method must give the same answer, so always verify by substituting back into both equations.
## Deep dive: word problems
Choose two unknowns, form two equations from the two conditions, solve, and state the answer with units. Typical types: ages (now, years ago, years later), numbers (two-digit number and reversed digits), fractions (add to numerator and denominator), speed (upstream and downstream, with and against wind), geometry (angles of cyclic quadrilateral), money (cost of items), and area/perimeter of rectangles.
**Common mistakes:** sign errors while multiplying through, forgetting that the speed downstream is u + v and upstream u - v, not checking both equations.

## Linear pair
Standard form | a1 x + b1 y + c1 = 0 ; a2 x + b2 y + c2 = 0 | 
Unique solution | a1/a2 != b1/b2 | lines intersect
No solution | a1/a2 = b1/b2 != c1/c2 | parallel lines
Infinite solutions | a1/a2 = b1/b2 = c1/c2 | coincident lines
Cross-multiplication | x/(b1c2 - b2c1) = y/(c1a2 - c2a1) = 1/(a1b2 - a2b1) | 
Two-digit number | 10x + y | tens digit x, units digit y
Reversed number | 10y + x | 
Speed downstream | u + v | u boat, v stream
Speed upstream | u - v |

Q: When is a pair of linear equations consistent?
A: When it has at least one solution.
Q: What is an inconsistent pair?
A: A pair with no solution (parallel lines).
Q: What is a dependent pair?
A: A consistent pair with infinitely many solutions (coincident lines).
Q: What does the graph of a linear equation in two variables look like?
A: A straight line.
Q: What condition gives a unique solution?
A: a1/a2 is not equal to b1/b2.
Q: What condition gives no solution?
A: a1/a2 = b1/b2 but not equal to c1/c2.
Q: What condition gives infinitely many solutions?
A: a1/a2 = b1/b2 = c1/c2.
Q: Name the algebraic methods to solve a pair of linear equations.
A: Substitution, elimination and cross-multiplication.
Q: How is the solution found graphically?
A: It is the point where the two lines intersect.
Q: How do you check a solution?
A: Substitute it in both equations.
