# Dust of Snow
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english dust of snow
@study
"Dust of Snow" by Robert Frost is a short eight-line poem in two stanzas. The speaker is in a bad mood when a crow shakes some snow from a hemlock tree onto him. This small natural event changes his mood and saves part of a day he had regretted. The poem shows that nature has the power to heal sadness through simple things. The crow and hemlock, usually symbols of sorrow and poison, bring joy and hope instead.
## Deep dive: summary
A crow sitting on a hemlock tree shakes snow onto the speaker. The simple event cheers him and rescues part of a day he had felt was wasted.
## Deep dive: symbols
Crow and hemlock normally suggest gloom, yet they change the speaker's mood.
## Deep dive: theme
Nature has the power to heal and renew.
## Deep dive: devices
Imagery, symbolism, contrast, and simple rhyme.
