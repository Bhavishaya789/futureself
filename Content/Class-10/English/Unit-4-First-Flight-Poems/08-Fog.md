# Fog
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english fog
@study
Carl Sandburg's very short poem "Fog" compares fog to a cat. The fog comes silently on little cat feet, sits looking over the harbour and city on silent haunches, and then moves on. The metaphor of a cat captures the stillness and sudden disappearance of fog. The poem is an example of imagist poetry with few words and a strong image.
## Deep dive: summary
Fog arrives quietly like a cat, rests over the harbour and city, then moves on.
## Deep dive: metaphor
Cat feet represent silent movement; sitting on haunches shows stillness; moving on shows impermanence.
## Deep dive: style
Imagist free verse in six lines.
