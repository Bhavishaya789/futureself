# The Trees
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the trees
@study
In "The Trees", Adrienne Rich describes trees in the forest moving towards the house. Through the night, the trees stir; they are no longer silent, and they push to go outside. Their roots are tangled and the leaves seek their own space. The poet is in the house, and the trees, like women in confined spaces, struggle for freedom. At the end the moon is up and the trees seem to be going out into the forest again. The poem symbolises the desire of women, or any living creature, to escape confinement and be free.
## Deep dive: summary
At night the trees inside the house struggle to go back to the forest. Their leaves and roots strain outward. The moon watches their escape.
## Deep dive: symbolism
Trees stand for living beings, especially women, who long for freedom from confinement.
## Deep dive: theme
Nature's desire for freedom and the need for liberty for all.
## Deep dive: devices
Personification, imagery, symbolism and free verse.
