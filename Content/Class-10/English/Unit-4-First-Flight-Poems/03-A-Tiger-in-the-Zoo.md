# A Tiger in the Zoo
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english a tiger in the zoo
@study
Leslie Norris's poem contrasts a tiger in a zoo cage with one in the wild. In the cage, the tiger moves with soft strides, stares into the dark and stalks around the bars, full of anger and a thirst for freedom. In his imagination, he stalks the jungle where he would hunt near the village at night; but now he is confined, ignoring the sights of visitors and the sounds of cars on the zoo's outside road. The poem draws sympathy for captive animals and criticises caging wild creatures.
## Deep dive: summary
A tiger pacing in a zoo cage is full of rage and longing. In imagination he roams the forest.
## Deep dive: contrast
Captive: soft footsteps, silent fury. Wild: power, hunting near villages and moving freely.
## Deep dive: message
Keeping wild animals in cages is cruel; they belong in their habitats.
## Deep dive: devices
Contrast, imagery, symbolism and personification.
