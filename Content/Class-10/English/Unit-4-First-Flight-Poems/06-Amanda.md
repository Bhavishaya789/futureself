# Amanda!
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english amanda!
@study
"Amanda!" by Robin Klein is a humorous poem showing a child, Amanda, who is constantly given instructions and scolded by an adult: do not bite your nails, sit up straight, do not slouch, eat your food. The child daydreams of freedom, imagining herself as a mermaid in a lagoon, and as an orphan living alone with no parents to scold her, like Rapunzel with her long hair. The poem is a gentle comment on how adults and children see the world and how constant nagging makes a child long for freedom.
## Deep dive: summary
Amanda is told what to do all day. She imagines herself as a mermaid and as an orphan like Rapunzel to escape.
## Deep dive: voices
The adult's commands are interspersed with Amanda's dreams, showing two views.
## Deep dive: theme
Children need freedom and imagination; constant correction hurts.
## Deep dive: devices
Contrast, humour, repetition, imagery and rhyme.
