# The Tale of Custard the Dragon
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the tale of custard the dragon
@study
Ogden Nash's ballad tells of Belinda, who lived in a little white house with a black kitten called Ink, a grey mouse called Blink, a yellow dog called Mustard and a dragon called Custard. Belinda was brave as a barrelful of bears and so were Ink, Blink and Mustard, who were all bold, but Custard the dragon cried for a safe cage and was a coward. One day a pirate came; everyone ran away except Custard, who fought and gobbled up the pirate. Everybody praised him but he said he was still a coward and wished for a safe cage.
## Deep dive: the characters
Belinda, Ink, Blink and Mustard boast about bravery. Custard, the dragon, admits he wants a cage.
## Deep dive: the pirate
When a pirate arrives, everyone runs except Custard, who destroys him.
## Deep dive: irony and moral
The one called coward saves everyone. He continues to call himself a coward, so the poem mocks both bravado and labels.
## Deep dive: devices
Simile, alliteration, rhyme, humour, ballad form.
