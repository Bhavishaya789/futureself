# How to Tell Wild Animals
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english how to tell wild animals
@study
Carolyn Wells's humorous poem gives funny tips on identifying wild animals by their traits, such as a roar for the lion, the spots on a leopard, a hug from a bear, the laughter of a hyena and the tears of a crocodile. It also mentions the Asian (Bengal) tiger, noting the safest way to learn is by seeing the animal is at a distance, though the speaker admits that if you meet them you will not live to tell. The poem is a parody of guidebooks with a comic tone.
## Deep dive: summary
The poem tells how to identify animals: the lion by roar, leopard by spots, bear by hug, hyena by laughter, and crocodile by tears. The Asian tiger is recognised by how the poet ends up.
## Deep dive: humour
The advice is absurd: identifying by hug or laugh is dangerous. The poem parodies natural-history guides.
## Deep dive: devices
Irony, exaggeration, rhyming couplets, and allusion to proverbs.
