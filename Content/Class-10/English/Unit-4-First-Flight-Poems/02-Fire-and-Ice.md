# Fire and Ice
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english fire and ice
@study
In "Fire and Ice", Robert Frost says some people believe the world will end in fire while others say ice. Based on his experience of desire, he favours fire; but if the world had to perish twice, he knows enough of hate to say that ice is also great and would suffice. Fire stands for desire and passion while ice stands for hatred and indifference. Both can destroy humanity. The poem is a warning against uncontrolled desire and hatred.
## Deep dive: summary
Frost compares two possible ends of the world. Fire stands for desire; ice for hatred. He favours fire from experience but says ice is equally destructive.
## Deep dive: symbols
Fire: greed, lust, anger. Ice: cold hatred, indifference. Both lead to destruction.
## Deep dive: message
Human emotions have the power to destroy; we must control them.
## Deep dive: devices
Symbolism, understatement, brevity and rhyme.
