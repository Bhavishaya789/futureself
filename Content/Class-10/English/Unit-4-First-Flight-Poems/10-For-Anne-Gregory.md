# For Anne Gregory
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english for anne gregory
@study
W. B. Yeats's poem is a conversation between a young man and a girl, Anne Gregory. The young man says that young men would not love her for herself because of her beautiful yellow hair, as hair hides what she really is. Anne says she can dye her hair brown, black or carrot and then they will love her for herself alone. The speaker replies that he has found a text saying only God, and not a young man, can love you for yourself alone and not for your yellow hair. The poem shows the difference between true love and love for outward beauty.
## Deep dive: summary
A young Anne Gregory is told that men love her only for her yellow hair. She says she will dye it. The speaker says only God can love one for oneself.
## Deep dive: theme
Love should be based on character; outer beauty is superficial.
## Deep dive: devices
Dialogue, irony, symbolism of hair, and simple rhymes.
