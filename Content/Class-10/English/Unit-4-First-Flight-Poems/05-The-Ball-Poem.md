# The Ball Poem
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the ball poem
@study
In "The Ball Poem" by John Berryman, the poet watches a little boy whose ball has bounced away and rolled into the water. The boy is deeply upset. The poet thinks of how the boy will learn through loss. He says that money cannot replace the ball because such loss is personal, and that the boy must learn to cope with loss and take responsibility. Standing there, the boy understands that things get lost and growing up involves handling grief. The poem teaches the first lesson in life: how to stand up after loss.
## Deep dive: summary
A boy loses his ball in the water. The poet reflects that the boy must learn to bear loss; money cannot replace lost things.
## Deep dive: symbolism
Ball: childhood and innocence. Harbour: the larger world of experiences.
## Deep dive: message
Growing up means learning to face loss with courage and responsibility.
## Deep dive: form
A reflective free-verse poem with a sympathetic tone.
