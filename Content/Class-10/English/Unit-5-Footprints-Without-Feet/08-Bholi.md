# Bholi
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english bholi
@study
K. A. Abbas's story tells of Sulekha, nicknamed Bholi (simpleton), the youngest daughter of Ramlal, the village Numberdar. She had smallpox as a child and had marks and a stammer. She was neglected until a school was set up in the village, and she was sent to school because her parents could not find a husband for her. Under a kind teacher she learned to read and write and grew confident. When Bishamber, a greedy groom, demanded dowry, she refused to marry him and decided to teach in her village school.
## Deep dive: summary
Neglected Sulekha, called Bholi, learns at school and becomes confident. When her groom demands dowry she refuses and becomes a teacher.
## Deep dive: themes
Education, self-respect, dowry as an evil, and the role of a kind teacher.
## Deep dive: character
Bholi is shy at first but later bold, wise and independent.
