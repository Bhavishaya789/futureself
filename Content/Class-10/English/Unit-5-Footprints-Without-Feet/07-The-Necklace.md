# The Necklace
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the necklace
@study
Guy de Maupassant's story is about Matilda Loisel, a pretty woman who is unhappy with her middle-class life. Her husband gets an invitation to a ball; she borrows a diamond necklace from her rich friend Madame Forestier. At the ball she is the centre of attraction but loses the necklace on the way home. They buy a replacement for 36,000 francs by borrowing money and spend ten years in hardship to repay. When Matilda meets Madame Forestier she learns that the original necklace was only paste worth 500 francs. The story shows vanity, pride, and irony.
## Deep dive: summary
Matilda borrows a necklace, loses it, and replaces it at the cost of years of hardship. She learns that the original was paste.
## Deep dive: irony
Her desire for show leads to ruin, and the original necklace was nearly worthless.
## Deep dive: themes
Vanity, honesty, discontent and consequences of appearances.
