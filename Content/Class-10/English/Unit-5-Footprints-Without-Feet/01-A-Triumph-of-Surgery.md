# A Triumph of Surgery
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english a triumph of surgery
@study
James Herriot, a veterinary surgeon, tells about Tricki, a small Pekingese dog belonging to rich Mrs Pumphrey. She overfeeds and pampers him with cream cakes, chocolates and biscuits, and he becomes very fat and lazy. Herriot is called when the dog loses appetite and is breathless. He advises exercise and puts Tricki in the hospital with other dogs, and prescribes a strict diet. After three weeks, Tricki becomes healthy and playful. Mrs Pumphrey, who believed that Herriot had performed an operation, praised him as a great surgeon. The story is humorous and shows the harmful effects of over-pampering.
## Deep dive: summary
Mrs Pumphrey's overfeeding made her dog Tricki ill. Herriot kept him on a diet and in the company of other dogs, and after three weeks Tricki recovered.
## Deep dive: irony
Mrs Pumphrey believes a surgical miracle has occurred, though no surgery was done.
## Deep dive: message
Over-pampering harms pets; proper diet and exercise heal.
