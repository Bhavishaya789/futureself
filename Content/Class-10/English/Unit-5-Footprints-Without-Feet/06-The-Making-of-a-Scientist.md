# The Making of a Scientist
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the making of a scientist
@study
Robert W. Peterson's biography-style essay tells of Richard Ebright, who from childhood was curious, a collector of butterflies, a reader, and an experimenter. His mother encouraged him and he read books, joined the Adult Butterfly Collectors; in school he studied monarch butterflies and won a prize. He studied the cause of the golden spots on the pupa in an experiment, and with the help of Dr Lincoln Brower, he published the result. He later studied at Harvard. The text shows how curiosity, hard work, patience and encouragement make a scientist.
## Deep dive: childhood
Richard was curious, collected butterflies and read widely, encouraged by his mother who gave him tools.
## Deep dive: the project
He studied monarch butterflies and found why spots appeared, with help from Dr Brower.
## Deep dive: qualities
Curiosity, patience, hard work, and support make a scientist.
