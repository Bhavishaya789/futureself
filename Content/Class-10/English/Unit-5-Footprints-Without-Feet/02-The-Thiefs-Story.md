# The Thief's Story
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the thief's story
@study
Ruskin Bond's story is about Hari Singh, a fifteen-year-old thief who meets Anil, a kind young writer, in a railway station. Hari pretends to be an orphan and wins Anil's trust, becoming his servant and learning to read and write. Hari plans to rob Anil, who has just received money for a story. He steals the cash, goes to the station, and then returns. Hari feels guilty and cannot leave. He puts the money back and returns, then decides not to steal again. Anil, who knew all along, gives him an opportunity to reform.
## Deep dive: summary
Hari, a thief, wins Anil's trust by posing as an orphan, learns to read, then tries to rob him. Overcome by guilt he returns the money and decides to reform.
## Deep dive: character
Hari is cunning but sensitive; Anil is kind, trusting and wise.
## Deep dive: theme
Trust, conscience and the power of kindness to reform.
