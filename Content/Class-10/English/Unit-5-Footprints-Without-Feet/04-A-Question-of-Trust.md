# A Question of Trust
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english a question of trust
@study
Victor Gollancz's story is about Horace Danby, a respectable lock-maker who has been a thief once a year to finance his love of rare books. He plans a robbery at Shotover Grange. While he is in the house, a young woman appears and he trusts her story; she turns out to be a thief herself. She takes the jewels, leaves him to be caught, and he is arrested. His trust in a stranger is his undoing.
## Deep dive: summary
Horace Danby robs a house to buy books, trusts a lady he meets inside and is locked in the safe room by her. She turns out to be a thief and the police arrest him.
## Deep dive: irony
A thief is outwitted by another thief; his trust is his downfall.
## Deep dive: theme
Question of trust and the dangers of blind faith.
