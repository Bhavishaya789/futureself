# The Book That Saved the Earth
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the book that saved the earth
@study
Claire Boiko's one-act play is set in the year 2200 and is a humorous science-fiction story told from the point of view of Martians. The Martian Captain, Noodle, and the Think-Tank (a talking computer) are sent to invade Earth. A Martian invader who found a book (Mother Goose rhymes) in a library misunderstood it as a violent book. The Think-Tank interprets the nursery rhymes, such as "Hey Diddle Diddle" and "Humpty Dumpty", as threats. The Martians fear Earth's power and retreat. The play satirises how misinterpretation causes fear and war.
## Deep dive: summary
In 2200, Martians prepare to invade Earth. They find a nursery-rhyme book and think it is Earth's war manual. Fearing Earth's power, they retreat.
## Deep dive: satire
The play mocks misreading, overconfidence and war.
## Deep dive: message
Books and knowledge protect civilisation; ignorance causes needless conflict.
