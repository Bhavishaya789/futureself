# The Midnight Visitor
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the midnight visitor
@study
Robert Arthur's story is a detective tale about Ausable, a clever secret agent, and Max, a rival agent. Fowler, a young writer, thinks Ausable is a boring man. Ausable meets Fowler in a hotel room, expecting a visit from the rival agent Max, who wants a secret paper. When Max arrives at midnight with a gun, Ausable cleverly outwits him with a bold trick and keeps the secret paper safe, showing great presence of mind. The story shows presence of mind.
## Deep dive: summary
Ausable, a cool-headed agent, outwits Max, who comes at midnight to steal a paper. Ausable bluffs, and then retrieves his safe paper.
## Deep dive: characters
Ausable: clever, calm. Max: greedy and rash. Fowler: naive observer.
## Deep dive: theme
Presence of mind and intelligence defeat violence.
