# Footprints Without Feet
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english footprints without feet
@study
H. G. Wells's story is about Griffin, a scientist who discovers how to make himself invisible but cannot reverse the process. Griffin is the main character in "Footprints without Feet". In the story, Griffin steals clothes, causes chaos in the village, steals money and tries to recover his belongings. He is invisible but his footprints are visible in mud and snow. He also needs to find ways to eat and keep warm. In the end the story shows the loneliness, selfishness and moral problems of invisibility.
## Deep dive: summary
Griffin, made invisible by experiment, causes chaos but cannot hide his footprints and faces cold and hunger.
## Deep dive: theme
Misuse of science and the loneliness of power.
## Deep dive: devices
Science fiction, humour, irony and suspense.
