# Literary Devices and Literature Answers
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english literary devices and literature answers
@study
Literary devices are techniques writers use to create effect. Important devices are simile, metaphor, personification, alliteration, onomatopoeia, hyperbole, irony, imagery, symbolism, repetition, oxymoron, transferred epithet and rhyme. Poetic terms: stanza, couplet, quatrain, refrain, rhyme scheme, free verse, ballad, lyric and sonnet. For literature questions, use the formula: answer the question directly, support with evidence from the text, and relate to theme or character. Extract-based answers should identify the speaker, context and significance. Long answers need an introduction, 2-3 points with textual support and a conclusion.
## Deep dive: devices
Simile (like/as), metaphor (is), personification, alliteration, onomatopoeia, hyperbole, irony, imagery, symbolism, repetition, oxymoron, transferred epithet.
## Deep dive: poetry terms
Stanza, couplet, quatrain, refrain, rhyme scheme, free verse, ballad, lyric.
## Deep dive: answering
Short: one or two sentences with evidence. Long: introduction, points with support, conclusion. Extract: speaker, context, explanation and significance.

Q: What is a literary device?
A: A technique used by a writer to create effect.
Q: What is a simile?
A: A comparison using like or as.
Q: What is a metaphor?
A: A direct comparison without like or as.
Q: What is personification?
A: Giving human qualities to non-human things.
Q: What is alliteration?
A: Repetition of the same initial consonant sound.
Q: What is onomatopoeia?
A: A word that imitates a sound.
Q: What is hyperbole?
A: Deliberate exaggeration.
Q: What is irony?
A: Saying or meaning the opposite of what is expected.
Q: What is imagery?
A: Language that creates pictures in the mind.
Q: What is symbolism?
A: Using an object to represent an idea.
Q: What is repetition?
A: Repeating a word or phrase for effect.
Q: What is oxymoron?
A: Combination of contradictory words.
Q: What is a transferred epithet?
A: Applying an adjective to the wrong noun.
Q: What is alliteration in "Peter Piper picked"?
A: Repetition of the p sound.
Q: What is a pun?
A: Wordplay using words with double meaning.
Q: What is a stanza?
A: A group of lines in a poem.
Q: What is a couplet?
A: Two rhyming lines.
Q: What is a quatrain?
A: A four-line stanza.
Q: What is a refrain?
A: A repeated line or phrase.
Q: What is a rhyme scheme?
A: The pattern of rhyming end sounds.
Q: What is free verse?
A: Poetry without regular rhyme or metre.
Q: What is a ballad?
A: A narrative poem in simple verse.
Q: What is a lyric?
A: A short poem expressing feelings.
Q: What is a sonnet?
A: A fourteen-line poem.
Q: What is an extract-based question?
A: A question on a passage taken from the text.
Q: What should you identify in an extract?
A: Speaker, context, meaning and significance.
