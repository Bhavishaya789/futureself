# Reading Comprehension and Vocabulary
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english reading comprehension and vocabulary
@study
The reading section tests understanding of unseen factual, discursive and literary passages through multiple-choice questions, short answers, vocabulary-in-context, inference and main-idea questions. Skills: skim for the main idea, scan for specific details, read the questions first, locate evidence in the text, and infer meaning from context. Vocabulary includes synonyms, antonyms, prefixes, suffixes, idioms, one-word substitutes and commonly confused words. Answers should be brief and in your own words, based only on the passage, with correct grammar.
## Deep dive: strategies
Read questions first, skim for the main idea, scan for evidence, and answer in your own words. For vocabulary-in-context, test each option in the sentence.
## Deep dive: question types
MCQs, short answers, inference, vocabulary, true/false, title. Support answers with evidence.
## Deep dive: vocabulary
Synonyms, antonyms, prefixes (un-, re-, dis-), suffixes (-ness, -ment), idioms and one-word substitutes broaden word power.
