# Analytical Paragraph
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english analytical paragraph
@study
An analytical paragraph (about 100 to 120 words) presents a clear, concise analysis of the data shown in a chart, graph, table or map. Steps: read the title and labels, note the highest and lowest values, trends, comparisons and percentages, write a topic sentence, give 3 to 4 key observations with figures, make a comparison or conclusion, and avoid personal opinions. Use present tense for general facts and past tense for past years, and link ideas with connectors such as whereas, while, however, in contrast, overall and therefore. Useful phrases: "increased steadily", "declined sharply", "remained constant", "accounts for", "the majority of", "comparatively".
## Deep dive: steps
Read the title; note highest, lowest and trends; compute percentages if needed; write topic sentence; add 3-4 key details with connectors; end with a conclusion.
## Deep dive: language
Increase: rose, grew. Decrease: fell, declined. Constant: remained steady. Compare with: whereas, while, in contrast.
## Deep dive: caution
Use only given data; avoid personal opinion; keep within 120 words.
