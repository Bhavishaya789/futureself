# Formal Letter Writing
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english formal letter writing
@study
A formal letter is written to an official, an editor, a principal, a company or the public. The CBSE format has: sender's address, date, receiver's designation and address, subject line, salutation (Sir/Madam), body (introduction, details, request), complimentary close (Yours faithfully) and signature with name. Types are letters of inquiry, placing an order, complaint, application for a job or for a school purpose, and letters to the editor. The language must be polite, concise and to the point, in three paragraphs, with a clear subject line and no abbreviations. Include all details such as quantity, price, delivery and date in orders, and facts, effects and remedy in complaints.
## Deep dive: format
Sender's address; date; receiver's designation and address; Subject; Sir/Madam; body in 3 paragraphs; Yours faithfully; signature and name.
## Deep dive: types
Inquiry: ask for details, rates, delivery. Order: list items, quantity, price, date. Complaint: facts, effects, action. Application: qualifications and availability. Letter to the editor: issue, cause, suggestions.
## Deep dive: tips
Be polite and precise; avoid contractions and slang; write within the word limit; proofread.

Q: What is a formal letter?
A: A letter written to an official or business for a specific purpose.
Q: Name the parts of a formal letter.
A: Sender's address, date, receiver's address, subject, salutation, body, complimentary close and signature.
Q: Where does the sender's address appear?
A: At the top left (or right) corner.
Q: Where is the date placed?
A: Below the sender's address.
Q: What is written in the receiver's address?
A: Designation, organisation and address.
Q: What is the subject line?
A: A short line stating the purpose of the letter.
Q: Which salutation is used when the name is unknown?
A: Sir or Madam.
Q: Which salutation is used for a named person?
A: Dear Mr/Ms (surname).
Q: How many paragraphs should the body have?
A: Usually three.
Q: What does the first paragraph contain?
A: The purpose and a brief introduction.
Q: What does the second paragraph contain?
A: The details, facts and reasons.
Q: What does the third paragraph contain?
A: The request, action expected and thanks.
Q: Which complimentary close is used for "Sir/Madam"?
A: Yours faithfully.
Q: Which complimentary close is used for a named person?
A: Yours sincerely.
Q: Where is the signature placed?
A: After the complimentary close, with the full name.
Q: What is a letter of inquiry?
A: A letter asking for information about products or services.
Q: What is a letter placing an order?
A: A letter ordering goods with quantity, price and delivery details.
Q: What is a complaint letter?
A: A letter reporting a problem and asking for remedy.
Q: What should a complaint include?
A: Facts, inconvenience and requested action.
Q: What is a job application letter?
A: A letter applying for a post with qualifications.
Q: What is a letter to the editor?
A: A letter giving views on a public issue to a newspaper.
Q: Which language style is used?
A: Formal, polite and impersonal.
Q: Are abbreviations acceptable?
A: No, avoid them.
Q: Are contractions acceptable?
A: No.
Q: How should the subject line be written?
A: Brief, underlined and in the form "Sub: ...".
Q: How do you start the body?
A: With "I am writing to...".
Q: How do you close the body?
A: With thanks or "I look forward to your reply."
Q: What is enclosure?
A: A document attached to the letter.
Q: What details go in an order letter?
A: Item, quantity, price, mode of payment and delivery date.
Q: What details go in a complaint letter?
A: Date of purchase, defect and replacement/refund demanded.
Q: What details go in an application?
A: Qualification, experience and contact information.
Q: How is the date written?
A: Day, month and year, such as 10 November 2026.
Q: Should one sign with a pen name?
A: No, use the real name.
Q: What is the word limit?
A: About 120 words.
Q: What do examiners check?
A: Format, content, expression and accuracy.
Q: Is a postscript used?
A: Rarely in formal letters.
Q: Can a formal letter include slang?
A: No.
Q: What tone suits a complaint?
A: Firm yet polite.
Q: What is the purpose of a letter to the editor?
A: To draw public attention to an issue.
Q: What is the format for the receiver in letters to the editor?
A: The Editor, name of the newspaper, address.
