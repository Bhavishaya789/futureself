# The Proposal
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the proposal
@study
"The Proposal" is a one-act farce by Anton Chekhov. Ivan Vassilevitch Lomov, a nervous and hypochondriac landowner, visits his neighbour Stepan Stepanovitch Chubukov to propose to his daughter Natalya Stepanovna. Before he can finish, he starts quarrelling with Natalya over who owns the Oxen Meadows. Chubukov joins in, and Lomov leaves angrily with palpitations. When they hear that he came to propose, Natalya wants him called back. The quarrel resumes over whose dog, Guess or Squeezer, is better. Finally, Chubukov joins their hands and they agree to marry while still arguing. The play satirises marriage for property.
## Deep dive: plot
Lomov comes to propose to Natalya but they quarrel about Oxen Meadows; he faints; after waking he is called back; they quarrel again about dogs; Chubukov joins them and orders champagne.
## Deep dive: characters
Lomov is nervous and sensitive; Natalya is proud and quick-tempered; Chubukov is loud and interfering.
## Deep dive: themes
The play satirises property-driven marriage, pride, and trivial disputes.
## Deep dive: devices
Farce, irony, humour, fast-moving dialogue and comic repetition.
