# From the Diary of Anne Frank
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english from the diary of anne frank
@study
Anne Frank, a Jewish girl from Germany, went into hiding in Amsterdam during the Second World War. She received a diary on her thirteenth birthday and addressed it as "Kitty". In these extracts she writes that paper has more patience than people and that she wants to share her thoughts with a true friend. She describes her family: her father Otto, mother, sister Margot, and her life at school, her friend Jacqueline (Jopie), her interest in Peter, and the restrictions imposed by the Nazis on Jews. The diary shows her loneliness, wit, maturity and need for understanding.
## Deep dive: background
Anne Frank was born in 1929 in Frankfurt and moved to Amsterdam. During Nazi occupation, her family hid from 1942 to 1944. She kept her diary until August 1944.
## Deep dive: reason for the diary
Anne had many friends but no real one; so she writes to Kitty, making it a confidant with whom she can be herself.
## Deep dive: restrictions
Jews had to wear the yellow star, hand in bicycles, not use trams or cars, shop only 3-5 p.m., and be home by 8 p.m.
## Deep dive: character
Anne is lively, witty, observant, and mature beyond her years; her diary becomes a symbol of hope amid persecution.
