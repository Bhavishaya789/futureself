# Two Stories about Flying
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english two stories about flying
@study
This chapter has two parts. In "His First Flight" by Liam O'Flaherty, a young seagull is afraid to fly while his two brothers and sisters have already learned. His parents, brothers and sisters taunt and encourage him. When his mother tempts him with a piece of fish from a distance, he is hungry and finally leaps off the ledge and discovers that he can fly. In "The Black Aeroplane" by Frederick Forsyth, a pilot flying from France to England at night enters a storm. A mysterious black aeroplane appears and guides him; he follows it and lands safely. At the control tower, the lady says no other plane had been in the sky. The pilot realises that his own guidance may have been a mystery.
## Deep dive: His First Flight
The young seagull's fear holds him back; his family cannot persuade him. Hunger and his mother's fish finally make him dive off the ledge. He discovers he can fly. The story shows that fear is overcome by action and encouragement.
## Deep dive: The Black Aeroplane
The pilot flies through a storm with failing instruments. A black plane leads him to safety. At the airport nobody saw another plane, leaving a mystery.
## Deep dive: themes
Overcoming fear, courage under pressure, support from family, and the unexplained.
