# A Letter to God
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english a letter to god
@study
"A Letter to God" by Gregorio Lopez y Fuentes is about Lencho, a poor farmer whose ripe fields of corn and beans are destroyed by a hailstorm after he waits eagerly for the rain. With unshaken faith, he writes a letter to God asking for one hundred pesos to sow his field again. The postmaster, moved by the letter, collects money from employees and friends but manages only seventy pesos. Lencho finds only seventy pesos in the envelope, believes God cannot have made a mistake, and writes again demanding the remaining thirty, blaming the post-office employees as thieves. The story shows faith, human kindness and irony.
## Deep dive: summary
Lencho, a farmer, longs for rain to ripen his corn. A sudden hailstorm ruins everything. He writes to God for one hundred pesos. The postmaster collects seventy pesos and sends them as God's reply. Lencho suspects the post office of stealing the remaining thirty and writes again.
## Deep dive: characters
Lencho is hard-working, simple and has unshakable faith but is also narrow in judgement. The postmaster is kind and imaginative, taking on the role of God to preserve faith.
## Deep dive: themes
Faith, kindness, the helplessness of farmers before nature, and irony: Lencho trusts God but not the people through whom God seems to act.
## Deep dive: literary devices
Similes (raindrops like new coins, hail like frozen silver), irony, and a simple third-person narrative.
