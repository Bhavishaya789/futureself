# The Sermon at Benares
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english the sermon at benares
@study
Gautama Buddha was born as Prince Siddhartha, left his palace, and attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree. His first sermon was at Sarnath near Benares (Varanasi). The chapter narrates the story of Kisa Gotami, a young woman whose only son died. Overcome with grief, she carried the body and begged for medicine. The Buddha asked her to bring a handful of mustard seed from a house where no one had died. She went door to door but found no such house. She realised that death comes to all and accepted it, and then became his disciple. The Buddha taught that life is full of suffering, that desire causes suffering, and that peace comes by following the Eightfold Path.
## Deep dive: background
The Buddha preached his first sermon near Benares and told the story of Kisa Gotami to show that death is universal. His teachings focus on suffering, its cause, its end and the path.
## Deep dive: the story
Kisa Gotami tried to save her dead son. The Buddha asked for mustard seed from a house untouched by death. She discovered that no such home exists and accepted the truth.
## Deep dive: teachings
Life brings suffering; desire is its root; liberation is possible through the Eightfold Path.
## Deep dive: moral
Understanding grief as shared helps us bear it.
