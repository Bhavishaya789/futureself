# Mijbil the Otter
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english mijbil the otter
@study
In this extract from "Ring of Bright Water", Gavin Maxwell tells how he brought a pet otter named Mij from the marshes of Iraq to England. Maxwell had been given the otter by Wilfred Thesiger and brought it in a box to England. During the flight, Mij escaped from the box, caused chaos in the aircraft, and was recaptured. In London, Mij was kept in Maxwell's flat and loved to play with water, a rubber ball and marbles. He was playful, curious, obstinate and affectionate. Maxwell loved him as a pet, and his fascination for water led him to hold the bath tub filled with water.
## Deep dive: the rescue
Maxwell was given an otter in Iraq by Thesiger. He named it Mijbil after the marsh village. He took Mij to England in a box.
## Deep dive: the flight
During the flight Mij escaped, creating chaos. Maxwell recaptured him, showing patience and love.
## Deep dive: life in London
Mij loved water, played with a ball, learnt to respond and enjoyed bathing. His behaviour showed fascination for water.
## Deep dive: themes
Love for animals, patience, humour, and curiosity of nature.
