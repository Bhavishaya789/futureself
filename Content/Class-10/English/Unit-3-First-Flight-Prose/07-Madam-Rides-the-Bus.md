# Madam Rides the Bus
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english madam rides the bus
@study
Vallikkannan's story is about Valli, an eight-year-old girl in a South Indian village who is fascinated by the bus that passes through the village. She saves coins and decides to take a ride to the nearby town and back. She boards the bus, enjoys the scenery, notices people and wonders about the world, and behaves like a mature lady to impress the conductor. On the return journey, she sees a dead cow on the road; this shocks her and ends her joy. The story shows childish enthusiasm, the transition to realism, and the contrast between dreams and harsh truth.
## Deep dive: plot
Valli saves coins, boards the bus in the morning, enjoys her ride and arrives in town, then returns, but sees a dead cow, which changes her mood.
## Deep dive: characters
Valli is curious, bold and sensitive; the conductor treats her kindly; the old woman and passengers reflect different attitudes.
## Deep dive: themes
Childhood curiosity, freedom, change from innocence to awareness, and the harshness of reality.
## Deep dive: devices
Contrast (joy vs sorrow), imagery of rural India, and symbolism of the dead cow.
