# Tenses
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english tenses
@study
A tense shows the time of an action. English has three main times (present, past, future), each with four forms (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous). Simple present: base verb (s/es with he/she/it); present continuous: is/am/are + ing; present perfect: has/have + past participle; simple past: second form; past continuous: was/were + ing; past perfect: had + past participle; simple future: will/shall + base verb; future continuous: will be + ing. Use simple present for habits and universal truths, present perfect for recent actions with present relevance, past perfect for the earlier of two past actions. In board exams, tenses are tested in gap-filling and error correction.
## Deep dive: the twelve tenses
Present: simple (writes), continuous (is writing), perfect (has written), perfect continuous (has been writing). Past: wrote, was writing, had written, had been writing. Future: will write, will be writing, will have written, will have been writing.
## Deep dive: usage
Simple present: habits and facts. Present perfect: recent actions or experience. Simple past: finished actions with time words. Past perfect: earlier past action. Use since for points and for for durations.
## Deep dive: common errors
Don't use continuous forms with state verbs (know, want, love). Use present perfect, not simple past, with already, yet, just. Use the correct subject-auxiliary agreement.
