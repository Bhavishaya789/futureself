# Modals
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english modals
@study
Modal auxiliary verbs (can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would, ought to, need, dare, used to) express ability, permission, possibility, obligation, advice, prohibition, request, offer and probability. A modal is followed by the base form of the verb, has no -s in the third person and no infinitive "to" (except ought to), and forms questions and negatives without do. Can/could show ability; may/might permission and possibility; must obligation and strong deduction; should/ought to advice and moral duty; will/would future and politeness; shall for suggestions and offers with I/we; need not for lack of necessity.
## Deep dive: forms
Modal + base verb: He can swim. Negative: cannot, must not, need not. Question: Can he swim?
## Deep dive: meanings
Ability: can, could. Permission: may, can. Possibility: may, might. Obligation: must, have to. Advice: should, ought to. Request: could, would. Offer: shall, will.
## Deep dive: errors
Never write "can sings" or "to" after modals (except ought to). Don't use two modals together.
