# Active and Passive Voice
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english active and passive voice
@study
In the active voice the subject performs the action; in the passive voice the subject receives the action. To change an active sentence into passive: the object of the active becomes the subject; the verb changes to the form of "be" in the same tense plus the past participle; and the active subject becomes the agent introduced by "by" (often omitted when unknown or unimportant). Structures: simple present - is/am/are + V3; simple past - was/were + V3; present perfect - has/have been + V3; future - will be + V3; present continuous - is/am/are being + V3; past continuous - was/were being + V3; modals - modal + be + V3. Only transitive verbs can be made passive. Imperative sentences use "Let ... be + V3" or "You are requested to". Questions begin with the auxiliary or wh-word in the passive.
## Deep dive: steps
1. Identify subject, verb, object. 2. Move object to subject position. 3. Use be (same tense) + V3. 4. Add by + former subject (if needed). 5. Adjust pronouns (I→me, he→him).
## Deep dive: structures
Present: is/am/are written. Past: was/were written. Perfect: has/have been written. Future: will be written. Continuous: is being written, was being written. Modal: can be written.
## Deep dive: questions and commands
Did he write the letter? → Was the letter written by him? Open the door. → Let the door be opened.
