# Gap Filling, Editing and Omission
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english gap filling, editing and omission
@study
In CBSE Class 10 grammar, three question types test usage: gap filling (choose the right word or form to complete a passage or sentence), editing (find the error in a line and write the correct word, often involving tense, articles, prepositions, agreement, and a word missing), and omission (find the missing word in a line and write it with the word before and after it). Strategies: read the whole passage first; check tense consistency, subject-verb agreement, prepositions, articles, conjunctions and the logical meaning; use the clues given. For sentence reordering, find the opening sentence, link pronouns to nouns, and use connectors.
## Deep dive: gap filling
Read the passage for context and choose forms that fit tense, number and meaning.
## Deep dive: editing
Typical errors: tense, subject-verb agreement, prepositions, articles, wrong word forms. Correct only the marked word.
## Deep dive: omission
A word is missing in each line; write the word before it, the missing word and the word after it.
## Deep dive: reordering
Find the opening line, track pronoun references and connectors, and test the logical flow.
