# Reported Speech
minutes: 30
yt: class 10 english reported speech
@study
Direct speech gives the exact words of a speaker inside quotation marks; indirect (reported) speech reports them without quotes, usually using "that". When the reporting verb is in the past tense, the tense of the reported clause shifts back: present becomes past, present continuous becomes past continuous, present perfect becomes past perfect, simple past becomes past perfect, will becomes would, can becomes could, may becomes might. Pronouns change according to the reporter, and time and place words shift: today to that day, tomorrow to the next day, yesterday to the previous day, now to then, here to there, this to that. For questions, use "asked" and normal word order with if/whether or the question word; for commands, use "ordered/told/requested" with an infinitive; for exclamations, use "exclaimed with joy/sorrow that".
## Deep dive: statements
He said, "I am tired." → He said that he was tired. Pronouns shift by the reporter, tense by backshift, time and place words as per rules.
## Deep dive: questions
She asked, "Where do you live?" → She asked where I lived. Yes-no questions use if/whether.
## Deep dive: commands, requests, exclamations
Commands: ordered/told + to-infinitive. Requests: requested. Exclamations: exclaimed with joy/sorrow that.
