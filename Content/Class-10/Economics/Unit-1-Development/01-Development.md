# Development
minutes: 35
yt: class 10 economics development
@study
Development means improvement in the quality of life, but different people have different goals. A rich farmer, a landless labourer and a girl in a village may each want different things, and goals can conflict (a dam helps some and displaces others). Income alone is not enough; people also seek equal treatment, freedom, security and respect. Countries are compared by per capita income (national income ÷ population) and by the World Bank classification: high income above $12,696 per annum is rich, low income $1,045 or less is low (2016 figures). Averages hide inequality. Other indicators are literacy, infant mortality rate, life expectancy, net attendance ratio and the Human Development Index (HDI) of the UNDP combining health, education and income. Kerala has better human development than Haryana despite lower per capita income because of public services. Sustainable development avoids using up resources that future generations need.
## Deep dive: different goals
A landless labourer wants regular work and higher wages; a rich farmer wants higher support prices and cheap labour; a girl may want equal chances in education. Because goals differ and can conflict, development is judged differently by different people. For a fair view, we look for goals that benefit all.
## Deep dive: national income and averages
National income is total goods and services produced in a year. Dividing by population gives per capita income. The World Bank uses it to classify countries. Averages are useful for comparison but hide inequality.
## Deep dive: other indicators
Health (life expectancy, infant mortality), education (literacy, attendance) and nutrition (BMI) matter. The UNDP HDI combines these with income. Kerala has high human development due to public services even though per capita income is lower than in Haryana.
## Deep dive: sustainability
Groundwater overuse and fossil fuel depletion threaten future generations. Sustainable development uses resources within their renewal limits.
