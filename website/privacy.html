FutureSelf privacy policy

By SpinDigital. Last updated: November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your study data (revision cards, mistakes, notes, flashcards, mock test results, timetable, reminders) is stored on your own device. When you are signed in it is also copied to your account in our database, so the same email shows the same data on your PC and your phone (see below). Photos of your written answers never leave your device.

What we store if you create an account

  • Your name, email address and a password hash (we never store your password).
  • Settings you sync between your devices, such as your PC and phone link.
  • Your study data while you are signed in: XP and daily study log, revision cards and notes, mock scores, exam date and goal, lessons done and quiz scores, timetable blocks and study sessions. It is stored under your account only, sent over HTTPS, and only you can read it. You can delete it any time with Delete my cloud data on the Profile page, or by emailing us, and it is removed when your account is deleted.

Parent or teacher link (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on, we store a summary of your study progress (streak, study minutes, answer accuracy, weak topic names, mock test scores, exam name and date, and the first name you set). Anyone who has your private link can see it. We do not include your email, notes, or questions. You can turn the link off at any time and it stops working immediately.

AI features

When you use the AI tutor, AI practice questions, or the answer checker, the text of your question and the lesson text it relates to are sent to our server and then to an AI provider (Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude as a fallback) to produce the reply. Do not type personal information into these boxes. We do not use your questions to advertise to you.

Crash and usage reports (you can turn them off)

The app can send anonymous error messages (with emails and tokens removed), the app version and platform, and a once-a-day "app opened" count so we can fix bugs and see how many people use the app. Turn this off in Study Hub, Report. These reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Phone permissions

The Android app asks for notifications and exact alarms (study reminders), and optional accessibility and overlay permissions for the study focus lock. These are used on your device only, to keep you focused. We do not read the content of other apps.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads. Data is processed by our hosting and database providers (Vercel and MongoDB Atlas) and the AI providers named above, only to run the service.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data by emailing us. You can use most of the app without an account.

Children

FutureSelf is for students. If you are under 18, use it with a parent or teacher's guidance.
